## Board decision

All compared methods must be methods for distilling many papers into wiki pages.

Final three-method set:

1. Current: **Packet-gated paper-to-wiki reconciliation** — claim/status-ledger anchored; reconciles existing paper-derived claim/evidence/trust/product artifacts so they do not contradict the wiki story.
2. Added alternative: **Source-first paper adjudication** — source/evidence anchored; adjudicates what the papers support, limit, refute, or leave unknown before claims/prose advance.
3. Added alternative/default framing: **Debate-map-to-wiki rebuild** — debate-map/prose anchored; builds reader-facing wiki prose from the debate map, binding every sentence back to claim/evidence/status caps.

Decision on rejected peer candidates:
- Evaluation-first readiness is a cross-cutting quality gate, not a peer method.
- Display-safety hygiene is a sequencing/safety phase, not a peer method.

Recommended default next framing: **Debate-map-to-wiki rebuild**, with source-first adjudication for disputed evidence and packet-gated reconciliation for safe product/claim carry-through.
