URLs:
- https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/86640?hl=en
- https://policies.google.com/terms?hl=en-US
Accessed: 2026-07-14
Product/version: Google Search Help; Google Terms effective 2024-05-22

> If a network you use ... seems to be sending automated traffic to Google Search, you might get the message “Our systems have detected unusual traffic from your computer network.”

> The message most likely shows a reCAPTCHA.

> You must not ... use automated means to access content from any of our services in violation of the machine-readable instructions on our web pages.

Scope caveat: Search traffic guidance is not Flow/Gemini-specific. It supports a challenge blast-radius concern, not a product-specific concurrency prohibition.
