# Gemini Deep Research background/concurrency evidence
URL: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop
Accessed: 2026-07-14
Product/version: Gemini Apps Deep Research Help current on access date

> In Gemini Apps, there are limits for: Daily research requests; Number of research requests you can run at the same time.

> While you wait for the report, you can leave the chat.

> It usually takes about 5-10 minutes to generate the report since Gemini analyzes many sources.

Verdict: VERIFIED that Gemini Deep Research supports more than one research request running at the same time, subject to product limits, and that a run continues after leaving its chat. This does not establish cross-product Flow+Gemini concurrency or safe simultaneous browser automation.
