- https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353334?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop
Accessed: 2026-07-14
Product/version: Google Flow Help current on access date

> In your Google Flow app, get messages about background successes, failures, and partial generation.

> If you close the app, Google Flow sends a generic push notification to your phone's lock screen.

> You can ask the Agent to create multiple variations of a video at once.

Verdict: VERIFIED that Flow generation can continue in the background after closing the mobile app, and that Flow supports a single request producing multiple variations. The public pages reviewed do not state a limit for multiple independent Flow requests running simultaneously or cross-product Flow+Gemini concurrency.
