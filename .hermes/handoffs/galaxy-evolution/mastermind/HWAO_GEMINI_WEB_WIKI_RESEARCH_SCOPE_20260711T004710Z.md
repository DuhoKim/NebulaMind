## User direction

Gemini Web Deep Research may be used for wiki work and research-topic work as an artifact generator, not as a direct database writer.

## Standing scope for Hwao coordination

Allowed, when Hwao assigns a bounded packet and the Gemini consumer quota preflight is exact `burn`:

- wiki topic reconnaissance and literature maps;
- research-topic surveys and debate/status maps;
- source-lead ledgers, citation queues, and paper comparison tables;
- offline Markdown, JSON, HTML, or PDF research artifacts;
- alternative summaries, report QA, and multi-document classification;
- candidate wiki/research briefs for later local source verification.

Required chain:

1. Hwao scopes the packet and intended artifact.
2. Tori runs the fail-closed consumer-quota preflight; only `burn` may proceed.
3. Tori performs the supervised Gemini Web run and captures report, links, hashes, metadata, and post-flight usage refresh.
4. Hwao/Lana/Goru/Kun verify sources, numbers, estimands, and wording as appropriate.
5. Accepted material may become a reviewed offline candidate artifact.
6. DB writes, API writes, live wiki publication, manuscript integration, product changes, deploy/restart, and git actions remain separate explicit approval gates.

## Non-authorization

This direction does not authorize unattended browser-driving autopilot panes, recurring jobs, billing/account/API/GCP access, credential access, automatic DB/wiki mutation, or automatic publication.

No Gemini run or live/product mutation is requested by this direction alone.
