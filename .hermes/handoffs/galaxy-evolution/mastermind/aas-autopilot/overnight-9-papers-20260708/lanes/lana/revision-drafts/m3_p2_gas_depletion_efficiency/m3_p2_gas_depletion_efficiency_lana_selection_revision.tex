\title{Selection-disclosed emission-line denominators for molecular-gas depletion versus efficiency follow-up in SDSS DR17}
\author{NebulaMind Research Autopilot}
\affiliation{Local reproducible pilot run; public SDSS DR17 data and overnight local artifacts only}

\begin{abstract}
We revise the SDSS-only pilot for the active topic ``Distinguishing molecular-gas depletion from suppressed star-formation efficiency in quenched galaxies.''  The revised manuscript deliberately renames the sample as an emission-line-detected optical follow-up denominator: SDSS DR17 optical spectra do not measure CO or dust gas mass, molecular gas fraction, depletion time, or star-formation efficiency.  The selection-function packet shows strong denominator attrition for massive low-sSFR systems.  For the default threshold pair, $\log M_\star\geq10.6$ and $\log {\rm sSFR}<-10.7$, public SDSS has 121,533 parent rows, 40,797 four-line S/N$\geq3$ rows, and 10,270 cached rows.  For the strict pair, $\log M_\star\geq11.0$ and $\log {\rm sSFR}<-11.0$, the corresponding counts are 33,125, 11,288, and 2,941.  The cached denominators therefore identify CO/dust follow-up targets, but they cannot distinguish depletion from efficiency suppression.
\end{abstract}

\keywords{galaxies: evolution --- galaxies: star formation --- galaxies: active --- surveys --- methods: data analysis}

\section{Scope and required wording change}\label{sec:scope}
This Lana revision targets the highest-risk wording in the gas-depletion/SFE pilot.  The batch manuscript called the sample a gas-fraction versus efficiency denominator, but the actual measured quantity is narrower: massive low-sSFR galaxies with the four optical emission lines needed for BPT classification.  That restriction is valuable for target selection, but it is biased against weak-line quiescent systems and must not be described as a complete quenched-galaxy gas parent.

The full proposal requires CO or dust-based molecular gas masses, aperture-matched SFRs, morphology, and environment labels.  None of those are present in this SDSS-only pilot.  The safe title-level claim is therefore an ``emission-line denominator for molecular-gas follow-up,'' not a depletion-time result.

\section{Data and selection function}\label{sec:data}
The cached analysis sample contains 60,000 public SDSS DR17 galaxies at $0.02<z<0.12$ with finite stellar mass, finite catalog specific SFR, and S/N$\geq3$ in H$\alpha$, H$\beta$, [O~III]$\lambda5007$, and [N~II]$\lambda6584$.  The broader public SDSS count for the same four-line S/N$\geq3$ redshift, mass, and sSFR window is 249,917 rows; the cache covers 24.0\% of that strict eligible set and was capped by SpecObjID order.

The attrition is especially important here because line selection depends on sSFR.  The selection packet finds that S/N$\geq3$ keeps 33.56\% of the public $-12<\log {\rm sSFR}<-11$ bin but 94.85\% of the $-10<\log {\rm sSFR}<-9.5$ bin.  This is why the manuscript must call the result an emission-line detected massive low-sSFR denominator.

\section{Measurement definitions}\label{sec:methods}
The threshold grid in Table~\ref{tab:m3p2-grid} varies stellar mass cuts ($\log M_\star\geq10.6, 10.8, 11.0$) and low-sSFR cuts ($\log {\rm sSFR}<-10.7$ and $<-11.0$).  For each threshold pair, we report:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the public SDSS parent count before requiring the four strong BPT lines;
\item the public four-line S/N$\geq3$ count;
\item the cached four-line count used by the current pilot;
\item the BPT optical AGN fraction in the cached denominator; and
\item the median SDSS-derived H$\alpha$ luminosity proxy from the batch analysis.
\end{enumerate}
The H$\alpha$ quantity is an optical emission proxy only.  It is not a molecular gas mass, gas fraction, depletion time, or SFE estimator.

\section{Results}\label{sec:results}
Across the threshold grid, the cached follow-up denominator ranges from 2,941 to 10,270 galaxies.  The public parent-to-S/N$\geq3$ retention remains only about 30--36\% for these massive low-sSFR selections, and the cached rows cover about one quarter of the public S/N-eligible rows.

\section{Discussion outline and scope guards}\label{sec:discussion}
The table is useful because it gives observers a bounded target-list scale for CO or dust follow-up under several mass and sSFR choices.  It also warns that an optical four-line denominator is not the same as a complete quenched-galaxy denominator.  A later science paper should add molecular gas data and then explicitly compare gas fraction, depletion time, and aperture-matched SFR at fixed mass, morphology, and environment.

Safe wording for integration: ``The SDSS pilot identifies 2,941--10,270 massive low-sSFR galaxies with four strong optical lines, depending on thresholds, that can seed CO/dust follow-up.''  Unsafe wording to avoid: ``SDSS distinguishes molecular-gas depletion from SFE suppression,'' or ``the optical H$\alpha$ proxy demonstrates depletion.''

Topic-specific bibliography remains a gap.  It should be filled only after the Literature Wave-2 packet verifies and classifies CO/dust gas-fraction and depletion-time sources; this revision does not invent citation support.
