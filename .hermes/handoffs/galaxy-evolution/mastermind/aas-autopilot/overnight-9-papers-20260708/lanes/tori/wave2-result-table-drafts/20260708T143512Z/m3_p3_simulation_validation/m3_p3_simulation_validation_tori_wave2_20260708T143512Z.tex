    \begin{abstract}
    We add the full 15-cell observed target vector to the simulation-validation pilot.  The SDSS cells span quenched fraction 0.001--0.856 and BPT optical AGN fraction 0.001--0.610 across mass and redshift.  These are observed validation targets only; no cosmological feedback model is accepted or rejected without forward-modelled mocks.
    \end{abstract}

    \section{Scope and data}
    This Wave-2 draft improves the active consolidated pilot for ``Forward-modelled validation of cosmological feedback prescriptions'' by replacing a short itemized result with an explicit result table.  It remains an SDSS-only denominator/proxy manuscript.  The full topic still requires simulation catalogs passed through SDSS/MaNGA/ALMA/X-ray/radio selection functions, aperture models, and noise models before any model-comparison claim.

    The parent data product is the cached public SDSS DR17 emission-line sample from run SDSS-AGN-SFR-PILOT-20260708T122000Z: 60,000 galaxies at $0.02<z<0.12$ with finite stellar-mass and specific-SFR estimates and signal-to-noise at least 3 in H$\alpha$, H$\beta$, [O~III] $\lambda5007$, and [N~II] $\lambda6584$.  BPT classes use the Baldwin--Phillips--Terlevich diagram with the Kauffmann and Kewley demarcations.  Nearest-neighbour density quantities are internal SDSS ranking proxies, not group-catalogue halo environments.
