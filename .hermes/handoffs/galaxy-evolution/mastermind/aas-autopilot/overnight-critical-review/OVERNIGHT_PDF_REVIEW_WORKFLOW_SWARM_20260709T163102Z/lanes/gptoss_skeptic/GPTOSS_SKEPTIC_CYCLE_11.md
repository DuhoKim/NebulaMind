### 6. Workflow / System Notes  

* **Carry‑forward Persistence:** The same **major figure‑caption** and **table‑size** problems were already flagged in Cycle 10. The fact that they persisted into Cycle 11 indicates a missing *gate* in the candidate‑copy pipeline where “previous‑cycle issues → must‑be‑fixed before new copy is generated” is not enforced.  

* **Suggested Guardrail:** Introduce a **pre‑commit checklist** that automatically scans the TeX for known placeholder patterns (`fig‑topic.pdf`, `TODO`, `\#\#\#` etc.) and aborts the “candidate‑copy” generation if any are found.  

* **Reproducibility Traceability:** Each paper’s key numbers are present in the `itemize` block, but the **raw numbers are not also stored in a machine‑readable CSV** that the PDF‑writing pilot could ingest. Adding a small `results.csv` (with columns: `paper, metric, value, error`) to the candidate package would make downstream automation safer.  

* **Documentation Consistency:** The `\section{Data Availability}` blocks are duplicated in many papers. Consolidating them into a shared macro (`\newcommand{\DataAvail}{…}`) would reduce the risk of divergence.
