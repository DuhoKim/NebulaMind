\begin{abstract}
This supplement packages eight denominator/proxy notes from the selection-aware SDSS BPT/sSFR pilot into one optical baseline atlas for future real-data follow-up. All counts and fractions remain conditional on a fixed 60,000-galaxy optical cache selected sequentially by \texttt{specObjID}; the atlas is therefore a baseline-and-checklist document, not a population-complete or causal-mechanism result. It collects environment, maintenance-heating, outflow, radio-jet, mass-bin, tracer, gas-depletion, and simulation-vector notes, while radio, X-ray, CO/HI, halo/group, resolved-outflow, and forward-model observables remain missing.
\end{abstract}

\keywords{galaxies: evolution --- surveys --- catalogs --- methods: observational --- methods: statistical}

\section{Purpose}
The main paper measures an optical association between BPT classification and catalog sSFR. Here, each atlas entry serves one of two roles: it is either an observed optical denominator or a target vector, and it is paired with the specific missing observables required before any physical inference is attempted. The entries span environment, maintenance heating, outflows, jet environments, mass-bin diagnostics, tracer thresholds, gas depletion, and simulation targets, but they share the same selection biases and missing data. For consistency with the flagship, the atlas uses broad optical BPT-selected language when the full optical-emission-line denominator is meant and names specific subsets only when the stricter selection matters. The SDSS/BPT/catalog references document the present optical denominators; the radio/X-ray/CO/HI/outflow/simulation references that appear later are future-data motivation rather than validation of the current measurements.

\section{Shared denominator limitations}
The atlas uses the same public-data backbone as the main paper: a fixed 60,000-galaxy subset. The strict public four-line S/N$\geq3$ parent count of 249,917 galaxies, and the corresponding 24.0\% cache coverage, are selection-context diagnostics rather than custody-backed independent result rows; the present custody receipt inventories the 60,000-row cache and topic JSON artifacts, not the public SQL count logs behind the full parent cascade. The four-line selection is sSFR-dependent and the sample is capped and non-random, so all counts and fractions are conditional denominators rather than population-complete measurements. The galaxy-by-galaxy stellar masses and catalog sSFR values are taken from the public MPA-JHU-style \texttt{galSpecExtra} table after the same SDSS joins used in the flagship \citep{sdssdr17,brinchmann2004,york2000}. Structural quantities such as \(R_{90}/R_{50}\), \texttt{fracDeV}, \texttt{petroR50}, and \texttt{petroR90} were not retained in the 60,000-row cache, so morphology cannot be controlled in this cycle. The fixed 3-arcsec SDSS fiber makes the optical quantities central-fiber proxies rather than global measurements, the strict four-line S/N cuts preferentially remove low-equivalent-width or passive systems, and the 55-arcsec fiber-collision limit distorts projected-neighbor statistics in dense regions. These are denominator limitations, not physical results, and they apply to every subsection below.

Public parent-count cascade values are used only as selection context in this atlas. They should be accompanied by explicit SQL/query receipts before they are promoted to independent measured results in a journal submission.

\section{Atlas notes}
Each atlas entry below follows the same pattern: artifact ID, observed optical baseline, missing observables, and the non-causal boundary. The missing observables listed in Table~\ref{tab:atlas-followup} are required before any physical inference from these optical baselines.

\subsection{Relative neighbor-count baseline: SDSS 10th-neighbor index for low-sSFR incidence}
Artifact \texttt{m1\_rp2\_environment\_quenching/analysis\_results.json} gives the 10th-neighbor projected-rank baseline in the 60,000-galaxy emission-line denominator. The high-index quartile has a low-sSFR emission-line fraction of 0.230 (3,456/15,000), while the low-index quartile has 0.181 (2,710/15,000); the bootstrap high-minus-low interval is [0.041, 0.059], and the adjusted linear-probability coefficient is 0.032 +/- 0.004. Missing observables are group catalogs, halo mass, central/satellite labels, and a spectroscopic fiber-collision correction at the 55-arcsec scale. This is an ordinal projected-neighbor rank, not a physical density or causal environment result.

\subsection{Maintenance-heating denominator: broad optical BPT-selected hosts in massive SDSS galaxies}
Artifact \texttt{m1\_rp3\_maintenance\_heating/analysis\_results.json} defines the optical duty-cycle denominator for maintenance-heating follow-up. The massive subset (\(\log M_\star \geq 10.8\)) contains 9,298 emission-line galaxies, of which 5,695 are low-sSFR; the broad optical BPT-selected fraction is 0.430 in the massive subset and 0.607 among massive low-sSFR objects. Missing observables are X-ray cavity or cooling-luminosity measurements, radio jet mechanical powers, halo-selected parent catalogues, and nondetection modelling. Future radio-loud baselines can be anchored to catalog work such as Best \& Heckman (2012) \citep{bestheckman2012}; the present optical denominator is still not a heating-to-cooling or jet-power measurement.

\subsection{High-excitation broad optical BPT-selected baseline: resolved kinematics follow-up}
Artifact \texttt{m2\_p1\_outflow\_escape\_recycling/analysis\_results.json} defines the high-excitation broad optical BPT denominator for resolved-kinematics follow-up. High-excitation broad optical BPT-selected candidates number 4,440 of 60,000 emission-line galaxies (0.074), and their median \(\log {\rm sSFR}\) is -11.53 versus -10.14 for the full denominator. Missing observables are resolved outflow velocities, halo potentials, molecular/ionized/neutral gas phases, and CGM recycling tracers. This is a follow-up denominator, not an escape-velocity or recycling result.

\subsection{Tracer-threshold census for multiphase follow-up}
Artifact \texttt{m3\_p1\_multiphase\_census/analysis\_results.json} compares optical tracer choices against one shared denominator before any multiphase census is attempted. Within the 60,000-galaxy sample, simple optical tracer definitions produce broad optical BPT-selected prevalence from 0.136 to 0.418, a factor of 3.1 before molecular, neutral, X-ray, or radio phases are added. Missing observables are ionized, molecular, and neutral tracers, X-ray or radio tracers, a shared parent denominator, and a consistent aperture model. This is a tracer-baseline note, not a multiphase outflow measurement.

\subsection{Low-sSFR optical denominator: baseline for future CO/HI gas measurements}
Artifact \texttt{m3\_p2\_gas\_depletion\_efficiency/analysis\_results.json} defines the denominator for CO/HI gas-fraction and depletion-time follow-up. The massive low-sSFR denominator contains 6,729 galaxies, its broad optical BPT-selected fraction is 0.549, and the median H-alpha luminosity proxy is \(\log (L_{\mathrm{H}\alpha}/\mathrm{erg\,s^{-1}}) = 40.061\), which is 0.66 dex lower than in massive star-forming emission-line galaxies. The H-alpha value is an aperture-corrected catalog proxy, not a direct total gas quantity. Missing observables are CO or dust-based molecular gas masses, atomic hydrogen masses, aperture-matched SFRs, morphology, and environment labels, together with radio, X-ray, and IFU observables. Catalogs such as xCOLD GASS/xGASS and matched stellar-population plus CO survey designs motivate the follow-up; the present value remains a line-luminosity proxy, not a cold-gas-mass estimate.

The follow-up ingredients are simulations \citep{eagle2015} passed through the exact optical S/N and fiber-aperture selection function used here, including the sequential 60,000-row \texttt{specObjID} cache cap, and then through the SDSS, MaNGA, ALMA, X-ray, and radio selection functions with aperture and noise models. Forward modelling should synthesize optical emission-line classifications, including BPT selection, before comparison to the atlas vector \citep{hirschmann2017}. Observed \(u-r\) colors are not intrinsic dust-free colors, so a realistic comparison also needs explicit dust treatment or radiative transfer, as in forward-model color work such as \citet{trayford2015}. Without those matched selection steps, any simulation comparison is not a valid test.

\section{Package decision}
These eight entries should remain supplementary until the missing observables are added. They are follow-up target definitions, denominator baselines, or appendix material under the main result, but not independent causal-mechanism papers in their current SDSS-only form. The observational role of each subsection is to define what real data are still needed, not to expand the current SDSS-only measurement set.

\section*{Data Availability}
This atlas uses public SDSS DR17 spectroscopy, photometry, emission-line measurements, and MPA-JHU-style value-added catalog tables only. No proprietary data were used. The custody receipt is \texttt{provenance/REAL\_DATA\_SOURCE\_CUSTODY.json}. It inventories the shared \texttt{SDSS\_AGN\_SFR\_PILOT\_20260708T122000Z/data/analysis\_sample\_bpt.csv} source cache (60,000 rows; SHA-256 \texttt{6f982fa5778c3900239149b28729f701390fe393a164b95236229adc1e422883}), the flagship matched-control anchor \texttt{analysis\_results.json} (SHA-256 \texttt{668ad7a67290600ff5028ae587d32ef239a09bd8627a480539f37e1927d659df}) and \texttt{data/matched\_agn\_sf\_pairs.csv} (8,146 rows; SHA-256 \texttt{4ea53af867cccccb2b68b81557ff84fe90ec3f13e0512ffbdc977fa7216996fd}), plus the eight atlas JSON artifacts listed in Table~\ref{tab:provenance-map}.
