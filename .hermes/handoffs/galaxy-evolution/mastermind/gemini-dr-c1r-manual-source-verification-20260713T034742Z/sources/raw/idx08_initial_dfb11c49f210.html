About Simba

Simba is a state-of-the-art suite of galaxy formation simulations for exploring the co-evolution of galaxies, black holes, and intergalactic gas within a cosmological context.

Simba employs novel multi-scale prescriptions for key physical processes governing galaxy evolution:

  • Gizmo with Meshless Finite Mass hydrodynamics
  • Grackle cooling including on-the-fly self-shielding
  • H2-based star formation and 11-element chemistry
  • Two-phase decoupled winds based on FIRE simulations
  • Torque-limited and Bondi black hole accretion
  • AGN jets, radiative winds, and X-ray feedback
  • On-the-fly dust production and destruction

Simba, tuned only to match the galaxy stellar mass function evolution and the M-sigma relation, reproduces a wide range of observations including:

  • Galaxy color and stellar-to-halo mass bimodality
  • Galaxy-black hole co-evolution
  • Galaxy atomic and molecular contents
  • Group and cluster X-ray scaling relations
  • The Lyman alpha forest at low redshifts
  • AGN luminosity functions
  • Dust-to-gas ratios out to high redshifts
  • Low-redshift CGM absorption lines
  • Sub-millimetre galaxy number counts
Many of these successes are unique to Simba, making it among the most advanced simulations to explore galaxy evolution and baryon cycling within cosmologically-evolving ecosystems.

    • Simba Data

    Simba snapshots and galaxy catalogs are available for each of 151 output redshifts from z=20 to z=0.

    The hdf5 Caesar catalogs contain many cross-matched galaxy & halo physical properties, photometry in 130+ FSPS bands, and progenitor/descendant links. To work with these catalogs, it's best to install Caesar.

    All files are listed by snapshot number. outputs.txt shows the mapping of [a z snap#]. In Caesar, you can get the nearest snapshot number to a desired redshift via: snapnum,snapz=caesar.progen.z_to_snap(redshift).

    Getting the data:

    • Clicking on the run name below (e.g. s50) will take you to the directory of snapshots for that run.
    • Clicking on the associated description will take you to the directory of galaxy catalogs.

    Flagship Run

    100 Mpc/h box, 2x1024^3 particles.

    s50: Full Simba physics

    Feedback Variants

    50 Mpc/h box, 2x512^3 particles.

    s50: Full Simba physics

    s50nox: No X-ray AGN feedback

    s50nojet: No X-ray or jet feedback

    s50noagn: No AGN feedback

    s50nofb: No SF or AGN feedback

    High-Res Run

    25 Mpc/h box, 2x512^3 particles.

    s50: Full Simba physics
