Abstract
We introduce the simba simulations, the next generation of the MUFASA cosmological galaxy formation simulations run with GIZMO’s meshless finite mass hydrodynamics. SIMBA includes updates to MUFASA’s sub-resolution star formation and feedback prescriptions, and introduces black hole growth via the torque-limited accretion model of Anglés-Alcázar et al. from cold gas and Bondi accretion from hot gas, along with black hole feedback via kinetic bipolar outflows and X-ray energy. However, SIMBA also yields an insufficiently sharp truncation of the z = 0 mass function, and too-large sizes for low-mass quenched galaxies. We show that SIMBA’s jet feedback is primarily responsible for quenching massive galaxies.

N2  - We introduce the simba simulations, the next generation of the MUFASA cosmological galaxy formation simulations run with GIZMO’s meshless finite mass hydrodynamics. Ejection velocities are taken to be ∼103kms−1 at high Eddington ratios, increasing to ∼8000kms−1 at Eddington ratios below 2 per cent, with a constant momentum input of 20L/c.
