What the literature appears to establish

Optically selected AGN hosts are associated with a median global star formation rate offset of -0.06 dex relative to matched controls. (https://academic.oup.com/mnrasl/article/458/1/L34/2589536)

Nuclear regions (2.5-arcsec aperture) of AGN hosts are associated with an elevated stellar-population-derived star formation rate offset (+0.21 dex) relative to morphological controls. (https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/539/4/3229/8120227)

Globally, both AGN hosts and control galaxies are associated with the "green valley" below the star formation main sequence. (https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/539/4/3229/8120227)

Subgrid physics in cosmological simulations alter green-valley transitioning populations, with EAGLE producing a broad continuous distribution and IllustrisTNG producing a sharp pile-up at an imposed SFR floor. (https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22268)

The WHAN diagram separates true weakly accreting AGN from "retired galaxies" ionized by hot evolved low-mass stars. (https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.4426)

The quenching of central galaxies is overwhelmingly predicted by integrated supermassive black hole mass (a proxy for integrated feedback history) rather than instantaneous AGN accretion output. (https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/512/1/1052/6482843)

Robust multi-band bulge-disk decompositions and Sérsic indices are available for 1.12 million SDSS galaxies to enable morphological control matching. (https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/ApJS/196/11)

Public catalogs provide distances to cosmic web filaments within the SDSS footprint to account for large-scale environmental variables. (https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/438/4/3465/1107139)

Optical spectroscopic follow-up of X-ray sources yields uniformly selected AGN samples that bypass traditional optical dust obscuration and signal-to-noise line-ratio limits. (https://www.sdss.org/dr18/bhm/programs/spiders/)

SDSS 3-arcsec fiber measurements require approximately a 20 percent covering fraction to represent total global properties, limiting fiber metrics at lower redshifts to bulge-dominated scales. (UNCITED_NOT_USABLE)

Active black hole accretion cycles operate on short stochastic timescales (0.1 to 10 Myr), whereas optical tracers of galactic stellar populations integrate over much longer temporal baselines (100 Myr to 1 Gyr). (UNCITED_NOT_USABLE)

What remains unknown

The direct causal link between the instantaneous, single-epoch BPT-identified state of an AGN and the long-term, structurally integrated specific star formation rate of its host galaxy.

Delineation of the bulge/aperture degeneracy: whether fiber-centered sSFR deficits are intrinsically tied to active nuclear processes or are artifacts of aperture scaling over mature, morphologically quenched spheroids.

The true extent of the denominator bias introduced by imposing strict S/N >= 3 cuts on BPT diagnostic lines, and how the exclusion of emission-weak or obscured populations alters association medians.

The distinct green-valley evolutionary pathways of the target sample: identifying whether the association is driven by rapid major-merger transitions or slow secular gas exhaustion.

Analysis/decision criterion

To bound the bulge-vs-nuclear-activity degeneracy: cross-match the target fiber-centered BPT sample with structural catalogs to ascertain if the association margin persists, shrinks, or disappears when morphological proxies (Sérsic index, B/T ratio) are strictly controlled.

To bound the emission-line selection bias: cross-correlate optical targets with X-ray selected catalogs to evaluate if the sSFR difference is strictly associated with BPT observability criteria or extends uniformly to all accreting populations in the redshift window.

To bound the retired-galaxy degeneracy: re-filter the active denominator using WHAN criteria to ascertain how much of the measured association is driven by contamination from pure stellar ionization in retired systems.

Overclaim risks and wording guardrails

Framing to avoid: Asserting the AGN "quenches" its host. → Neutral alternative: Describing the statistical co-occurrence and association between optical AGN signatures and specific star formation rate offsets.

Framing to avoid: Claiming the severe sSFR deficit "proves" AGN feedback is clearing gas. → Neutral alternative: Stating the fiber-centered association establishes an empirical baseline for future causal tests.

Framing to avoid: Stating our results "show" or "capture" that active nuclei are responsible for the deficit. → Neutral alternative: Stating our analysis maps the divergence in catalog sSFR distributions for broad optical BPT-selected galaxies relative to matched controls.

Introduction first-paragraph caveat requirement: Must explicitly state that the finding is a morphology-uncontrolled association-only result and does not establish a causal mechanism, quenching event, or feedback interaction.

Introduction second-paragraph caveat requirement: Must explicitly state the metric is fiber-centered, selection-limited, and denominator-bound, remaining indistinguishable from bulge-fraction, concentration, or aperture-sampling associations without further structural control.

Do-not-use until verified

Retracted: Ellison et al. (2016) reported a -0.12 dex / 25 percent star-formation under-abundance (corrected to -0.06 dex).

Retracted: The claim that the -1.309 dex study invariant is "Highly commensurable" or "rests remarkably close" to absolute median nuclear SFR values (e.g., -1.34 dex or -1.55 dex) from Gatto et al. (2025). This was a false comparison of unlike estimands.

Retracted: Any direct comparison or commensurability between the -1.309 dex matched-control difference and absolute sSFR quantities (-11.0 dex from Piotrowska; -14.85 or -11.71 dex from TNG/EAGLE) without explicit, estimand-mismatch caveats.

Retracted: Any wording that our statistic "confirms," "captures," "reflects," "establishes," or demonstrates aperture dependence, nuclear suppression, nuclear enhancement, bulge dominance, concurrent black-hole fueling, secular central gas depletion, gas clearing, active feedback, or quenching.
