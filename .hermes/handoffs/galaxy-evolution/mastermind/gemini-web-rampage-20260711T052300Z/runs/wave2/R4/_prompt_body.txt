# Deep Research request — Reservoir depletion vs suppressed star-formation efficiency in quenched galaxies

**Request ID:** `REQ_RAMPAGE_R4_20260711T052300Z`
**Role:** Literature analyst for a galaxy-evolution research journal. Published work only; no

**Question:** For quenched, quenching, green-valley, and post-starburst galaxies, what published
evidence discriminates between (a) reservoir removal/depletion/starvation and (b) retained gas
forming stars inefficiently (suppressed SFE), and what does each study's own analysis say limits
that discrimination?

**Required deliverables (markdown sections, in order):**
1. `## Evidence table` — one row per study/sample (favor 2018+; earlier anchors labeled):

3. `## Central vs global` — studies distinguishing central-kpc depletion from galaxy-wide gas loss;

4. `## Population fractions` — any published fractions of quenched systems that are gas-poor vs

5. `## AGN association tests` — published tests linking either scenario to AGN presence/history,
