 Galaxy Formation Simulation Predictions 
Research Websites
(1) Identify major galaxy-formation simulations like IllustrisTNG, EAGLE, SIMBA, FIRE, ROMULUS, and their successors.
(2) Search for literature comparing the feedback-relevant predictions of these simulations to observational data.
(3) Distinguish which of these comparisons employ genuine forward modeling, such as mock observables and selection functions, versus those relying on raw simulation statistics.
(4) For the forward modeling papers, determine the specific biases and uncertainties quantified by the authors themselves.
(5) Construct an observable-pipeline map detailing how physical simulation outputs are translated into mock observables.
(6) Compile a selection-function ledger documenting the observational constraints and selection effects applied to the mock data.
(7) Evaluate the per-observable discrimination power to assess how effectively forward-modeled observables constrain feedback physics compared to raw statistics.
(8) Gather information on public data products, known failure modes in the pipelines, existing gaps in the methodology, and relevant links to prepare the final structured report.
