 AGN Heating Duty Cycle Research 
Research Websites
(1) Search for astrophysics literature on AGN maintenance-heating (radio-mode/kinetic/jet-driven) duty cycles in clusters, groups, and massive galaxies, prioritizing publications from 2018 onwards.
(2) Extract observational duty-cycle determinations, including the methods (X-ray cavities, radio-AGN fractions, SZ), selection criteria, environment, halo/stellar mass range, redshift range, timescale assumptions, and stated caveats.
(3) Investigate energy-balance studies comparing cavity power (P_cav) to cooling luminosity (L_cool), noting the balance ratios, uncertainties, duty-cycle treatments, and systematics like 4pV/enthalpy and projection effects.
(4) Analyze the mass regime coverage across these studies to map where robust duty-cycle determinations exist and explicitly identify gaps, especially at the low-halo-mass end.
(5) Review recent population studies utilizing eROSITA, LOFAR, MeerKAT, and uGMRT to identify how their findings on AGN heating and duty cycles add to or contradict earlier Chandra-era cavity statistics.
(6) Identify and compile direct disagreements or conflicting results in the literature regarding duty cycles or heating-vs-cooling balances, capturing both sides' numbers and stated reasons without resolving the debate.
(7) Find proposed future or discriminating observational tests in the literature aimed at determining the duty cycle at the galaxy scale, noting the feasibility statements and the specific roles or limitations of instruments like JWST and ALMA.
(8) Collect standard citation identifiers (arXiv ID, DOI, ADS bibcode, or URL) for all sources referenced in the preceding steps to ensure accurate attribution in the final report.
