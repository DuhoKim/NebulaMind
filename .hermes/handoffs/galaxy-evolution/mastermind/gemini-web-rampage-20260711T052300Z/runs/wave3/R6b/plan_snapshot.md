 Galaxy Simulation Feedback Comparison 
Research Websites
(1) Search academic databases for papers published up to July 2026 comparing galaxy-formation simulations like IllustrisTNG, EAGLE, SIMBA, FIRE, and ROMULUS with observations regarding AGN feedback and quenching.
(2) Categorize the found literature into studies using forward modeling techniques such as mock spectra, radiative transfer, and X-ray or radio mocks versus studies relying solely on raw simulation statistics.
(3) For specific observables including outflow incidence, quenched fractions, molecular gas fractions, X-ray cavities, radio-AGN fractions, and sSFR distributions, map the relevant papers and extract any quantified biases along with their uncertainties.
(4) Analyze the forward-modeling papers to compile a ledger of modeled versus ignored selection effects for the surveys they reference, paying attention to flux limits, aperture, resolution, and detection thresholds.
(5) Scan the collected literature to determine which observables are cited as effectively discriminating between different feedback implementations, recording the associated residuals and uncertainties.
(6) Investigate the public data portals of the major simulations to verify if the specific data fields required to generate the mock observables are accessible, collecting the corresponding URLs.
(7) Identify documented cases in the literature where forward-modeled results disagreed with raw-statistics comparisons for the exact same quantity, noting the specific numbers, author explanations, and source attributions.
(8) Cross-reference the list of target observables against the collected literature to isolate any observables lacking published forward-modeled comparisons to highlight as research gaps.
