### 1. Research-Topic Strategy
The current strategy is robust and operationally sound. By anchoring the proposals with a strict denominator baseline (P0) before branching into mechanism tests (P1: Escape vs. Recycling, P2: Radio-jet coupling) and regime boundaries (P3), it ensures that downstream causal claims are not artifacts of selection bias or aperture effects. The emphasis on matched controls and explicit, quantifiable decision criteria (e.g., median escaping fraction > 50%, coupling efficiency ratios) sets a high standard for separating physical feedback from mere association.

### 2. Galaxy Evolution Page & The Physical Story
The draft effectively advances the narrative from historical taxonomy (Hubble sequence, color bimodality) to a physical baryon cycle (supply, consumption, feedback, recycling). It successfully shifts the focus to causal structure and gas phases, which is exactly the modern paradigm. The "budget" framing ($\dot{M}_{\mathrm{in}}$ vs SFR vs $\dot{M}_{\mathrm{out}}$ vs $\dot{M}_{\mathrm{recycle}}$) provides an excellent pedagogical backbone that grounds abstract concepts in measurable quantities. The page rightly treats quenching as a family of pathways rather than a monolithic event.

### 3. RP-1's Influence on the Wiki
RP-1 is handled correctly in the current draft. It is appropriately utilized as an empirical denominator baseline and a stress-test for selection effects, rather than being paraded as causal proof of AGN quenching. The wiki correctly cites the 8,146 matched pairs and the offset to illustrate a statistical association within a strict $S/N \ge 3$ cut, while strictly maintaining the guardrail that this does not constitute physical proof of feedback. Future edits must ensure this boundary is never blurred; RP-1 remains local support material for control-sample methodologies, not a definitive physical mechanism.

### 4. Highest-Value Next Revisions
