Debate-map-to-wiki rebuild

Downstream/default method: start from a reviewed debate map and build reader-facing wiki prose, while binding every sentence back to claims, evidence, and certainty caps.

Input: many papersWork: organize support, limits, disagreement, uncertaintyOutput: wiki prose + claim/evidence/trust surfaces

Pipeline: papers → ledger → debate map → wiki prose → product chips/trust surfaces

When to use it

Use when

When the main need is the final Galaxy Evolution wiki page: a coherent reader narrative that represents agreements, disagreements, limits, and uncertainty.

Do not mistake it for

Unsafe if treated as free-form prose-first writing. It must never let prose lead evidence; every sentence needs a binding and modality cap.

Method details

Inputs

  • Reviewed claim/status ledger and evidence bindings.
  • Research-status/debate map with named positions, counterpositions, uncertainty, and missing-critic checks.
  • Wording contract mapping certainty/status to maximum prose modality.

Work

  • Draft wiki sections from the debate map, not from isolated legacy claim chips.
  • Bind every prose sentence to claim/evidence rows or mark it as an open question.
  • Emit a reconciliation plan for product claim chips and trust surfaces after the prose map is safe.

Outputs

  • Docs-only wiki prose draft.
  • Sentence-binding JSONL/table.
  • Wording-contract check.
  • Derived-claim and product-chip reconciliation plan for later gated packets.

Stop gates

  • Any prose sentence lacks a ledger/evidence binding.
  • A sentence exceeds its certainty cap, e.g. turns “can/in some systems” into universal fact.
  • The narrative rescues a legacy claim instead of rendering the debate map.
