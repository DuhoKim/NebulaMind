Three ways to distill many papers into wiki pages

The board compared the current method with two alternatives. All three methods share the same basic goal: turn a plethora of papers into trustworthy wiki prose, with claim/evidence/trust surfaces that can be audited.

Board decision

Selected comparison set

The two methods added beside the current one are Source-first paper adjudication and Debate-map-to-wiki rebuild. Evaluation-first readiness and display-safety hygiene stay as overlays: they govern all methods but are not peer methods for producing wiki pages.

Recommended default for the next Baseline phase: Debate-map-to-wiki rebuild, with source-first adjudication used when evidence is disputed and packet-gated reconciliation used when product/claim artifacts need safe mutation.

Shared invariant

Input

Many papers, source texts, source positions, and paper metadata.

Work

Organize what papers say, where they disagree, what they limit, and what remains uncertain.

Output

Wiki-page prose plus supporting claim, evidence, status, and trust surfaces.

Source-first paper adjudication

Upstream method: start from the papers themselves, adjudicate what each source supports, limits, refutes, or leaves unknown, then allow claims and wiki prose to follow.

Anchor: papers and evidence positions

METHOD_HOME_SOURCE_FIRST_PAPER_ADJUDICATION

Debate-map-to-wiki rebuild

Downstream/default method: start from a reviewed debate map and build reader-facing wiki prose, while binding every sentence back to claims, evidence, and certainty caps.

Anchor: research-status/debate map and wiki prose
