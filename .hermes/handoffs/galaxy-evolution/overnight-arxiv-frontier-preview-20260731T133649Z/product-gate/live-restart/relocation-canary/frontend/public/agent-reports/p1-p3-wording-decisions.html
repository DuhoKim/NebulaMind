Plain-English summary

Three old galaxy-evolution claims say AGN (black-hole) feedback broadly heats (2298 & its hidden duplicate 2924) or expels (2299) gas from the reservoirs of massive galaxies. That language sounds like a settled, universal mechanism — but the attached evidence is simulation- or system-specific, and our newer, more careful successor claims (2945 / 2946) say this support must stay conditional. This board asks you to pick how to soften or retire each one.

Separately, claim 2572 currently states a plain correlation, yet its one pinned paper actually argues that halo mass — not central properties — is the primary driver. So the claim reads as contradicted by its own refutes evidence. You choose either a cautious re-wording (a guard against over-claiming primacy) or a stricter, openly-disputed version. Claim 2573 stays separate.
