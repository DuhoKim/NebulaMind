Mission

Turn large volumes of published papers into clear, trustworthy prose: claims, evidence, contradictions, and readiness/trust summaries with citations.

UI/runtime features wait unless they directly increase ingestion throughput, evidence quality, contradiction finding, trust/readiness scoring, or final prose synthesis.

PAPER_PROSE_DISTILLATION_BOARD_V1

North-star metrics

  • Papers processed into structured claim/evidence records per day.
  • Share of claims grounded by cited evidence spans, not generic summaries.
  • Share of papers/claims with explicit support/counter/neutral stance labels.
  • Contradictions surfaced with enough context for a human to adjudicate.
  • Final prose paragraphs accepted as clear, cited, uncertainty-aware, and useful.

Lane board

L1 — Corpus intake and inventory

Owner: Goru mechanical lane, Hermes verifies

State: next

Know what papers exist, which ones have metadata gaps, and which tranche should be processed first.

  • Inventory current paper universe and counts without DB writes.
  • Rank candidate tranches by relevance, metadata completeness, and contradiction potential.
  • Produce a 25-paper pilot manifest with stable IDs and source URLs.

Done when: A manifest exists with paper_id/arxiv/doi/title/year/source_url plus reason-for-inclusion and known gaps.

L2 — Paper-to-claim extraction

Owner: Lana reasoning lane, Hermes verifies schema

State: next

Convert each paper into atomic claims and evidence snippets that can be checked later.

  • Define the distillation JSONL contract: paper, claim, evidence_span, stance, uncertainty, limitations.
  • Run a small manual/guided extraction on 3 papers to calibrate claim granularity.
  • Record failure modes: vague claim, missing method, unsupported conclusion, duplicate claim.

Done when: Each pilot paper has 3-8 atomic claims with quoted/located evidence snippets and caveats.

L3 — Contradiction and trust/readiness

Owner: Lana plus Goru cross-check

State: next

Find where papers agree, disagree, or are too weak to support prose.

  • Build a stance matrix over pilot claims: support / counter / mixed / insufficient.
  • Create contradiction cards with the exact papers and claim text involved.
  • Assign readiness labels: ready for prose, needs adjudication, needs more evidence, reject/low confidence.

Done when: Every synthesis candidate has a readiness label and a reason, with unresolved contradictions explicit.

L4 — Prose synthesis

Owner: Hermes drafts, Lana reviews

State: next

Turn structured evidence into readable, cited paragraphs without overstating certainty.

  • Design paragraph templates: what is known, what is disputed, why it matters, readiness/caveat.
  • Draft 5 prose examples from the pilot tranche with citations to paper IDs and evidence snippets.
  • Review for overclaiming, missing caveats, and human readability.

Done when: At least 5 concise prose blocks are citation-backed, uncertainty-aware, and reviewable by a human.

L5 — Evaluation and human adjudication

Owner: Hermes board, Goru metrics, human final judgment

State: next

Measure whether the distillation is actually reliable and useful.

  • Create a rubric: groundedness, contradiction handling, citation precision, clarity, uncertainty.
  • Score pilot outputs and list the top recurring defects.
  • Define stop/go thresholds before any DB publication or product surface work.

Done when: A pilot scorecard says what passed, what failed, and what must improve before scale-up.

L6 — Product/UI only as support

Owner: Hermes gatekeeper

State: gated

Use UI only to expose corpus/prose progress or let humans review evidence faster.

  • Keep current PR #72 paper surfaces as support tools.
  • Do not start new feature work unless it unblocks one of L1-L5.
  • Any deploy/restart remains separately approval-gated.

Done when: Every feature request names the distillation bottleneck it removes and the metric it improves.
