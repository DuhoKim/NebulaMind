# Galaxy Evolution — Research proposal agenda (Method 1)

> Proposed, evidence-aware studies derived from the Method 1 Galaxy Evolution synthesis. Each card states what the current source basis already reports, what remains unknown, and the exact measurement and data that would decide it. These are research designs for future work — not accepted results, and naming a survey means the study would use it, not that it already settles the question.

6 evidence-aware proposed studies.

## RP-1 — A causal test of whether AGN feedback suppresses star formation

**Research question.** At fixed stellar mass and environment, do AGN-hosting galaxies show a measurable star-formation deficit attributable to feedback energy, rather than to shared mass/environment?

**What studies already show.**
- The source basis is non-committal on a direct causal link: its assembled evidence for internal AGN regulation neither confirms nor refutes it.
- It does record a mechanism — kinetic-mode jets and outflows mechanically interacting with the surrounding medium.
- It reports AGN-linked shutdown at cosmic noon as a discussed rapid channel, and hot-halo heating support mainly from simulations.

**What remains unknown.** Whether a matched population shows a star-formation deficit tied to AGN energy, and which gas phase (molecular reservoir vs replenishing inflow) actually responds.

**Survey/data plan.**
- SDSS-MaNGA or MUSE — spatially resolved star-formation histories and gas kinematics around the AGN (the response).
- ALMA CO — molecular gas mass and depletion time (the fuel).
- Chandra/XMM-Newton — X-ray AGN power (the energy budget).
- VLA/LOFAR — jet power and duty cycle (the kinetic channel).

**Analysis/test.** Compare resolved SF suppression and molecular gas content between mass-, redshift-, and morphology-matched AGN and non-AGN control samples, as a function of AGN power and mode.

**Expected result or decision point.** A calibrated star-formation deficit (or its absence) at fixed mass/environment, with an energy budget sufficient (or not) to drive it — promoting or bounding the causal claim.

**Caveats.** Matched controls can stay confounded by selection; AGN variability/duty cycle means a snapshot may miss the active phase.

_Provenance: source basis claim 2929 (non-committal/unverified); kinetic mode 2915; rapid-quenching channel 2913._

## RP-2 — Where internal (mass-driven) quenching separates from environmental quenching

**Research question.** At fixed stellar mass, does local environment add a measurable quenching excess beyond internal processes, and in which mass/redshift regime do the two channels separate versus stay degenerate?

**What studies already show.**
- The source basis reports quenching as jointly regulated by internal and environment-linked processes.
- It treats their separability and relative priority as sample-, redshift-, and definition-dependent (a debated point).
- A minority of its evidence supports a clean separation while most is inconclusive.

**What remains unknown.** Which combination of mass, redshift, and environment makes the channels separable, and whether satellite-versus-central status is the controlling variable.

**Survey/data plan.**
- SDSS/GAMA — low-redshift group/cluster environment and quenched fractions.
- COSMOS/CANDELS + JWST — environment at cosmic noon.
- DESI — large mass/redshift-matched parent samples (the denominator).
- Euclid/Rubin (LSST) — wide-area environment and weak-lensing halo mass.

**Analysis/test.** Hold stellar mass fixed and vary environment (and vice versa); measure the environmental quenching excess at fixed mass across redshift.

**Expected result or decision point.** A separation map: the mass/redshift/environment regime where an environmental quenching excess is (or is not) detectable at fixed mass.

**Caveats.** Environment proxies and halo-mass estimates carry scatter that widens the separation boundary; satellite/central mis-assignment.

_Provenance: source basis claim 2931 (debated); satellite quenching 2934; environment-morphology 2932._

## RP-3 — An observed heating-versus-cooling balance for maintenance quenching

**Research question.** In massive halos, does AGN heating balance radiative cooling closely and frequently enough to maintain quiescence when measured observationally, not just in simulations?

**What studies already show.**
- The source basis records maintenance heating as reported but model/simulation-dependent.
- Its support comes chiefly from simulations rather than a measured prevalence.
- Direct observational backing (X-ray cavities, hot-halo balance) is sparse in its evidence set.

**What remains unknown.** The observed frequency and time-averaged sufficiency of heating versus cooling across a mass-selected population — not just individual cavity systems.

**Survey/data plan.**
- Chandra — X-ray cavities and their mechanical power.
- XMM-Newton/eROSITA — hot-halo thermodynamics and cooling luminosity.
- VLA/LOFAR — radio-mode AGN duty cycle.
- IllustrisTNG/HORIZON-AGN-style simulations — forward-modeled predictions to test against, labeled as simulations.

**Analysis/test.** Measure cavity/heating power versus cooling luminosity across a mass-selected sample; compare the observed balance and duty cycle to the simulation predictions.

**Expected result or decision point.** An observed bound on how often heating balances cooling — converting the model expectation into a measured prevalence, or refuting it.

**Caveats.** Duty-cycle incompleteness (snapshots miss time-averaged heating); cavity detectability biases toward the most powerful systems.

_Provenance: source basis claim 2946 (reported / simulation-supported)._

## RP-4 — A prioritized evidence-gap map for the narrative-only sections

**Research question.** Which parts of the galaxy-evolution narrative currently carry no direct evidence in the method basis, and in what order should named surveys ground them?

**What studies already show.**
- The source basis directly evidences only a small part of its own narrative — coverage concentrates on AGN feedback and quenching.
- Dark-matter-halo connections, gas supply, environment, chemical enrichment, and the high-redshift/reionization frontier are carried as narrative without attached evidence.
- Only two of its nine sections carry any directly evidenced statement.

**What remains unknown.** Which under-evidenced section is most tractable to ground first, and the expected evidence yield of each candidate survey per section.

**Survey/data plan.**
- DESI — spectroscopy for gas, metallicity, and environment.
- SDSS legacy — statistical baselines.
- Euclid/Rubin — scale and large-scale structure.
- JWST — the high-redshift/reionization frontier.

**Analysis/test.** Score each thematic section by current evidential support and by the estimated yield of each candidate survey; produce a ranked observational to-do list.

**Expected result or decision point.** A reusable evidence-gap ranking that turns narrative-only sections into a concrete data-collection order.

**Caveats.** Yield estimates are judgement calls; a bare section is a data gap, not a weak-science verdict.

_Provenance: source basis per-section coverage (two of nine sections directly evidenced)._

## RP-5 — Robustness of conclusions to evidence accounting (methods appendix)

**Research question.** Do the method's qualitative conclusions change when support is counted by distinct study rather than by record, and when incompletely catalogued references are resolved?

**What studies already show.**
- The method basis stores evidence as records that can repeat a study — its evidenced claims show 43 records collapsing to 26 distinct studies.
- Several references are catalogued only as identifiers, without resolved titles or metadata.
- A pile of records can therefore overstate independent support.

**What remains unknown.** Whether any claim's confidence level shifts under distinct-study counting, and how much interpretation depends on the unresolved references.

**Survey/data plan.**
- The method's existing literature corpus (no new observations).
- NASA ADS / local bibliography — metadata resolution only, explicitly not observational evidence.

**Analysis/test.** Recompute per-claim support on a distinct-study basis, resolve the identifier-only references, and compare to the record-based tally.

**Expected result or decision point.** A methods-appendix result on whether conclusions are stable against evidence-accounting choices, plus a corrected distinct-study support level per claim.

**Caveats.** Metadata resolution adds no scientific weight; abstract-only and citation-linking limits.

_Provenance: source basis evidence tables (record-versus-study counts; identifier-only references)._

## RP-6 — Pre-registered acceptance criteria for AGN-feedback conclusions (methods appendix)

**Research question.** What explicit observational thresholds would justify promoting an AGN-feedback statement from tentative to established?

**What studies already show.**
- The method basis places its AGN-feedback statements below an accepted level — one non-committal, one simulation-reported.
- It promotes or demotes them by qualitative judgement rather than by stated criteria.

**What remains unknown.** The quantitative thresholds — independent-study support, observation-versus-simulation mix, effect size and consistency — that should gate promotion.

**Survey/data plan.**
- The combined observational programs above (RP-1/RP-2/RP-3 measurements) as the inputs a promotion decision would draw on — proposed evidence to gather.
- NASA ADS / local corpus — independent-study counting only.

**Analysis/test.** Specify thresholds and apply them retrospectively to the current AGN-feedback statements to see which would promote, hold, or demote.

**Expected result or decision point.** A reproducible acceptance rule set that other syntheses could adopt, reducing subjective confidence judgements.

**Caveats.** Thresholds encode value choices and must be set transparently and revisited.

_Provenance: source basis AGN feedback & quenching section (statements 2929 non-committal, 2946 reported)._

## Scope and limitations

These are proposed studies derived from the current Method 1 synthesis; they are hypotheses and study designs for future work, not accepted claims, and not tied to catalogued evidence. Method 1's confidence scale is internal and not comparable across methods.
