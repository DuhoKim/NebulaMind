Proposed research programme (Method 1)

Packet-gated reconciliation synthesis · 6 proposed studies with linked prior evidence
Scope note. This prospectus presents proposed studies derived from the Method 1 Galaxy Evolution synthesis. For each proposed study we state the prior evidence already assembled in the source basis — with links to those records — the specific question that remains open, the measurement and data that would resolve it, and the analysis that would decide the result. These are research designs for future work, not accepted results; named surveys denote data the study would use, and a linked record denotes evidence the source basis attaches to a statement, not proof of the proposed hypothesis.

RP-1

A causal test of whether active galactic nucleus feedback suppresses star formation

Research question

At fixed stellar mass and environment, do AGN-hosting galaxies exhibit a measurable star-formation deficit attributable to feedback energetics rather than to shared mass or environment?

Prior evidence

The source basis attaches the following records to internal AGN feedback; it registers every one as non-committal for this statement — they neither corroborate nor refute a causal role — and several are catalogued only as arXiv identifiers. A recognised kinetic mechanism (jets and outflows coupling to the surrounding medium) and a rapid cosmic-noon shutdown channel are described qualitatively.

  1. Surveying the Whirlpool at Arcseconds with NOEMA (SWAN) IV arXiv:2604.15438 non-committal
  2. arXiv:2512.05584 arXiv:2512.05584 non-committal
  3. A large, long-lived, slowly-expanding superbubble across the Perseus Arm arXiv:2512.21927 non-committal
  4. arXiv:1507.06366 arXiv:1507.06366 non-committal
  5. arXiv:0901.1880 arXiv:0901.1880 non-committal
  6. arXiv:1203.2926 arXiv:1203.2926 non-committal
  7. arXiv:2403.17145 arXiv:2403.17145 non-committal
  8. arXiv:2009.11175 arXiv:2009.11175 non-committal

Open question

Whether a mass-, redshift-, and morphology-matched population exhibits a star-formation deficit attributable to AGN energetics, and which gas phase — the molecular reservoir or the replenishing inflow — responds.

Survey/data plan

  • SDSS-MaNGA or MUSE integral-field spectroscopy: spatially resolved star-formation histories and gas kinematics around the nucleus (the response).
  • ALMA CO: molecular gas mass and depletion time (the fuel).
  • Chandra and XMM-Newton: X-ray luminosity and AGN mechanical/radiative power (the energy budget).
  • VLA and LOFAR: jet power and radio duty cycle (the kinetic channel).

Analysis and decisive test

Compare resolved star-formation suppression and molecular gas fraction between matched AGN and non-AGN control samples as a function of AGN power and mode.

Expected result / decision point

A calibrated star-formation deficit at fixed mass and environment, accompanied by an energy budget sufficient (or demonstrably insufficient) to drive it, thereby promoting or bounding the causal statement.

Caveats

Matched controls may remain confounded by residual selection; AGN duty-cycle variability implies a single epoch can miss the active phase.

Source basis: claim 2929 (non-committal); kinetic mode 2915; rapid-quenching channel 2913.

RP-2

Locating the regime in which internal and environmental quenching separate

Research question

At fixed stellar mass, does local environment contribute a measurable quenching excess beyond internal processes, and in which mass–redshift regime do the two channels separate rather than remain degenerate?

Prior evidence

The source basis attaches the following records to the joint internal/environmental quenching question. It registers four as supporting a separable environmental role and the remainder as non-committal; the supporting subset is listed first.

  1. Euclid Quick Data Release (Q1): Quenching precedes bulge formation arXiv:2511.02964 supporting
  2. A Statistical Study of HI Gas in AGN-Hosting and Satellite Galaxies arXiv:2606.25367 supporting
  3. Systematically Measuring Ultra-Diffuse Galaxies IX arXiv:2606.03652 supporting
  4. Contrasting evolutionary pathways of fast- and slow-rotators arXiv:2603.18292 supporting
  5. The role of environment and AGN feedback in quenching local galaxies arXiv:2401.12953 non-committal
  6. Surveying the Whirlpool at Arcseconds with NOEMA (SWAN) IV arXiv:2604.15438 non-committal
  7. arXiv:1712.04452 arXiv:1712.04452 non-committal
  8. arXiv:1308.5224 arXiv:1308.5224 non-committal
  9. Environmental Quenching of High-Redshift Galaxies: Interpreting JWST arXiv:2605.03008 non-committal
  10. Small and Complex II: Characterizing the Disk and Stellar Populations arXiv:2605.25075 non-committal
  11. arXiv:0901.1880 arXiv:0901.1880 non-committal
  12. arXiv:2605.31052 arXiv:2605.31052 non-committal
  13. arXiv:2501.00986 arXiv:2501.00986 non-committal

Open question

Which combination of stellar mass, redshift, and environment renders the channels separable, and whether satellite-versus-central status is the controlling variable.

Survey/data plan

  • SDSS and GAMA: low-redshift group and cluster environment with quenched fractions.
  • COSMOS/CANDELS with JWST: environment at cosmic noon.
  • DESI: large mass- and redshift-matched parent samples (the denominator).
  • Euclid and Rubin (LSST): wide-area environment and weak-lensing halo mass.

Analysis and decisive test

Hold stellar mass fixed and vary environment (and conversely); measure the environmental quenching excess at fixed mass as a function of redshift.

Expected result / decision point

A separation map delineating the mass–redshift–environment regime in which an environmental quenching excess is detectable at fixed mass.

Caveats

Environment proxies and halo-mass estimates carry scatter that broadens the separation boundary; satellite/central mis-assignment.

Source basis: claim 2931 (debated); satellite quenching 2934; environment–morphology 2932.

RP-3

An observed heating-versus-cooling balance for maintenance quenching

Research question

In massive haloes, does AGN heating balance radiative cooling closely and frequently enough to maintain quiescence when measured observationally, rather than inferred from simulations alone?

Prior evidence

The source basis attaches the following records to sustained maintenance heating and registers each as supporting; the set is predominantly simulation-based, and direct observational balance measurements are correspondingly sparse.

  1. On the quenching of star formation in observed and simulated central galaxies arXiv:2112.07672 supporting
  2. Quenched fractions in the IllustrisTNG simulations arXiv:2008.00005 supporting
  3. AGN-driven quenching of star formation: morphological and dynamical implications arXiv:1301.3092 supporting
  4. The HORIZON-AGN simulation: morphological diversity of galaxies arXiv:1606.03086 supporting
  5. Self-regulated growth of supermassive black holes by a dual jet-heating AGN arXiv:1108.0110 supporting
  6. Chaotic cold accretion on to black holes arXiv:1301.3130 supporting
  7. arXiv:2508.06707 arXiv:2508.06707 supporting
  8. arXiv:2403.17145 arXiv:2403.17145 supporting

Open question

The observed frequency and time-averaged sufficiency of heating relative to cooling across a mass-selected population, as opposed to individual cavity systems.

Survey/data plan

  • Chandra: X-ray cavities and their mechanical power.
  • XMM-Newton and eROSITA: hot-halo thermodynamics and cooling luminosity.
  • VLA and LOFAR: radio-mode AGN duty cycle.
  • IllustrisTNG- and HORIZON-AGN-style simulations: forward-modelled predictions to be tested, explicitly labelled as simulations.

Analysis and decisive test

Measure cavity heating power against cooling luminosity across a mass-selected sample and compare the observed balance and duty cycle with the simulation predictions.

Expected result / decision point

An observational bound on the frequency with which heating balances cooling, converting a model expectation into a measured prevalence or refuting it.

Caveats

Duty-cycle incompleteness (epoch sampling misses time-averaged heating); cavity detectability biases toward the most powerful systems.

Source basis: claim 2946 (reported / simulation-supported).

RP-4

A prioritised evidence-gap programme for the narrative-only sections

Research question

Which components of the galaxy-evolution narrative currently carry no directly attached evidence in the source basis, and in what order should named surveys ground them?

Prior evidence

No direct prior evidence is attached in the source basis for the narrative-only sections; that absence is itself the premise of this programme. The relevant prior material is the synthesis's own coverage record, which shows that only two of nine sections carry a directly evidenced statement.

Coverage record: Method 1 evidence & coverage map (local).

Evidence-gap limitation: because these sections have no attached records, the coverage record documents the gap rather than filling it with per-section study links.

Open question

Which under-evidenced section is most tractable to ground first, and the expected evidence yield of each candidate survey per section.

Survey/data plan

  • DESI: spectroscopy for gas, metallicity, and environment.
  • SDSS legacy: statistical baselines.
  • Euclid and Rubin: scale and large-scale structure.
  • JWST: the high-redshift and reionization frontier.

Analysis and decisive test

Score each thematic section by current evidential support and by the estimated yield of each candidate survey, producing a ranked observational programme.

Expected result / decision point

A reusable evidence-gap ranking that converts narrative-only sections into a defined data-collection order.

Caveats

Yield estimates are judgements; an unevidenced section indicates a data gap, not a weak-science verdict.

Source basis: per-section coverage record (two of nine sections directly evidenced).

RP-5

Robustness of the synthesis to evidence accounting (methods appendix)

Research question

Do the synthesis's qualitative conclusions change when support is counted by distinct study rather than by record, and when incompletely catalogued references are resolved?

Prior evidence

The relevant prior material is the synthesis's own evidence tabulation, linked below: its evidenced statements comprise 43 records that reduce to 26 distinct studies, and a subset of references is catalogued only as arXiv identifiers without resolved metadata.

Coverage record: Method 1 evidence & coverage map (local).

Open question

Whether any statement's confidence level shifts under distinct-study counting, and how sensitively interpretation depends on the unresolved references.

Survey/data plan

  • The synthesis's existing literature corpus (no new observations).
  • NASA ADS and the local bibliography: metadata resolution only, explicitly not observational evidence.

Analysis and decisive test

Recompute per-statement support on a distinct-study basis, resolve the identifier-only references, and compare against the record-based tally.

Expected result / decision point

A methods-appendix result establishing whether the conclusions are stable under evidence-accounting choices, with a corrected distinct-study support level per statement.

Caveats

Metadata resolution confers no scientific weight; abstract-only and citation-linking limits apply.

Source basis: evidence tabulation (record-versus-study counts; identifier-only references).

RP-6

Pre-registered acceptance criteria for AGN-feedback conclusions (methods appendix)

Research question

What explicit observational thresholds would justify promoting an AGN-feedback statement from tentative to established?

Prior evidence

The prior material is the set of AGN-feedback records already assembled in this synthesis (enumerated under RP-1 and RP-3, and summarised in the coverage record linked below); these statements presently sit below an accepted level and are promoted or demoted by qualitative judgement.

Coverage record: Method 1 evidence & coverage map (local).

Open question

The quantitative thresholds — independent-study support, observation-versus-simulation mix, effect size, and consistency — that should gate promotion.

Survey/data plan

  • The combined observational programmes of RP-1, RP-2, and RP-3 as the inputs a promotion decision would draw on (proposed evidence to gather).
  • NASA ADS and local corpus: independent-study counting only.

Analysis and decisive test

Specify thresholds and apply them retrospectively to the current AGN-feedback statements to determine which would promote, hold, or demote.

Expected result / decision point

A reproducible acceptance rule set adoptable by other syntheses, reducing subjective confidence judgements.

Caveats

Thresholds encode value choices and must be set transparently and periodically revisited.

Source basis: AGN feedback & quenching section (statements 2929 non-committal, 2946 reported).
