## Debate-map spine for Method3

1. Mechanism / ejective feedback — widely supported in scoped contexts.
   - Reader point: AGN/SMBH feedback can inject energy, drive outflows, or deplete/hear gas in selected systems.
   - Guard: do not make this a universal quenching explanation.

2. Outflow prevalence / frequency — emerging and sample-limited.
   - Reader point: selected samples show AGN-associated outflow signatures, but incidence depends on tracer, selection, and redshift.
   - Guard: do not turn a selected-sample or single-case result into a population-wide frequency.

3. Dominance debate — actively debated.
   - Reader point: AGN feedback is one quenching axis among several; whether it dominates depends on context.
   - Guard: keep central/bulge/black-hole predictors, halo/environment/satellite channels, strangulation, stripping, gas retention, low star-formation efficiency, stellar feedback, and recycling visible.

4. Maintenance / preventive heating — model-dependent or bounded in this corpus.
   - Reader point: preventive heating is distinct from ejective outflows.
   - Guard: simulations and models can motivate mechanisms, but they do not by themselves establish observed prevalence.

5. Gas reservoir response — actively debated.
   - Reader point: some AGN hosts show central gas depletion, while other quenched systems retain gas or have low star-formation efficiency.
   - Guard: do not imply gas removal alone explains all quenching.

6. Alternatives and countercases — widely supported as required context.
   - Reader point: non-AGN channels are not footnotes; they are part of the scientific map.
   - Guard: do not collapse environmental, halo, satellite, central, stellar-feedback, and gas-supply pathways into AGN feedback.

7. Simulation / model scope — model-dependent.
   - Reader point: simulations explain possible mechanisms under assumptions.
   - Guard: do not render simulations as observed population facts.

## Reader-facing sentence plan

S01 — Orientation sentence
- Planned role: define galaxy evolution for this page as the coupled history of stars, gas, black holes, dark matter halos, and environments.
- Debate-map basis: overview / regulated baryon-cycle framing.
- Reader need: understand that the page is not only a list of mechanisms, but a map of how mechanisms interact.
- Wording guard: avoid implying one master cause.
- Later binding need: broad overview sources only after review.

S02 — Quenching frame sentence
- Planned role: introduce quenching as a major transition in galaxy evolution and as a multi-channel process.
- Debate-map basis: overview and alternatives/countercases axes.
- Reader need: know why star formation shutting down is central to the page.
- Wording guard: say “can be regulated by” or “can involve,” not “is caused by” one pathway.
- Later binding need: sources spanning mass-linked and environment-linked quenching.

S03 — Scoped AGN mechanism sentence
- Planned role: state that AGN/SMBH feedback is a real, scoped mechanism that can affect gas through jets, winds, turbulence, heating, or starvation.
- Debate-map basis: mechanism_ejective_feedback; AGN Feedback & Quenching focus claims.
- Reader need: know why AGN feedback belongs in the page.
- Wording guard: include “in selected systems” or equivalent context.
- Later binding need: bind to mechanism and observed outflow/support rows after lane review.

S04 — Outflow evidence sentence
- Planned role: explain that molecular, ionized, or neutral outflows are observed in selected AGN-host or massive-galaxy samples.
- Debate-map basis: outflow_prevalence_frequency.
- Reader need: see concrete observational grounding without overgeneralization.
- Wording guard: frequency is sample/tracer/redshift dependent; single cases are examples, not prevalence anchors.
- Later binding need: selected-sample observational sources only.

S05 — Mode distinction sentence
- Planned role: distinguish ejective/outflow feedback from preventive or maintenance heating.
- Debate-map basis: maintenance_heating_prevention and simulation_model_scope.
- Reader need: avoid thinking all AGN feedback claims mean the same physical channel.
- Wording guard: do not merge model-dependent heating with observed outflow prevalence.
- Later binding need: split observational and simulation/model sources.

S06 — Reservoir caution sentence
- Planned role: state that gas-reservoir evidence is mixed: central depletion can occur, but retained reservoirs or low star-formation efficiency complicate simple removal stories.
- Debate-map basis: reservoir_response.
- Reader need: understand why “AGN removed the gas” is too simple as a general statement.
- Wording guard: distinguish central-kiloparsec depletion from galaxy-wide gas absence.
- Later binding need: reservoir and low-SFE rows after review.

S07 — Dominance debate sentence
- Planned role: make the central debate explicit: AGN feedback may be important, but its dominance relative to other quenching channels remains context-dependent.
- Debate-map basis: dominance_debate.
- Reader need: know what is still debated rather than reading only a consensus story.
- Wording guard: avoid “the dominant cause” unless a later bound source supports a narrow scope.
- Later binding need: debate-axis sources and competing predictor rows.

S08 — Alternatives sentence
- Planned role: list the main alternative or companion pathways: central structure, black-hole/bulge correlations, halo and satellite environment, strangulation, stripping, stellar feedback, recycling, gas retention, and low star-formation efficiency.
- Debate-map basis: alternatives_countercases.
- Reader need: see the multi-channel map in one place.
- Wording guard: keep alternatives visible as real pathways, not as caveats that are immediately dismissed.
- Later binding need: sources for each family, likely split across sentences if too dense.

S09 — Redshift/sample scope sentence
- Planned role: explain that some rapid shutdown claims at cosmic noon or in massive galaxies are important but sample- and model-dependent.
- Debate-map basis: AGN Feedback & Quenching focus claims and outflow/sample-scope axes.
- Reader need: understand that redshift and mass selection matter.
- Wording guard: do not turn z~1.5–3 selected cases into a universal high-redshift rule.
- Later binding need: high-redshift selected-sample sources only after review.

S10 — Simulation scope sentence
- Planned role: describe simulations as tests of mechanisms and assumptions, not as direct measurements of observed prevalence.
- Debate-map basis: simulation_model_scope.
- Reader need: understand what simulations can and cannot settle.
- Wording guard: mark simulation-backed statements as model-bounded.
- Later binding need: simulation/model rows separated from observational rows.

S11 — Reader takeaway sentence
- Planned role: synthesize the page’s Method3 stance: galaxy quenching is best presented as a context-dependent interaction among AGN feedback, gas supply, internal structure, halo/environment pathways, and observational selection.
- Debate-map basis: all seven axes.
- Reader need: leave with the main state-of-the-field summary.
- Wording guard: use “context-dependent,” “sample-dependent,” and “multi-channel” rather than a one-cause conclusion.
- Later binding need: only after Lana/Hwao review of the wording.

S12 — Transition / open-questions sentence
- Planned role: transition from settled mechanisms to open questions: prevalence, dominance, reservoir response, and maintenance heating remain the areas that need careful evidence binding.
- Debate-map basis: outflow_prevalence_frequency, dominance_debate, reservoir_response, maintenance_heating_prevention.
- Reader need: know which parts are still live debates.
- Wording guard: state uncertainty as a useful result, not as a failure of the page.
- Later binding need: next P2/P3 review before citations or claim chips.
