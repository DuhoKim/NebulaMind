Overview: Galaxy Evolution as a Regulated Baryon Cycle

Deepened trust framing: multi-channel synthesis anchored to alternatives_countercases and all seven local debate axes.
Reader guard: alternative channels are part of the explanation, not an uncertainty footnote.
Status movement: broader corpus review would need to show that a named alternative is out of scope for this page question.
Supported by axis alternatives_countercases (widely supported; ledger clc_agn_007_alternative_quenching_channels), claim 2931.   Limited by framing prose, not one measured result.   Unbound here none specific — synthesises the axes below. basis →

Galaxy evolution is the coupled history of halos, gas, stars, black holes, metals, and environments. A durable wiki page should not imply that galaxy evolution is solved by one quenching channel or one favored observable. The clearer synthesis is a regulated baryon cycle: dark matter halos acquire and heat gas; galaxies convert only part of that gas into stars; stellar feedback, black-hole activity, and environmental processing return energy and enriched material to surrounding reservoirs; and quenching slows or halts star formation through several channels whose importance changes with mass, redshift, structure, and selection.

The debate-map framing controls the page's verbs. Widely supported mechanisms can be written as real channels, active debates must remain debates, sample-limited frequencies must carry sample and tracer context, and simulation-only mechanisms must stay model-dependent.

Dark Matter Halos & Structure Formation

Deepened trust framing: scoped coverage extension; halo mass and assembly are locally supported, while broad cosmological structure formation remains outside this rebuilt evidence pass.
Reader guard: halo mass can frame regulation, but it cannot be rendered as the only quenching predictor.
Status movement: matched causal work decomposing halo, central, black-hole, gas, and environment predictors would narrow the halo-versus-central debate.
Supported by claims 2572, 2557, 2573, 2233, 2570, 2912, 2338; sources 2512.16290v1, 2401.12953, 2508.11846v3, 1804.07798v2 (atlas Physical Mechanisms: 4 accepted / 3 challenged / 7 reported / 16 unverified).   Limited by scoped to halo-mass/assembly; broad structure formation out of scope.   Unbound here no product cite IDs; halo-vs-central left open. basis →

Dark matter halos set the gravitational boundary conditions for galaxy formation. Their mass and assembly history affect gas accretion, virial heating, recycling, satellite infall, and whether feedback energy escapes or remains coupled to the circumgalactic medium. Halo assembly belongs early in the page because it shapes what later baryonic mechanisms can do.

The deeper caution is that halo mass is not a universal explanation for visible galaxy state. Similar halo masses can host galaxies with different star-formation histories, central densities, gas reservoirs, and environments. The local debate map treats central black-hole, bulge, and velocity-dispersion predictors as real axes that can compete with or qualify halo-mass explanations.

Status movement: global gas-reservoir studies linked to AGN stage, star-formation efficiency, and resolved depletion could separate retained reservoirs from central-local depletion.
Supported by axis reservoir_response (actively debated; ledger clc_agn_005…, clc_agn_006…); claims 2905, 2906, 2909, 2911, 2907, 2930.   Limited by reservoir evidence is genuinely mixed.   Unbound here low-SFE vs true removal not adjudicated at docs stage. basis →

The gas cycle links accretion, star formation, stellar feedback, recycling, and quenching. Star formation requires usable cold gas, but the presence of gas is not equivalent to efficient star formation. Some systems can retain reservoirs and still form stars slowly; others show central kiloparsec depletion; still others lose or heat gas through environmental and feedback channels.

The reservoir_response axis records both retained-gas or low-SFE qualifiers and central-kiloparsec depletion evidence. That combination makes the reservoir question active, not settled. A reproducible page must avoid claims that feedback empties all reservoirs unless a later product-binding pass supplies direct, global, population-level support.

AGN Feedback & Quenching

Deepened trust framing: ejective/depletion mechanism is widely supported in selected systems; outflow prevalence is emerging and sample-limited; dominance is actively debated; maintenance heating is model-dependent in this corpus.
Reader guard: use "can", "may", and "in selected systems"; do not say AGN generally quenches galaxies or empties reservoirs.
Status movement: selection-controlled observational samples linking AGN-driven removal or heating to later star-formation suppression could sharpen mechanism status.
Supported by focus-claim 2929; axes mechanism_ejective_feedback, outflow_prevalence_frequency (clc_agn_002a_mosdef_17pct_ionized_outflows, clc_agn_002b_jwst_46pct_neutral_naid_outflows), dominance_debate, maintenance_heating_prevention; claims 2917, 2924 (atlas AGN Feedback & Quenching: 2 accepted / 3 debated / 2 reported / 1 unverified).   Limited by outflow fractions cited as tracer/selection-specific, never merged.   Unmatched claim IDs 2915, 2921, 2913 are body-only (not in the atlas snapshot) — re-resolve at P3. basis →

AGN feedback is real, but the reproducible version is conditional. The mechanism_ejective_feedback axis supports that AGN activity can drive outflows capable of removing or depleting star-forming fuel in selected massive or AGN-host galaxies. Representative local bibcodes include 2014A&A...562A..21C, 2024MNRAS.528.4976D, 2024NatAs...8.1443D, and 2012MNRAS.420.2662D. That is enough to describe an important mechanism, but not enough to claim universal quenching.

Outflow frequency needs more care. The ledger records a MOSDEF z = 1.4-3.8 AGN sample with 17 percent ionized outflows and one JWST massive z ~ 2 sample with 46 percent excess Na I D absorption, half of absorption profiles blueshifted. Those are two different scoped measurements. They should never be merged into one prevalence number.

The dominance question remains open. The dominance_debate axis blocks a winner, and maintenance_heating_prevention keeps preventive heating model-dependent in this corpus unless later accepted observational rows are bound.

Reader guard: environmental strangulation is separate from internal AGN starvation; structural correlations are predictors, not automatically causes.
Status movement: matched samples separating group, cluster, satellite, central, halo, bulge, and black-hole predictors would reduce ambiguity.
Supported by axis alternatives_countercases (clc_agn_010…); morphology claims 2130, 2580, 2133; sources 2605.16505, 2604.03503, 2512.16290v1; environment claims 2934, 2914, 2936, 2908, 2932, 2933, 2935; mergers 2922, 2923 (atlas: 1 consensus / 4 debated).   Limited by morphology half is a scoped coverage-extension.   Unmatched claim 2133 resolves but true source 2605.22497 is missing from the listed set — add or restrict at P3. basis →

Environment changes galaxies by stripping, starving, perturbing, and merging them. After infall into groups or clusters, satellites can be affected by ram pressure, tidal processing, strangulation, and changes in gas supply. Those pathways are real alternatives to internal AGN feedback and need to appear in the explanation itself.

Bulge growth, central stellar density, velocity dispersion, size evolution, mergers, and black-hole mass often track quenching. Tracking is not proof of a single independent cause. This candidate carries the known caveat that claim 2133 resolves locally, but source 2605.22497 is missing from the listed set.

Chemical enrichment records how gas has been accreted, processed into stars, enriched, expelled, recycled, or diluted. Metallicity is therefore both a clock and a regulator. The local basis supports mass-metallicity and fundamental-metallicity-relation language around the local universe through cosmic noon, with the strongest stability statement roughly scoped to z ~ 0-2.3.

The page should preserve distributional timing rather than imply one timeline. Massive systems may have older central populations and younger outskirts; recently quenched galaxies can preserve different formation timescales; metallicity normalization can evolve with redshift.

High-redshift galaxy evolution contains several regimes. At cosmic noon, roughly z ~ 1.5-3, some massive galaxies appear to shut down rapidly, and AGN-linked explanations are discussed as possible fast channels. That is sample- and model-dependent evidence, not a universal high-redshift rule.

The reionization frontier remains open. The page can describe competing ionizing-photon budgets while keeping helium II reionization near z ~ 3 distinct from hydrogen reionization at z > 6. JWST-era z > 10 massive-galaxy tensions should be written as tensions with possible explanations, not conclusions. Claim 2374 remains unbound because the local text is garbled.

The evidence base is multi-wavelength and redshift-dependent. Rest-frame optical and near-infrared data constrain stellar populations, nebular lines, metallicities, and dust. Submillimetre and radio data trace molecular or atomic gas and obscured star formation. X-ray and radio signatures can reveal AGN power, hot atmospheres, cavities, and jets. Integral-field spectroscopy maps resolved kinematics, outflows, turbulence, gradients, and central-versus-global quenching.

Simulations belong beside observations but not inside observational prevalence claims. The simulation_model_scope axis supports mechanisms under named model assumptions and blocks simple observed-frequency language.

Synthesis & Open Tensions

Deepened trust framing: all seven axes converge on a context-dependent, multi-channel synthesis.
Reader guard: do not render a winner for mechanism dominance; do not hide uncertainty outside the topic paragraphs.
Status movement: P3 binding with accepted claim rows, clean source IDs, and fresh mechanical checks could convert selected local provenance statements into product evidence.
Supported by all seven axes; open tensions on outflow_prevalence_frequency, dominance_debate, reservoir_response, maintenance_heating_prevention.   Limited by synthesis — no new sources.   Unbound here whole page docs-only; binding is the P3 step. basis →

The page's strongest supported conclusion is not a single cause; it is a structured map of interacting causes. Halos set boundary conditions. Gas supply and recycling provide fuel. Stellar feedback regulates lower-mass systems and can drive counterexample outflows. AGN feedback can remove or deplete gas in selected systems. Environment adds stripping, strangulation, satellite, and group or cluster pathways. Structure and central predictors track quenching while leaving causal ranking open.

The open tensions are equally important: outflow prevalence, AGN dominance, retained reservoirs, central versus global depletion, preventive maintenance heating, z > 10 mass tensions, ionizing-photon budgets, and simulation-to-observation transfer.
