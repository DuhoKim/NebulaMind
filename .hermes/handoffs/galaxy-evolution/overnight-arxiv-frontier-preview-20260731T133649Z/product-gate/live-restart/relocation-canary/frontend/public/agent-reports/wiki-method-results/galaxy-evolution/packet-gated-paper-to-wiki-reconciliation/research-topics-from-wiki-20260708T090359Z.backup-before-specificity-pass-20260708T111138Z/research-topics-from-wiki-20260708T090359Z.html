RP-1

Testing whether AGN feedback directly regulates star formation

Research aim

Establish whether active galactic nucleus (AGN) feedback causally regulates or shuts down star formation in massive galaxies, rather than merely correlating with quiescence.

Background & significance

Galaxy-evolution syntheses invoke AGN feedback as a primary regulator of the baryon cycle, but the direct observational link between AGN activity and suppressed star formation remains weakly constrained: much of the assembled support neither clearly confirms nor rejects a causal role. A causal test — not another correlation — is the missing step.

Survey/data plan

Integral-field spectroscopy from SDSS-MaNGA and SAMI to map spatially resolved star-formation histories around AGN; ALMA CO and dense-gas imaging to measure molecular reservoirs; Chandra and XMM-Newton to quantify AGN power; VLA and LOFAR to trace jet/kinetic activity. Each contributes one link in the chain (fuel, trigger, energetics, response); none is treated here as already-decisive evidence.

Study design

Assemble mass-, redshift-, and morphology-matched AGN and non-AGN control samples; compare resolved star-formation suppression, molecular gas content, and outflow signatures as a function of AGN power and mode.

Expected contribution

A controlled, causal-leaning test of AGN regulation that would move the mechanism from plausible to demonstrated (or bounded) in real galaxies.

Feasibility & caveats

Largely archival and feasible now; the main risk is that matched control samples remain confounded by selection, so results should be framed as conditional on the matching quality.

Provenance: derived from the Method 1 wiki's treatment of internal AGN feedback (claim 2929, currently non-committal / unverified).

RP-2

Disentangling internal (mass-driven) quenching from environmental quenching

Research aim

Map the conditions — stellar mass, redshift, and local environment — under which internally driven quenching and environment-driven quenching become separable versus degenerate.

Background & significance

Whether a galaxy stops forming stars because of its own mass-linked processes or because of its surroundings is genuinely contested: a minority of studies support a clean separation while most are inconclusive. The disagreement is likely a conditions problem, not a single missing measurement.

Survey/data plan

SDSS and GAMA group catalogues for a low-redshift environmental baseline; COSMOS/CANDELS and JWST deep fields to extend to cosmic noon; Euclid and Rubin (LSST) for wide-area environment and weak-lensing halo mass. Proposed as the sampling grid across mass and environment — not as existing proof of either channel.

Study design

Hold stellar mass fixed and vary environment (and vice versa) to isolate each channel; quantify where the quenched fraction responds to mass, to environment, or to their interaction.

Expected contribution

A quantitative 'where-do-the-channels-separate' map that replaces the settled/unsettled framing with explicit conditions.

Feasibility & caveats

Feasible with existing and near-term surveys; caveat is that environment proxies and halo-mass estimates carry scatter that must propagate into the separation boundary.

Provenance: derived from the Method 1 wiki's treatment of joint internal/environmental quenching (claim 2931, debated).

RP-3

Observational evidence for sustained maintenance heating in massive halos

Research aim

Find direct observational support for sustained AGN heating as a maintenance mechanism in massive systems, where current backing is largely simulation-based.

Background & significance

Sustained low-level heating that keeps massive galaxies quiescent is a standard ingredient of galaxy-formation models, but the evidence for it in this synthesis comes chiefly from simulations rather than measured prevalence. An observational program is needed to test the model expectation.

Survey/data plan

Chandra imaging of X-ray cavities and their mechanical power; XMM-Newton and eROSITA for hot-halo thermodynamics; VLA and LOFAR for radio-mode AGN duty cycles; compared against IllustrisTNG- and HORIZON-AGN-style simulation predictions. Simulations here are the benchmark to test, not the evidence base.

Study design

Measure cavity/heating power versus radiative cooling luminosity across a mass-selected sample; test whether heating balances cooling closely enough, and often enough, to maintain quiescence.

Expected contribution

Would convert a model-dependent expectation into an observed (or refuted) prevalence, closing a key gap between simulations and data.

Feasibility & caveats

Cavity and hot-halo measurements are demanding but established; caveat is duty-cycle incompleteness — snapshots may miss time-averaged heating.

Provenance: derived from the Method 1 wiki's treatment of maintenance heating (claim 2946, reported / simulation-supported).

RP-4

A prioritized observational agenda for under-supported parts of the galaxy-evolution picture

Research aim

Identify which parts of the standard galaxy-evolution narrative are currently least backed by direct evidence, and rank them by how tractably new data could ground them.

Background & significance

Large portions of the synthesis — dark-matter-halo connections, gas supply, environment, chemical enrichment, high-redshift and reionization — are presented as narrative rather than being tied to specific evidence. A deliberate prioritization would direct effort where it most improves the picture.

Survey/data plan

Wide statistical surveys as the proposed grounding source: DESI spectroscopy, SDSS legacy imaging/spectroscopy, Euclid and Rubin (LSST) for scale, and JWST for the high-redshift frontier. Listed as candidate data to recruit, not as current support.

Study design

Assess each thematic section for its current evidential support, estimate the evidence yield of each candidate survey, and produce a ranked observational to-do list.

Expected contribution

A transparent, reusable prioritization that turns 'mostly narrative' sections into a concrete research roadmap.

Feasibility & caveats

A synthesis/meta-analysis task, feasible now; caveat is that yield estimates are judgement calls and should be revisited as surveys deliver.

Provenance: derived from the Method 1 wiki's coverage map (only two of nine sections carry direct evidence; the rest are narrative-only).

RP-5

How robust are galaxy-evolution conclusions to how evidence is counted?

Research aim

Test whether qualitative conclusions change when supporting literature is counted by distinct study rather than by record, and when incompletely catalogued records are fully resolved.

Background & significance

Evidence for a given statement can be tallied by record or by distinct study; a pile of records can overstate independent support when several point at the same work. A robustness check quantifies how much interpretation depends on the counting convention and on record completeness.

Survey/data plan

The method's existing literature corpus, with the NASA ADS bibliographic database proposed as the resolution source for incompletely catalogued references. No new observations required; this is a methods study.

Study design

Recompute per-topic support on a distinct-study basis, resolve under-specified references, and compare the resulting support levels to the record-based tally.

Expected contribution

A methods-appendix result establishing whether the synthesis's conclusions are stable against reasonable changes in evidence accounting.

Feasibility & caveats

Fully feasible with local and bibliographic resources; caveat is that resolution is metadata work and does not itself add scientific weight.

Provenance: derived from the Method 1 wiki's evidence tables (record-vs-study counts; several references catalogued only as identifiers).

RP-6

Defining acceptance criteria for AGN-feedback conclusions

Research aim

Propose explicit, pre-registered observational thresholds that would justify promoting AGN-feedback statements from tentative to established.

Background & significance

The AGN-feedback theme carries the synthesis's least-settled mechanism statements. Without agreed criteria for what counts as sufficient evidence, promotion decisions stay subjective. A pre-registered standard makes them reproducible.

Survey/data plan

The combined observational programs above (resolved star-formation regulation, channel separation, and maintenance-heating measurements) as the inputs a promotion decision would draw on — proposed evidence to gather, not evidence in hand.

Study design

Specify quantitative thresholds (e.g., minimum independent-study support, required observational-versus-simulation mix, effect-size and consistency requirements) and apply them retrospectively to current AGN-feedback statements.

Expected contribution

A reproducible acceptance standard that other syntheses could adopt, reducing subjective trust judgements.

Feasibility & caveats

A methodology proposal, immediately actionable; caveat is that thresholds encode value choices and should be set transparently and revisited.

Provenance: derived from the Method 1 wiki's AGN feedback & quenching section (its bound statements sit below an 'accepted' level).

Scope and limitations

Research proposals are derived from the current Method 1 Galaxy Evolution synthesis; they are hypotheses and study designs for future work, not accepted claims and not tied to catalogued evidence.

Every proposal names proposed data — surveys, instruments, archives, or simulations the study would use — which is distinct from evidence already assembled in the source synthesis. Method 1's confidence scale is internal to Method 1 and is not directly comparable to the other method agendas.
