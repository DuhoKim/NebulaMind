Galaxy Evolution — three independent methods

A reader's guide to the three method wiki candidates and how their evidence + trust work. Static preview — not the live product wiki.

These three methods do NOT share a trust scale. Each uses a different trust vocabulary, from a different local basis, at a different granularity (claim vs evidence-row vs section). Do not compare or combine their "trust" labels across methods — there is no common score. Pick a method by approach fit below. Full detail: the cross-method trust legend.

Which method for which purpose

Method 1 · packet-gated

Reconciled same-format page

Best if you want the baseline page's own 30 claim chips preserved in a same-format wiki with honest labeling.

Trust: local page-inventory status (reported / debated / unverified) — but only for 3 of 30 chips.

The other 27 chips are unbound-local (evidence in the product DB — a closed gate): unknown here, not high-trust. arXiv links only where present in local data.
