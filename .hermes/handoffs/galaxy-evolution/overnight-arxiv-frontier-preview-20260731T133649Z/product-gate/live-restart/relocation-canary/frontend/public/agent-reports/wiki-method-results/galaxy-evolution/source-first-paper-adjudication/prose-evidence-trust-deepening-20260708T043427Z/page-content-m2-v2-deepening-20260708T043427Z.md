## Overview: Evidence-First Galaxy Evolution

Galaxy evolution is a regulated baryon-cycle problem. Halos gather gas, galaxies turn only part of that gas into stars, feedback moves energy and material among gas phases, and environment changes the boundary conditions. The Method2 contribution is narrower: it asks which source positions can support cautious public prose about AGN feedback, gas removal, stellar-feedback alternatives, and maintenance heating.

The local trust language in this candidate is method-local. "Accepted full" means the source position can support a direct claim in this Method2 ledger. "Accepted-limited" means the source can contribute only with scope, model-dependence, source-type, or caution wording preserved. "Excluded" means the source remains visible but is held out of public support. "Rejected" means it must not support the claim.

The corrected deterministic count used here is array-derived from the evidence-trust map: 21 claim-support positions, 2 accepted full, 19 accepted-limited, 2 excluded, and 12 rejected. ## Dark Matter Halos & Structure Formation

Dark matter halos set the gravitational stage but do not decide the visible galaxy outcome alone. Gas accretion, cooling, feedback coupling, merger history, and environment determine how much baryonic material turns into stars and how long the reservoir remains available.

This section stays mostly unhighlighted because the Method2 evidence package is not a halo-formation source set. Its value is in constraining how feedback should be phrased inside a broader halo-and-baryon-cycle article: AGN feedback can be important without becoming a one-parameter explanation for galaxy evolution.

## Gas Supply, Star Formation & Feedback

Feedback prose must separate outflow from permanent reservoir loss. Gas can leave a star-forming region, later fall back, heat a surrounding phase, stir turbulence, or locally compress gas. A source-first article should not convert every outflow detection into a quenching sentence.

## AGN Feedback & Quenching

AGN feedback is best treated as mode-, phase-, scale-, and environment-dependent. Jets, winds, turbulence, cavities, and heating can matter, but their effect changes with gas phase and measurement frame. Some rows are direct observations, some are review syntheses, and many are accepted-limited because the source itself supports a narrower statement than the headline might suggest.

## Environment, Morphology & Structural Growth

Environment can strip, starve, perturb, and transform galaxies, but the Method2 evidence set does not provide a broad new environment claim. It provides context controls: group-scale AGN feedback should not be converted into a universal isolated-galaxy statement, and M51 rows should stay single-galaxy-scoped.

This matters because environment and internal feedback can produce similar passive outcomes. A careful article should keep external processing, central feedback, gas recycling, and morphology distinct unless a source directly supports their connection.

## Chemical Enrichment & Cosmic Timing

Chemical enrichment and outflow histories record how baryons move through the galaxy, halo, and circumgalactic medium. Method2 adds a non-AGN control through stellar-feedback rows, which prevents the AGN section from absorbing every outflow or quenching statement.

## High-Redshift & Reionization Frontier

High-redshift material in this candidate qualifies feedback and quenching mechanisms; it does not settle the reionization source budget or the early massive-galaxy abundance problem. Those questions require their own source-position ledgers.

The evidence discipline is therefore conservative: high-redshift rows can constrain how feedback is framed, but they should not be borrowed to create unsupported claims about all early galaxies or reionization.

## Observational Evidence & Surveys

The Method2 evidence set mixes observations, simulations, and review synthesis. Direct observations anchor some claims, while simulations and reviews provide model-dependent or synthesis-level support. That distinction is part of the trust layer, not a footnote.

All citation markers here are `cite-unmatched`: they refer to local Method2 source-position IDs, not product evidence IDs. A later product-layer step would need to resolve local IDs to product citations before any live wiki use.

## Synthesis & Open Tensions

The most defensible synthesis is conditional. AGN feedback is real but scoped; outflows can remove or suppress star-forming gas in some systems; stellar feedback remains a necessary alternative and qualifier; gas removal competes with recycling; maintenance heating is model-dependent; and radio/kinetic modes matter but carry review-source and coupling caveats.

The 28060 caveat keeps the sign of feedback honest: AGN activity can locally compress gas and promote star formation. The excluded rows keep bookkeeping and single-source stacking from inflating support. The rejected rows preserve the negative boundary of the method. This candidate deepens prose by making those constraints visible rather than hiding them behind a single count.
