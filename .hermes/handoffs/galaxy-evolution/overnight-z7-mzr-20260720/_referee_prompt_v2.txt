ABSTRACT:
We report a bounded, automated, descriptive result (not a validated measurement) on the gas-phase mass-metallicity relation (MZR) at z>7. Placing an SDSS local anchor (N=203,599 star-forming galaxies, Tremonti 2004 [T04] scale) and the JWST census of Nakajima 2023 onto one abundance scale via the Kewley & Ellison (2008) metallicity-dependent T04->PP04-O3N2 cubic, and comparing only within the stellar-mass overlap logM in [8.0,9.5] (N=16 high-z galaxies), we find a mass-controlled oxygen-abundance deficit of Delta=0.45 dex below the local relation (bootstrap 95% CI [0.28,0.62], excludes 0). We then forward-model the JWST emission-line selection function two independent ways (a Monte-Carlo mock-parent->line-luminosity->NIRSpec-detection model and a first-principles/empirical estimate). Both agree the selection bias is now BOUNDED: Delta_sel ~ 0.10 dex (range 0.04-0.20) for the strong-line channel that drives the matched sample. The selection-corrected deficit is 0.25-0.41 dex (central ~0.35), CI still excludes 0: selection accounts for only ~10-45% of the offset (>=55% residual), so it cannot explain the deficit away. The result is therefore SELECTION-BOUNDED, not a clean detection, because: (i) the two selection estimates differ ~2x; (ii) the surviving signal leans on the strong-line calibration transfer -- the Te/auroral channel, which is MORE selection-biased, does not independently confirm; (iii) single-survey (Nakajima+23, N=16); (iv) no z>7 simulation comparator.
