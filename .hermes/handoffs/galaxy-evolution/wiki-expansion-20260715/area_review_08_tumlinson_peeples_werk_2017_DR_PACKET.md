## 2. Established Findings

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REV08-E01 | review_synthesis | The CGM is gas outside a galaxy's stellar disk/ISM and inside its virial radius. | Geometric working definition; boundaries can overlap satellites, outflows, and the IGM. | High as a review convention, not a sharp physical discontinuity. | [REV08-R00] |

## 3. Open Debates and Tensions

| ID | Topic | Competing positions | Why unresolved / boundary | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REV08-D01 | Cool-cloud survival | Rapid hydrodynamic shredding versus survival/growth aided by cooling, conduction suppression, magnetic fields, or continual regeneration. | Cloud sizes, interfaces, and microphysics were unresolved observationally and numerically. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P007] [REV08-P018] [REV08-P055] [REV08-P056] |
| REV08-D02 | O VI ionization and temperature | Collisionally ionized gas near 10^5.5 K versus photoionized or non-equilibrium interfaces/cooling flows. | One O VI column does not fix density, path length, temperature, or total mass. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P041] [REV08-P042] [REV08-P061] [REV08-P069] [REV08-P070] |
| REV08-D03 | CGM baryon closure | Large cool/warm reservoirs versus still-unmeasured hot and low-density phases. | Radial extrapolation, metallicity, UV background, and halo-mass normalization dominate. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P003] [REV08-P005] [REV08-P073] |
| REV08-D04 | Metallicity as an origin tag | Very low metallicity suggests fresh accretion and high metallicity suggests outflow/recycling, but mixing and ionization create overlap. | Metallicity alone cannot uniquely reconstruct trajectory or launch time. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P046] [REV08-P054] [REV08-P064] |
| REV08-D05 | Inflow versus outflow identification | Major-axis co-rotation and minor-axis absorption are statistical clues, but projection and recycling make individual classifications degenerate. | Requires galaxy geometry, component kinematics, metallicity, and preferably multiple sightlines. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P024] [REV08-P046] [REV08-P052] |
| REV08-D06 | Hydrostatic versus dynamic halos | Virialized pressure-supported coronae versus halos continually disturbed by accretion, fountains, cooling, and nonthermal support. | Sparse X-ray profiles and uncertain boundary conditions prevented a unique model. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P042] [REV08-P050] |
| REV08-D07 | Feedback-model dependence | Different wind loading, velocity, hydrodynamic coupling, and recycling prescriptions yield different CGM columns and phase masses. | Galaxy-integrated calibration does not uniquely constrain halo-scale gas histories. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P044] [REV08-P058] [REV08-P060] |
| REV08-D08 | Simulation-observation comparison | Matching one ion profile can hide wrong temperature, density, metallicity, or gas origin distributions. | Synthetic spectra require the same UV background, ionization, resolution, and sample selection as observations. | [REV08-R00] [REV08-P033] [REV08-P045] [REV08-P046] |
