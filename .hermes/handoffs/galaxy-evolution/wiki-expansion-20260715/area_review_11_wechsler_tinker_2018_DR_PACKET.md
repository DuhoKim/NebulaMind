## 3. Open Debates and Tensions

| ID | Topic | Competing positions | Why unresolved / boundary | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REV11-D01 | Galaxy assembly bias | Mass-only occupation versus explicit coupling of galaxy properties to halo formation history or concentration. | Halo masses and secondary properties are noisy and correlated; environment can mimic the signal. | [REV11-P008] [REV11-P010] [REV11-P017] [REV11-P030] [REV11-P041] |
| REV11-D02 | Origin of SHMR scatter | Merger/accretion history, concentration, spin, baryonic stochasticity, and measurement error as dominant contributors. | Inferred intrinsic scatter depends on deconvolution and cross-correlated galaxy observables. | [REV11-P006] [REV11-P009] [REV11-P018] [REV11-P046] |
| REV11-D03 | Optimal abundance-matching proxy | Present Vmax/M, peak V or M, infall values, or compound concentration-sensitive proxies. | Satellite stripping/disruption, resolution, and orphan galaxies change small-scale clustering. | [REV11-P011] [REV11-P014] [REV11-P041] |
| REV11-D04 | Satellite quenching mechanism | Starvation/strangulation, ram pressure, tidal effects, and preprocessing with different timing. | Demographics constrain delay and fade times better than a unique physical mechanism. | [REV11-P031] [REV11-P044] |
| REV11-D05 | Low-mass SHMR | Steep abundance-matching extrapolation versus broader/scattered dwarf occupation and incomplete luminous samples. | Ultra-faint completeness, reionization, tidal histories, and dynamical mass conversion are uncertain. | [REV11-R00] [REV11-P001] [REV11-P002] [REV11-P022] |
| REV11-D06 | Orphan galaxies | Track galaxies after numerical subhalo loss versus omit them or absorb their effect in HOD profiles. | Disruption and dynamical-friction times depend on resolution and baryonic structure. | [REV11-P007] [REV11-P033] [REV11-P041] |
| REV11-D07 | Baryonic modification of halo statistics | Apply dark-matter-only mass/profile calibrations versus include contraction, expansion, and mass redistribution. | Effects are scale-, mass-, and feedback-model dependent and can rival precision targets. | [REV11-P012] [REV11-P040] |
| REV11-D08 | Halo-mass inference method | Clustering/HOD, abundance, groups, weak lensing, satellite dynamics, X-ray, and SZ constraints have different degeneracies. | Stellar mass, projection, miscentering, mass calibration, selection, and cosmology prevent method-free comparison. | [REV11-P005] [REV11-P006] [REV11-P042] [REV11-P045] |
