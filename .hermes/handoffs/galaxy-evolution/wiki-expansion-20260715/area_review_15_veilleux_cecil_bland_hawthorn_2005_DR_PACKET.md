## 3. Open Debates and Tensions

| ID | Topic | Competing positions | Why unresolved / boundary | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REV15-D01 | Driving channel | Hot thermal pressure versus radiation pressure, cosmic rays, magnetic stresses, jets, or AGN winds. | Multiple channels coexist; coupling and phase diagnostics were incomplete. | [REV15-P003] [REV15-P005] [REV15-P010] [REV15-P033] [REV15-P036] |
| REV15-D02 | Thermalization and mass loading | Efficient supernova/wind thermalization with modest loading versus strong cooling and heavy entrainment. | The free wind is faint; X-rays emphasize interfaces, leaving energy and mass injection degenerate. | [REV15-P003] [REV15-P005] [REV15-P015] [REV15-P016] |
| REV15-D03 | Cool-cloud acceleration | Ram-pressure entrainment of pre-existing clouds versus in-situ cooling/fragmentation and other forces. | Hydrodynamic cloud destruction times conflicted with observed cool-gas extent and speed. | [REV15-P008] [REV15-P009] [REV15-P022] [REV15-P028] |
| REV15-D04 | Escape versus fountain | Permanent escape into the IGM versus slowing, mixing, and recycling through a halo fountain. | Projection and uncertain halo potential/drag prevent trajectory reconstruction. | [REV15-P007] [REV15-P011] [REV15-P017] [REV15-P021] [REV15-P031] |
| REV15-D05 | Mass and energy census | Cool/warm phases dominate measured mass versus hot phase dominates energy/metals. | Different tracers have incompatible density, geometry, ionization, and filling-factor biases. | [REV15-P009] [REV15-P015] [REV15-P016] [REV15-P022] [REV15-P026] |
| REV15-D06 | High-z impact | Winds as principal IGM-enrichment/feedback mechanism versus limited transport and strong recycling. | Sparse direct halo constraints and parameterized cosmological winds allowed broad outcomes. | [REV15-P012] [REV15-P013] [REV15-P020] [REV15-P025] [REV15-P039] |
| REV15-D07 | AGN/starburst partition | AGN-dominated versus starburst-dominated power in composite ULIRGs and nuclear outflows. | Bolometric corrections, duty cycles, obscuration, coupling, and spatial overlap are uncertain. | [REV15-P010] [REV15-P033] [REV15-P036] |
| REV15-D08 | Wind threshold | A sharp star-formation intensity threshold versus a continuous probability/sensitivity relation. | Area definitions, inclination, tracer depth, and selection can create or blur a threshold. | [REV15-P004] [REV15-P027] [REV15-P030] |
