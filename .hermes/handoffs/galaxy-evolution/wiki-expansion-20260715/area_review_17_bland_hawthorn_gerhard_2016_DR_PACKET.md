## 1. Review identity and scope map

- Review: Bland-Hawthorn & Gerhard (2016), *The Galaxy in Context: Structural, Kinematic, and Integrated Properties*.
- Identity: DOI `10.1146/annurev-astro-081915-023441`; arXiv `1602.07702`; ADS `2016ARA&A..54..529B`.
- Scope: integrated Milky Way properties, bulge/bar, disks, solar constants, gas kinematics, stellar/dark halos and assembly history, with frame and component boundaries explicit.
- The review is synthesis; load-bearing claims below cite independently reconciled primary sources.

## 2. Established findings

### REV17-E01
- Epistemic type: synthesis
- Bounded statement: The Milky Way is an L-star barred disk and lies near the green valley in external-galaxy comparisons, but its integrated placement depends on dust and population assumptions.
- Component/frame/regime: whole Galaxy / external context
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P004, REV17-P019, REV17-P022, REV17-P023

### REV17-E02
- Epistemic type: measurement synthesis
- Bounded statement: A common-IMF synthesis gives a current star-formation rate near 1.65 solar masses per year and a stellar mass of order five to six times 10^10 solar masses.
- Component/frame/regime: whole Galaxy; Kroupa/Chabrier-like IMF conventions
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P004, REV17-P019, REV17-P046

### REV17-E03
- Epistemic type: measurement + dynamical modeling
- Bounded statement: The central box/peanut structure is the vertically thickened part of the bar; red-clump maps and cylindrical rotation strongly favor a bar-built bulge.
- Component/frame/regime: inner about 3 kpc
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P005, REV17-P006, REV17-P007, REV17-P008, REV17-P032

### REV17-E04
- Epistemic type: measurement + gas modeling
- Bounded statement: The planar bar extends beyond the box/peanut bulge to about 5 kpc, while its angle and pattern speed remain model-dependent.
- Component/frame/regime: inner disk; Sun-Galactic-centre geometry explicit
- Confidence: medium-high
- Sources: REV17-P039, REV17-P042, REV17-P043, REV17-P044

### REV17-E05
- Epistemic type: measurement + modeling
- Bounded statement: The stellar disk has population-dependent radial and vertical scales; a single thin/thick double exponential is an approximation rather than a unique decomposition.
- Component/frame/regime: disk; geometric versus mono-abundance populations
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P002, REV17-P003, REV17-P026, REV17-P027, REV17-P028

### REV17-E06
- Epistemic type: measurement
- Bounded statement: Geometric, chemical, age and kinematic definitions of the thick disk select overlapping but non-identical stellar populations.
- Component/frame/regime: solar cylinder and wider disk
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P002, REV17-P012, REV17-P026

### REV17-E07
- Epistemic type: measurement synthesis
- Bounded statement: Pre-Gaia geometric and dynamical constraints converged near R0 about 8.2 kpc and circular speed near 235-240 km/s, with covariance between them.
- Component/frame/regime: Galactocentric frame; LSR/circular speed distinct
- Confidence: medium-high
- Sources: REV17-P010, REV17-P045, REV17-P046

### REV17-E08
- Epistemic type: kinematic calibration
- Bounded statement: The Sun has a substantial peculiar velocity relative to the LSR; asymmetric-drift treatment is essential, especially for the V component.
- Component/frame/regime: heliocentric to LSR transform
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P011, REV17-P047

### REV17-E09
- Epistemic type: dynamical inference
- Bounded statement: Milky Way virial-mass estimates cluster around 10^12 solar masses but span a factor of roughly two because tracers, anisotropy and halo definitions differ.
- Component/frame/regime: dark halo; Mvir/Rvir definition required
- Confidence: medium
- Sources: REV17-P016, REV17-P017, REV17-P046, REV17-P048

### REV17-E10
- Epistemic type: measurement
- Bounded statement: The stellar halo contains coherent streams and is not globally phase mixed, so simple equilibrium inference is unsafe for unfiltered samples.
- Component/frame/regime: outer stellar halo
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P025, REV17-P048

### REV17-E11
- Epistemic type: dynamical modeling
- Bounded statement: Local vertical dynamics and global mass models require a dark component, but local density estimates covary with disk surface density and equilibrium assumptions.
- Component/frame/regime: solar neighborhood / global halo
- Confidence: medium-high
- Sources: REV17-P003, REV17-P016, REV17-P046

### REV17-E12
- Epistemic type: simulation + gas modeling
- Bounded statement: Inner-Galaxy gas terminal velocities contain strong non-circular bar and spiral streaming; they cannot be read as an axisymmetric rotation curve without modeling.
- Component/frame/regime: inner gas disk
- Confidence: high
- Sources: REV17-P001, REV17-P014, REV17-P042, REV17-P043, REV17-P044

### REV17-E13
- Epistemic type: simulation + contextual inference
- Bounded statement: The Galaxy has experienced a comparatively quiet recent merger history, while the halo retains signatures of earlier accretion.
- Component/frame/regime: assembly history
- Confidence: medium
- Sources: REV17-P025, REV17-P035, REV17-P037

## 3. Open debates and tensions

### REV17-D01
- Competing positions: A short fast bar inferred from local resonances versus a longer slower bar favored by photometric and gas-flow models.
- Why unresolved: Pattern speed, bar length, spiral coupling and solar constants are covariant.
- Sources: REV17-P013, REV17-P039, REV17-P042, REV17-P043, REV17-P044

### REV17-D02
- Competing positions: A compact classical bulge in addition to the box/peanut bar versus an almost pure-disk, bar-built bulge.
- Why unresolved: Population mixing, extinction and model degeneracy limit the classical-bulge fraction.
- Sources: REV17-P006, REV17-P031, REV17-P032, REV17-P038

### REV17-D03
- Competing positions: One geometric thick disk versus a continuum of mono-abundance populations with varying scale lengths/heights.
- Why unresolved: Geometric, chemical, age and kinematic selections do not map one-to-one.
- Sources: REV17-P002, REV17-P012, REV17-P026

### REV17-D04
- Competing positions: Disk scale length near 2-3 kpc with estimator/population dependence.
- Why unresolved: Star counts, dynamics, passbands, flare/warp treatment and radial breaks differ.
- Sources: REV17-P002, REV17-P003, REV17-P027, REV17-P028, REV17-P046

### REV17-D05
- Competing positions: Competing R0, V0 and solar-motion combinations.
- Why unresolved: Maser, S-star and local-kinematic constraints have different frame and covariance structures.
- Sources: REV17-P010, REV17-P011, REV17-P045, REV17-P046, REV17-P047

### REV17-D06
- Competing positions: A relatively light halo below 10^12 solar masses versus heavier estimates near 1.5-2 times 10^12 solar masses.
- Why unresolved: Satellite/escape-speed/BHB tracers and virial definitions differ; anisotropy is uncertain.
- Sources: REV17-P016, REV17-P017, REV17-P046, REV17-P048

### REV17-D07
- Competing positions: A smooth equilibrium stellar halo versus a stream-rich disequilibrium halo.
- Why unresolved: Selection, substructure excision and incomplete transverse velocities change Jeans results.
- Sources: REV17-P025, REV17-P048

### REV17-D08
- Competing positions: The Galaxy as a genuinely transitioning green-valley system versus an ordinary star-forming spiral shifted by dust and viewing geometry.
- Why unresolved: Integrated color/SFR reconstructions are indirect and IMF/dust dependent.
- Sources: REV17-P004, REV17-P019, REV17-P022, REV17-P023

## 5. What remained unknown in 2016

### REV17-U01
- Unknown: The bar pattern speed and whether the long bar and box/peanut are one continuous structure.
- Decisive need: Full 6D bulge/disk kinematics and self-consistent gas+stellar models.
- Sources: REV17-P005, REV17-P039, REV17-P044

### REV17-U02
- Unknown: The classical-bulge mass fraction, if any.
- Decisive need: Extinction-robust chemistry, ages and non-axisymmetric dynamical decomposition.
- Sources: REV17-P006, REV17-P032, REV17-P038

### REV17-U03
- Unknown: A population-resolved map of disk scale length, height, flare and warp.
- Decisive need: Uniform distances, ages, abundances and selection functions across the disk.
- Sources: REV17-P002, REV17-P026

### REV17-U04
- Unknown: The dark-halo virial mass, shape and radial density profile.
- Decisive need: Distant tracer proper motions, streams and disequilibrium-aware models.
- Sources: REV17-P016, REV17-P025, REV17-P048

### REV17-U05
- Unknown: Whether local velocity substructure primarily traces bar resonances, spiral structure or perturbations.
- Decisive need: Time-dependent non-axisymmetric models with precise phase-space data.
- Sources: REV17-P013, REV17-P014

### REV17-U06
- Unknown: The timing and mass spectrum of the Galaxy’s accretion history.
- Decisive need: Large chemically tagged 6D samples tied to cosmological simulations.
- Sources: REV17-P025, REV17-P035, REV17-P037
