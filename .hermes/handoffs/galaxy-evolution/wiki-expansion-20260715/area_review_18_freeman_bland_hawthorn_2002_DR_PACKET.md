# Review Base 18 — Freeman & Bland-Hawthorn 2002 — Curated Deep Research Packet

> Advisory/reference-only synthesis. The raw Deep Research result is preserved separately. No wiki, database, trust, deployment, publication, repository, or conversation mutation is authorized.

## 1. Review identity and scope map

- Review: The New Galaxy: Signatures of Its Formation (2002).
- Composite identity: DOI `10.1146/annurev.astro.40.060401.093840`; arXiv `astro-ph/0208106`; ADS `2002ARA&A..40..487F`.
- Scope: review-era near-field cosmology, galactic archaeology, stellar populations, chemistry, dynamics and Local Group comparison.
- Boundary: the review is synthesis; primary sources carry load-bearing observations and calibrations. No post-2002 evidence is imported.

## 2. Established findings

### REV18-E01
- Bounded statement: Halo assembly evidence supports accretion of fragments alongside any early dissipative collapse; this is not a claim that every halo star is accreted.
- Component/epoch/regime: stellar halo; 1962-2002 evidence
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV18-P003], [REV18-P020], [REV18-P025], [REV18-P026], [REV18-P043]

### REV18-E02
- Bounded statement: The geometrically and kinematically selected thick disk is distinct from the thin disk and contains old, alpha-enhanced populations.
- Component/epoch/regime: solar cylinder; selection-dependent
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV18-P007], [REV18-P041], [REV18-P042]

### REV18-E03
- Bounded statement: The present thin disk limits large recent heating events, but merger mass-ratio and epoch limits remain model-dependent.
- Component/epoch/regime: thin disk; post-settling epoch
- Confidence: moderate
- Sources: [REV18-P017], [REV18-P018], [REV18-P043]

### REV18-E04
- Bounded statement: The luminous stellar halo is a low-mass component with an approximately steep power-law density profile.
- Component/epoch/regime: stellar halo; equilibrium/global approximation
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV18-P004], [REV18-P005]

### REV18-E05
- Bounded statement: Sagittarius supplies direct evidence of ongoing disruption and accretion in the Milky Way.
- Component/epoch/regime: Sagittarius/outer halo; ongoing
- Confidence: very high
- Sources: [REV18-P003], [REV18-P019]

### REV18-E06
- Bounded statement: Local halo stars show clumping in velocity/integral-of-motion space after spatial phase mixing.
- Component/epoch/regime: Hipparcos solar-neighbourhood sample
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV18-P002], [REV18-P013]

### REV18-E07
- Bounded statement: Old globular clusters across several Local Group systems have mutually consistent formation ages within 1990s isochrone errors.
- Component/epoch/regime: Local Group globular clusters
- Confidence: moderate
- Sources: [REV18-P008], [REV18-P039]

### REV18-E08
- Bounded statement: Extremely metal-poor stars show large neutron-capture abundance scatter beyond stated measurement errors.
- Component/epoch/regime: halo stars below [Fe/H] about -2.5; 1D-LTE
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV18-P006], [REV18-P009], [REV18-P010], [REV18-P044]

### REV18-E09
- Bounded statement: The solar-neighbourhood disk lacks a single tight monotonic age-metallicity relation.
- Component/epoch/regime: local F/G dwarfs; selection and age-scale dependent
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV18-P001]

### REV18-E10
- Bounded statement: Different nucleosynthetic channels populate distinct element-ratio dimensions on different delay times.
- Component/epoch/regime: chemical-evolution interpretation
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV18-P009], [REV18-P027], [REV18-P028], [REV18-P035], [REV18-P044]

### REV18-E11
- Bounded statement: Collisionless simulations predict abundant subhalo structure around Milky-Way-mass halos.
- Component/epoch/regime: dark-matter-only simulations
- Confidence: high within model
- Sources: [REV18-P014], [REV18-P015], [REV18-P016], [REV18-P023]

### REV18-E12
- Bounded statement: Chemical tagging is a testable reconstruction proposal, not an achieved 2002 measurement; it depends on intra-cluster homogeneity and precise differential spectroscopy.
- Component/epoch/regime: review-era method proposal
- Confidence: moderate
- Sources: [REV18-P001], [REV18-P006], [REV18-P009], [REV18-P041]

## 3. Open debates and tensions

### REV18-D01
- Competing positions: Rapid dissipative collapse versus protracted fragment accretion.
- Why unresolved in 2002: Inner/outer-halo samples and component definitions were insufficient for a unique decomposition.
- Sources: [REV18-P025], [REV18-P026], [REV18-P043], [REV18-P045]

### REV18-D02
- Competing positions: Missing satellites: absent halos versus dark halos.
- Why unresolved in 2002: Collisionless counts did not calibrate reionization and feedback well enough to map subhalos to luminous dwarfs.
- Sources: [REV18-P014], [REV18-P015], [REV18-P016], [REV18-P023]

### REV18-D03
- Competing positions: Bulge made by early collapse/mergers versus a buckled bar.
- Why unresolved in 2002: Extinction and sparse 3D kinematics permitted both chemical and morphological interpretations.
- Sources: [REV18-P011], [REV18-P012]

### REV18-D04
- Competing positions: Globular clusters formed in situ versus imported with satellites.
- Why unresolved in 2002: Ages, chemistry and orbits were too incomplete for cluster-by-cluster origin assignments.
- Sources: [REV18-P008], [REV18-P032], [REV18-P039]

### REV18-D05
- Competing positions: Birth-radius chemical gradients versus radial redistribution.
- Why unresolved in 2002: Observed scatter mixed intrinsic ISM inhomogeneity, gas flow and stellar orbital evolution.
- Sources: [REV18-P001], [REV18-P029], [REV18-P033], [REV18-P034]

### REV18-D06
- Competing positions: Synchronous low/high-mass star formation versus sequential self-enrichment.
- Why unresolved in 2002: Cloud-scale chronology and chemical homogeneity were not measured at tagging precision.
- Sources: [REV18-P030], [REV18-P031]

### REV18-D07
- Competing positions: Discrete merger heating versus cumulative/internal thick-disk heating.
- Why unresolved in 2002: Age-velocity and abundance samples could not isolate one unique heating history.
- Sources: [REV18-P007], [REV18-P018], [REV18-P041], [REV18-P042]

### REV18-D08
- Competing positions: Smooth equilibrium halo versus persistent accretion substructure.
- Why unresolved in 2002: The tiny local six-dimensional sample did not represent the global halo.
- Sources: [REV18-P002], [REV18-P004], [REV18-P019], [REV18-P045]

## 5. What remained unknown in 2002

### REV18-U01
- Unknown: Whether chemical tagging uniquely recovers dissolved birth sites.
- Why it mattered: Requires demonstrated within-cluster homogeneity below between-cluster separation.
- Decisive need: Differential multi-element measurements of known clusters and field stars.

### REV18-U02
- Unknown: The global six-dimensional substructure of the halo.
- Why it mattered: A local stream could not determine the full accretion tree.
- Decisive need: Microarcsecond astrometry and distant-halo radial velocities.

### REV18-U03
- Unknown: The magnitude of radial stellar redistribution.
- Why it mattered: Current radius could not safely be equated with birth radius.
- Decisive need: Chemo-dynamical disk maps plus controlled resonant-scattering models.

### REV18-U04
- Unknown: Why predicted subhalos so outnumbered luminous satellites.
- Why it mattered: This tested both dark-matter structure and baryonic suppression.
- Decisive need: Deeper satellite searches and calibrated reionization/feedback simulations.

### REV18-U05
- Unknown: Accurate absolute ages for isolated field stars.
- Why it mattered: Age errors blurred the sequence of disk and halo formation.
- Decisive need: Improved distances, stellar tracks, composition scales and independent clocks.

### REV18-U06
- Unknown: The bulge orbital distribution and formation channel.
- Why it mattered: Dust and missing proper motions prevented merger/secular discrimination.
- Decisive need: Infrared spectroscopy and astrometry through the inner Galaxy.

## 8. Composite identity and boundary ledger

- Component mismatch: geometric, kinematic and chemical definitions of thin disk, thick disk, halo, bulge and satellite populations are not interchangeable.

- Age/distance mismatch: turnoff ages retain distance, extinction, helium and stellar-track dependence.
- Dynamical mismatch: smooth Jeans/global profiles are not evidence against local streams; instantaneous coordinates are not conserved actions.
- Simulation boundary: collisionless subhalo predictions are model context, not luminous-satellite counts.
- Raw custody is preserved unchanged; the canonical packet performs no live mutation.
