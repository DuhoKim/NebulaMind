# Review Base 19 — Sánchez 2020 — Curated Deep Research Packet

> Advisory/reference-only synthesis. The raw Deep Research result is preserved byte-for-byte, including an inherited #18 wrapper-label defect; the embedded result and exact conversation belong to #19. No live mutation is authorized.

## 1. Review identity and scope map

- Review: Spatially Resolved Spectroscopic Properties of Low-Redshift Star-Forming Galaxies (2020).
- Composite identity: DOI `10.1146/annurev-astro-012120-013326`; arXiv `1911.06925`; ADS `2020ARA&A..58...99S`.
- Scope: optical IFS of low-redshift galaxies, resolved ionization, stellar populations, star formation, gas metallicity and local/global scaling relations.
- Boundary: Sánchez 2020 is synthesis; primary papers carry load-bearing measurements.

## 2. Established findings

### REV19-E01
- Bounded statement: Fossil-record fits generally recover older central than outer stellar populations in nearby galaxies, consistent with average inside-out assembly but dependent on SPS/SFH assumptions.
- Sample/scale/regime: CALIFA spaxels/annuli; roughly kpc resolution
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P010], [REV19-P011], [REV19-P012], [REV19-P048]

### REV19-E02
- Bounded statement: Within star-forming spaxels, star-formation-rate surface density correlates with local stellar-mass surface density (resolved SFMS).
- Sample/scale/regime: Halpha-selected, roughly kpc regions
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P008], [REV19-P022]

### REV19-E03
- Bounded statement: Undisturbed star-forming disks show a characteristic negative oxygen-abundance gradient when radius is normalized by effective radius.
- Sample/scale/regime: HII-region/spaxel measurements; deprojected disk
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P031]

### REV19-E04
- Bounded statement: Extended low-ionization emission in retired regions can be powered by old stellar populations rather than nuclear accretion.
- Sample/scale/regime: LIER/HOLMES classifications; EW and line-ratio dependent
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P007], [REV19-P013], [REV19-P014]

### REV19-E05
- Bounded statement: Local gas-phase oxygen abundance correlates with local stellar-mass surface density, with saturation at high density on a chosen strong-line scale.
- Sample/scale/regime: star-forming kpc regions
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P009], [REV19-P030], [REV19-P045], [REV19-P050]

### REV19-E06
- Bounded statement: Molecular-gas surface density and SFR surface density correlate on kpc scales, with roughly constant depletion time in normal disks under a stated alpha_CO.
- Sample/scale/regime: matched CO and SFR maps
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P016], [REV19-P017], [REV19-P025]

### REV19-E07
- Bounded statement: Strong-line oxygen calibrations preserve relative trends more robustly than their absolute abundance zero points.
- Sample/scale/regime: HII-region spectra; calibration dependent
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P042], [REV19-P044]

### REV19-E08
- Bounded statement: High-mass systems can show central oxygen-profile flattening or drops, conditional on ionization masks and abundance calibrator.
- Sample/scale/regime: inner disk/bulge, below about 0.5 Re
- Confidence: moderate
- Sources: [REV19-P015], [REV19-P031]

### REV19-E09
- Bounded statement: Integrated scaling relations can emerge from the spatial distribution and weighting of resolved relations, but this is not by itself causal proof.
- Sample/scale/regime: resolved-to-integrated comparison
- Confidence: moderate-high
- Sources: [REV19-P020], [REV19-P023], [REV19-P045]

### REV19-E10
- Bounded statement: Departure from the star-forming main sequence is associated with lower cold-gas fractions and may also involve efficiency changes.
- Sample/scale/regime: global/aperture-matched nearby samples
- Confidence: moderate-high
- Sources: [REV19-P018], [REV19-P019]

### REV19-E11
- Bounded statement: IFS survey conclusions are bounded by reconstructed PSFs and physical sampling of roughly kpc scale, which blend HII regions and diffuse emission.
- Sample/scale/regime: CALIFA/MaNGA/SAMI survey designs
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV19-P001], [REV19-P002], [REV19-P003], [REV19-P046]

### REV19-E12
- Bounded statement: Resolved MZR studies generally find any secondary SFR dependence weaker and more sample/calibrator sensitive than the primary mass relation.
- Sample/scale/regime: CALIFA/MaNGA/SAMI star-forming samples
- Confidence: moderate
- Sources: [REV19-P021], [REV19-P023], [REV19-P047], [REV19-P049]

## 3. Open debates and tensions

### REV19-D01
- Competing positions: Is the rSFMS directly regulated by stellar surface density, or inherited through gas scaling relations?.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Matched resolved molecular-gas coverage and causal controls were incomplete.
- Sources: [REV19-P008], [REV19-P016], [REV19-P017], [REV19-P025]

### REV19-D02
- Competing positions: Does inside-out quenching reflect gas removal/starvation, reduced efficiency, or morphological stabilization?.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Local gas masses and alpha_CO were uncertain in central high-pressure regions.
- Sources: [REV19-P018], [REV19-P019], [REV19-P028]

### REV19-D03
- Competing positions: Direct-Te versus photoionization strong-line abundance scales.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Weak auroral lines disappear in metal-rich centers and model scales can differ by several tenths of a dex.
- Sources: [REV19-P042], [REV19-P044]

### REV19-D04
- Competing positions: Constant versus locally varying CO-to-H2 conversion factor.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Independent gas-mass constraints at matched resolution were sparse.
- Sources: [REV19-P017], [REV19-P018], [REV19-P025]

### REV19-D05
- Competing positions: Whether an SFR-dependent fundamental metallicity relation exists locally.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Signal changes with calibrator, aperture, mass range and selection.
- Sources: [REV19-P021], [REV19-P023], [REV19-P047], [REV19-P049]

### REV19-D06
- Competing positions: Leakage from HII regions versus in-situ HOLMES as the dominant DIG/LIER source.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Kiloparsec PSFs mix multiple ionizing sources and geometries.
- Sources: [REV19-P007], [REV19-P013], [REV19-P014]

### REV19-D07
- Competing positions: Whether mergers/AGN permanently quench or temporarily redistribute central star formation.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Resolved cold-gas and controlled post-merger time sequences were limited.
- Sources: [REV19-P026], [REV19-P028], [REV19-P036]

### REV19-D08
- Competing positions: Universal normalized oxygen gradient versus morphology/environment-dependent families.
- Why unresolved in 2020: Different radial domains, deprojections, calibrators and interacting-system cuts changed results.
- Sources: [REV19-P015], [REV19-P031], [REV19-P044]

## 4. Key measurements and calibrations

### REV19-N01
- Measurement/calibration: Characteristic disk oxygen gradient.
- Value/equation: about -0.10 +/- 0.03 dex per Re.
- Sample/scale: 0.5-2 Re, deprojected star-forming disk.
- Uncertainty/status: selection and strong-line-calibration dependent.
- Sources: [REV19-P031]

### REV19-N02
- Measurement/calibration: Retired-region WHAN threshold.
- Value/equation: EW(Halpha) below 3 angstrom, with line-ratio classification.
- Sample/scale: spaxel/region.
- Uncertainty/status: continuum subtraction and DIG mixing dependent.
- Sources: [REV19-P007]

### REV19-N03
- Measurement/calibration: Resolved-SFMS slope.
- Value/equation: log Sigma_SFR = alpha log Sigma_* + beta, alpha about 0.7-1.0.
- Sample/scale: star-forming roughly kpc regions.
- Uncertainty/status: fit method, IMF, dust, S/N and covariance dependent.
- Sources: [REV19-P008], [REV19-P022]

### REV19-N04
- Measurement/calibration: Molecular depletion time.
- Value/equation: tau_dep = Sigma_H2/Sigma_SFR about 2 Gyr.
- Sample/scale: normal nearby disk regions, kpc scale.
- Uncertainty/status: alpha_CO, SFR tracer and lifecycle sampling dependent.
- Sources: [REV19-P016], [REV19-P017]

### REV19-N05
- Measurement/calibration: Marino O3N2 calibration.
- Value/equation: 12+log(O/H)=8.533-0.214 O3N2.
- Sample/scale: HII regions on direct-Te empirical scale.
- Uncertainty/status: about 0.18 dex random for direct calibration.
- Sources: [REV19-P044]

### REV19-N06
- Measurement/calibration: Marino N2 calibration precision.
- Value/equation: about 0.16 dex random for direct-Te calibration.
- Sample/scale: HII regions.
- Uncertainty/status: N/O and ionization-parameter sensitivity.
- Sources: [REV19-P044]

### REV19-N07
- Measurement/calibration: Kewley maximum-starburst BPT curve.
- Value/equation: log([OIII]/Hbeta)=0.61/(log([NII]/Halpha)-0.47)+1.19.
- Sample/scale: dereddened emission-line ratios.
- Uncertainty/status: model upper envelope, not an empirical pure-SF cut.
- Sources: [REV19-P005]

### REV19-N08
- Measurement/calibration: Large-survey physical resolution.
- Value/equation: order 1-1.5 kpc after PSF reconstruction.
- Sample/scale: CALIFA/MaNGA/SAMI typical nearby samples.
- Uncertainty/status: redshift and instrument dependent; not HII-region resolution.
- Sources: [REV19-P001], [REV19-P002], [REV19-P003], [REV19-P046]

### REV19-N09
- Measurement/calibration: CALIFA DR3 scope.
- Value/equation: 667 galaxies; 1576 datacubes across V500/V1200/COMBO products.
- Sample/scale: z about 0.005-0.03.
- Uncertainty/status: diameter selection and setup-dependent coverage.
- Sources: [REV19-P046]

### REV19-N10
- Measurement/calibration: Inside-out fossil-record structural ratio.
- Value/equation: half-mass radii smaller than half-light radii on average; strongest reported ratios depend on mass/morphology.
- Sample/scale: CALIFA stellar-population fits.
- Uncertainty/status: SPS, dust, age-metallicity degeneracy and radial coverage dependent.
- Sources: [REV19-P048]

## 5. What remained unknown in 2020

### REV19-U01
- Unknown: The multiphase inflow/outflow/fountain mass budget.
- Why it mattered: It sets gas supply and metal redistribution.
- Decisive need: Matched ionized, neutral and molecular disk-halo kinematics.

### REV19-U02
- Unknown: An absolute gas-phase abundance scale.
- Why it mattered: Yield and baryon-budget inference cannot tolerate calibration offsets.
- Decisive need: Auroral/far-IR/recombination-line anchors across metallicity.

### REV19-U03
- Unknown: Spatial IMF variation.
- Why it mattered: It would bias both Sigma_* and Sigma_SFR.
- Decisive need: High-S/N gravity-sensitive absorption maps with independent dynamical constraints.

### REV19-U04
- Unknown: Whether z~0 radial archaeology traces actual progenitor evolution.
- Why it mattered: Migration and SPS degeneracy can mimic inside-out history.
- Decisive need: Matched resolved observations across cosmic time plus forward models.

### REV19-U05
- Unknown: Three-dimensional separation of arms, DIG and projected structures.
- Why it mattered: Inclination and PSF blending contaminate local classifications.
- Decisive need: Higher-resolution spectral imaging and 3D forward convolution.

### REV19-U06
- Unknown: How star-formation laws change below a complete GMC lifecycle scale.
- Why it mattered: Gas and young-star tracers decorrelate at tens to hundreds of parsecs.
- Decisive need: Matched ALMA/optical maps with age-resolved region statistics.
