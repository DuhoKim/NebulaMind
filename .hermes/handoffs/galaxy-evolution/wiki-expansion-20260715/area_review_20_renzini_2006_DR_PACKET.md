# Review Base 20 — Renzini 2006 — Curated Deep Research Packet

> Advisory/reference-only synthesis. Raw Deep Research custody is preserved separately. No wiki, database, trust, deployment, publication, repository, or conversation mutation is authorized.

## 1. Review identity and scope map

- Review: Stellar Population Diagnostics of Elliptical Galaxy Formation (2006).
- Composite identity: DOI `10.1146/annurev.astro.44.051905.092450`; arXiv `astro-ph/0603479`; ADS `2006ARA&A..44..141R`.
- Scope: local and high-redshift early-type stellar populations, scaling relations, formation epochs, demographic evolution and model tensions through 2006.
- Boundary: formation of stars, quenching, morphological transformation and final assembly are separate clocks.

## 2. Established findings

### REV20-E01
- Bounded statement: The bulk of stars in massive cluster early-type galaxies formed early, consistent with formation redshift at least about 3 under passive-SFH assumptions.
- Population/redshift/environment: cluster ETGs, local through z~1.3
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P001], [REV20-P006], [REV20-P008], [REV20-P022], [REV20-P034]

### REV20-E02
- Bounded statement: Field spheroids show younger luminosity/SSP-equivalent ages and more recent activity on average than mass-matched cluster counterparts.
- Population/redshift/environment: field versus cluster ETGs to z~1.2
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P014], [REV20-P036], [REV20-P046], [REV20-P047]

### REV20-E03
- Bounded statement: Stellar formation duration anticorrelates with galaxy mass: more massive ETGs are older and more alpha-enhanced.
- Population/redshift/environment: local ETGs across velocity dispersion
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P035], [REV20-P036], [REV20-P041], [REV20-P046]

### REV20-E04
- Bounded statement: Massive passive galaxies already existed by z~1-2, while lower-mass red-sequence membership continued to build.
- Population/redshift/environment: massive ETGs and red sequence
- Confidence: moderate-high
- Sources: [REV20-P006], [REV20-P010], [REV20-P011], [REV20-P040]

### REV20-E05
- Bounded statement: The comoving abundance of passive ETGs becomes less secure and generally lower beyond z~1, with strong selection and cosmic-variance dependence.
- Population/redshift/environment: high-redshift cosmological fields
- Confidence: moderate
- Sources: [REV20-P010], [REV20-P011], [REV20-P040]

### REV20-E06
- Bounded statement: SSP-equivalent ages are strongly luminosity weighted, so small young components can dominate optical age diagnostics.
- Population/redshift/environment: integrated unresolved stellar populations
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P009], [REV20-P038], [REV20-P046]

### REV20-E07
- Bounded statement: The cluster color-magnitude relation is very tight and its slope is primarily consistent with a mass-metallicity sequence under passive models.
- Population/redshift/environment: Coma/Virgo and distant clusters
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P008], [REV20-P027], [REV20-P034]

### REV20-E08
- Bounded statement: Fundamental-Plane zero-point evolution is broadly consistent with passive M/L evolution but is altered by sample selection and progenitor bias.
- Population/redshift/environment: ETGs to z~1.3
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P014], [REV20-P018], [REV20-P019], [REV20-P026], [REV20-P037], [REV20-P039], [REV20-P047]

### REV20-E09
- Bounded statement: Absolute stellar masses and M/L depend strongly on the low-mass IMF and SPS prescription; Chabrier-like and Salpeter-like normalizations are not interchangeable.
- Population/redshift/environment: SPS modeling
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P009], [REV20-P015], [REV20-P029]

### REV20-E10
- Bounded statement: Massive ETGs show enhanced alpha-to-iron ratios, consistent with short dominant star-formation timescales under chemical-evolution assumptions.
- Population/redshift/environment: local massive ETGs
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P035], [REV20-P036], [REV20-P046]

### REV20-E11
- Bounded statement: ULIRGs and high-redshift starbursts are plausible dissipative progenitor phases, but object-by-object ancestry was not established in 2006.
- Population/redshift/environment: ULIRGs/SMGs, high-z
- Confidence: moderate
- Sources: [REV20-P011], [REV20-P024]

### REV20-E12
- Bounded statement: Morphological, red-color and passive-spectral selections identify overlapping but non-identical ETG populations.
- Population/redshift/environment: local/high-z survey selection
- Confidence: high
- Sources: [REV20-P002], [REV20-P005], [REV20-P037], [REV20-P047]

## 3. Open debates and tensions

### REV20-D01
- Competing positions: CMR scatter from metallicity alone versus residual age/frosting variation.
- Why unresolved in 2006: Optical colors suffer age-metallicity degeneracy and young light dominates.
- Sources: [REV20-P008], [REV20-P009], [REV20-P027], [REV20-P038], [REV20-P046]

### REV20-D02
- Competing positions: Fundamental-Plane tilt from stellar populations/IMF versus structural non-homology/dark matter.
- Why unresolved in 2006: High-z resolved dynamics and IMF-independent masses were unavailable at scale.
- Sources: [REV20-P004], [REV20-P012], [REV20-P018], [REV20-P019], [REV20-P031]

### REV20-D03
- Competing positions: Small apparent FP evolution versus strong progenitor-bias correction.
- Why unresolved in 2006: Individual descendants cannot be followed and newly quenched systems enter samples over time.
- Sources: [REV20-P037], [REV20-P039], [REV20-P047]

### REV20-D04
- Competing positions: Salpeter-like versus turnover IMF.
- Why unresolved in 2006: Unresolved populations do not directly count low-mass stars, shifting M/L by order two.
- Sources: [REV20-P009], [REV20-P015], [REV20-P029]

### REV20-D05
- Competing positions: Nearly constant versus substantially declining massive-ETG number density to z~1.
- Why unresolved in 2006: Selection, completeness, cosmic variance and passive corrections differed.
- Sources: [REV20-P010], [REV20-P011], [REV20-P040]

### REV20-D06
- Competing positions: Post-starbursts as major transformation phases versus minor frosting on old spheroids.
- Why unresolved in 2006: Optical SSP ages poorly constrain the young mass fraction.
- Sources: [REV20-P009], [REV20-P017], [REV20-P046], [REV20-P047]

### REV20-D07
- Competing positions: Supernova feedback alone versus AGN feedback for massive-halo quenching.
- Why unresolved in 2006: Feedback coupling and cooling prescriptions were parameterized in 2006 models.
- Sources: [REV20-P016], [REV20-P036]

### REV20-D08
- Competing positions: Main-sequence/RGB-dominated versus strong TP-AGB contribution to intermediate-age near-IR light.
- Why unresolved in 2006: TP-AGB lifetimes and mass loss were poorly calibrated.
- Sources: [REV20-P009], [REV20-P029], [REV20-P038]

## 4. Key measurements and calibrations

### REV20-N01
- Measurement/calibration: Cluster FP M/L evolution.
- Value/equation: Delta log(M/L_B) approximately -0.46 z.
- Population/redshift/environment: cluster ETGs to z~1.
- Uncertainty/status: passband, IMF, cosmology, selection and passive-SFH dependent.
- Sources: [REV20-P037], [REV20-P039]

### REV20-N02
- Measurement/calibration: Field FP M/L evolution.
- Value/equation: roughly -0.55 z in review synthesis; individual samples can be steeper.
- Population/redshift/environment: field ETGs to z~1.
- Uncertainty/status: mass and morphology-selection dependent.
- Sources: [REV20-P014], [REV20-P047]

### REV20-N03
- Measurement/calibration: Age-metallicity degeneracy.
- Value/equation: partial log age / partial [Fe/H] at fixed B-V approximately -0.9.
- Population/redshift/environment: SSP isochrone colors.
- Uncertainty/status: model and passband heuristic.
- Sources: [REV20-P030], [REV20-P038]

### REV20-N04
- Measurement/calibration: Worthey 3/2 rule.
- Value/equation: factor 3 metallicity error mimics factor 2 age error.
- Population/redshift/environment: broad-band optical SSPs.
- Uncertainty/status: heuristic for old populations.
- Sources: [REV20-P038]

### REV20-N05
- Measurement/calibration: Cluster CMR intrinsic scatter.
- Value/equation: less than about 0.05 mag in U-V/V-K.
- Population/redshift/environment: Coma/Virgo early types.
- Uncertainty/status: selection, photometric and aperture dependent.
- Sources: [REV20-P008]

### REV20-N06
- Measurement/calibration: Alpha enhancement range.
- Value/equation: [Mg/Fe] roughly 0.05-0.3, increasing with velocity dispersion.
- Population/redshift/environment: local ETGs above about 100 km/s.
- Uncertainty/status: index-response and SSP-equivalent interpretation dependent.
- Sources: [REV20-P035], [REV20-P036], [REV20-P046]

### REV20-N07
- Measurement/calibration: Local spheroid stellar-mass share.
- Value/equation: review-era estimate about 75%, with pure ellipticals about 22%.
- Population/redshift/environment: z~0 census.
- Uncertainty/status: IMF, decomposition and dust dependent.
- Sources: [REV20-P021]

### REV20-N08
- Measurement/calibration: Field-cluster SSP-equivalent age offset.
- Value/equation: field ETGs about 1-2 Gyr younger on average.
- Population/redshift/environment: mass-matched z<1 samples.
- Uncertainty/status: progenitor and luminosity-weighting dependent.
- Sources: [REV20-P014], [REV20-P036], [REV20-P047]

### REV20-N09
- Measurement/calibration: GOODS-N field FP evolution.
- Value/equation: mean d log(M/L_B)/dz about -0.72 with quoted statistical/systematic terms.
- Population/redshift/environment: 226 morphologically selected spheroid/bulge systems at 0.2<z<1.2.
- Uncertainty/status: luminosity bias and mass dependence retained.
- Sources: [REV20-P047]

### REV20-N10
- Measurement/calibration: Red-sequence stellar-mass growth.
- Value/equation: order factor 2 since z~1 in COMBO-17 analysis.
- Population/redshift/environment: red-selected galaxies.
- Uncertainty/status: color selection, mass completeness, IMF and merger interpretation dependent.
- Sources: [REV20-P010]

## 5. What remained unknown in 2006

### REV20-U01
- Unknown: Sizes and structural evolution of passive galaxies beyond z~1.5.
- Why it mattered: It distinguishes compact formation from later dry envelope assembly.
- Decisive need: Large rest-frame-optical high-resolution samples.

### REV20-U02
- Unknown: Physical coupling of AGN feedback.
- Why it mattered: Semi-analytic quenching required energy injection without resolved coupling.
- Decisive need: Multiphase outflow and cooling-flow measurements plus resolved simulations.

### REV20-U03
- Unknown: IMF variation with galaxy mass and epoch.
- Why it mattered: It rescales M/L, stellar mass and inferred formation efficiency.
- Decisive need: Gravity-sensitive spectra plus dynamical/lensing masses.

### REV20-U04
- Unknown: The detailed field red-sequence assembly path at 0<z<1.
- Why it mattered: Number-density studies disagreed on transformation rates.
- Decisive need: Wide mass-complete spectroscopy with uniform morphology/color selection.

### REV20-U05
- Unknown: Resolved age and abundance gradients at high redshift.
- Why it mattered: Integrated spectra could not distinguish dissipative cores from later envelope growth.
- Decisive need: AO/space-resolved spectroscopy with matched local diagnostics.

### REV20-U06
- Unknown: The TP-AGB near-IR contribution near 1 Gyr.
- Why it mattered: It can shift high-z stellar masses by order two.
- Decisive need: Empirical intermediate-age cluster calibration and improved stellar tracks.
