### Established findings

- [REV06-E01] For massive main-sequence galaxies at z≈1–3, CO and dust surveys report molecular-gas mass fractions of order 0.3–0.5, substantially above local analogs. [REV06-P001] [REV06-P004] [REV06-P014]
- [REV06-E02] Integrated molecular-gas depletion time varies with redshift and displacement from the star-forming main sequence; starbursts consume gas faster than main-sequence systems. [REV06-P015] [REV06-P021]
- [REV06-E03] Cold-accretion and gas-regulator models link cosmological inflow to the evolution of cold-gas content and star-formation rates. [REV06-P028] [REV06-P031] [REV06-P035]
- [REV06-E04] The CO-to-H₂ conversion factor rises toward low metallicity as an increasing fraction of molecular gas becomes CO-dark. [REV06-P006] [REV06-P008]
- [REV06-E05] For integrated molecular gas, the global star-formation relation is close to linear for main-sequence systems, while mixing disks and starbursts introduces offsets and slope changes. [REV06-P003] [REV06-P017]
- [REV06-E06] Massive star-forming disks at z≈1–3 show turbulent ionized-gas kinematics with intrinsic dispersions of order 40–80 km s⁻¹. [REV06-P018] [REV06-P019]
- [REV06-E08] Long-wavelength Rayleigh–Jeans dust continuum can serve as an ISM-mass proxy within calibrated dust-temperature and metallicity regimes. [REV06-P009] [REV06-P024] [REV06-P025]
- [REV06-E09] Star-formation-law models calibrated across galactic environments place the efficiency per free-fall time at order one percent. [REV06-P034]
- [REV06-E10] Gas-rich disk-instability simulations connect radial inflow and compaction to central mass growth and subsequent quenching pathways. [REV06-P030] [REV06-P033] [REV06-P039]
- [REV06-E11] The star-forming main sequence remains relatively tight, with scatter of order 0.3 dex, across a broad redshift range. [REV06-P010] [REV06-P011] [REV06-P012]
- [REV06-E12] Nearby resolved maps show H I surface-density saturation and an H₂-dominated star-formation relation in denser regions. [REV06-P007] [REV06-P016]

### Open debates and tensions

- [REV06-D01] The exact dependence of the CO-to-H₂ conversion factor on shielding, metallicity, and radiation field remained uncertain, especially for low-metallicity high-redshift systems. [REV06-P006]
- [REV06-D02] Whether the dust-to-gas ratio stays approximately linear with metallicity or steepens in low-metallicity and high-redshift regimes remained an important calibration uncertainty. [REV06-P009] [REV06-P024]
- [REV06-D03] The literature still allowed both a disk–starburst bimodality and a smoother continuous sequence of star-formation efficiencies. [REV06-P015] [REV06-P023] [REV06-P027]
- [REV06-D04] Available scaling data did not cleanly distinguish a depletion-time dependence on redshift from one tied to Hubble or internal dynamical times. [REV06-P001]
- [REV06-D06] The survival of giant high-redshift clumps versus rapid disruption by feedback remained unsettled. [REV06-P022]
- [REV06-D07] Variation in integrated depletion time could reflect true local efficiency changes, changing structure, or both. [REV06-P003]
- [REV06-D08] Gas-regulator models were not unique because mass loading, inflow metallicity, and equilibration times remained degenerate. [REV06-P028] [REV06-P031]

### Measurements, model benchmarks, and calibrations

- [REV06-N01] A Milky-Way-like conversion factor αCO≈4.36 M☉ (K km s⁻¹ pc²)⁻¹, including helium, is the baseline for near-solar-metallicity main-sequence disks. [REV06-P006]
- [REV06-N02] The deferred packet adopts a low-metallicity correction approximately αCO∝(Z/Z☉)⁻¹·⁵ to (Z/Z☉)⁻² within its stated regime. [REV06-P006] [REV06-P008]

- [REV06-N04] The retained main-sequence-offset fit uses tdep∝(sSFR/sSFRMS)^−0.44. [REV06-P001]
- [REV06-N07] A representative intrinsic velocity-dispersion range for z≈1–3 star-forming disks is σ₀≈40–80 km s⁻¹, subject to beam-smearing corrections. [REV06-P018] [REV06-P019]
- [REV06-N08] A solar-metallicity baseline dust-to-gas ratio of order 100 underlies common dust-continuum gas-mass conversions, with strong caveats away from the calibrated regime. [REV06-P009] [REV06-P024]

### Unknowns and uncertainties as of 2020

- [REV06-U01] Direct 21-cm constraints on the cosmic evolution of atomic gas remained limited well below cosmic noon, leaving the H I replenishment pathway weakly constrained. [REV06-P016]
- [REV06-U03] CO and dust become difficult total-gas tracers in low-metallicity high-redshift dwarfs, leaving their molecular reservoirs uncertain. [REV06-P006]
- [REV06-U05] Uncertain multi-phase outflow mass-loading factors prevented a unique observational test of feedback prescriptions in gas-regulator models. [REV06-P028] [REV06-P031]
