# NebulaMind five-paper V2 publication plan

Marker: `NEBULAMIND_FIVE_PAPER_V2_PUBLICATION_PLAN_20260723`

## Authority and scope

Direct user instruction: “now apply all to the videos and publish on Youtube”.

Authorized:
- rebuild all five paper explainers locally;
- use the technically preferred Shimmer canary voice;
- upload five new videos to the owned NebulaMind YouTube channel;
- attach verified manual English captions;
- make the five new videos public.

Not authorized and therefore excluded:
- unlisting or deleting the five V1 videos;
- changing NebulaMind website embeds or source;
- Git commit/push/merge;
- runtime build, restart, or deployment.

## Audience and teaching contract

Audience: curious non-specialists who have not read the papers.

Each video must teach, in order:
1. what the topic means in ordinary language;
2. the paper's question;
3. why the comparison is difficult;
4. what data or method changes the comparison;
5. the central result in intuitive units;
6. a robustness or cross-check;
7. the main limitation;
8. a one-sentence recap and exact status boundary.

## Audio contract

- OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini-tts`, voice `shimmer`, provider speed `0.75` after the long-form 0.8 synthesis measured 126.03 WPM.
- Provider speed `0.75`; no post-synthesis time compression.
- Whole-script synthesis for conversational continuity.
- Lossless PCM WAV retained as the source master.
- Final mix near -16 LUFS, true peak at or below -1.5 dBTP.
- Actual pace target 105–125 WPM.
- Manual SRT derived from the exact approved script and aligned to the exact narration.
- Independent local Faster-Whisper check for substantive facts and caveats.

## Visual contract

- 2560×1440, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, fast-start.
- One dominant idea per scene; large mobile-readable typography.
- Flow astronomer identity master only in silent title/outro bookends; no implied speaking and no lip-sync requirement.
- Paper-specific cards, comparisons, and source-page imagery; no invented scientific geometry.
- Captions supplied as manual YouTube sidecars, not burned as tiny paragraph text.

1. refuse exact-title duplicates;

4. insert and verify manual English captions;
