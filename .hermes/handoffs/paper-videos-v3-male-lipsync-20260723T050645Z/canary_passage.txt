The core evidence is five strictly unlensed Pollock galaxies from redshift nine point three to nine point nine. Their oxygen abundances use direct electron-temperature measurements, which infer gas temperature from an auroral line. A dex is a logarithmic unit. Against the Curti comparison, the five galaxies average zero point six nine plus or minus zero point zero three dex lower in oxygen abundance, leaving about one fifth the nearby level. Leave-one-out tests show a zero point zero four dex spread. Replacing the extrapolated Curti comparison with the Andrews and Martini relation measured at these masses moves the deficit from zero point six nine to about zero point six five dex. The zero point zero four dex shift is roughly ten percent.
