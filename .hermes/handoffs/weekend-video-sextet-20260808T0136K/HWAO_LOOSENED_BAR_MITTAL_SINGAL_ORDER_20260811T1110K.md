artifact**.

**Why do two analyses of identical data differ by a factor of three?**

selection-contaminated regions, estimator, kinematic-null construction, magnitude/redshift cuts.

**Or does it hide in unstated choices?** If it does, this is another dead end and we need to know
that from reading, not after a fourth brief. That is the single question this order exists to
settle.
