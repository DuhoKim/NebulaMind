## The trap that is specific to these four — read before drafting

**For each of these lanes, the most natural-sounding motivation is itself a result claim.**

- fesc — "there is a photon-budget shortfall" is a *result*. Tori already blocked this lane's

- brightend — "JWST found too many bright early galaxies" is a *result*, and not this lane's to

- mzr-anchor — "the mass–metallicity relation evolves" is a *result*.
- mzr-census — "the archive is inadequate" is a *result*.

So the motivation must explain **why the question is worth asking** without smuggling in **what
the answer turns out to be**. That is a genuinely harder sentence to write than spin's, and it is
where I expect drafts to fail. Conditional framing throughout: *if* such a shortfall existed, here
is why it would matter — never *there is one*.

at all — which is a real and interesting stake, and a different kind of stake. A borrowed
metallicity-physics opening would be both wrong and unsupported.
