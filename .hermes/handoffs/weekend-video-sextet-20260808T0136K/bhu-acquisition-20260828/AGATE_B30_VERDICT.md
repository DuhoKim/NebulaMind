PROPOSAL_REFUTED_ALL

I have evaluated the proposal to re-tier Entries 5, 37, and 49 to THEORETICAL-OBSTRUCTION. None of the three qualify under the strict preregistered rule, and they should all remain in CONSISTENCY-ONLY.

**1. Entry 37 (Smoller & Temple 2003): REFUTED**
Entry 37 is a constructive existence paper. Finding an "if and only if" condition on a single constructed parameter (`sigma <= 1/3`) is simply characterizing the boundary of that specific solution space. Promoting this would cross the exact line warned about in B29: it would allow any paper that classifies its own parameter space to be relabeled as an obstruction against the remainder of that space. It is mathematically constructive and not a class-wide no-go theorem.

**2. Entry 5 (Khakshournia 2010): REFUTED**
Entry 5 solves the specific problem of matching FRW to vacuum Schwarzschild and finds that it requires a null shell with a calculated surface pressure. This is a physical description of a junction, not an impossibility theorem. Computing that a matching requires a shell is not proving that the model "cannot exist" or is obstructed; it is just determining the properties of the model. It remains constructive.

**3. Entry 49 (Blau, Guendelman & Guth 1987): REFUTED (PROOF LOCATION)**
Entry 49 outlines the initial singularity problem but explicitly cites the Penrose theorem and "work currently in progress" (Entry 48) for the actual proof. The corpus must adopt the convention that the THEORETICAL-OBSTRUCTION label goes to the paper containing the actual proof, not the papers that summarize or cite it. Otherwise, a single theorem would be double-counted across every paper that references it. The correct next step is to acquire and audit Entry 48.

**4. The Definition of CONSISTENCY-ONLY:**
B30 attempts to sharpen the definition of CONSISTENCY-ONLY to mean the strict negation of a no-go theorem in order to force a re-tiering. Because none of these three papers are actually impossibility proofs, this argument is moot. They are constructive or descriptive papers that make no falsifiable observational predictions, which is exactly what CONSISTENCY-ONLY is meant to hold.
