## 1. The question, in one paragraph

"Our universe is the inside of a black hole" is a family of published ideas, not one theory. This
corpus is every peer-reviewed paper we could verify that makes or tests that identification — 51
papers across 11 branches, plus 7 support-role measurement papers (58 numbered entries), each
resolved against the Crossref DOI record; preprints and popular pieces are context only. Every paper
has been read at full text; the source-limited count is zero as of 2026-09-02. The question put to
each paper was the same: **does it say anything an observation could contradict, and if so, has the
observation been made?** The answers sort into five bins, and the story of the corpus is the story of
those bins.

## 2. The five bins, in everyday words (tally after 2026-09-02: 5 / 8 / 4 / 30 / 4 = 51)

- **CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER (5):** the paper states a number and a threshold an observation can hit or
  miss. Each carries two further axes the tier does not: its **standing** (fired or live) and its
  **warrant** (does the theory actually produce that falsifier, or was it borrowed). Entries 7, 44, 1
  fired; 31, 51 live.
- **QUALITATIVE-DIRECTIONAL (8):** a predicted sign, direction or scale, without a calibrated
  amplitude. Entries 6, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 54, 56.
- **PROSPECT (4):** names a route to a test but no target. Entries 9, 16, 21, 47.
- **CONSISTENCY-ONLY (30):** shows the picture can be made to agree with what is known; no
  distinguishing observational statement. The bulk of the corpus.
- **THEORETICAL-OBSTRUCTION (4):** a proof that a construction cannot work in a stated domain, owned
  by the paper that proves it (ownership-of-proof rule, 2026-08-29). Entries 22, 5, 48, 4 (4's domain narrowed 2026-09-02 to what its algebra derives).

The record insists on one discipline worth repeating here: **tier count is not live-falsifier
count.** "Five calibrated" describes the shape of five claims; whether the family can still be killed
by observation is a different sentence, given in §8.

## 3. What fired (three calibrated falsifiers, with their targets scoped)

- **Entry 7 — Brown, Lee & Rho 2008 (kaon condensation).** Claim: the Brown–Bethe kaon-condensate
  equation of state caps neutron stars near 1.5 M☉, so a star at ≳ 2 M☉ kills the chain. PSR
  J0740+6620 at 2.08 ± 0.07 M☉ did. **What fired is the instrument chain, not cosmological natural
  selection (CNS)** — the paper gives CNS only "serious doubt". Warrant: no pinned challenge in this
  corpus, which is a statement about what is filed, not a proof the derivation is sound.
- **Entry 44 — Pourhasan, Afshordi & Mann 2014 (out of the white hole).** Claim: the Sec. 4 thermal
  5D model predicts exact scale invariance, n_s = 1. Planck 2018: n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042, 8σ away;
  the authors concede ">5σ" themselves. **What fired is the Sec. 4 model, not the holographic
  framework**; the authors' successor is an uncomputed ~4% correction sized to the measurement.
  Warrant explicit and undisputed for the fired claim. This was the family's first falsifier to fire
  against one of its own cosmologies rather than an instrument.
- **Entry 1 — Pathria 1972 (the founding identification), FIRED 2026-09-02.** Claim, in his own
  words: "we must have K = +1 and Λ ≤ Λ_c" — a closed, decelerating dust universe. That is
  satisfiable only for q₀ > 1/2 or q₀ < −1. Planck 2018 gives q₀ = −0.527 ± 0.011, inside the
  forbidden gap by 43σ and 94σ; the measured Λ is 10.9× his printed ceiling. Warrant explicit and
  author-supplied; his 1972 arithmetic reproduces (`pathria_standing_verify.py`). Blind double
  (codex, agy) both FIRED_CANDIDATE; Duho stamped. Receipts `PATHRIA_STANDING_RECONCILIATION_20260902.md`.

## 5. What cannot be scored, and why — the Gaztañaga 60° cutoff (entries 23–27, plus 56)

The corpus's sharpest observational claim: the causal horizon should erase CMB correlations beyond
about 60°, and the sky's large-angle correlation is indeed weak. Three lane programs, all this week,
established exactly what the papers fix and what they do not:

- **Program (A), the freedom map (`PROGRAM_A_FREEDOM_MAP_20260902.md`, rev 3, gated):** the scale is
  the papers' own and **non-circular** — χ_§ = 3.149 c/H₀ from Ω_Λ measured by supernovae and BAO,
  giving 57.4°, rounded by the paper to 60 ± 3. (The large-angle deficit was known before these
  papers; the honest word is non-circular, not predicted in advance — a correction the 2026-09-02
  poll made to the lane's own packet.) The amplitude is **not the papers'**: they supply no
  perturbation prescription (three seats READING_C, a fourth CLASS_REFUTED against the one proposed
  formalisation); the author himself writes that no initial-conditions model exists (2003.11544
  L466) and cites it as "in preparation". Two natural formalisations of the cutoff sentence are
  provably incompatible (Paley–Wiener).
- **Phase (b), like-for-like (`PHASEB_RESULT_RECONCILIATION_20260902.md`, pre-registered, controls
  passed, blind-doubled):** on the masked Planck SMICA map with one estimator for data and every
  simulation, the observed deficit sits at the 0.15–0.20 percentile under ΛCDM and at 0.4–2.8%
  under the lane's causal-cutoff completions (best 2.2–2.8%). Roughly 15× better, never above ~3%,
  and the improvement belongs to the lane's exploratory completions, not to the theory.
- **Program (C), the flux route (`PROGRAM_C_FLUX_RESULT_20260902.md`, four seats blind-agreed,
  numeric control):** the papers' one derived condition, Φ(χ>χ_§)=0, imposed at linear order on the
  perturbed solution, constrains only the sky monopole when applied to our causal region (every
  C_ℓ for ℓ ≥ 1 unchanged) and admits no continuous power spectrum when applied to every observer.
  It is not the missing amplitude.

## 6. What the theorems forbid (four obstructions, each scoped as proven)

- **Entry 4 — Knutsen 2009:** smooth matching of an FLRW perfect-fluid interior to a vacuum exterior
  at Pathria's boundary r_b = 1 forces a static sphere merged with its horizon — "cannot describe
  our expanding universe". Shell-bearing junctions not excluded. Matched pair with entry 5.
- **Entry 5 — Khakshournia 2010:** for Pathria's pressureless closed-FRW interior with 0 ≤ Λ ≤ Λ_c,
  joined at its equatorial maximum-expansion surface (χ = π/2) identified with the horizon of a
  Schwarzschild–de Sitter exterior carrying the same Λ and the stated dust-mass relation, the
  Barrabès–Israel null junction is not smooth: [K_uu] ≠ 0. It therefore carries a pressure-only null
  shell, p = ρa/4. Shell-bearing realizations and other FRW/black-hole junction classes are not excluded.
- **Entry 22 — Easson 2026:** Proposition 1 excludes identifying the natural trapped slicing with exact
  FRW. Proposition 2 bounds nondegenerate comoving no-shell closed-FRW daughters of static,
  asymptotically flat, finite-ADM parents; its flat/open limb additionally assumes curvature
  regularity, regular affine ends and ANEC. Shells, modified asymptotics, non-FRW or non-comoving
  evolution, and added bulk stress-energy are outside the result. Adjudicated 2026-08-31 (three
  seats): it **restricts, does not kill**, the Gaztañaga construction — the flat/open limb constrains
  a hypothetical complete version, not the published one.
- **Entry 48 — Farhi & Guth 1987:** in classical GR, no spacetime in the proved class exists: an
  asymptotically flat parent with a well-defined noncompact Cauchy development, the null energy
  condition, an unperturbed spherically symmetric false-vacuum region past r = 1/χ, and nonsingular
  initial data. The nonspherical case is not decided; quantum and white-hole escapes are
  author-delimited. Domain as proven, not inflated.

## 8. The honest answer to "can this family still be killed by observation?"

- **Directly, one route:** entry 31's 2.5 M☉ bar. A confirmed neutron star above it fires CNS as

- **Conditionally, one:** entry 51's ECKS floor, for any model that inherits the premise, once the
  floor is reproduced (the version of record, pinned 2026-08-30, does not show the step either).
- **Directionally, one sign:** entry 54's closed curvature, watched.
- **Not at all, as published:** the 60° cutoff, until its author publishes the initial-conditions
  model; and the Popławski chain, until someone derives a transfer function from parent to interior.
- **Already dead:** the founding identification (1), the kaon-condensate instrument chain (7), and
  the white-hole thermal model's exact scale invariance (44).
