## 2 · The five research questions

Each card: **the question · corpus receipts · data/access · falsification shape · seat & budget ·
tier-risk (the STOP it ends on).**

### RQ-A — The Roupas oscillation amplitude (entry 21) · **recommended first**

- **Question.** Roupas (entry 21, tier PROSPECT) makes a *published* "detectable" claim with a named
  instrument band (**μHz–Hz, LISA-class**) but **never computes an amplitude or rate**. Does a number
  exist, and does it clear any real detector floor?
- **Receipts.** Entry 21 pin (read + gated, PROSPECT); memo Ranked §4 ("if a number exists, this
  becomes a fifth calibrated falsifier; if not, it reclassifies to PROSPECT-without-a-number").
- **Data/access.** None paywalled — the paper's own equations + **public LISA/PTA sensitivity
  curves**. Self-contained.
- **Falsification shape.** Compute strain/rate amplitude A in the μHz–Hz band. If A sits above a
  fielded/planned sensitivity curve → **a fifth calibrated falsifier** (non-detection at design
  sensitivity refutes the oscillating-interior claim). If A is below any conceivable floor → the
  "detectable" claim is **demonstrably not detectable**; entry 21 demotes to PROSPECT-without-a-number
  by derivation.
- **Seat & budget.** Smallest in the set: one focused derivation (~1 seat-session) + a two-seat
  adversarial gate on the amplitude and the band-mapping. No data pipeline.
- **Tier-risk / STOP.** Ends in a tier change either way → your decision.

### RQ-B — The Popławski interior transfer function (entries 8–12, 51)

- **Question.** The Einstein–Cartan torsion bounce is the corpus's **only multi-paper mechanism with
  explicit field equations**. Does *any finite-amplitude interior observable* survive the bounce from
  parent-hole parameters to us — "the transfer function the literature never wrote"?
- **Receipts.** Entries 8–12 pins; entry 51 VoR + 2013 erratum (ring machinery corrected, floor
  unchanged); spin-density parameter Ω_S ≈ −8.6×10⁻⁷⁰ (memo §4).
- **Data/access.** None new — all held. Needs a symbolic-GR derivation capability (the bounce +
  perturbation transfer).
- **Falsification shape.** If a finite interior amplitude survives → *that* amplitude is a new
  falsifier (predict it, absence refutes). If it is zero / exponentially suppressed → the whole
  Popławski branch is **consistency-only by *derivation* rather than by omission** — a firm negative
  result that resolves entries 8–12's status.
- **Seat & budget.** Heaviest: multi-step GR/Einstein–Cartan derivation, several seat-sessions, likely
  a symbolic-algebra pass and an independent recomputation gate. Highest risk of "no clean number."
- **Tier-risk / STOP.** New falsifier *or* a branch-wide demotion → your decision.

### RQ-C — The Gaztañaga causal-horizon cutoff (entries 23/25/26)

- **Question.** The Gaztañaga bounce series implies a **power-spectrum cutoff at the causal horizon**
  and ties the **CMB low quadrupole** to it. Can the cutoff scale be fixed **from first principles**,
  independently of the low-ℓ anomaly it explains? (Only then is it a falsifier, not a fit — memo
  overclaim-pattern 1, "fitted-not-predicted.")
- **Receipts.** Entries 23/25/26 pins (read + gated); memo Ranked §3.
- **Data/access.** **Planck public likelihoods / low-ℓ power spectra** (free, public) — but a real
  likelihood pipeline is the cost here.
- **Falsification shape.** If k_cut is fixed a priori, the CMB must show suppressed power below it;
  Planck low-ℓ inconsistent with the predicted cutoff → refuted. **The whole result hinges on
  breaking the circularity** (scale fixed independently of the quadrupole) — if that can't be done,
  the honest finding is "explains but does not predict."
- **Seat & budget.** Medium: a CMB-likelihood pipeline + derivation of the cutoff scale + gate. The
  data-pipeline dependency makes it costlier than A/D despite no paywall.
- **Tier-risk / STOP.** A genuine CMB falsifier (first in the corpus) *or* confirmation it's a fit →
  your decision.

### RQ-D — The Easson cross-programme obstruction map (entry 22)

- **Question.** Entry 22 (Easson 2026, THEORETICAL-OBSTRUCTION) is a **no-go on minimal regular-BH
  cosmologies**. Which published BHU interiors does it **kill, restrict, or spare** — Dymnikova
  (18/19), Bronnikov (20), Roupas (21), Gaztañaga (25/26), Popławski (11)?
- **Receipts.** Entry 22 pin + its stated domain (added in Lane 1); the six interior entries' pins;
  memo Ranked §5.
- **Data/access.** None — pure theory-internal, all pins held.
- **Falsification shape.** Not a data-falsifier: a **meta-falsifier**. For each interior, either it
  satisfies Easson's hypotheses (→ killed/restricted, retiring or bounding that row) or a published
  counterexample evades them (→ spared, and *that* is the finding). One theorem, applied carefully,
  can **retire several consistency-only rows at once** or expose a gap in the no-go.
- **Seat & budget.** Medium: literature-synthesis + theorem-application across six interiors +
  adversarial gate on each mapping (the ownership-of-proof discipline applies). No data pipeline.
- **Tier-risk / STOP.** Multiple potential tier moves (demotions/retirements) → your decision, likely
  batched.

### RQ-E — Watch the one clean live falsifier (entry 31) · lowest cost, ongoing

- **Question.** Smolin's bar (2.5 M☉) is the corpus's cleanest live falsifier and currently **1.36σ
  short and drifting away**. Is the neutron-star upper-mass frontier moving *toward* or *away* from
  the bar as new measurements land?
- **Receipts.** Entry 31 + support entries 29/30/33/34/35 (the ~2 M☉ pulsar + heavy-NS measurements);
  Romani 2025 (2.35 ± 0.11 M☉).
- **Data/access.** **Public NS-mass literature** (arXiv, ATNF) — no paywall. Cross-links to the
  spin-parity/DESI edge below.
- **Falsification shape.** Entry 31's existing shape: a *securely-measured* NS above 2.5 M☉ (by any
  method, per the corrected method-agnostic reading) fires it. Lane 2's role = a standing watch that
  flags when a new central value crosses, or when the drift reverses.
- **Seat & budget.** Trivial: a periodic literature check + tripwire, like the DESI Ω_k watcher. No
  derivation.
- **Tier-risk / STOP.** Only a *confirmed* crossing fires it — and that firing is itself the STOP.

## 4 · Recommendation & sequencing

1. **RQ-A (Roupas)** first — cheapest, self-contained, only live shot at a *new* falsifier. One
   derivation decides it.
2. **RQ-D (Easson map)** second — also no new data, and its payoff (retiring several rows with one

   falsifier, but it carries a real data-engineering cost.
5. **RQ-B (Popławski)** last — highest effort, highest chance of "no clean number," but the most
   fundamental (it's the only mechanism with field equations).
