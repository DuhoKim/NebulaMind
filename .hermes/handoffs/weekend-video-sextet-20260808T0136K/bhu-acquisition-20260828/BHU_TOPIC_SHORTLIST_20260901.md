## Ranked shortlist (my synthesis)

### #1 — Calibrate (or kill) the causal-horizon CMB cutoff  ★ most-converged, recommended
- **Question:** can a *physically admissible* stochastic completion of the Gaztañaga 60° cutoff (boundary
  action + a state-selection principle, normalized to A_s at high ℓ) yield a definite S₁/₂ / low-ℓ prediction —
  or is there a **no-go theorem** that every admissible completion keeps a free IR amplitude?
- **Why it's the strongest:** it directly extends the audit's headline (the one real a-priori BHU prediction is
  scale-fixed but amplitude-free), and **both outcomes are publishable** — a calibrated falsifier OR a no-go
  theorem. Non-circular by construction (fix axioms + high-ℓ normalization, hold out low-ℓ). Theory + public
  Planck NPIPE only. kimi adds held-out cross-checks (the C(θ) shape past 60°, octupole planarity, low-ℓ E-mode
  → LiteBIRD). **This is the honest formalization of the new-physics program I already ran** (which found
  UNDETERMINED): turn that into a bounded band + a proof of exactly what's free.
- **Risk:** likely lands on the no-go/underdetermination side (worst case is still a theorem, not nothing).

### #2 — The rotating-parent test: spin-parity + CMB axis (Duho's interest)  ★ strong, data-driven
- **Question:** does spiral-galaxy handedness show a dipole along a **CMB-fixed axis chosen a priori** (power
  asymmetry / low-ℓ alignment), with redshift-independent amplitude — the cross-probe coincidence a rotating
  parent requires?
- **Why it's strong:** directly your standing interest; and kimi's design fixes the fatal flaw of prior
  spin-dipole claims (Longo, Shamir) — axes pre-registered from the CMB *before* touching spin data + z-tomography
  as a systematics veto. Public data only (DECaLS/Galaxy-Zoo spins in VizieR ~10⁶–10⁷ spirals; Planck NPIPE axes).
- **Result-shape:** a measured dipole on a pre-registered axis (>3σ + z-independence + catalog-independence = a
  real BHU-consistent signal) OR a bound D ≲ few×10⁻³ (a genuine contribution against the contested ±1–2% claims).
- **Risk:** null is the likely outcome → a bound paper (still publishable in this contested area); annotation/PSF
  systematics must be controlled with algorithmic classifiers.

### #3 — The Einstein–Cartan bounce transfer function  ◇ deepest theory
- **Question:** derive the full gauge-invariant scalar perturbation system through the Popławski torsion bounce →
  a unique parent→daughter transfer matrix T_ij(k) (→ n_s, isocurvature, PBH abundance), or prove the published
  background isn't closed enough to predict them (a closure no-go).
- **Why:** attacks the exact gap RQ-B found (background yes, perturbation transfer no) in the corpus's only
  multi-paper mechanism. Pure theory (symbolic action + a stiff BVP + CLASS/CAMB). Higher effort/risk; the payoff
  is either several falsifiable numbers or a rank-deficiency theorem.

**Also-rans:** Smolin-extremization stress-test (kimi #3 — MESA nuclear-rate grids test whether BH yield is
