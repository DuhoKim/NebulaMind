## Bars (Duho's, non-negotiable)

- **Frontier, not low-hanging:** attack a contested and/or open question, not a z~0 anchor or a restatement.
- **Non-circular:** the result must not be derivable only by fitting the data it aims to predict.
- **Tractable:** achievable with available or near-term data / a doable derivation — say exactly what.
- **Original + defensible:** a real new result or method, with a conclusion that survives adversarial review.

   μK⁴ vs ΛCDM ~34,900; principled completions land ~6,000–22,000 — the cutoff moves the right way but doesn't
   reach the deficit and can't be pinned.)
