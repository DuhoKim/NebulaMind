## 1. The existence conditional

### What is actually derived

The necessity of the negative string contribution is not merely posited. The paper starts from the Friedmann equation and its Wheeler–DeWitt minisuperspace potential. With vacuum, radiation, and a `p=-rho/3` component, equations (11)–(13) give

`U(a) = (m_Pl c^2 / 2 l_Pl^2) [(k-B_s)a^2 - a^4/r_0^2]`.

For the desired well/barrier shape, the quadratic coefficient must be positive: `k-B_s > 0`. Consequently:

- for a flat model, `k=0`, a barrier requires `B_s<0`;
- for an open model, `k=-1`, it requires `B_s<-1`;
- with no string component (`B_s=0`), neither flat nor open curvature gives this barrier.

The paper states the result directly after equation (13): a negative `B_s`, exemplified by strings with a negative deficit angle, mimics positive curvature and produces the barrier that makes nonzero-energy open/flat birth by tunneling possible. Equations (14)–(17) then quantize the well and constrain the allowed levels. The three cases later use `B_s ≃ 1-3×10^-6`, `-1-3×10^-6`, and `-3×10^-6` for closed, open, and flat births respectively.

Thus a counterexample—an open or flat tunneling solution with the specified potential and `B_s=0`, while retaining the other model assumptions—would refute the result. This is the same logical warning that entries 52/53 supplied: an “exists only with/when” statement can encode a real no-solution region even when the paper is constructively framed.

### Why the paper-level tier still does not move

The exclusion is narrower and plays a different operative role from entries 52/53. Those papers headline and derive a threshold below which **no dynamical closed-universe solution exists** in their stated Einstein–Cartan systems. Here the paper asks what matter content supplies a barrier and then constructs that content. The no-string subcase is the algebraic complement of the enabling condition inside one selected Wheeler–DeWitt tunneling mechanism; it does not purport to exclude all flat/open universe births, all quantum-cosmology potentials, or all black-hole-interior mechanisms.

The abstract, section-3 probability calculation, and conclusion are organized around positive existence and comparison:

- strings supply the needed barrier;
- all three curvature cases have tunneling probabilities much larger than closed birth from “nothing” under the chosen inputs;
- the flat case is called most plausible because it needs only a very small string admixture;
- the closing sentence says the construction makes a universe inside a regular black hole something one can “at least not exclude.”

Under the established paper-level operative-contribution convention, entry 19 therefore remains `CONSISTENCY-ONLY`. Its prose should nevertheless record the source-owned claim-level result:

> In the paper's vacuum+radiation+`p=-rho/3` minisuperspace model, the open and flat tunneling barrier requires `k-B_s>0`; without a sufficiently negative `B_s` component, the specified open/flat tunneling channel has no barrier.

Calling this only an “enabling mechanism” is incomplete; calling it a general impossibility of open/flat birth would be overbroad.
