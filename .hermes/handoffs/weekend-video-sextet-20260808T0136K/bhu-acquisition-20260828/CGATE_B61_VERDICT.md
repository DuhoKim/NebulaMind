## 1. Sign convention and current constraints

The sign mapping is verified, not assumed.

- Entry 54 defines its parameter in Eq. 27 as `Ω_k ≡ -k(1/H_0)^2`. Its closed FLRW construction has `k > 0`, hence **closed implies `Ω_k < 0`**. An open geometry has `Ω_k > 0`.
- DESI DR2 Eq. 3 writes the transverse comoving distance for positive `Ω_K` with the `sinh` branch. That is the open-universe branch; closed curvature instead uses the trigonometric branch under analytic continuation. DESI therefore uses the same convention: **`Ω_K > 0` is open**.
- Planck independently confirms the mapping in prose: it calls models around its negative `Ω_K` constraint “closed models.”

DESI DR2 Table 5, in the `ΛCDM+Ω_K` block, reports:

| Dataset | Table value | `Ω_K` | Offset from flatness | Direction |

The arithmetic is direct: `2.3/1.1 = 2.09` and `25/41 = 0.61`. Among the pinned modern constraints, DESI+CMB is the tightest explicit open-side result, but approximately 2.1σ is a hint, not a confirmed curvature detection. DESI's own accompanying sentence is dispositive about its claimed status: it finds no significant preference for a non-flat ΛCDM model.

The remaining pinned evidence does not provide independent open-side confirmation:

Calling `+0.0023 ± 0.0011` “confirmed” would treat a single roughly 2σ, model-and-dataset-dependent displacement as a detection despite the collaboration's explicit contrary interpretation and the absence of concordant ACT, earlier DESI, or Planck evidence. The pinned record supports **open-side tension/hint**, not confirmed openness.
