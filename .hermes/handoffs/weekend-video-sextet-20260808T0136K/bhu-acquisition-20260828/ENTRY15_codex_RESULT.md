AUDIT_HOLDS_CONSISTENCY_ONLY

1. **The construction is not derived from an action in this paper.** The note says that the constructive limiting-curvature implementation belongs to ref. [3]: its action has higher-derivative terms parameterized by a nondynamical scalar and is designed to approach local de Sitter geometry at high curvature (lines 29–39). It then says explicitly that extending that construction to a four-dimensional Schwarzschild black hole is ongoing (lines 41–42), and the conclusion leaves construction of a higher-derivative theory admitting the required Schwarzschild/de Sitter solution to future work (lines 365–372). What is actually written down and manipulated here is the ordinary Schwarzschild metric and its geodesic equations, equations (1)–(11) (lines 123–220), followed by a spatial-length/topology matching argument using Schwarzschild and FRW/de Sitter metrics, equations (12)–(17) (lines 246–311). The replacement of the singularity by a de Sitter initial surface is therefore the assumed setup depicted in the Penrose diagram (lines 74–85), not a solution derived here from a stated modified-gravity action. The abstract's claimed independence is independence from “the details of general models” (lines 12–17), restated as independence from “specific models” (lines 68–73). That is a scope claim based on positing common causal features, not an argument demonstrated by deriving the transition for a general action or surveying conditions under which all such models share them.

2. **No long inflationary epoch is derived; the paper instead proposes avoiding one.** The abstract says the problems “might be solved naturally, not necessarily requiring a long period” of inflation (lines 12–16), and Section II says the scenarios considered have a de Sitter bounce “presumably of too short duration” to solve the standard problems by inflation (lines 90–94). It supplies no duration, number of e-folds, exit dynamics, or energy scale for that bounce. The de Sitter parameter appears only as an unspecified \(H\) in the standard scale-factor forms (lines 297–305), and no equation connects it to data or fixes a limiting-curvature value. Thus “long” is not a prediction of the baby universe here, and the limiting scale remains free/unprovided.

3. **The observer mapping yields a causal-structure picture, not an earned internal observable.** The paper maps the mother's \(r=0\) locus to the baby's \(t=0\) initial surface and says infalling matter arriving at different mother times appears at one baby time in different places (lines 76–85). It argues conditionally for homogeneous/isotropic initial matter through pre-bounce causal contact and thermalization (lines 110–119, 221–229). It also claims flat spatial slicing, \(k=0\), by matching the linear divergence of spatial lengths (lines 278–311; summarized at lines 353–359). But no junction conditions or dynamical transition select the slicing—the required theory is still future work (lines 365–372)—so this is conditional on the assumed matching rather than a calibrated prediction. For perturbations the paper expressly says a quantitative theory remains to be worked out (lines 312–318); Hawking radiation as inherited fluctuations is only suggested, with a qualitative freeze-out condition \(\lambda\gg c/H\) (lines 319–324). It gives no spectrum, amplitude, correlation, relic abundance, or other observer-level discriminator.

4. **The information-loss and natural-selection discussions add no testable content here.** Information preservation is asserted because pure states falling through the hole “emerge in the new universe as pure states” (lines 325–344), but there is no unitary evolution map or observable accessible to either side. The “survival of the fittest” passage is explicitly a summary of Smolin: daughter universes inherit characteristics and only black-hole-producing universes reproduce; the authors immediately note that Smolin supplies no quantitative realization (lines 58–67). This paper does not derive mutations of constants, a fitness landscape, an extremum of black-hole production, or any comparative prediction under variation of constants. It therefore makes no CNS-type tuned-constants claim that could inherit a constants/black-hole-production falsifier from entry 31.

5. **Tier consequence: CONSISTENCY-ONLY holds.** The paper checks some consequences of an assumed black-hole-to-de-Sitter causal construction: geodesics can provide causal contact only under stated circumstances (lines 194–229), and an assumed Schwarzschild/de Sitter matching is argued to select \(k=0\) (lines 259–311). But the transition itself is not produced by this paper's action, the observable perturbation calculation is explicitly missing, the bounce scale and duration are unfixed, and the information/CNS claims have no measurable map. The apparent directions—homogeneity, flatness, Hawking-seeded structure—depend on assumed closure/matching or remain suggestions, so under A(a) they do not earn QUALITATIVE-DIRECTIONAL. There is likewise no proposed measurement program sufficient for PROSPECT and no numerical threshold for CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER.

Plainly: this paper explores what could follow if a black-hole singularity were replaced by a baby de Sitter universe, but it does not build that replacement in four dimensions. Its calculations illuminate causal contact and a conditional flat-slicing match; they do not determine the bounce's scale or duration, predict measurable perturbations or relics, or turn information transfer or cosmological natural selection into a test. The existing consistency-only tier should therefore remain unchanged.
