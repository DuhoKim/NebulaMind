## 0. The "completely independent of" remark (disclosed prior finding)

Line 16–17: "Our conclusions are completely independent of the details of general models." Restated at lines 71–73:
"the conclusions described in this paper are independent of the specific models." This is a CLAIM, not an argument.
The paper never proves model-independence; it surveys scenarios [1]–[8] (lines 27–58) and asserts it will "discuss a
number of features that all such scenarios have in common" (lines 71–72). The independence rests on the shared
Penrose-diagram causal structure (Fig. 1, lines 74–85), not on any theorem. Meanwhile the actual arguments DO lean on
a specific model ingredient — the limiting-curvature de Sitter matching from ref. [3] (lines 33–37) — so the claim is
framing, overreaching its own derivation.

## 1. The construction: imported, not derived here

The LCH implementation via non-dynamical scalar φ with potential V(φ) and higher-derivative terms is explicitly
CITED, not constructed: "A constructive implementation of the limiting curvature hypothesis was discovered in [3]"
(lines 34–37). The 1+1-dimensional nonsingular black hole is likewise cited to [4] (lines 38–39). Crucially, the
four-dimensional case — the one the paper's scenario needs — is admitted to be incomplete: "We are currently
extending the construction of [3] to discuss the four-dimensional Schwarzschild black hole" (lines 41–42), and the
Discussion lists as future work "to construct a higher derivative theory of gravity which will provide a realization
of the above mentioned model" (lines 365–372). Equations present IN this paper: Schwarzschild metric (1, line 127),
geodesic EOMs (2)–(5) (lines 131–147), orbit integrals (6)–(11) (lines 154–208), FRW metric (12, line 248),
Friedmann density (13, line 255), size integrals (15),(17) (lines 294, 308), de Sitter scale factors (16)
(lines 299–304), density matrices (18) (lines 330–334). NONE of these is an action or field equation for the LCH
theory; all are standard GR geometry and matching integrals. The BH-interior → de Sitter transition is taken from
[3] and applied; the paper's own contribution is the geodesic/causal analysis and the topological matching argument.

## 2. The "long period of cosmological inflation": nothing derived, no scale fixed

The abstract (lines 15–16) claims the SBB problems "might be solved naturally, not necessarily requiring a long
period of cosmological inflation" — i.e. the paper's point is that long inflation is NOT needed. The bounce itself is
conceded to be "presumably of too short duration to solve the problems of standard cosmology" (lines 92–94). No
duration, energy scale, or exit mechanism for the de Sitter phase is derived anywhere. The de Sitter scale H appears
only symbolically (lines 298–304, 323); no value is given, and nothing ties the limiting-curvature value to any
observation. It is not even discussed as a free parameter — it is simply absent. "Long" is qualitative throughout.

## 3. Observer mapping: one conditional consequence, mostly causal-structure picture

The mapping is stated at lines 76–85: "the location r = 0 from the viewpoint of an observer in the mother universe
becomes the initial time surface t = 0 for an observer in the baby de Sitter universe," with matter entering "at the
same time (t = 0) but in different places." Observable-type consequences for an observer inside the baby universe:
(a) Spatial flatness k = 0 — the closest thing to an earned observable. Derived via a topological matching argument:
the daughter's spatial size computed in Schwarzschild coordinates diverges linearly (integral (15), lines 294–296),
and matching to the de Sitter side selects k = 0 (lines 310–311; restated lines 353–359). But this is conditional on
the assumed matching construction and on infinite spatial extent; it is not derived from dynamics.
(b) Perturbations: explicitly NOT worked out — "a quantitative theory of the origin and evolution of fluctuations
remains to be worked out. Here, we mention some ideas" (lines 316–317). Cosmic strings (line 318) are noted to have
problems with the observed Doppler peak; Hawking-radiation fluctuations freezing at λ >> c/H (lines 319–324) are a
suggestion with no spectrum, amplitude, or tilt. No relic, no curvature-sign prediction beyond the conditional k = 0,
no inherited-perturbation calculation. The horizon-problem "solution" is itself partial: photons from r ≈ 1.9m reach
only ≈114° (lines 194–202), deep-interior orbits reach at most ≈80° (lines 213–217), and the paper concedes "one
cannot solve the horizon problem by considering interactions which occur solely inside the horizon" (lines 228–229),
falling back on pre-horizon equilibration (lines 218–220).

## 4. Information loss and "survival of the fittest": no testable content, no CNS claim

Information loss (lines 325–344): the argument is that pure states falling into the black hole "emerge in the new
universe as pure states and the information loss problem is avoided" (lines 342–344). No mechanism, no testable
consequence — a consistency remark. The "survival of the fittest" analogy (line 63) is SMOLIN's idea [9], cited in
the literature review, with the paper itself noting "Smolin provides no quantitative realization of his model"
(line 65). This paper makes NO CNS-type claim: it says only that a daughter "will take on certain properties of the
mother universe" (lines 23–24) and inherits spatial topology (lines 262–263). It never claims constants are tuned or
selected for black-hole fecundity, so it does not intersect entry 31's falsifier. Philosophy-adjacent, not physics
content.

## Plain-language paragraph

This paper is a motivation note, not a derivation. It says: if our universe were born inside a black hole (using a
bounce construction that another paper built and that is not yet extended to four dimensions), then several standard
cosmology puzzles would not stand in the way — matter falling in can thermalize before entering the new universe,
the new universe would be spatially flat for topological-matching reasons, and information would not be lost. It
derives none of the machinery that makes the bounce happen, gives no numbers for how big, how long, or how lumpy the
new universe is, and its structure-formation story is openly labeled "ideas" for future work. The one prediction-like
statement (flatness) is conditional on the assumed construction, so it is a consistency property, not a forecast.
Nothing here conflicts with the corpus and nothing here earns an observable, so the CONSISTENCY-ONLY tier is correct.
