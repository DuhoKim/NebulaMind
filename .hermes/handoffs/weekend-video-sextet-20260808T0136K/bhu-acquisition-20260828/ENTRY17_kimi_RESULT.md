Entry 17 — Chakrabarty, Abdujabbarov, Malafarina & Bambi (2020), "A toy model for a baby universe inside a black hole" (arXiv 1909.07129). Blind deep audit, kimi seat, 2026-09-02. Sole source read: ../bhu-reading-20260823/sources/1909.07129_clean.txt. All receipts are line numbers in that file.

1. THE MATCHING
Collapsing cloud: homogeneous dust, explicitly the Oppenheimer-Snyder-Datt (OSD) model — "described by homogeneous dust, i.e. the Oppenheimer-Snyder-Datt (OSD) model" (L64), refs OS/Datt at L420-424. NOT Lemaitre-Tolman. Interior metric is FRW form (eq 7, L97); exterior is Schwarzschild at all times (L55, L147-152).
Junction formalism: Darmois-Israel (L99). The collapse-to-FRW matching is on the space-like surface t_i = t_B (eq 8, L101). First junction conditions give r_II = [a_I(t_B)/a_II(t_B)] r_I (eq 12) and a_II(t_B) = sqrt(k_II/k_I) a_I(t_B) (eq 13, L116-122). Second junction condition is satisfied smoothly because ȧ_i(t_B) = 0 on both sides (eqs 14-16, L124-135): "continuous matching is possible at the surface t_i = t_B since ȧ_i(t_B) = 0" (L133-134). So the I-II bounce surface needs NO surface layer.
The surface layer appears at a different junction: interior region I vs Schwarzschild exterior region III across the time-like cloud boundary. There "continuity of the second fundamental form can not be satisfied in this case. The extrinsic curvature being not same on the two sides of the collapsing boundary implies that the shell r_I = r_Ib must carry a delta-like surface stress-energy tensor S_ab" (L173-176, eq 24). Derived in-paper: K^I (eq 25, L178), K^III (eq 26, L181-184), shell density rho_Sigma (eq 27, L187) and shell mass M_Sigma = rho_Sigma x 4 pi r_Ib^2 (eq 28, L191). Fig 2 caption states the accounting: "The shell's density increases in such a way that M_Sigma + r_Ib^3 M^eff_I/2 = M_Sch at all times" (L194). Sign: positive — the interior effective mass is time-dependent and "at the boundary the effective mass is lower than the corresponding Schwarzschild mass" (L160), so the shell carries the positive deficit. Energy-condition status: the bounce itself requires SEC violation — "the minimal requirement in order to obtain a bounce leading to a new universe is that the matter source (effective or not) violates the strong energy condition" (L70-72, cited to visser2/barcelo2, i.e. Molina-Paris & Visser L431-433 and Barcelo et al L428-430). The shell is declared non-physical: "must be understood not as a physical matter field but as a consequence of the semi-classical corrections in the interior" (L161-162). Derived here vs cited: the layer's NECESSITY and its stress are derived in this paper (extrinsic curvatures computed both sides); the SEC-violation requirement and the rho_eff = rho - rho^2/rho_cr modification (eq 2, L74) are cited (BMM = Bambi-Malafarina-Modesto, refs 13/14 of the paper, L353-358). The junction-layer finding of the prior CGATE note is confirmed and is the paper's own result, not a citation.

2. THE BOUNCE
No dynamics for the phase transition. The collapse halt is semi-derived through the imported effective density (eq 3, L79; bounce time/scale eqs 4-6, L82-94; bound-case bounce density rho_B = (1 - k a(t_B)/m) rho_cr < rho_cr, eq 6, L92-93). But the transition itself is imposed by hand: "We shall now treat the hyper-surface Sigma given by t_I = t_B as a phase transition at which the collapsing matter turns into an expanding FRW universe" (L95), and the paper disowns the mechanism: "the model does not describe the mechanism of the phase transition at the bounce, since that would require a quantum gravitational theory of matter. Therefore the model does not predict what matter content would be allowed in the expanding phase" (L59). Free parameters: collapsing side m_I, k_I (L68); critical density rho_cr, "assumed to have rho_cr of the order of the Planck density" (L91) — a scale assumption, not a derivation; expanding side m_II, k_II, and omega in [-1,1] (L137), fixed only by the constraint m_II/a_II^{3(omega+1)} = m_I/a_I^3 at t_B (L141), with rho_cr optionally different across the surface (L142). The choice is displayed as arbitrary: Fig 1 uses omega = 1/3 (L144), Fig 6 uses omega = 0 (L308).

3. OBSERVERS
(a) Exterior: the key premise is ASSUMED, not derived — "we assume that no departure from the classical black hole solution exists outside the horizon" (L40), "The geometry outside the horizon is assumed to be described by Schwarzschild at all times" (L55), "we consider the exterior space-time to be a classical black hole and assume that semi-classical corrections do not alter the exterior geometry" (L161). Given the assumption, invisibility follows: "observers at infinity will only detect the formation of a classical black hole without any evidence of the bounce" (L56; repeated L313). Structural support is derived: in all three completion cases "the matching occurs inside the horizon for the region (III), and therefore it remains causally disconnected from the black hole exterior" (L203). But the paper itself flags the tension: the interior effective mass is not conserved, and "This may be interpreted as a sign that semi-classical effects must propagate to the exterior region" (L160) — the shell at the boundary is precisely the patch that keeps the assumption standing. Net: conclusion is assumption-driven with a derived causal-disconnection skeleton.
(b) Interior: geometric statements only, all derived: expanding FRW with curvature k_II and free omega; apparent-horizon analysis eqs 42-46 (L289-305) — at the bounce r_ah = 1/sqrt(k_i) (eq 46, L305); "The apparent horizon forms at a constant radius at the time of bounce and increases as the expansion proceeds" (L309). NO statement anywhere about what an internal observer would measure as relics of the parent's collapse (no curvature-sign measurement claim, no relic discussion).
(c) Our universe: NO claim that we live inside such a baby universe. The only gesture is that the model "support[s] Smolin's idea of universes with black hole being favoured via a selection mechanism" (L54). Nothing about our universe's origin.

4. THE SMOLIN LINK (L54)
CNS is described as Smolin's picture, not claimed here: "it was suggested that universes with black holes arise naturally through a selection process where new universes with slightly different values of fundamental constants are created inside black holes" (L43-44, citing Smolin ref 24, L387-388), with Ellis's critical analysis cited (L45, ref 25) and Easson-Brandenberger (ref 26, L393-394 — this project's entry 15). The paper's own sentence is compatibility-only: the bounce "naturally allows for an expanding universe to develop inside the black hole, thus supporting Smolin's idea" (L54). No constants-inheritance mechanism, no variation law, no mass threshold, no population argument. Nothing in the paper touches entry 31's falsifier — there is no CNS-type claim to falsify.

5. TIER CONSEQUENCE — CONSISTENCY-ONLY, argued
The paper delivers exactly one derived result: a consistent Darmois-Israel matching (collapsing OSD with effective-density bounce to expanding FRW to Schwarzschild exterior) EXISTS, at the price of (i) an imposed, mechanism-free phase transition at rho_cr (L59, L95) and (ii) a non-physical positive surface-stress layer at the cloud boundary (L173-176, L161-162). Everything observational is assumed away (Schwarzschild exterior, L40/L55/L161) or left free (omega, k_II, m_II, rho_cr; L137-142). There are no numbers to calibrate, no threshold the lane could own (the condition m > (16k/3)sqrt(k/rho_cr), L90, is an internal consistency condition for two real roots of the effective dynamics, not a claimed physical threshold for baby-universe formation), no falsifier, and no CNS claim. A toy model demonstrating kinematic consistency of a bounce-driven interior, with the junction-layer necessity derived — that is CONSISTENCY-ONLY. Token: AUDIT_HOLDS_CONSISTENCY_ONLY.

PLAIN-LANGUAGE PARAGRAPH
This paper builds a toy spacetime in which a collapsing dust ball, saved from its singularity by an assumed quantum-style density cutoff, turns around at a chosen moment and continues as an expanding closed universe hidden inside the black hole. The authors honestly do the matching math and find it works only if two things are granted: the bounce itself is declared, not explained — they say plainly that the mechanism would require a quantum theory of matter they do not have — and a thin, explicitly non-physical layer of stress on the cloud's surface must absorb the mismatch between the quantum-corrected interior and the strictly classical Schwarzschild exterior they assume. Because they assume the outside stays exactly Schwarzschild, the conclusion that distant observers see nothing of the bounce is built in rather than discovered, though they do show all the exotic matching happens behind the horizon. The nod to Smolin is a citation of his idea, not a version of it: no constants, no inheritance, no threshold. So the entry stands as a consistency demonstration — such an interior can be glued together — and nothing more; CONSISTENCY-ONLY holds.
