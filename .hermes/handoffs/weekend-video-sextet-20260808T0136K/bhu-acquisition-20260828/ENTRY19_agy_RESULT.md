The paper assumes a de Sitter (positive-curvature) interior for the black hole as a starting premise rather than deriving it, as noted in the abstract: "birth of universes inside regular black holes with the de Sitter interior replacing a singularity." Additionally, the author evaluates the geometries of the universes born inside and concludes that the most likely outcome is a flat universe, not a closed one (Line 1175: "the most plausible case is the birth of a flat universe"). Per Duho ruling A(a), since the positive-curvature interior is assumed and not derived, it does not meet the criteria for a directional tier and correctly remains CONSISTENCY-ONLY.
