## 1. Construction and provenance

The paper constructs a state of many holographic CFTs by joining two copies of a spatial geometry with tubes; the corresponding Lorentzian bulk is a connected multi-boundary wormhole whose interior is a big-bang/big-crunch region containing a lattice of black holes (lines 29–32, 52–68). In the explicit calculation the boundary theory is two-dimensional and the bulk is three-dimensional (line 70). Negative-Λ Einstein gravity makes each matter-free patch locally AdS\(_3\), and time reflection fixes a hyperbolic spatial surface \(\Sigma\); the paper then gives

\[
ds^2=-dt^2+\cos^2(t/\ell)\,d\Sigma^2,
\qquad a(t)=\cos(t/\ell),
\]

as eq. (3) (lines 71–77). Thus the displayed scale factor is established for the paper's locally AdS\(_3\), time-symmetric glued solutions, though the introduction says that the explicit construction follows MMV (lines 33, 41). It is not a new general cosmological evolution law.

The cell volume is derived in this paper from the hyperbolic polygon area/angle-deficit formula:

\[
V/\ell_{AdS}^2=2p\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{q}\right),
\]

eq. (5), with the derivation stated at lines 85–89 (and previewed as eq. (2) at lines 42–45). The horizon length \(R\), inter-hole distance \(D\), and Schottky parameter \(\rho\) are related by the paper's derived eq. (14),

\[
\tanh(D/2)=\rho,\qquad \cosh(R/4)=1/\rho^2,
\]

with its hyperbolic-pentagon derivation described at lines 169–172. The critical scale is then obtained from the paper's numerical action comparison: for an infinite square lattice, \(R_{crit}\simeq7.06\ell\), hence \(r_{crit}=R_{crit}/(2\pi)\simeq1.12\ell_{AdS}\) (lines 342–351). Combining this with eq. (14) gives \(D_{crit}\simeq1.3\ell_{AdS}\), as reported at line 35. Line 351 instead prints a “critical separation” \(d_{crit}\simeq1.2\ell_{AdS}\); the source does not explain the notation/rounding difference, so the defensible receipt for \(D_{crit}\) is eq. (14) plus the reported critical \(R\), not an independent universal number.

The dominance condition is least Euclidean gravitational action for a *fixed boundary geometry*: both action differences in lines 138–149 must be negative. For the square \(n\times n\) lattice this is eq. (9); in the infinite-lattice limit the crossing condition is eq. (11), lines 152–157. Numerically, the cosmological saddle dominates for \(r>1.12\ell_{AdS}\) (lines 350–354), equivalently at sufficiently small \(\beta\), where the correlated operator sum

\[
\sum_i {\cal O}^1_i {\cal O}^2_i e^{-\beta\Delta_i}
\]

is dominated by black-hole microstates and is the canonical ensemble at each fixed insertion location (lines 25–28, 33). The comparison is only against the three natural saddles preserving the lattice symmetry (lines 124–149); the abstract likewise says “other natural candidates that preserve the symmetries” (line 10). It is therefore not a proof of dominance over every possible saddle or over an observational ensemble of universes.

The older ingredients are identified rather than silently claimed: Maldacena–Maoz supplied the original analytic-continuation-from-a-Euclidean-wormhole proposal (line 49); MMV supplied the heavy-particle/ensemble motivation and the earlier conical-defect solutions (lines 18–22, 359–363), and the paper says its explicit 3D construction follows MMV (lines 33, 41). The volume formula, Schottky matching, regulated action calculation, and quoted critical values are calculations presented here (lines 85–89, 158–172, 194–239, 342–351).

## 2. Dimension and cosmological-constant sign

The controlled results are explicitly for a 2D CFT with a 3D bulk (line 70) and \(\Lambda<0\): the setup begins with a holographic CFT dual to a negative-Λ gravitational EFT (line 52), while the explicit field equation uses \(\Lambda=-1/\ell^2\) (lines 383–400). The cosmology is a recollapsing AdS dust/black-hole cosmology: heavy particles act as pressureless dust and give the “\(\Lambda<0\) matter cosmology” (lines 18–20), and its interior has past and future singularities (lines 66–68, 112–115).

The strongest realism phrase is hedged: negative-Λ theories have “interesting and potentially realistic” solutions with zero, positive, or negative *spatial curvature* (line 13). Spatial-curvature sign is not the sign of \(\Lambda\). The paper nowhere derives or claims a continuation to \(\Lambda>0\). For higher dimensions it says only that it “expect[s]” the general picture to be “qualitatively the same,” and says actual solutions would generally require numerics (line 356). It gives no mechanism by which the recollapsing negative-Λ solution reproduces a presently accelerating positive-Λ-like universe. Its only direct comparison says that black holes much smaller than the cosmological scale, “as in our own universe,” occur only in a subdominant saddle in this construction (line 355); the anthropic/projection remark is explicitly speculative (“might,” “Perhaps”) at lines 36–38.

## 3. Observables

No quantity here is a calibrated observable that our universe can contradict. The scale factor, bang/crunch behavior, spatial curvature, critical radius, and spacing all belong to the negative-Λ AdS\(_3\) construction and are expressed in units of \(\ell_{AdS}\) (lines 71–89, 342–364). The action threshold tests which bulk filling dominates a specified Euclidean CFT path integral, not a measured population of astrophysical black holes (lines 116–121, 138–157). The entropy discussion says large correlations/entropy are required (lines 20–24), but supplies no observational entropy bound. There is no CMB prediction, no inferred curvature bound for our universe, no observational recollapse time, and no map from \(\ell_{AdS}\) or \(\beta\) to measured cosmological parameters. The paper itself characterizes our-universe-like small black holes as a rare, subdominant component and offers only a possible projection as future work (lines 355–356).

## 4. Relation to the BHU claim

This is best labeled **holographic interior cosmology**, not a claim that our universe is the interior of one parent black hole. The authors say precisely that “the cosmology is the interior geometry of a multi-boundary Lorentzian wormhole” (line 31), and that from each asymptotic region the geometry looks like a black-hole exterior while the connected interior contains \(n\) black holes and evolves as a big-bang/big-crunch cosmology (lines 64–68). In the explicit geometry each funnel supplies half of a two-sided BTZ black hole, whereas the finite region is the cosmological spacetime containing a lattice of black holes (lines 109–115). Thus there are horizons and an interior cosmology, but the construction is a cosmology built from—and holographically encoded through—the interiors/second asymptotic regions of many black holes, not a derived single-parent “our universe inside a black hole” scenario.

## 5. Tier consequence

**Retain CONSISTENCY-ONLY.** The construction and its internal saddle threshold are genuinely calculated, so the paper is stronger than an unsupported analogy. But its number is a dominance threshold internal to an AdS\(_3\) CFT path integral, among a restricted symmetric saddle set; it is not a number calibrated to our universe. The 4D extension is only a qualitative expectation, positive \(\Lambda\) is not supplied, and there is no observable map or falsification criterion. Accordingly there is no basis for QUALITATIVE-DIRECTIONAL, PROSPECT, or CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER promotion. **A(a): no tier-adjacent challenge packet**—the relevant threshold is present, but the missing element is the physical bridge to a 4D accelerating universe, not merely a missing numerical value.

In plain language: this paper does real, technically explicit work inside a special toy universe. It builds a many-black-hole cosmology in three-dimensional anti-de Sitter gravity and computes when that geometry wins a particular holographic saddle competition. What it does not do is show that our four-dimensional, accelerating universe sits inside a black hole, or give any observation that could test that claim. Its equations support consistency and microscopic construction within AdS holography, while the connection to our universe remains speculative and uncalibrated.
