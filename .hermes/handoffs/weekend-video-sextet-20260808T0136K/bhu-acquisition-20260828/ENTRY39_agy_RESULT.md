### 2. The deciding argument

The bounce occurs when `da/dt = 0`, yielding the condition `\epsilon = -\epsilon_S` (1105.6127_clean.txt:92). By combining this with relativistic thermodynamics, the paper computes the temperature and energy density at the bounce (1105.6127_clean.txt:135, 142). It then uses scaling relations (`\epsilon_R \sim a^{-4}` and `\epsilon_S \sim a^{-6}`) to relate the bounce scale factor to present-day cosmological parameters (1105.6127_clean.txt:222).
