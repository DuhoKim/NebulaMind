## 1. The junction

The paper takes the minus side to be a pressureless, positively curved (closed) FRW universe with cosmological constant \(\Lambda\), writing its retarded-coordinate metric and Friedmann equations explicitly (lines 64–82). The plus side is the vacuum Schwarzschild–de Sitter geometry,
\(f=1-2M/r-\Lambda r^2/3\), with the same \(\Lambda\) (lines 83–92). It also states the dust-mass relation \(M=(4\pi/3)\rho r^3|_\Sigma\) (lines 88–92).

The matching hypersurface is the maximum-radius surface of the expanding FRW region (lines 98–100). The calculation evaluates it at \(\chi=\pi/2\) (lines 152–161), while the paper's setup identifies that maximum-expansion radius with the black-hole event horizon, hence a null hypersurface (lines 18–23, 37–41, 48–55). Continuity of the induced metric produces the relations in Eq. (7), including \(r|_\Sigma=a\sin\chi\) (lines 98–104); the tangent vectors, transverse null vectors, induced metric, and pseudo-inverse are then calculated in Eqs. (8)–(12) (lines 104–136).

The junction method is the Barrabès–Israel null-shell formalism, which the author explicitly says is being used (lines 53–55). The definitions/formalism for transverse curvature and shell stress are cited to references [4] and [5] (lines 124–139, 169–176; references at lines 225–227). But the metric specialization, basis, matching relations, and both sides' transverse curvatures are computed in this paper (lines 98–168).

## 2. The result

The paper obtains

\[
K_{uu}^-=-\frac12 f_{,r}+2\pi\rho a,
\qquad
K_{uu}^+=-\frac12 f_{,r}
\]

on \(\Sigma\) (lines 152–168). With its convention that brackets denote the jump across \(\Sigma\) (lines 57–60), and as fixed by its pressure equation, the arithmetic is

\[
[K_{uu}]=K_{uu}^+-K_{uu}^-
=-\frac12f_{,r}-\left(-\frac12f_{,r}+2\pi\rho a\right)
=-2\pi\rho a\ne0
\]

for nonzero dust density and scale factor. Thus Eq. (17) gives

\[
p=-\frac{1}{8\pi}[K_{uu}]
=-\frac{1}{8\pi}(-2\pi\rho a)
=\frac{\rho a}{4},
\]

exactly the displayed sign and magnitude (lines 169–179). The angular transverse-curvature components agree, \(K_{\theta\theta}^-=K_{\theta\theta}^+=r|_\Sigma\), with the corresponding \(\phi\phi\) equality (lines 141–147, 164–170). The author therefore explicitly identifies a null shell “with vanishing energy density, but a surface pressure” (lines 169–179), and writes \(S^{ab}=p g_*^{ab}\) (lines 181–183). “Pressure-only” is therefore established, not merely inferred from a nonzero jump.

## 3. Domain

The stamped domain is accurate and appropriately bounded.

The range \(0\leq\Lambda\leq\Lambda_c\) enters through Pathria's construction and the existence/identity of the maximum FRW radius and black-hole horizon: the paper recounts that range, defines \(\Lambda_c\), and says neither \(R_s\) nor \(R_{\max}\) exists for \(\Lambda>\Lambda_c\) (lines 29–41). That existence statement is attributed to Pathria rather than newly proved here. Once this null matching exists, the final jump has no explicit \(\Lambda\), because the common \(-\tfrac12 f_{,r}\) terms cancel; nevertheless, the paper does not establish the stamped construction beyond the stated range.

The stated dust-mass relation is part of the exterior specification (lines 88–92), and the derivation of \(K^-_{uu}\) expressly uses the Friedmann equations, the exterior \(f\), the matching equations, and the maximum-expansion evaluation (lines 152–161). The conclusion therefore should remain tied to those assumptions rather than generalized to arbitrary matter or mass assignments.

The particular surface is essential to what is proved: the calculation is for the null hypersurface at maximum expansion, evaluated at \(\chi=\pi/2\), identified with the horizon (lines 98–104, 152–161). A different matching surface or junction class is not analyzed by this computation. The paper notes Knutsen's distinct smooth-matching analysis along a timelike hypersurface and relates the present surface only as its horizon/null limit (lines 198–209). It does not prove that every alternative surface, every timelike junction, or every shell-bearing realization fails. Accordingly, the stamped savings clause—“Shell-bearing realizations and other FRW/black-hole junction classes are not excluded”—is necessary.

The paper's operative conclusion is limited: “the transition from the expansion to contraction ... located on the event horizon ... can only be done through a null shell” characterized by surface pressure (lines 189–197). It mentions that Knutsen regarded Pathria's idea as untenable, but clearly attributes that assessment to Knutsen (lines 198–206); it does not turn its own calculation into an unrestricted claim that Pathria's model, or all universe/black-hole models, are impossible.

## 4. Ownership of proof

The no-smooth-junction result for this construction is proved here. Barrabès–Israel and related references supply the null-hypersurface machinery (lines 53–55, 124–139, 169–176, 222–227), while Khakshournia performs the application: induced-metric matching, basis construction, side-by-side transverse curvatures, their jump, and the resulting shell pressure (lines 98–183). The abstract likewise reports the paper's own examination and finding that the matching is not smooth (lines 15–23). Knutsen is cited only for the distinct timelike analysis (lines 198–209).

## 5. Tier consequence

**THEORETICAL-OBSTRUCTION holds with the stamped domain.** The paper's operative result is precisely an exclusion of a smooth null matching for the specified Pathria construction: its directly calculated nonzero \([K_{uu}]\) forces a pressure-only shell. This is not merely a parameter constraint encountered incidentally inside an otherwise successful construction. Neither widening nor narrowing is supported: widening would overgeneralize beyond the chosen maximum-expansion/horizon null surface and assumptions, while narrowing would omit assumptions and outcomes the calculation expressly covers. The result excludes the smooth version, not the same construction with its derived shell and not other junction classes.

In plain language: for exactly the Pathria setup named in the stamp, the inside and outside geometries agree on the spherical part of the null boundary but disagree in one longitudinal curvature component by \(-2\pi\rho a\). That mismatch is real and is calculated in this paper; it requires a lightlike shell with no surface energy density and pressure \(\rho a/4\). The paper therefore blocks a smooth joining at that horizon, while leaving shell-supported versions and differently posed matchings open.
