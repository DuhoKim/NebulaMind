## 1. The claim of physical inequivalence (brief axis 1)
The paper's claim is narrower than the abstract's wording suggests. The abstract says the two solutions "are different, physical realizations of general relativity" (line 21), but the Discussion states the precise content: the solutions are "physically different with regard to the nature of the interior sheet and equivalent with regard to the nature of the static exterior sheet (region I)" (line 250). Locally, the geometry is the same Schwarzschild vacuum solution in isotropic coordinates: the metric (1) (line 35) is invariant under r → r' = r_g²/16r (lines 40–46), so the region r < r_g/4 "is an image of the other exterior sheet" (line 59) — an isometry, explicitly analogized to the method of image charges (line 60).

What the paper proves about r < r_g/4:
- Regularity: by curvature invariants. The Kretschmann scalar R R = 12 r_g² r⁻⁶ (1 + r_g/4r)⁻¹² is "finite everywhere ... going to zero as r→∞ and r→0" (line 56, components at lines 50–52). So the interior sheet is regular and asymptotically flat at r → 0.
- White-hole equivalence: by the coordinate argument, not by new dynamics. Applying transformation (2) to the geodesic equations turns infalling motion inside into outgoing motion in r': "An infalling radial geodesic motion inside a black hole appears, in terms of the new radial coordinate r′, as an outgoing motion from a white hole (the time reversal of a black hole)" (line 117). In the Kruskal picture, the particle "enters region III ... which has no curvature singularities and is mathematically equivalent to the asymptotically flat exterior of a white hole" (line 245).

So: not a distinct local solution — a distinct global spacetime built from two Schwarzschild exterior sheets "glued together ... at their common event horizon" (line 258), with Rindler's elliptic identification of the two antipodal future event horizons imposed as the extra global ingredient (lines 230–231). The "physical difference" is a difference of interior topology/identification plus a static-vs-nonstatic interior (line 251), not a different local metric.

## 2. Geodesic completeness via Raychaudhuri (brief axis 2)
The discontinuity is derived, not assumed: for infall from infinity, θ = −(3/2)√(r_g/r³) · sgn(1 − r_g/4r) / (1 + r_g/4r)³ (line 164, Eq. 15); θ decreases from 0 to −3/(2r_g) (line 168), "undergoes a discontinuity, jumping to 3/(2r_g)" at r = r_g/4 (line 169), then decreases back to zero (line 170), which "prevents θ from decreasing to −∞" and "guarantees that timelike geodesics ... are complete" (lines 171–172).

But the discontinuity is inseparable from the patching at the throat — and the paper itself concedes the junction is not vacuum-smooth. It states the metric "is not a solution of the vacuum Einstein equations but it requires the presence of a nonzero energy-momentum tensor source Tμν that is divergent and violates the energy conditions at the throat of the wormhole" (line 260), with an explicit delta-function source R⁰₀ = δ((r − r_g/4)²)/4 (line 264, Eq. 25) and a delta contribution to the curvature scalar at the throat (line 268), interpreted (citing Guendelman et al.) as "a lightlike brane self-consistently interacting with gravity" (line 269). Israel-style thin-shell formalism is not used by name, but the surface-layer content is acknowledged via the distributional source. The 1935 Einstein–Rosen double-sheet identification is invoked (line 47, citing ER), and Rindler's elliptic identification does the completeness work in Kruskal coordinates (lines 229–231, 244). Penrose's theorem (line 158) is evaded precisely because its premise — "reasonable energy conditions" — fails at the throat (line 260). Verdict on the axis: the discontinuity is a derived property of the congruence on this glued geometry, and it is located exactly at the energy-condition-violating surface layer; completeness holds only for this non-vacuum, identified spacetime, which the paper is transparent about.

## 3. "For distant observers, both solutions are indistinguishable" (brief axis 3)
This is a direct consequence of the exterior sheet being the standard Schwarzschild exterior: r > r_g/4 maps to the Schwarzschild form under r_S = r(1 + r_g/4r)² (line 57), and the two solutions are "equivalent with regard to the nature of the static exterior sheet (region I)" (line 250). The paper does concede the exterior untestability explicitly: "Since the two solutions are indistinguishable for distant observers, which can see only the exterior sheet, the nature of the interior of a physical black hole cannot be satisfactorily determined, unless an observer enters or resides in the interior region" (line 272); "only experiment or observation can reveal the nature of the infalling radial motion" (line 271). Interior-observer statements are made: the particle's proper time is finite to the horizon (line 100, Eq. 8), continues through it, "does not end in this region, but extends to infinity" (line 246); local velocity peaks at c at the horizon and decreases back to zero (line 120); the interior is static for the Einstein–Rosen case versus nonstatic for Schwarzschild (line 251).

## 4. "Observed astrophysical black holes may be Einstein–Rosen bridges, each with a new universe inside" (brief axis 4)
This is a suggestion, not a derivation. The abstract phrases it as "These results suggest ..." (line 24), extended to "our own Universe may be the interior of a black hole existing inside another universe" (line 25). Nothing about the new universe's expansion, curvature, or content is derived in this paper; the Discussion only notes the condition "would be satisfied if our Universe were the interior of a black hole existing in a bigger universe" (line 273, citing Pathria et al.), offers a time-orientation argument for an exploding white hole "like the expanding Universe" (lines 274–275), and flags dust collapse in isotropic coordinates and Schwarzschild–de Sitter/Kerr generalizations as "subjects of further study" (line 277). This is pure classical GR throughout: the torsion mechanism of the later ECSK entries (9–12) is nowhere invoked; the only non-vacuum element is the classical delta-function brane at the throat (lines 260–269).

## 5. Tier consequence (brief axis 5)

The paper provides (a) a regular, geodesically complete global extension built from the Schwarzschild solution via an isometry plus an imposed global identification, and (b) an explicit admission that the exterior is observationally indistinguishable from Schwarzschild (lines 22, 250, 271–272). There is no derived exterior observable, no number, and no proposed test — the paper itself says the interior's nature "cannot be satisfactorily determined, unless an observer enters" (line 272). That caps it below QUALITATIVE-DIRECTIONAL. The construction is also not vacuum-GR-clean (delta-source throat violating energy conditions, lines 260–268), which keeps it a conditional existence/consistency result rather than a directional claim about real black holes. The prior 2026-08-30 finding re-derives cleanly: "does not exist in isotropic coordinates" for Schwarzschild (line 258) is a chart/domain statement, not an obstruction — confirmed. No threshold or number is missing; the lane owns nothing further. CONSISTENCY-ONLY stands.

## Plain-language paragraph
This paper takes the Schwarzschild geometry written in isotropic coordinates, notices that the map r → r_g²/16r makes the region inside the horizon a mirror image of a second exterior universe, and argues that an infalling particle therefore passes through the horizon into a regular white-hole exterior where its clock never runs out. The price of that happy ending is stated honestly in the paper itself: the gluing requires an energy-condition-violating sheet of matter at the throat, and the completeness trick depends on accepting Rindler's identification of the two future horizons as physical. Everything a distant observer can ever measure is identical to an ordinary black hole, so the "new universe inside" is an untestable suggestion the author defers to future work. It earns its place in the Popławski spine as the GR-only consistency anchor — a coherent picture, not evidence — and the CONSISTENCY-ONLY tier holds.
