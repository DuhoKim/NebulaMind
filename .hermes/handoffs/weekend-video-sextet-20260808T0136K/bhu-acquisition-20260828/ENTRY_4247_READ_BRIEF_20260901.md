## The tier taxonomy — pick the ONE that fits each paper

- **CONSISTENCY-ONLY** — reproduces or is consistent with known results / builds a construction; makes no
  prediction the outside world could contradict.
- **QUALITATIVE-DIRECTIONAL** — a signed, testable direction on an observable, DERIVED from the model
  (e.g. a *derived* Ω_k<0 closed universe — per Duho ruling A(a), closure counts ONLY if derived, not an
  assumed k=+1 ansatz).
- **CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER** — a number WITH a threshold that observation could cross; state quantity,
  threshold, and what measures it.
- **THEORETICAL-OBSTRUCTION** — a proof/no-go that a whole class of models cannot work. Ownership-of-proof:
  the paper must DO the proving in-text, not cite someone else's theorem.
- **PROSPECT** — a testable idea whose amplitude/number the paper does not supply.

**A stronger tier is NOT earned by:** numbers above Planck density / at a self-conceded validity limit;
internal-consistency parameters; values borrowed from the data they are compared to; or an *assumed*
closed ansatz.
