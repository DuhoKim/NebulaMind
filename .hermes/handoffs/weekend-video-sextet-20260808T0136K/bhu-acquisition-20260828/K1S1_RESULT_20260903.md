- Raising the primordial amplitude makes more halos, more stars, and therefore more stellar black holes; the count rises
  monotonically through the observed value. The premise that our universe sits at a local MAXIMUM of black-hole production

  semi-analytic model and its declared nuisance box. The sign is structural (erfc grows with σ), which is also why it is a

  Smolin needs, but a monotone fall is not a maximum either: the premise is "consistent, not confirmed" on θ₂.
- The PBH channel contributes nothing at the Planck amplitude, so stage 1's A_s result stands or falls on the stellar channel;
  stage 2 (population synthesis) tests that channel with real binary physics.
