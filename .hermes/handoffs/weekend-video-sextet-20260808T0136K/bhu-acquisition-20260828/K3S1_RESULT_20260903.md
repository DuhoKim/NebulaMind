## 2. The finding, in words

density is (¾)·n/V (operator-ordered; ¼·n/V as a c-number), linear in n: cross terms between particles vanish because the mean spin
of each is zero. The square of the mean is zero. Neither printed closure — ⅛(ℏcn)² (entries 9, 11; entry 10 L121) nor ¾ n² (entry 10
L113) — follows from the unpolarized average. Each is a CONVENTION:
