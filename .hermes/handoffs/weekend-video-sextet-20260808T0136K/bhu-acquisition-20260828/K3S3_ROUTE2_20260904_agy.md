## 1. Integration of Eq. (14)
Entry 10's Eq. (14) reads (lines 162-164):
`dT/T - (3 \alpha h_n^2 / 2 h_*) T dT + da/a = 0`

Isolating `da/a` and integrating:
`da/a = (3 \alpha h_n^2 / 2 h_*) T dT - dT/T`
`\ln(a) = (3 \alpha h_n^2 / 4 h_*) T^2 - \ln(T) + C`
`a(T) = (e^C / T) \exp(3 \alpha h_n^2 T^2 / 4 h_*)`

This integration EXACTLY reproduces the functional form of the printed Eq. (15), where the constant of integration $e^C$ is identified by the source as $a_r T_r$.

## 2. Critical temperature from the minimum condition
Imposing the minimum-scale-factor condition $da/dT = 0$ on the integrated $a(T)$ gives:
`(1/a) da/dT = (3 \alpha h_n^2 / 2 h_*) T - 1/T = 0`

`T_{cr} = \sqrt{2 h_* / 3 \alpha h_n^2}`
This symbolically matches the paper's printed Eq. (16).

## 3. R at the bounce
The magnitude of the spin contribution to the energy density is `|\tilde{\epsilon}| = \alpha n^2 = \alpha (h_n T^3)^2 = \alpha h_n^2 T^6`.
The ordinary energy density is `\epsilon = h_* T^4`.
The ratio is `R = |\tilde{\epsilon}| / \epsilon = (\alpha h_n^2 / h_*) T^2`.

Evaluating this ratio at $T = T_{cr}$ yields:
`R(T_{cr}) = (\alpha h_n^2 / h_*) * (2 h_* / 3 \alpha h_n^2) = 2/3`
The value of $R$ at the bounce is exactly the rational number `2/3`.
