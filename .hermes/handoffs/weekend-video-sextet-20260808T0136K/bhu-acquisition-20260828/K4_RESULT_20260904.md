## 2. The finding

**Limb 2 of the falsifier does not fire, and all four seats agree on that.** The perturbed Darmois junction is neither
F1 nor F2: it does not touch only the monopole, and it does not annihilate the perturbation field.

**But it does not close as a boundary condition either.** For every multipole `ℓ ≥ 2` and every `m` with
`−ℓ ≤ m ≤ ℓ`, the Schwarzschild vacuum exterior retains free Zerilli data — one free function of time per `(ℓ, m)`,
equivalently two real Cauchy data functions, unconstrained by the junction and subject only to reality and
finite-energy/regularity conditions. The Darmois conditions are local to the matching surface; the Zerilli equation in
the exterior needs data at infinity as well, and the junction does not supply it.

**Consequence:** the junction cannot fix the interior mode structure without an added physical assumption, so there is
no derived `C_ℓ` modification to score. **Nothing to push through the estimator; the study stops before the data.**

Adding "no incoming radiation from past null infinity" (or a reflecting condition at infinity) removes the freedom and
makes the exterior response unique. **That is an additional physical assumption, not a consequence of the Darmois
conditions**, and the prereg forbids manufacturing one (§5 class 4, §9). It is not obviously wrong physics — it is the
standard astrophysical choice — but it is a choice, and this model's own literature does not make it. Recording the
freedom is the result; picking the completion would have manufactured the answer.
