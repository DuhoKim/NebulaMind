relation.** A ceiling `ρ ≤ ρ_Ce` constrains a density; converting a density bound into a mass bound requires knowing
how volume scales with mass, and the source supplies no such relation. Its ledger marks ABSENT: a defined invariant
black-hole density (no local scalar or proper-volume average is specified); a defined mass and bounding surface (no

angular-momentum domain and matching conditions — for which the paper itself says the full coupled equations must be
solved (**L604–612**).

Two attempts to bind that same premise failed, which under prereg §10 requires class 3 **without importing an unstated
model**.

**Route B** attacked the same gap from the other side and exhibited the freedom concretely. Three readings of "the mass
density of a black hole", each admissible because the source excludes none:

| completion | density measure | derived floor |
|---|---|---|
| A | Euclidean mean inside `R_s` | **2.70 × 10¹⁴ kg** |

**Different completions, different floors, and one with no floor: that is class 3 by definition.**
