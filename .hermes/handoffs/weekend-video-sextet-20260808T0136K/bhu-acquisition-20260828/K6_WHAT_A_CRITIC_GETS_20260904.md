## 1. The strongest objection: the missing step may be obvious rather than absent

A critic can say: everyone in the field knows a black hole's "density" means `M` over the Schwarzschild volume; the
author omitted a line every reader can reconstruct, and calling that "underdetermined" is pedantry dressed as audit.

**Where the objection lands.** It is a fair account of how the sentence would be read by a working relativist, and the
lane should not pretend the reading is exotic.

**Where it fails.** The obvious completion gives `2.7 × 10¹⁴ kg`, **not** the printed `10¹⁶ kg` — 1.57 decades away and
outside the paper's own order of magnitude. So the reconstruction a critic calls obvious does not reproduce the
printed number, which means either a different completion was intended or something else differs. That is precisely
what "unreproduced from the stated inputs" records, and it is why the finding is not pedantry.

the only defence available, and the result states the raw decade separation (1.57) so a reader can apply their own
threshold.
