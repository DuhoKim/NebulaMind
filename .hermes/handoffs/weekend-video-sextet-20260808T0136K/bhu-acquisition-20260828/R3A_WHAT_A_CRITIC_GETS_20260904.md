## 1. The strongest objection: "free parameter" is how physics normally works

A critic can say: every effective model carries parameters not derived from first principles, and calling that a
finding is not a finding. Λ in ΛCDM is not derived either. The paper is explicit that a QFT derivation is owed, so the
audit has discovered only what the authors already wrote.

**Where it lands.** Fairly. Nothing here was hidden by the authors, and the result says so.

**Where it does not.** The value of the finding is not "β is undetermined" but **where β sits in the chain**: the
paper's reported `n_s`, `r` and `α_s` are sensitive to β alone (L373–374), and those observables are then compared with
Planck. A free parameter carried into a published observational comparison is a different object from a free parameter
sitting in a formalism, and the record now distinguishes them.

## 2. The FITTED/FREE call could go the other way, and one seat said so

codex read L459–463 as a fit and it is not an unreasonable reading — "agreement… for a particular range of the
particle production coefficient" is what fitting looks like from outside. The third seat's discriminator (a fitted
parameter would not leave a 6σ gap) is persuasive but it is an inference about intent from behaviour, not a proof.

**A critic who insists on FITTED gets a different label and the same underlying facts:** β is not derived, and the
observables depend on it. **The class differs; the substance does not.** That is stated here rather than defended away.

## 3. β_cr's derivation is real and should not be lost

`β_cr = 1/929.0915` for standard-model content **is** derived, and this result says so explicitly. A summary that
reported only "β is free" would understate the paper. The construction fixes a bound and leaves the value inside it
open.
