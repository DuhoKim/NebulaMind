## 2. The finding

The chain is not in dispute: `Λ ≡ 3/r_S²` (**L28**) with `r_S = 2GM_T`, so `Λ` is constant **iff** `M_T` is constant,
which would give `w = −1` exactly with no evolution available.

**The question was whether `M_T` is constant as a consequence of the construction or as an assumption imposed on it.
The source answers it, and the answer is: assumption.**

- **L134–135:** the mass inside `χ` is constant "for matter-dominated fluid when `ρ ∼ a⁻³`" — a *conditional*.
- **L136–137:** "in the early stages of the expansion, when the energy density is dominated by radiation or a fluid
  with a different equation of state, **the mass inside `χ` is a function of `τ`**."
- **L138–140, Eq. (10):** "**If we want** `M_T` … to be constant throughout the evolution, we need the junction `χ*`
  … to be a function of time `τ`."
- **L143–144:** "More generally, `M` could be a function of time."

**L138 is the decisive sentence and it is conditional.** The paper does not derive that `M_T` is constant; it adopts
constancy as a requirement and engineers a **time-dependent junction `χ*(τ)`** to deliver it. Equation (10) is the
device that satisfies the assumption, not a proof of it.

So the construction **does not forbid** evolving dark energy. A variant with `M_T(τ)` is admissible on the printed
text — L143–144 says so outright — and would give a time-dependent `Λ`, hence `w ≠ −1`.

## 3. What this costs the pattern, and what it does not

**The counterexample I hoped for is not here.** R3B was ranked second precisely because it was the only candidate that
might show a BHU construction fixing a magnitude rigidly enough for data to hit. It does not: the rigidity is a
modelling choice, and the corpus's shape/magnitude pattern survives this test intact.

**But the finding is more useful than the counterexample would have been.** It says the `w = −1` prediction attributed
to this construction is **not a prediction of the construction** — it is a consequence of an assumption the author
states conditionally and could relax. A future evolving-`w` measurement would therefore **not** refute this model; it
would select the `M_T(τ)` branch the paper already permits.

**And the prediction never discriminated anyway.** C3: ΛCDM gives `w = −1` exactly too. Even had the rigidity held,
the prereg's class-4 precedence would have applied. That expectation was declared in advance, before any of this was
computed.
