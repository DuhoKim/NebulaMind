Direct answer on `CENSUS_COMPLETE`: **REACHABLE.** Its domain is not “a typical corpus”; it is any completed census in which every included claim has `REPRO_EXACT` or `REPRO_FAILED`. The concrete one-claim state E exhibits that domain, and the same routing works for a many-paper corpus if every included claim lands in either arithmetic outcome. Nothing in §§1–4 requires a real corpus to contain a blocked, absent-input, no-derivation, or not-evaluable claim. If even one included claim does carry any such non-arithmetic outcome, however, §4 makes `CENSUS_PARTIAL` true and gives it precedence over `CENSUS_COMPLETE`; moreover COMPLETE's “every included claim” condition then fails. Thus one such claim anywhere forces PARTIAL, assuming no still-earlier study-level class applies, but that contingent possibility does not make COMPLETE unreachable.

No verdict is UNREACHABLE, so there is no blocking-clause section to supply.
