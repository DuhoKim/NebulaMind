## Direct answer on `CENSUS_COMPLETE`

`CENSUS_COMPLETE` is **REACHABLE**, including for a real corpus containing many papers. A concrete route is the 100-paper input above: every included claim lands in one of the two arithmetic outcomes, and no earlier study-level stop applies. The text imposes no requirement that a realistic corpus must contain a blocked, absent-input, no-derivation, or not-evaluable claim.

However, one such included claim anywhere is sufficient to prevent complete filing: §4.2 applies when “**at least one included claim carries a non-arithmetic outcome**” and says `CENSUS_PARTIAL` “**takes precedence over `CENSUS_COMPLETE`**.” Thus C-A, C-B, C-D, or C-N anywhere in the otherwise arithmetic corpus routes to `CENSUS_PARTIAL`, assuming no still-earlier stop class applies. This makes completeness demanding, not unreachable. Whether the actual pinned corpus qualifies is unknown because the preregistration says it has not been run.

There is also a degenerate textual path: with zero included claims, “every included claim” is vacuously arithmetic and the partial condition is false. V19's §10.13 explicitly says a proposed zero-denominator clause remains escalated and was not applied. This path is not needed for the reachability result because the nonempty 100-paper construction above already reaches the class.

## Dependence on §1

All per-claim routes depend first on the two seats agreeing that the passage is an included quantitative claim under §1's marked boundary: a numeral “the paper asserts as a result of its own.” If that judgement instead remains disputed after two reconciliation attempts, §1 and §4 route the study to `CENSUS_DENOMINATOR_DISPUTED` before any claim tally. The exhibition therefore establishes reachability under agreed §1 inclusion; it does not silently make that inclusion boundary mechanical.

## Unreachable verdicts and blocking clauses

None. All six operative §3 outcomes and all seven §4 study-level classes have a concrete licensed route. The retired `REPRO_AFTER_CHOICE` is not an operative §3 outcome and is therefore not a verdict requiring exhibition.
