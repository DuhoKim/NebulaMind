Direct answer to the `CENSUS_COMPLETE` suspicion: **REACHABLE.** Corpus size does not itself force a non-arithmetic outcome. A real corpus of many papers can file `CENSUS_COMPLETE` if its agreed, nonzero set of included claims all receive exactly one arithmetic-group outcome (`REPRO_WITHIN_STATED_PRECISION` or `REPRO_FAILED`), C6 passes, and no earlier-precedence class applies. The outcome does not require every claim to reproduce successfully: `REPRO_FAILED` is in the arithmetic group. Conversely, one included claim anywhere in the corpus carrying `REPRO_BLOCKED`, `REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT`, `REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED`, or `REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE` makes the §4.2 partial predicate true. With no earlier stop/audit class, `CENSUS_PARTIAL` then files before `CENSUS_COMPLETE`; that single claim therefore prevents complete for that realized corpus. The document does not assert that the pinned corpus necessarily contains such a claim, so it does not make `CENSUS_COMPLETE` unreachable in practice as a declared class.

Every §3 outcome and §4 class is reachable. Therefore there are no UNREACHABLE verdicts and no blocking-clause quotations to report.
