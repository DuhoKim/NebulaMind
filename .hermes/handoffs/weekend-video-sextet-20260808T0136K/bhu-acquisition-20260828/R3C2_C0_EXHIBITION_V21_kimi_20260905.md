- Why that does not make the class unreachable: each of the four forcing outcomes is triggered by a property of the input corpus — an unstated derivation, a named source that is unpinned or non-matching, an input traced to no source, an over-cap or unmachined computation. No clause in §1–§9 requires any included claim to have such a property. - Two consequences worth recording. (1) `CENSUS_COMPLETE` is not a verdict that the corpus reproduces: `REPRO_FAILED` is in the arithmetic group, so a census in which every number fails to reproduce still files `CENSUS_COMPLETE` provided every claim was decidable and the audit passes — the class asserts complete decidability, not success.
