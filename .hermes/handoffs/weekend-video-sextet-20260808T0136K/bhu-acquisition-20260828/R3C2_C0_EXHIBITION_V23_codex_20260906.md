Direct answer to the `CENSUS_COMPLETE` suspicion: **REACHABLE.** It can be filed for a real, nonempty corpus of any size if every included claim lands in the two-member arithmetic group and `C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS`, with no higher-precedence stop class. The concrete two-claim corpus above is sufficient to exhibit the route; adding further claims preserves the route if each also has a sufficient stated recipe and lands in `REPRO_WITHIN_STATED_PRECISION` or `REPRO_FAILED`. But a single included claim anywhere in the corpus carrying `REPRO_BLOCKED`, `REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT`, `REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED`, or `REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE` makes the §4 `CENSUS_PARTIAL` condition true. The explicit filing order places `CENSUS_PARTIAL` before `CENSUS_COMPLETE`, so that single claim forces `CENSUS_PARTIAL`. This makes completeness demanding, not unreachable. Whether the pinned corpus actually satisfies it cannot be inferred from the preregistration alone and is not decided in this C0 exhibition.
