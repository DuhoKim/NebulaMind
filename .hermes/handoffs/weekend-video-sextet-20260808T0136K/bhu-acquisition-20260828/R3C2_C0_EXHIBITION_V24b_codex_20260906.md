A row for a later class requires the earlier conditions to be false. In particular, a missing audit seed accompanying a blocked claim produces AUDIT_FAILED, and an unresolved outcome split accompanying an origin dispute produces OUTCOME_DISPUTED. A stop leaves later controls NOT_RUN; their nonexecution does not generate another filed outcome.

CENSUS_COMPLETE is REACHABLE. The 100-paper witness exhibits a nonempty many-paper corpus whose every included claim reaches the arithmetic group and whose audit passes. Completeness does not require numerical success: replacing any or all of its 4.0 results with 5.0 produces audited REPRO_FAILED outcomes and still permits CENSUS_COMPLETE. Nor does a chosen input prevent completeness under option (c).

A single included BLOCKED, INPUT_ABSENT, NO_DERIVATION_STATED or NOT_EVALUABLE claim anywhere prevents COMPLETE for that particular corpus. With no earlier stop and a passing audit, it forces PARTIAL. If an earlier stop applies, precedence files that class instead.
