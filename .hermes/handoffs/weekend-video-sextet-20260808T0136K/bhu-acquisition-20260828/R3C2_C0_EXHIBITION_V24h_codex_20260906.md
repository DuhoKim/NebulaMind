The dispute witnesses deliberately specify differing seat records: the document makes disagreement itself an input to a stop class. It does not require both incompatible readings to be correct. In particular, C3 explicitly leaves semantic reason-code precedence to independent readers and the auditor. Arithmetic or provenance mistakes that survive a prescribed reconciliation therefore provide concrete dispute inputs without changing the numerical recipe.

**CENSUS_COMPLETE is REACHABLE.** The 100-paper example exhibits a nonempty many-paper corpus with every included claim in the arithmetic group and a passing full audit. The all-FAILED variant also qualifies. A single blocked, absent-input, no-derivation-stated or not-evaluable included claim prevents COMPLETE for that particular corpus. It files PARTIAL when all earlier filing conditions are absent; a control failure, surviving dispute, failed audit or failed seal instead files the applicable earlier class. Corpus size alone supplies no rule requiring a non-arithmetic claim. Whether the actual pinned corpus satisfies completeness remains unestablished by this exhibition.

`REPRO_WITHIN_STATED_PRECISION` and `REPRO_FAILED`**.”

**Definition dependence:** the operative §1 asks whether the number follows “from the paper's own recipe applied to the inputs it states.” Its SEAT-REDACT markers separate the provenance question from the seat-visible reproduction question; they are not an operative HELD marker. §2.4 and §3 consume PRINTED or STANDARD inputs, irrespective of origin. P therefore reproduces with an IMPORTED root, and its separate script-computed rests_on is USES_IMPORTED. That retired token and the historical REPRO_EXACT name are not additional current outcomes. The historical held-definition passages do not override the adopted option (c). This reachability result depends on that settled status-based rule and on §1's own-result inclusion rule; it does not assume a derivation-only interpretation.
