CENSUS_COMPLETE is REACHABLE. The controlling §4 clause is: “every included claim carries exactly one outcome from the arithmetic group of §3, with `C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS`.” Section 3 defines that group as exactly REPRO_WITHIN_STATED_PRECISION and REPRO_FAILED. Fixture M satisfies this over 89 papers and 890 included claims; even its all-REPRO_FAILED variant satisfies it. Chosen, fitted, imported or undeclared provenance does not itself prevent arithmetic reproduction or completeness: provenance is reported separately.

A single blocked, absent-input, no-derivation-stated, or not-evaluable claim prevents CENSUS_COMPLETE for that particular corpus. With controls, agreements, audit and receipts passing, §4 files CENSUS_PARTIAL. If a higher-precedence stop applies, that stop is filed instead. Thus the suspicion correctly identifies a strict completeness requirement, but does not establish unreachability. Whether the actual pinned corpus satisfies it requires the census and cannot be inferred from this exhibition.

The zero-denominator proviso does not override audit precedence. For wholly empty enumerations on both sides, the latter C6 failure condition is false, but the text supplies no numerical convention for a zero-denominator dispute rate; this exhibition does not invent an audit PASS for that case. CENSUS_PARTIAL has independent positive-denominator witnesses, so no §4 class depends on that degenerate route for reachability.
