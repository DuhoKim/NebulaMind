THE SUSPICION — CENSUS_COMPLETE, ANSWERED DIRECTLY: REACHABLE.

What the text forces, quoted:
- §4.1: "CENSUS_COMPLETE — every included claim carries exactly one outcome from the arithmetic group of §3, with C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS. A denominator of zero files CENSUS_PARTIAL with the empty enumeration named; no census is complete over nothing."
- §4.2: "CENSUS_PARTIAL — … at least one included claim carries a non-arithmetic outcome (REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED, REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT, REPRO_BLOCKED, REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE), or the denominator is zero. … INCONCLUSIVE, and it takes precedence over CENSUS_COMPLETE."
- §4 precedence: "… CENSUS_AUDIT_FAILED, CENSUS_PARTIAL, CENSUS_COMPLETE" — file the first whose condition holds.

Routing when a non-arithmetic claim exists anywhere in the corpus: §4.2's condition holds the moment one included claim files REPRO_BLOCKED, REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT, REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED or REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE; the precedence ladder then stops at CENSUS_PARTIAL before ever reaching CENSUS_COMPLETE. So yes — a single blocked, absent-input or no-derivation-stated claim anywhere in the corpus forces CENSUS_PARTIAL (provided no earlier stop class holds). The document itself flags exactly this asymmetry in C0's own rationale: "CENSUS_COMPLETE requires every included claim to carry an arithmetic-group outcome, which a single blocked or absent input in the whole corpus is enough to prevent."

Why that does NOT make CENSUS_COMPLETE unreachable: reachability under C0 is existential — "exhibit a concrete input that produces it". Each non-arithmetic §3 class is contingent on the paper's own state: REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED needs a paper that states no procedure; REPRO_BLOCKED needs a named source outside the manifest or a value mismatch at the cited line; REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT needs an input neither printed nor traced; REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE needs a timeout or missing machinery. No clause routes any claim into those classes unconditionally. The exhibited input (table B, row CENSUS_COMPLETE): papers A and B, denominator 2, every claim arithmetic-group, every control PASS in both seats, enumerations/inputs/outcomes/origins agreed, seed supplied and recorded, auditor's complete enumeration matching, reconstructions complete, every row MATCH, dispute rate 0% → C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS. On that input every earlier §4 condition is false, the precedence ladder falls through its last rung, and CENSUS_COMPLETE is filed with both tallies. A concrete input exists; the verdict CAN OCCUR; CENSUS_COMPLETE is reachable — in principle and in practice, since nothing in the text precludes a corpus whose papers all state their recipes and print or pin every input. Whether the pinned 89-text corpus will actually file it is the census's own empirical question; the design does not pre-decide it. Both prior C0 rounds under the settled wording, and even the V9 seats under the held clause (§10.3), found CENSUS_COMPLETE reachable; this exhibition agrees.
