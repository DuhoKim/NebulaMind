THE CENSUS_COMPLETE SUSPICION — answered directly
=====================================================================
REACHABLE.

Routing. CENSUS_COMPLETE's condition (§4.1) is an existential condition on the corpus: every included claim carries an arithmetic-group outcome, C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS, and the denominator is not zero (a zero denominator files CENSUS_PARTIAL by §4.1's own clause). Each of the four non-arithmetic outcomes is gated on a specific defect that a corpus may simply not contain: REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED requires the paper to state no procedure; REPRO_BLOCKED requires a named source that is not a pinned enumerable text or whose cited line does not machine-match; REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT requires an input neither printed nor traced to any named source; REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE requires the 120-second cap to be exceeded or machinery to be missing. No clause in §2-§4 forces any of those defects onto every corpus.
