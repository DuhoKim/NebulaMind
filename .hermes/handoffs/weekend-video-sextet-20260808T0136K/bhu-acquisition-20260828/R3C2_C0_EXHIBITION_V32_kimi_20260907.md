C. THE SUSPICION — CENSUS_COMPLETE, answered directly: REACHABLE
=====================================================================

Question: in a real corpus of many papers, is there any input under which CENSUS_COMPLETE can actually be filed — or does a single blocked, absent-input, or no-derivation-stated claim anywhere force CENSUS_PARTIAL, making CENSUS_COMPLETE unreachable in practice?

Answer: REACHABLE. There is an input under which it is filed; section B exhibits one.

Routing, shown clause by clause:
1. What the class requires (§4.1): "every included claim carries exactly one outcome from the arithmetic group of §3, with C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS." The arithmetic group (§3) is "exactly REPRO_WITHIN_STATED_PRECISION and REPRO_FAILED."
2. An included claim whose inputs are all PRINTED/STANDARD and whose attempt completes within the cap files one of those two outcomes — and REPRO_FAILED, an unreproduced number, is INSIDE the group. Completeness is not success; it is termination in the arithmetic.
3. The corpus in which every included claim is of that kind, with a nonzero denominator and a passing audit, satisfies no earlier class in the §4 precedence — controls pass everywhere, no split, no denominator, outcome or origin dispute, audit PASS, no non-arithmetic outcome — and therefore files CENSUS_COMPLETE, the last limb. The one-claim corpus of section B is exactly such an input.
4. The pressure the suspicion names is real but it is a property of corpora, not a defect of the text. A single REPRO_BLOCKED, REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT, REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED or REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE claim anywhere does force CENSUS_PARTIAL — §4.2's own words: "INCONCLUSIVE, and it takes precedence over CENSUS_COMPLETE." A zero denominator files CENSUS_PARTIAL too: "no census is complete over nothing."
5. But no clause forces any non-arithmetic claim to exist. The document does not require any corpus to contain an unprinted-and-unobtainable input, an absent input, an underived numeral, or an uncomputable recipe; those are facts about papers, and measuring them is the census. What the design removes from COMPLETE's path: author-set numerals (a grid size, a cutoff) leave the denominator entirely via the exclusion ledger (AUTHOR_SPECIFIED_INPUT, §3); unreproduced claims stay inside the arithmetic group; origin disputes at or below 10% and audit inclusion disputes at or below 10% are reported, not stops.
6. Therefore: CENSUS_COMPLETE is reachable exactly over corpora with a nonzero denominator, zero non-arithmetic outcomes, and a passing audit — as rare in a messy real corpus as clean corpora are, and as common; that is an empirical fact the census exists to discover, not a reachability gap. The class CAN OCCUR; C0 asks nothing more.
