CENSUS_COMPLETE is **REACHABLE**, including for a corpus of many papers: B is an explicit 89-paper, 100-claim witness. The operative requirement is arithmetic evaluability for every included claim, not successful reproduction of every claim and not derivation-only provenance. Chosen, fitted, imported and undeclared PRINTED inputs remain consumable. A failed arithmetic reproduction can therefore be part of a complete census.

A single included BLOCKED, INPUT_ABSENT, NO_DERIVATION_STATED or persisting NOT_EVALUABLE outcome does prevent COMPLETE for that particular corpus state. With a passing audit and no earlier stop, §4 files PARTIAL; if audit fails, AUDIT_FAILED takes precedence; if an earlier stop applies, that earlier class wins and later controls are NOT_RUN. Thus “a single such claim always forces PARTIAL” needs the no-earlier-stop qualification. Nothing in the operative text requires every possible many-paper corpus to contain such a claim. Whether the actual pinned corpus does contain one cannot be established from this pre-registration alone and was not assumed here.
