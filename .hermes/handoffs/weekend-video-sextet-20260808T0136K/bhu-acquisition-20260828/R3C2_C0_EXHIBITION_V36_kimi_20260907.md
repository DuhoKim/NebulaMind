THE SUSPICION — `CENSUS_COMPLETE`: REACHABLE.

The forcing half of the suspicion is TRUE as a matter of precedence. §4.2 files
`CENSUS_PARTIAL` when "at least one included claim carries a non-arithmetic outcome
(`REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED`, `REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT`, `REPRO_BLOCKED`, `REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE`),
or the denominator is zero", and §4's filing order puts `CENSUS_PARTIAL` after
`CENSUS_AUDIT_FAILED` and immediately before `CENSUS_COMPLETE`, with the words "INCONCLUSIVE,
and it takes precedence over `CENSUS_COMPLETE`." So yes: a single blocked, absent-input,
no-derivation-stated, or not-evaluable claim anywhere in the corpus forces `CENSUS_PARTIAL`,
and over a real 89-text corpus at least one such claim is likely — C0's own rationale (§5)
says exactly this: "`CENSUS_COMPLETE` requires every included claim to carry an
arithmetic-group outcome, which a single blocked or absent input in the whole corpus is enough
to prevent."

But prevention-in-expectation is not unreachability. `CENSUS_COMPLETE`'s condition is a
satisfiable universal, not a contradiction: a corpus in which every included claim is
self-contained — every input PRINTED or STANDARD, every recipe stated, every attempt finishing
