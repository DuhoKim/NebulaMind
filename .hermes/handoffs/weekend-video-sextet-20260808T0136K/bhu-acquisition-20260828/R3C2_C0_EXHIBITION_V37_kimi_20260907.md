## C. The named suspicion: is `CENSUS_COMPLETE` reachable in practice?

**Answer: REACHABLE.**

The suspicion is correct about the routing and wrong about the conclusion. The routing it names
is real and is stated twice in the operative text: §4.2 files `CENSUS_PARTIAL` when "at least
one included claim carries a non-arithmetic outcome (`REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED`,
`REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT`, `REPRO_BLOCKED`, `REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE`), or the denominator is zero", and
gives it precedence — "INCONCLUSIVE, and it takes precedence over `CENSUS_COMPLETE`". So yes: a
single blocked, absent-input, no-derivation-stated, or not-evaluable claim anywhere in the
corpus forces `CENSUS_PARTIAL`, and no tally can file both.

But C0's question is existential: is there ANY input under which `CENSUS_COMPLETE` can be filed?
The exhibited input in §B row 1 is such an input, and nothing in the text forbids it:

1. `CENSUS_COMPLETE`'s predicate (§4.1) is a condition on the corpus, not a self-contradiction:
   "every included claim carries exactly one outcome from the arithmetic group of §3, with
   `C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS`". A corpus in which every paper states a procedure for every claim it
   prints, states or traceably pins every input, and whose recipes are mechanically evaluable in
   120 seconds satisfies it. Whether the pinned corpus IS such a corpus is exactly what the
   census exists to measure — the class being hard to reach on a real corpus is the study's
   finding, not a design defect.
2. The class is non-vacuous by construction: §4.1's own clause "A denominator of zero files
   `CENSUS_PARTIAL` with the empty enumeration named; no census is complete over nothing" closes
   the degenerate path, so the exhibited input must (and does) carry a positive denominator.
3. The §1 exclusion ledger keeps the denominator honest without smuggling: numerals that could
   never reproduce by construction (equation numbers, dates, `AUTHOR_SPECIFIED_INPUT`, etc.) are
   excluded at §1 "by definition and not by taste", before the denominator — they never enter
   the set that could force `CENSUS_PARTIAL`.
4. The full routing: §1 inclusion (two seats agree) → §2 attempts (every claim's inputs
   `PRINTED`/`STANDARD`; every recipe completes in the cap) → §3 arithmetic group for every
   claim → C1/C2/C3 PASS both seats → no denominator, outcome, or origin dispute → C6
   `C6_AUDIT_SAMPLE=PASS` → §4 precedence: no stop class holds, §4.2's condition absent →
   `CENSUS_COMPLETE`, with the `rests_on` tally beside the reproduction tally (one pass, two
   tallies).

Reachability in principle is therefore exhibited. Frequency in practice is the measurement the
census makes; C0 judges only that the class CAN occur, and it can.
