Direct answer to the `CENSUS_COMPLETE` suspicion: **REACHABLE**, but only for an input corpus in which every included claim receives an arithmetic-group outcome. S-COMPLETE above is such an input even if a real corpus contains many papers: corpus size alone creates no non-arithmetic outcome. Conversely, one claim anywhere in the included denominator filed as `REPRO_BLOCKED`, `REPRO_INPUT_ABSENT`, `REPRO_NO_DERIVATION_STATED`, or `REPRO_NOT_EVALUABLE` after two attempts triggers §4.2. That routes the study to `CENSUS_PARTIAL`, which expressly “takes precedence over `CENSUS_COMPLETE`.” Thus a single such claim prevents a complete filing for that corpus, but does not make `CENSUS_COMPLETE` existentially unreachable. Whether the actual corpus permits completion cannot be learned from this preregistration alone and was not assessed here.

The §4 filing precedence stated directly in the operative class text resolves the only named overlap between complete and partial: partial takes precedence. Other stop/void classes are individually reachable through the independent inputs above; this exhibition does not invent an unstated global precedence among audit failure, control failure/split, denominator dispute, and origin dispute.
