The specifically requested “no printed size–mass relation and no printed mass bound” case does have a predicate-level consequence also described by class 4: its completion-free allowed masses have no positive lower bound. It nevertheless **files only class 3**. This is separation by limb, not separation of the underlying physical predicates.
