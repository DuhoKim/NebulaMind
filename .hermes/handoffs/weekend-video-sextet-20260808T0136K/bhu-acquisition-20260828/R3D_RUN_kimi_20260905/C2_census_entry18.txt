  236| This condition guarantees that the matter described by the stress-energy
  237| tensor satisfying it does not prevent monotonic decrease of expansion of
  238| congruences of timelike geodesics. In other words, it means that in a
  239| gravitational collapse when the gravity becomes dominant it leads to an
  240| unlimited contraction.
  241|       One way to avoid singularity during a gravitational collapse was pro-
  242| posed by Gliner [2,14] who suggested that at very high densities such that
  243| all kinds of particles lose their identity matter undergoes a transition into a
  244| vacuum like state described by the stress-energy tensor (2) and the de Sit-
  245| ter geometry (1). The very important feature of the de Sitter geometry
  246| is the divergence of the geodesic congruences. In this case gravity acts in
  247| such a way that the trajectories of the freely moving (along geodesics) test
  248| particles behave as if they were repulsed from the center [15]. With this
  249| in mind, Gliner suggested that if a vacuum-like physical state could be
  250| achieved during a collapse then further contraction would be stopped and
  251| such a vacuum-like state could be a final state in a gravitational collapse
  252| instead of a singularity.
