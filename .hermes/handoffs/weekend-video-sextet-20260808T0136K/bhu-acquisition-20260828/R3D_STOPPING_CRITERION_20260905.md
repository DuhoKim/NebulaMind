## The criterion, declared now

**A finding is SUBSTANTIVE if applying it would change at least one of:**

1. **which outcome class a given run files**;
2. **whether a control can be executed, or passed, by a conforming seat**;
3. **whether any declared outcome, class or condition is reachable**;
4. **what the study can conclude** — the falsifier's reach, or the meaning of a verdict;
5. **an arithmetic value, interval or comparison** the study tests against.

**Everything else is COSMETIC:** wording, tidying, stale phrasing, formatting, redundancy, explanatory prose,
history, and cross-references that mislead no procedure.

## What happens to each

- **SUBSTANTIVE** → repaired, new version, its own C0 and its own two-seat gate. As tonight.
- **COSMETIC** → **ANSWERED in the reconciliation and recorded as knowingly NOT repaired, with the reason.** It
  does **not** produce a new version. The finding is preserved; the decision to decline it is visible.

## The stopping point

**The next version on which neither seat raises a SUBSTANTIVE finding is FINAL for design purposes**, and I will
name it as final in the document and in the state file, with its hash. **After that, no further repair is made on
a cosmetic finding**, and any later substantive finding — from a seat, from Duho, or from me — reopens the design
explicitly rather than silently.

## Why declare it before the gate rather than after

**Applied after the findings are known, this rule would be tunable**: I could classify whatever came back as
cosmetic and declare victory, or as substantive and keep going, and either would be defensible after the fact.
**Declared before, it binds me to a test I wrote without knowing what it would judge.** That is the same discipline
this lane requires of every preregistration it audits, applied to the lane's own stopping decision.

## What a clear does and does not mean — restated because it does not change

**The gate certifies the DESIGN.** It does not start the study. **R3D does not run without Duho's word — no limb,
no seat, no derivation, no census.** A final version is a finished *design*, waiting on him.
