# RQ-D codex result — Easson obstruction map

I independently read the pinned Easson source and the seven pinned interior sources. I did not consult the other seat. This is a theory-domain map only; I make no tier change.

## Controlling clauses, verified from Easson

- **Proposition 1** (`2606.25023_clean.txt:185-190, 223-250`): the natural trapped slicing of a one-function static spherical parent has spatial geometry `R × S^2`, hence is Kantowski–Sachs and not exact FRW. This proposition does not require a junction, asymptotic flatness, or energy condition.
- **Proposition 2** (`2606.25023_clean.txt:740-778`): a **no-shell, `k=+1` closed FRW**, nondegenerately and comovingly matched to a static asymptotically flat finite-ADM parent cannot expand to arbitrarily large scale factor. This is boundedness, not nonexistence: a finite expanding/recollapsing branch or tuned static endpoint survives.
- **Theorem 1, flat/open limb** (`2606.25023_clean.txt:637-665, 842-901`): a non-static, curvature-regular `k=0` or `k=-1` FRW spacetime with regular affine ends cannot be both null complete and ANEC-consistent. All of those predicates matter.
- **Named escapes** (`2606.25023_clean.txt:903-918, 989-994`): shell stress, modified asymptotics, a non-FRW/non-comoving daughter, or an added smooth bulk sector. For flat/open models, giving up curvature regularity, completeness, ANEC, FRW, or the flat/open class also exits the conjunctive no-go.

## Meta-count and payoff

- **KILLS: 0/7.** No published construction was shown to meet every hypothesis of an Easson obstruction while also asserting the exact prohibited conclusion.
- **RESTRICTS: 3/7 — entries 18, 25, 26.** Proposition 1 blocks an exact-FRW reading of Dymnikova's static trapped region; Theorem 1 blocks a future claim that the Gaztañaga flat cloud simultaneously has regular curvature, regular affine ends, null completeness, and ANEC. It does not put those papers in Proposition 2's `k=+1` class.
- **SPARES: 4/7 — entries 11, 19, 20, 21.** Each exhibits a source-owned named escape: torsion/additional bulk stress; quantum nucleation plus strings/radiation; non-FRW Kantowski–Sachs plus phantom stress and de Sitter far asymptotic; or an anisotropic horizon shell.

The payoff is therefore a clean domain boundary, not mass retirement: Easson directly rules out the minimal route from a static trapped band to an exact FRW daughter and sharply constrains minimal complete flat/open or unbounded closed daughters, while these published interiors mostly occupy nonminimal escape sectors. No tier has been changed.
