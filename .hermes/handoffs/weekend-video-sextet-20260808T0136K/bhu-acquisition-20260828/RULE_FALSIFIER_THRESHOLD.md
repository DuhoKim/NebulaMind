# Scheme rule: does a falsifier require an author-stated rejection threshold?

Written 2026-08-29 10:25 KST, at Blanc's instruction, to settle the A6 `FIRES` split by a rule
rather than by a third vote. Blanc's reasoning for refusing a third seat is correct and worth
recording: agy and codex **cite the same DESI numbers**. This was never an evidential
disagreement — they disagree about what a test needs in order to fire. A third vote is another
opinion on a definition.

## The question

Entry 25 says a measured `w ≠ −1` "challenges" the model. DESI DR2 reports 3.1σ for evolving dark
energy. The paper states **no rejection threshold** — no σ level, no odds, no condition under
which the author would give the model up.

- **agy:** FIRES: YES. The contradiction is measured; that is enough.
- **codex:** FIRES: UNDETERMINED. The paper supplies no rejection rule, and the 3σ bar my script
  used was **invented by the script, not stated by the source**.

## The rule

> **1. FALSIFIABILITY is a property of the theory's content, not of its author's candour.**
> A directional contradiction is sufficient. If a model's own equations forbid an observable
> outcome, it is falsifiable by that outcome — whether or not the author ever wrote down a
> threshold. **An author-stated threshold is NOT required to tier a claim CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER.**
>
> **2. FIRING requires a threshold, and if the author did not supply one, WE supply it and OWN it.**
> This programme may not report a falsifier as *having fired* against a bar the source never
> stated, while implying the source stated it. Any firing verdict absent an author threshold must
> name the bar, name who chose it, and record that it is ours.
>
> **2a. PROCEDURE — case by case, recorded each time.** *(Duho, 2026-08-29 10:35, via Blanc.)*
> **No blanket σ.** Every entry names **its own** bar, **who chose it**, and **why that bar suits
> that claim**. His reasoning: *a sharp geometric prediction and a vague trend should not share
> one threshold.*
>
> **"No bar chosen" is no longer an acceptable resting state.** It was honest, but it meant
> nothing in the corpus could ever be declared failed — which is not what the programme is for.

## Why both halves are necessary

**Half 1 exists because the alternative rewards evasiveness.** If an author-stated threshold were
*required* for falsifiability, any theorist could immunise a theory by declining to say what would
refute it. That makes falsifiability a property of the writing rather than of the physics, and it
gets the Popperian point backwards — a theory that forbids an outcome forbids it regardless of
whether its author admits as much.

**Half 2 exists because of everything last night established.** The 3σ bar in `a6` was invented by
my script and then reported as though the test had a threshold. That is precisely the generating
defect of the whole night — *a claim asserting more than its source supports* — occurring one
level up, in the scheme rather than in a regex. Codex caught it, and its instinct was right even
though `UNDETERMINED` is the wrong permanent label.

**So each seat had half of it.** agy is right that the contradiction is real and does not wait on
the author's permission. Codex is right that our bar was ours and was being passed off as the
test's. Neither is right alone, which is why a third vote would not have settled it.

## Applied to entry 25

**Tier: unchanged, QUALITATIVE-DIRECTIONAL.** This rule does not reopen it, because entry 25 was
refused promotion for a *different* reason — the qualifier inside its own falsification sentence,
*"not **solely** caused by"* r_S, which licenses an additional component that absorbs any
deviation. That is an auxiliary-hypothesis problem, not a threshold problem. **No tier change, so
no stop.**

**FIRES: a named bar, then the structural reason it does not matter.**

### The bar for entry 25 — set, owned, justified

> **Bar:** `w ≠ −1` at **≥3σ in a CMB-independent and a CMB-dependent dataset combination
> simultaneously.**
> **Chosen by:** Tori, 2026-08-29 10:40 KST. **This is ours. The paper states no threshold.**
> **Why this bar for this claim:** the prediction is *exact and parameter-free* — Λ = 3/r_S² with
> r_S the fixed exterior Schwarzschild radius forces `w = −1` identically, with nothing to absorb
> a small deviation. A sharp prediction of that kind does not need a 5σ discovery bar; 3σ is
> enough to contradict it. But DESI's significance runs **1.7σ–4.2σ across dataset combinations**,
> so a single combination reaching 3σ could be one sample's systematics. Requiring **two
> independent combinations** is what the spread justifies, and is why this bar is not the same
> one a vague trend would get.

**Status against that bar, from the pinned DESI DR2 paper:**

| combination | significance | ≥3σ? |
|---|---|---|
| DESI + CMB (no SNe) | **3.1σ** | yes |
| DESI + SNe (no CMB) | up to **3.3σ** | yes |
| weakest quoted combination | 1.7σ | no |

**The bar is MET.** Two independent combinations, one CMB-free and one SNe-free, both ≥3σ.

### And the auxiliary defeats it anyway — which is the useful part

A measured `w ≠ −1` refutes only the claim that r_S is the **whole** cause of acceleration,
because the paper's own falsification sentence says acceleration would then be *"not **solely**
caused by"* r_S. The BHU metric survives with an added component.

**So entry 25 survives a bar it actually meets.** That is a much stronger statement than "no bar
was chosen": the claim is not protected by our failure to set a threshold, nor by the data being
weak. It is protected **structurally**, by an escape clause the author wrote into the same
sentence as the falsifier. Under the rule, the honest entry is:

> Measured: DESI DR2, 3.1σ for evolving dark energy (DESI+CMB alone), pinned at
> `2503.14738_clean.txt`. Range across dataset combinations: 1.7σ–4.2σ.
> Author threshold: **none stated**.
> Programme bar: **≥3σ in two independent combinations — set by Tori, ours, justified above.**
> Bar status: **MET** (3.1σ DESI+CMB, 3.3σ DESI+SNe).
> **FIRES: NO — defeated by the auxiliary, not by the evidence.** The contradiction is real,
> measured, and clears our bar. It refutes only "r_S is the whole cause". The metric survives.

## What this rule changes elsewhere

It is now the criterion for every future `FIRES` question in this corpus. The immediate
consequence is that **`UNDETERMINED` is no longer an acceptable output** where the evidence is
known and only a bar is missing. The correct output names the measurement, records that no bar
exists, and says who would have to set one.
