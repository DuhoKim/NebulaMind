**Entry 1 (Pathria 1972)**
Token: `CHALLENGE_CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER`
Justification: Pathria explicitly derives numerical thresholds for the universe's density, cosmological constant, and radius by plugging in observational values for the Hubble parameter ($H_0$) and deceleration parameter ($q_0$). These inequalities provide a calibrated, measurable falsifier for the proposed model.
Decisive quote: "Some of the immediate consequences of this picture may be expressed in terms of inequalities... But using H0 = 75 km s-1 (Mpc-1) and q0 = 1 as 'representative values' ... 1.5 x 10^-29 g cm^-3 < \rho_0 \le 2.3 x 10^-29 g cm^-3" (p. 299)

**Entry 2 (Good 1972)**
Token: `CONFIRM_CONSISTENCY_ONLY`
Justification: This one-page letter to the editor proposes a purely speculative, philosophical idea of infinitely nested black and white holes ("Chinese universes"). It contains no mathematical construction, no calibrated numbers, and no distinguishing statistics.
Decisive quote: "I shall argue here that the whole of our observable universe is probably a black hole... Although the theory seems grandiose, as far as I can see it is the only possible consistent interpretation of the steady-state concept..." (p. 15)

**Entry 3 (Stuckey 1994)**
Token: `CONFIRM_CONSISTENCY_ONLY`
Justification: The paper is a pedagogical exercise showing how to join Friedmann dust with Schwarzschild vacuum. The author acknowledges the result is an illustration of methodology rather than an astrophysically predictive model, providing no new calibrated falsifiers or owned obstruction theorems.
Decisive quote: "While this result is not necessarily of astrophysical importance, the methodology has been used extensively by cosmologists and astrophysicists." (p. 788)

**Entry 4 (Knutsen 2009)**
Token: `CHALLENGE_THEORETICAL-OBSTRUCTION`
Justification: Knutsen critically re-examines Pathria's model and explicitly proves an impossibility result himself. He mathematically demonstrates that matching the FLRW metric with the exterior vacuum forces the fluid sphere to be static, making it structurally impossible to describe our expanding universe.
Decisive quote: "Hence, this choice of boundary value for the comoving radial coordinate just means that the fluid sphere is static, and the surface of the fluid sphere merges with its event horizon. Thus this particular model cannot describe our expanding universe." (p. 277)
