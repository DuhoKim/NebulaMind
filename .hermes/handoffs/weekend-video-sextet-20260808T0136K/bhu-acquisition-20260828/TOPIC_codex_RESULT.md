# BHU research-topic candidates — CODEX seat (theory/derivation)

**Single strongest: Candidate 1 — derive the Einstein–Cartan bounce transfer matrix and its CMB/PBH observable.** It attacks a missing equation rather than an anomalous datum, has a clean null/no-go outcome if the system is not predictive, and can produce several falsifiable numbers from parameters fixed away from the observables being forecast.

## Rank 1 — The Einstein–Cartan bounce as a calculable primordial transfer function

1. **The question.** Starting from the minimally coupled Dirac–Einstein–Cartan action, can one derive the complete gauge-invariant scalar perturbation system through the torsion bounce and thereby establish a unique parent-to-daughter transfer matrix—and hence a calculable scalar spectrum, isocurvature fraction, and PBH abundance—or prove that the published BHU background is not closed enough to predict them?

2. **Why it is a frontier.** Entries 8–12 supply a homogeneous torsion-bounce background but no quadratic perturbation action; the corpus audit found that adiabatic conservation was being assumed rather than derived, while spin/number isocurvature permits \(\delta p_{\rm nad}\neq0\), the PRD-85 bounce is cusp-like, and the particle-production coefficient \(\beta\) is free. This is the live missing dynamical link in the most developed BHU mechanism: whether perturbations traverse the black-hole interior/bounce finitely and predictively is explicitly unresolved, not a cataloguing question.

3. **Tractability.** A small theory team can (i) integrate out the algebraic torsion in the Dirac–Einstein–Cartan action, retaining the axial-current four-fermion operator; (ii) construct the second-order scalar action with metric, density, velocity, and spin/number perturbations; (iii) count constraints and propagating modes before choosing any closure; (iv) regularize or resolve the published cusp and compute the long-wavelength and numerical transfer matrices; and (v) pass the resulting primordial covariance to CLASS/CAMB and, where enhanced, a standard Press–Schechter/peaks PBH calculation. Symbolic tensor algebra plus a one-dimensional stiff ODE boundary-value solver is sufficient; no new data acquisition is required. Background parameters are taken from the published torsion solution and ordinary late-time/high-\(\ell\) cosmology, not from low-\(\ell\) anomalies or PBH bounds.

4. **Result-shape.** The primary result is a matrix \(T_{ij}(k)\) with a declared infrared limit and numerical stability domain. It yields pre-registered numbers such as \(n_s\), running, tensor-to-scalar ratio, the correlated isocurvature fraction \(\beta_{\rm iso}\), and any feature scale/amplitude, followed by Planck/CMB-S4 and asteroid-PBH exclusion tests. An equally publishable negative is a closure theorem: the quadratic action has an unfixed entropy/spin mode or singular matching, so no unique \(T_{ij}\) follows from the claimed model; state the minimum extra operator/state needed to restore predictivity.

5. **Non-circularity + risk.** Derive the action, degrees of freedom, vacuum prescription, and transfer matrix before comparison with low-\(\ell\), isocurvature, or PBH data; calibrate only on independently measured background/high-\(\ell\) quantities. The main risk is that a fermion-fluid coarse graining or bounce regulator introduces irreducible microphysical freedom. That is not a failed project if expressed as a proved rank deficiency/non-uniqueness result, but it may prevent a single observational forecast.

## Rank 2 — An action-selected stochastic completion of the 60° causal cutoff

1. **The question.** Does the minimal local quadratic theory of scalar perturbations on a finite Gaztañaga causal patch, with its boundary state fixed by covariance, positivity, and Hadamard/adiabatic short-distance conditions, select a unique renormalized infrared covariance and therefore a falsifiable joint distribution for \(S_{1/2}\) and \(C_{2\ldots10}\); or can one prove that every such completion retains at least one measurable IR normalization parameter?

2. **Why it is a frontier.** Entries 23–27 genuinely fix the cutoff scale near \(60^\circ\) (\(\ell\sim3\)) but their \(\Phi\) condition is an integrated gravitational flux, not a Dirichlet condition on the perturbation field. The completed audit established that compact support or a mode basis alone leaves the covariance/occupations free, and that maximum entropy has no maximizer; plausible completions give \(S_{1/2}\sim6{,}000\)–\(22{,}000\,\mu{\rm K}^4\), versus Planck \(\sim1{,}150\) and \(\Lambda\)CDM \(\sim34{,}900\). The open problem is therefore sharply narrowed: supply an actual boundary action and quantum state, not another window ansatz.

3. **Tractability.** Write the most general rotationally invariant local quadratic bulk-plus-boundary action through a fixed derivative order on the causal ball; impose self-adjointness, reflection positivity, the Hadamard UV form, and matching to the measured high-\(\ell\) \(A_s,n_s\). Classify self-adjoint extensions/boundary counterterms analytically, compute their mode covariance, and propagate each admissible class through a modified CLASS/CAMB likelihood-free Monte Carlo. The calculation is finite-dimensional after the derivative truncation and can be performed by a small team using spectral Bessel methods and existing Boltzmann code.

4. **Result-shape.** Best case: a normalized covariance with no low-energy free parameter and a predeclared ensemble prediction—median and 5–95% interval—for \(S_{1/2}\), \(C_2\), \(C_3\), and their covariance, tested without refitting. More conservatively, publish a no-predictivity theorem giving the exact number of surviving boundary/state parameters and a profile likelihood envelope; a decisive target is whether all admissible natural completions have \(P(S_{1/2}\le1{,}200\,\mu{\rm K}^4)<1\%\), which would show that the scale prediction cannot explain the amplitude anomaly even after principled completion.

5. **Non-circularity + risk.** The operator content and state-selection axioms are fixed from locality, unitarity/positivity, UV behavior, and high-\(\ell\) normalization; Planck large-angle data are held out until the prediction is frozen. The chief risk is the likely theorem that a relevant boundary mass or zero-mode occupation survives every stated principle. That would block calibration, but it is still a defensible result if it proves exactly which new datum/physical postulate is required; simply choosing the boundary coefficient to match \(S_{1/2}\) would be circular and is excluded.

## Rank 3 — Re-derive the Einstein–Cartan minimum-black-hole mass and turn it into a PBH-window falsifier

1. **The question.** Do the field equations and stated Cartan density in entry 51 mathematically imply any universal minimum four-dimensional black-hole mass, and if so what is its value and dependence on fermion species/spin state; if not, can one prove that the printed \(\sim10^{16}\,\mathrm{kg}\) floor requires an unstated length, particle number, or collapse criterion?

2. **Why it is a frontier.** Entry 51 is one of only two live calibrated falsifiers and its claimed floor lies inside the still-open asteroid-mass PBH window, yet six attempted dimensional/physical routes from its stated density miss the printed mass by 3.1–4.1 decades and the connecting derivation is absent. The unresolved issue is stronger and more useful than checking whether a new constraint crossed the bar: determine whether torsion theory owns a mass floor at all and replace an unreproduced assertion with a covariant derivation or a scoped no-go.

3. **Tractability.** Starting from the Hehl–Datta/Einstein–Cartan equations, solve relativistic collapse or equilibrium for a spin fluid/Dirac wave packet using the stated density scale, with explicit definitions of mass (Misner–Sharp/ADM), horizon formation, particle number, equation of state, and spin polarization. First derive scaling bounds analytically; then shoot the spherical ODE/PDE system over fermion mass, degeneracy, and polarization. Compare the resulting mass threshold only afterward with current femtolensing, neutron-star capture, evaporation, and dynamical PBH bounds. This is a bounded analytic-plus-numerical project; acquiring the publisher version of entry 51 remains due diligence but is not the scientific calculation.

4. **Result-shape.** Produce either (a) \(M_{\min}=M_{\min}(m_f,g,s,\mathrm{EOS})\) with uncertainty/sensitivity bands and a clearly defined detection below which the model is falsified, or (b) a theorem/counterexample showing that fixed Cartan density alone supplies no universal mass floor because configurations with different particle number or radius cross it. A particularly sharp deliverable is a corrected order-of-magnitude bar with all assumptions explicit, then a forecast of what PBH mass measurement or exclusion range would actually test it.

5. **Non-circularity + risk.** The threshold is derived solely from the action, matter content, and a predeclared horizon/stability criterion; PBH observations do not set any coefficient. The main risk is that the answer is matter-model dependent rather than universal, weakening the advertised falsifier. The project remains publishable if it maps that dependence and proves why no single \(10^{16}\,\mathrm{kg}\) bar follows without extra microphysics; it should not promote a collider production exclusion into a four-dimensional mass measurement.
