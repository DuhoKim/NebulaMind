## 1. What the sweep concluded, and at what confidence

**Fifteen corpus entries examined. Not one tier changed.**

Twelve were hand-picked, three were drawn at random as a selection-bias control (seeded from a
git sha fixed before the draw). Six were put through adversarial gates — two fresh-context seats
each, briefed to break the finding.

**Two promotion candidates were proposed and both were refused by both seats:**

- **Entry 25** (Gaztañaga, *BHU Part I*). Its conclusion contains a real falsification sentence:
  *"The BHU can also be challenged by a measurement a[t] the DE equation of state ω ≠ −1."*
  Refused — the qualifier is **inside the same sentence**: acceleration is *"not **solely** caused
  by"* r_S, which licenses an extra component that absorbs any deviation.
- **Entry 23** (Gaztañaga, *The size of our causal Universe*). Predicts a CMB correlation cutoff
  at **θ ≃ 60 ± 3 deg**. Refused — the ±3 is read **off** the observed CMB curve and run
  *backwards* to infer Ω_Λ. The forward derivation is real; the **calibration** is not.

**The opposite-error hypothesis was tested and refuted, not merely unfound.** The lane went
looking specifically for entries tiered *too weak* — a CONSISTENCY-ONLY paper concealing a number
and threshold. Six such candidates were examined across three author lines (Gaztañaga, Popławski,
Smoller–Temple) and three frameworks. In every case the number turned out to be borrowed from the
data it was checked against, placed beyond observability, or carrying a free parameter.

**Confidence: the tier verdicts are sound; the instrument that corroborated them was not.**
Mid-night, two seats condemned the check battery — `HARNESS_UNSOUND`, `FALSE_PASS_POSSIBLE: YES`.
One check was **false on its own input while printing PASS**. Every tier conclusion rests on
**quoted source text** that the seats read independently, and six on gates that attacked the
reading directly. **None rests on the harness.** That distinction is the confidence statement.
