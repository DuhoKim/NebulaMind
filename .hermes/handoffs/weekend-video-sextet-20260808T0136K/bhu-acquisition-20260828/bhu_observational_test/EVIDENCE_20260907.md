### **COMPETING EXPLANATION**

GR black-hole ringdowns have discrete quasinormal modes; Kerr spectroscopy fits modes and tests the no-hair relations while accounting for source spin and emitted mode energy. [Berti, Cardoso & Will, *PRD* 73, 064030 (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.73.064030) explicitly treats multimode measurement, resolvability, spin and mode-energy uncertainties. Roupas's published §4 itself discusses ordinary exterior light-ring modes concealing interior differences. Hence a measured ordinary early ringdown need not distinguish his construction; a generic ringdown detection is not evidence specifically for a de Sitter core.
