## 2. The result that bears on the black-hole-universe question
At the bounce the criterion defines, the ratio of the torsion correction to the ordinary energy density is

    |ε̃|/ε at bounce = κα n₀²/(κα n₀² − Σ²)  ≥ 1  whenever the bounce exists, for ANY α > 0, diverging as Σ² → κα n₀².

So **the bounce always sits where the spin correction is at least the whole ordinary energy density** — an order of magnitude
past the 0.1 small-correction threshold K3 step 3 declared, in both rows and across the entire α interval. Our own K3 result
(the four-fermion term is a 2/3 correction at the Dirac row's turning point; free-gas closure controlled only below ~0.32 T_cr)
therefore applies a fortiori: the regime that produces the bounce is the regime where the closure supplying its strength is not
under control. That is a statement about the mechanism, not about anyone's arithmetic.

## 3. What this does and does not address
It addresses: the Weyssenhoff spin-fluid Einstein–Cartan bounce in Bianchi I, with the assumptions listed in the
pre-registration §1, and the published production prescriptions of GRG 53, 18 as printed.
It does not address: the Kantowski–Sachs system the source actually solves; whether our universe is inside a black hole; any
black-hole-universe model not built on this mechanism; or what happens if a microscopically derived production law with its
energy transfer is supplied — that is the obvious next study, and this result names exactly what it would have to supply.
**Nothing here proves or disproves black-hole-universe cosmology in general, and no such claim is made.**
