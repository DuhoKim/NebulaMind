### S1 — published KS equations, cross-checked with arXiv v2

S1 uses Kantowski–Sachs, not Bianchi I. Eq. (18) defines a directional bounce; adjacent text extends the definition to X and a and distinguishes the three bounce times. Eq. (32) is the author's singularity-avoidance inequality, not a common turning-point equation.

Definitions: X(t), Y(t) directional scale factors; a volume scale factor; H mean Hubble parameter; dot=d/dt; c=1, κ=8πG. ε and p are thermodynamic density and pressure; n_f fermion number density; α=κ(ℏc)²/32. Tildes denote effective totals. u is four-velocity, θ expansion, σ² shear, ω² rotation, w four-acceleration; semicolon means Levi-Civita derivative. Comoving KS has zero rotation and acceleration. The s in dθ/ds is the worldline parameter, distinct from entropy density s elsewhere.

\[
-R_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu=-\kappa(\tilde\epsilon+3\tilde p)/2,\qquad
-\kappa(\tilde\epsilon+3\tilde p)/2>2\sigma^2.
\]
The shear-free relativistic specialization sets σ²=0 in (32); it is not separately numbered or solved as a FLRW collapse in S1.
