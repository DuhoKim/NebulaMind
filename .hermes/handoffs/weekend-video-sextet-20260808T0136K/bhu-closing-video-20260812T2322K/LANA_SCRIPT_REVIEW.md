  they spin are called pulsars, and their mass can be measured." Card 01: replace "gives pulsars a
  number to check" with "gives us a number to check on collapsed stars" (the term arrives properly
  in card 03). Card 05 may then use "pulsars" freely.
- **R2 (card 04): earn "solar masses" at first use.** "…about one and a half times the mass of our
  Sun — one point five solar masses." Subsequent uses unchanged.
- **R3 (card 02): delete "torsion" from narration** ("Another says the collapse rebounds — a bounce —
  instead of crushing to a point"; the diagram tile may keep the word). "programmes" → "proposals"
  (avoids the TV-programme misread on YouTube). "physical constants" → append "— the fixed numbers
  built into physics."
- **R4 (card 05): one framing sentence before the numbers, plain gloss of credibility.** Prepend:
  "Weighing a star thousands of light-years away is hard, so every measurement comes with an
  uncertainty range." And after the two intervals: "At the everyday confidence level, the second
  measurement clears two Suns; demand near-certainty, and it does not quite." Exact figures stay in
  narration once each and on-screen as drafted — the nuance is mandatory; the gloss makes it
  survivable.
- **R5 (card 06): replace the word "disjunction"** with "two possible readings, joined by an 'or'."
  The card's content is already the plain explanation; only the term is unearned.
- **R6 (card 09): replace the packet sentence in narration** with: "even a real lopsidedness in
  galaxy spins would not point to a black-hole origin, because more than one kind of universe could
  produce it." The exact packet phrase may remain on-screen if wanted; spoken, it is a wall.
- **R7 (card 10): replace "neither a small result nor a finite-precision null can be scored"** with
  its meaning: "find a small effect, and the idea neither wins nor loses; find nothing, and it still
  does not lose — with no predicted size, no outcome settles it." Keep "The hunt had a source. It did
  not have a target." — that line is doing exactly what this audience needs.
- **R8 (card 08): de-jargon three terms.** "pre-data forecast" → "a prediction made before the data —
  this one came after"; "rotating-frame mechanics" → "equations for spinning systems"; "acceptance
  range" → "a range that would count as a hit or a miss."
- **R9 (card 11): same treatment.** "numerical acceptance region" → "a pass-or-fail range";
  "signature unique to birth behind a parent horizon" → "a fingerprint only a black-hole birth would
  leave — something ordinary rotation or a bounce cannot mimic."
- **R10 (card 01): three small de-jargons.** "not a NebulaMind research frontier" → "a personal
