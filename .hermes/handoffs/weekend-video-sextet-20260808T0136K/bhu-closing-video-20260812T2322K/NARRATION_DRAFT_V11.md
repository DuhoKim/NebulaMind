## Card 04 — assertion heading

**One cosmological-natural-selection chain puts a low ceiling on neutron-star mass**

One proposal — called cosmological natural selection — says universes have children: every black hole buds off a new universe with slightly different physics. Over many generations, that would tune physics toward making black holes. In the Brown–Lee–Rho chain our record surveyed, the Brown–Bethe maximum neutron-star mass is about one and a half times the mass of our Sun—one point five solar masses. Their paper says a neutron star at approximately two solar masses or above would put that chain in ‘serious doubt or simply falsify’ it. That's a real test — a number that could be proven wrong.

Source: C04, C05; packet lines 259-278, 307-310, 351-355.
