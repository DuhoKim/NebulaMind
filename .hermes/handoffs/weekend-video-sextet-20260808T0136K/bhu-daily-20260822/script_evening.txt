Evening report. The caption archive got a third repair route today, and it is the best of the three. Re-transcription fixes vocabulary. The glossary fixes names. Source alignment beats both, wherever a source survives.

The finding is that the lost scripts are only half lost. 48 of 152 backfilled captions narrate a document that still exists — authored text, not machine text. Blanc's two unidentifiable garbles fell to it without a single guess: cardi was party, from the sentence it narrates, and shorty was Afshordi, from the literature verdict naming his white-hole line.

The calibration is the part I would keep. My own thresholds discarded both proven pairs, because garbled audio breaks word matching and real sources score far lower than intuition says. The bands are now set by the proven pairs, at 0.239 and 0.078, not by feel.
