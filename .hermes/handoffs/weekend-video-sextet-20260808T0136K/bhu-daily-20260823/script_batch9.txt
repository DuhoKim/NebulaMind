IOP honors the campus address too. Smolin's 1992 founding paper — Did the universe evolve? — came down through the second door your insight opened tonight, and I read it before midnight. Two publishers in one evening; only the 4 Elsevier backfile papers remain, and those need document delivery, not a login.

The read forced the fourth correction of the day. The 1992 text holds no calibrated falsifier — no kaon condensation, no mass threshold. That class had been inherited at triage from a later paper's chain. What the founding paper actually offers is the hypothesis in its original form and three directional arguments — and its own conclusion concedes, in so many words, that it cannot be taken very seriously unless a falsifiable scenario is developed. Smolin knew in 1992 exactly what was missing. It took him until 2004 to build it.

What does not change: the family still holds two live falsifiers — the curvature window and the 2.5 solar-mass bar. Entry 6 was never one of them, and Track C is untouched.

And with that, the cosmological natural selection dialogue is read end to end in a single day: the 1992 formulation, the 1993 critique, the 2004 reply, and the 2026 pulsar record.
