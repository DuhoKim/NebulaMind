# Visual Plan for BHU Neutron Star Explainer

**Status:** Plan only. No generated media, no credits spent, no Veo renders.

## General Design Directives
- **Legibility First:** All readable text must be generated using an image model (e.g., Imagen/Midjourney/DALL-E) or comped in post, never Veo. Video generation models struggle with text stability.
- **Background:** Cosmic footage or starfields should be used exclusively as a dim, low-contrast backdrop (opacity ~20-30%) to ensure foreground infographic text and diagrams pop.
- **Style:** Clean, flat-design infographics. High-contrast colors for data points (e.g., neon blue/orange) against dark cosmic backgrounds.
- **On-Screen Text:** Every panel must carry the script's assertion heading as highly legible on-screen text.

---

## Panel-by-Panel Infographic Plan

### Panel 01: The Verdict
- **Heading:** This specific chain fails its own second neutron-star test
- **Visual:** A stark, high-contrast title card. A faint, slow-moving or static deep-space background. Central focus is purely typographic to establish the "stakes → method → verdict" immediately.

### Panel 02: The Two-Limb Falsifier Structure
- **Heading:** This cosmic chain offered 2 checkable neutron-star tests
- **Visual:** A branching flowchart or "two limbs" diagram.
  - A central node ("Brown-Lee-Rho Chain") splitting into two distinct paths.
  - **Path A:** "Mass Ceiling (Heavy Single Star)"
  - **Path B:** "Binary Mass Asymmetry (>4% Difference)"
  - Both paths end in open checkboxes, visually establishing the "either test counts" logic.

### Panel 03: The Mass Ladder (Limb 1)
- **Heading:** The famous mass-ceiling test reaches serious doubt, not strict falsification
- **Visual:** A vertical "Mass Ladder" chart (Y-axis = Solar Masses, $M_\odot$).
  - A solid line at **1.5 $M_\odot$** labeled "Brown-Bethe Maximum".
  - A dashed/warning line at **2.0 $M_\odot$** labeled "Safe Falsification Threshold".
  - A data point plotted for **PSR J0740+6620** at 2.08. 
  - The crucial detail: The error bar ($\pm 0.07$) visibly dips *just* below the 2.00 line, visually explaining why it reaches "serious doubt" but fails the strict 95.4% falsification standard.
  - Show 3 other generic timing systems securely above the 1.5 line.

### Panel 04: The 4% Rule
- **Heading:** The same paper gives an independent 4% binary test
- **Visual:** A focus on the "OR" logic.
  - Highlight Limb B from the Panel 02 diagram.
  - A visual representation of a binary star system (two abstract spheres) with a scale or balance beneath them.
  - A marker set strictly at a **4%** threshold. 

### Panel 05: 19.3% vs 4% Comparison (Limb 2)
- **Heading:** PSR J1913+1102 exceeds the 4% limit by a wide margin
- **Visual:** A horizontal bar chart or gauge comparing the threshold to reality.
  - **Target Line:** 4% (Falsification Limit).
  - **Observed Bar:** A massive bar stretching to **19.3%**, with a tight error bracket ($\pm 0.7\%$).
  - The visual must emphasize that the observed value is nearly 5x the required threshold, making the failure unmistakable.

### Panel 06: The Sealed Rule
- **Heading:** A sealed rule keeps the result from following the answer
- **Visual:** A cryptographic lock icon or a "hash-sealed" document graphic overlaid on a checklist of criteria (e.g., "Dynamical timing masses only", "95.4% confidence"). This represents the pre-registered rigor that excluded the 2.35-sun star model and kept the 19.3% verdict intact.

### Panel 07: What Dies and What Survives
- **Heading:** The neutron-star prediction dies; broader ideas do not
- **Visual:** A hierarchical tree diagram.
  - Top node: "Black-Hole Universe Cosmology" (Intact/Glowing).
  - Middle node: "Cosmological Natural Selection" (Intact/Glowing).
  - Bottom node: "Brown-Bethe Route / 1.5 $M_\odot$ Max" (Crossed out / Faded out).
  - Visually communicates that a specific route is dead, but the parent hypotheses survive.

### Panel 08: The Forgotten Test
- **Heading:** The forgotten test had already finished
- **Visual:** The completed version of the Panel 02 branching diagram.
  - **Path A (Mass Ceiling):** Marked with a "⚠️ Doubt" or inconclusive symbol.
  - **Path B (4% Asymmetry):** Marked with a definitive "❌ Falsified" or red 'X'.
  - The panel visually lands the conclusion: the famous test was a near-miss, but the forgotten test definitively broke the chain.
