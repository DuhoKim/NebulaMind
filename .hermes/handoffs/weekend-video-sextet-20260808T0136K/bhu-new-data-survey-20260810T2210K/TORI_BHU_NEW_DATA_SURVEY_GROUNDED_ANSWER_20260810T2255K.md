- This survey is research scoping only: no result, claim, video, publication, or lane unblock follows, and the spin-lane freeze still forbids BHU support [51].
- The answer is `NEW_PUBLIC_DATA_EXISTS_BUT_NO_READY_BHU_DISCRIMINATOR_UNDER_CURRENT_CONSTRAINTS`, because relevant new public products exist but none combines a frozen signed handedness field, documented orientation convention, mirror audit, independent footprint, and quantitative BHU forecast [1][45][47].

## What is genuinely new and usable

- The public HSC DR3 spin catalogue is a genuinely newer signed-spin dataset than Galaxy Zoo 1, and Stiskalek and Desmond already applied a Bayesian monopole, dipole, and quadrupole analysis to it [45].
- That independent HSC analysis found no significant anisotropy and reported decisive evidence for the isotropic model, so Duho's instruction means this result should be believed and used as a constraint rather than re-derived in search of another answer [45][51].
- Patel and Desmond separately collected all public binary-spin catalogues available to them and found every analysis consistent with isotropy within three standard deviations [42].
- Iye, Yagi, and Fukumoto found that the cleaned local SDSS sample gave `sigma_D = 0.29` and was compatible with a random spin distribution [39].
- These null results constrain preferred-axis observations but do not uniquely falsify BHU, because Popławski's primary BHU paper offers a qualitative inherited-axis possibility rather than a forecast with fixed amplitude, redshift dependence, sky mask response, or unique likelihood [1].

## Provenance grades

- DESI Legacy Surveys DR10 is `DOCUMENTED` for shape and position angle: its catalogue defines `phi = arctan2(e2,e1)/2`, while pinned Tractor source defines the angle east of north [3][10].
- DESI Legacy DR10 has no signed handedness field, and an ellipse position angle is parity-blind modulo 180 degrees [3][10].
- Galaxy Zoo DECaLS is `DOCUMENTED — NOT A HANDEDNESS PRODUCT`, because its published schema and paper define morphology questions but no clockwise or counterclockwise output [6][7].
- The Galaxy Zoo DECaLS training pipeline randomly flipped images horizontally and vertically, which is useful for morphology invariance but prevents the released output tree from being treated as a chirality classifier [7].
- Galaxy Zoo DESI is `DOCUMENTED — NOT A HANDEDNESS PRODUCT`, because its 8.7-million-galaxy catalogue provides 29 Galaxy Zoo morphology measurements but no clockwise or counterclockwise target [8].
- In both modern Galaxy Zoo products, `spiral-winding` means tight, medium, or loose arm winding, not handedness direction [7][8].
- DESI spectroscopy DR1 is `DOCUMENTED — SUPPORT FIELDS ONLY`, with public coordinates, identifiers, and fitted redshifts but no morphology, position angle, or handedness field [11].
- DESI spectroscopy DR2 is `UNDOCUMENTED` for this survey because the official release index still identifies DR1 as the latest verified public release and provides no public DR2 data product [14].
- SDSS DR17 and DR18 are `DOCUMENTED — POSITION ANGLE ONLY`, with fitted-profile angles east of north but no signed chirality; DR18 also reuses earlier imaging rather than supplying an independent new imaging epoch [16][17].
- Euclid Q1 is `DOCUMENTED — POSITION ANGLE AND MORPHOLOGY ONLY`, with a stated counter-clockwise position-angle convention but no signed handedness field in its public morphology product [19].
- Rubin DP1 and EDP2 are `UNDOCUMENTED — INELIGIBLE UNDER THE PUBLIC-DATA-ONLY RULE`, because access is limited to Rubin data-rights holders and the public schema provides no handedness field or complete signed parity convention [24][27].
- The public HSC DR3 spin catalogue is `UNDOCUMENTED FOR SIGN MAPPING`, because its CSV exposes `direction_cw_ccw` as plus or minus one without a primary data dictionary mapping each sign to clockwise or counterclockwise [49].
- The missing HSC sign mapping does not change a sign-invariant zero-versus-nonzero anisotropy test, but it blocks a named directional claim tied to a particular pole [45][49].
- The claimed DESI Legacy DR8 handedness catalogue is `UNDOCUMENTED — FILE UNAVAILABLE`, because its author page says the data download is coming soon and does not provide a frozen file, checksum, sign mapping, row manifest, or duplicate policy [47].
- Planck 2018 is `DOCUMENTED — ALREADY STUDIED`, and its collaboration paper confirms mild large-scale temperature anomalies while claiming no unambiguous cosmological non-Gaussianity or corresponding polarization anomaly [30].
- ACT DR6.02 is `DOCUMENTED — PARTIAL-SKY SUPPORT`, with public `enmap` FITS and WCS products that cannot replace Planck for a full-sky low-multipole axis test [33].
- SPT-3G D1 is `DOCUMENTED — PARTIAL-SKY SUPPORT`, with public HEALPix temperature and polarization products, an equatorial footprint, and an IAU polarization convention rather than a full-sky low-multipole replacement [36].

## Answer to Duho's data-era hypothesis

- The data-era hypothesis survives in principle because a controlled pipeline can perform deliberate mirror tests that were unavailable for unrecoverable 2008 catalogue bookkeeping [7][51].
- The specific modern catalogues inspected here do not yet solve the problem, because the released Galaxy Zoo classifiers have no chirality target and their published `winding` outputs describe arm tightness [6][7][8].
- Mirror evaluation can audit invariance or implementation bias, but it cannot recover a directional target that was absent from training and the output schema [6][7][8].
- Training a new directional head or creating fresh annotations would be a new study and is outside Duho's public-data-only, no-new-labelling boundary [51].

## What may be leveraged without overstating the science

- The HSC DR3 isotropy result and the Planck collaboration's anomaly assessment can be leveraged now as existing constraints rather than repeated analyses [30][45].
- DESI DR1 spectroscopy can support a future cross-match or redshift robustness audit using already-frozen spin labels, but it cannot create handedness and would not be a unique BHU test [11][45].
- Legacy, SDSS, and Euclid position angles can support projected-orientation studies only if the theory first predicts that parity-even observable; they must not be substituted for clockwise or counterclockwise spin [10][16][19].
- Before any future study design, the theory must specify a discriminating statistic and forecast, while the data must freeze sign convention, frame, mirror transform, duplicate policy, row selection, and cross-survey matching [1][42][47].
- Until those two sides are documented, the honest conclusion is that there is new public data to cite and constrain the question, but no ready public dataset that completes an honest BHU study under the current rules [1][45][51].
