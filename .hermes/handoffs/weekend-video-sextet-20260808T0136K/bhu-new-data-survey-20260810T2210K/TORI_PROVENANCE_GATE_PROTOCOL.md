# Tori provenance feasibility gate — BHU new-data survey

Black-hole-universe cosmology is Duho's standing personal research interest, not a ranked frontier in this corpus. It is his project; implying mainstream priority would misrepresent the field.

This is research scoping only. The spin lane freeze still forbids BHU support in that video. No result, claim, video, publication, or lane unblock follows.

For every dataset named in `HWAO_BHU_NEW_DATA_SURVEY_ORDER_20260810T2210K.md`, inspect primary documentation before any study design and record:

1. the exact public product;
2. the fields a possible study would actually consume;
3. their documented meaning;
4. whether a handedness/chirality field exists;
5. if a handedness or position-angle field exists, its orientation/frame convention;
6. whether the public image product carries sufficient WCS/projection provenance to reconstruct display parity;
7. one grade only: `DOCUMENTED`, `UNDOCUMENTED`, or `NOT-YET-CHECKED`;
8. the exact missing item when not documented.

`DOCUMENTED` means a primary survey/data-release/paper source states the needed semantics and orientation/frame convention. A catalogue with no chirality field cannot be promoted to a chirality catalogue. Open model code does not create an output class the model was not trained to predict. Where the intended observable is absent, record that fact instead of inventing a study design.

Public data only; no new labelling. Existing catalogue-bias studies are treated as evidence, not re-derived. No data download larger than needed for schema/header inspection.
