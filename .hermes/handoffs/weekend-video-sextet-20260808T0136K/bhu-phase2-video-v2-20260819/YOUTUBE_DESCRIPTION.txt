The four published papers behind the "our universe was born inside a black hole" idea — each explained in plain language, with the papers' own plots where they exist, and what a strict equation-by-equation audit found in each.

Chapter guide:
1. Cosmology with torsion (Phys. Lett. B 694, 2010; arXiv:1007.0587) — spin twists spacetime; at crushing density the twist pushes back and the universe bounces instead of banging. No figures in this paper. Audit: the key averaging step is never derived (a factor-6 swing), and the journal's correction notice can't be read — we recomputed the numbers.
2. The spinor-torsion bounce (Phys. Rev. D 85, 2012; arXiv:1111.4595) — same idea, different engine, and this paper's own two plots shown and walked through: the scale-factor dip-and-bounce and the near-Planck temperature spike. Audit: this paper calls the first one's foundation "not self-consistent" — the two bounce engines disagree by about 730x and cannot both be true.
3. The universe inside a black hole (ApJ 832, 2016; arXiv:1410.3881) — every black hole as a nursery; the parent's mass sets the baby's starting size. No figures in this paper. Audit: the horizon matching is a conjecture, and the flagship numbers silently assume a 1-meter starting size.
4. Collapse with torsion (IJMPA 40, 2025; arXiv:2509.11468) — a star collapsed with torsion on. Across all 4 papers, no equation carries the parent's spin through the bounce; the collapse papers mention a spinning parent in exactly 1 sentence. Audit: our derived ceiling caps inherited spin near 1 part in 10^27, and a frozen-ratio theorem shows the bounce neither smooths nor creates lopsidedness.

Reality check: the helium fossil record (Dutta & Scherrer, Phys. Rev. D 82, 2010; arXiv:1006.4166 — their plots shown) allows the chain's torsion whisper with 45 orders of magnitude to spare: consistent, and therefore invisible. Stacking every generosity, the best signal sits 10,000 to 100,000 times below what counting every observable galaxy could detect.

Caveats carried on screen: both bounces sit at or above the Planck scale treated classically; the correction notice remains unread (recomputations stand in its place); the derived chain awaits external theorist review.
