## 2. Register rules for gpt1 (from the diagnosis, binding)

1. One idea per beat; a beat = 1–2 short sentences (target ≤14 w/s panel average; nothing
   like v2-P08's 33).
2. Analogy BEFORE mechanism, every time a mechanism appears (the five art-backed analogies:
   compass-needle fabric, compressed spring, clapping crowd, spinning skater, fossil
   thermometer; plus coin-flip floor and volume-knob stack).
3. A number never shares a sentence with a new concept; every number gets a felt comparison
   and a held-frame beat (gpt3 pacing: ≥1.5 s hold after each number panel-line).
4. Re-gloss any term not used in the previous 2 panels (torsion, shear, scale factor get
   re-glossed at reuse; don't define-once-and-run).
5. Question bridges between chapters ("so how do you add up…?", "where is the spin bridge?",
   "how loud could it possibly be?").
6. The five frozen facts land only after their concepts are built (placements in §3);
   panel 01's verdict is the sole cold-open exception and must itself be plain (F3 appears
   there inside the one built metaphor).
7. Must-not-say list, three permitted equations, attribution/illustration chips, honest
   no-plots cards — all v2 rules carry (the brief's standing list).
