# V3 visual implementation plan

This build deliberately does not reuse the v2 panel look. It uses only v2's gateway/ffmpeg plumbing ideas.

## Narrative arc

1. Ask whether a baby universe can keep its parent's spin, and give the closed verdict immediately.
2. Build the four-paper chain one mechanism at a time: twist, spring, averaging, cusp, mass map, missing bridge.
3. Derive the causality ceiling and the frozen-ratio condition.
4. Test the chain against helium and the all-galaxy counting floor.
5. Reconnect the four papers and return to the closed ceiling.

## Binding visual language

- 1920×1080, 120 px safe margin, 12-column grid.
- SF Pro display headings at 64–82 px; body annotations at 36–44 px; chips at 28 px.
- Exact palette: #0B0C10, #1F2833, #FFFFFF, #C5C6C7, #66FCF1, #45A29E, #FF4C4C.
- Every panel is content. There are no divider cards.
- Each panel has cumulative or replacement states; analogy appears before mechanism, and number/result overlays appear last.
- Paper pixels remain intact, large, and attributed. Cyan walkthrough cursors are overlays and do not change the data.
- The generated cold-open is used full-bleed only in panel 01. Every other concept-art slot is a deterministic programmatic fallback from DESIGN_SYSTEM.md Part 2 and carries the exact NebulaMind illustration chip.

## Timing

- Narration inputs are byte-identical to STORYBOARD.json.
- Hermes gateway gpt-4o-mini-tts, alloy voice, deliberately slowed from default rather than sped up.
- Each panel begins with lead silence and ends with an explicit panel-turn breathing gap.
- Planned storyboard durations total 674 seconds; any panel extends rather than compressing the voice.

## QA

- Freeze input hashes and five asset pins before rendering.
- Verify all 16 stills are 1920×1080.
- Verify the three permitted overlay equations and no others.
- Decode and transcribe each panel from the final MP4.
- Numbers and all protected contract phrases are non-cosmetic; any such residual forces regeneration.
