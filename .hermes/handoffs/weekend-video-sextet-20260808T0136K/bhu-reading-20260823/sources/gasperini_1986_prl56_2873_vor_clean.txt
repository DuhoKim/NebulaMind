               In the framework of the simplest Einstein-Cartan generalization of the standard cosmological
            model, it is shown that an inflationary phase may occur at a sufficiently early epoch, during which
            the dominant contributions to the effective energy-momentum       tensor are represented by the spin
            density of the matter sources. The general features of this inflationary scenario are briefly dis-
            cussed, and in particular the possibility of obtaining an expansion rate different from that of the
            standard exponential inflation is stressed.
