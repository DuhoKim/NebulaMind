# Goru Phase 0 Novelty Sweep (Prior Art)

## Route A — Axis-model handedness amplitude
**Current best global-rotation/isotropy bounds:**
- *Planck 2015 results. XVIII. Background geometry and topology* (Planck Collaboration) constrains the global vorticity (rotation) of Bianchi VII$_h$ models to $(\omega/H)_0 < 7.6 \times 10^{-10}$ at the 95% confidence level.
- Saadeh et al. 2016 ("How Isotropic is the Universe?", *Phys. Rev. Lett.*) constrains the anisotropic expansion associated with the vector mode (vorticity/rotation) to $(\sigma_V/H)_0 < 4.7 \times 10^{-11}$ at the 95% confidence interval.

**Rotation-to-galaxy-handedness amplitude calculation:**
- The literature contains studies on Tidal Torque Theory (TTT) linking the primordial tidal field to the direction/orientation of galaxy spins (e.g., Jounghun Lee 2021 "How the Galaxy Stellar Spins Acquire a Peculiar Tidal Connection?").
- Lior Shamir has published extensive observational evidence on large-scale asymmetry in galaxy spin directions (e.g., "New evidence and analysis of cosmological-scale asymmetry in galaxy spin directions" 2022).
- However, an explicit analytic calculation mapping a global background rotation magnitude $\Omega$ to a specific observable amplitude $A(\Omega)$ of galaxy handedness asymmetry remains absent.
**Verdict:** partially-done (cite Shamir 2022, Lee 2021). The explicit amplitude derivation $A(\Omega)$ from a defined global rotation appears novel.

## Route B — Torsion-bounce (Popławski) observables
**Stated observables and published constraints:**
- Popławski's primary observable prediction relies on the spin-spin contact interaction of fermions, which becomes a repulsive force at extremely high densities, avoiding a singularity and leading to a "Big Bounce" (e.g., "The simplest origin of the big bounce and inflation", 2018; "Gravitational collapse with torsion and universe in a black hole", 2025). 
- Additional parity violation statements are largely macroscopic: Popławski proposes our universe inherited a preferred axis of rotation from the parent black hole, producing an asymmetry in the handedness of spiral galaxies. 
- Constraints: Parity-odd, spin-dependent interactions in the low-energy limit are tightly constrained by precision metrology and quantum sensing.
**Verdict:** already-done (cite Popławski 2018, Popławski 2025). The observables proposed either lack a uniquely measurable local magnitude at present densities or rest on already-constrained macroscopic asymmetries.

## Route C — The birth fingerprint (BHU-unique signatures)
**Claimed BHU-unique-signature papers:**
- A sweep of the local corpus (`BHU_LITERATURE_BASELINE.json`) and literature hosts reveals various papers discussing observables inside a black hole (e.g., Gaztanaga 2021 "A peek outside our Universe", Roupas 2022 "Detectable universes inside regular black holes").
- However, none of these papers claim a robust, uniquely calibrated "smoking gun" signature that differentiates a Black Hole Universe birth from a generic nonsingular bounce cosmology or standard anisotropic models. Proposed signatures remain indistinguishable from generic anomalies. 
**Verdict:** novel. No paper in the current sweep has successfully published an observable fingerprint that only a parent black-hole birth would leave.
