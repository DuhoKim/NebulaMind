# Phase 2 Ingredients: ECSK Torsion-Bounce and Popławski Literature

## 1. Published Critiques and Replications of the ECSK Bounce
**Spin-fluid averaging debate:**
- "A primary technical critique is that the spin-fluid model is often derived as a 'particle approximation' of the integrated conservation laws in ECSK gravity. Research has noted that this particle approximation for Dirac fields—which are the fundamental fermionic sources of spin—is not self-consistent. Critics argue that treating Dirac spinors simply as a classical spin fluid ignores the more complex dynamics of the spinor fields themselves."
- "The spin-fluid description has been criticized for violating the cosmological principle. The inclusion of a macroscopic spin-density vector in the fluid model can introduce preferred directions or constraints that are difficult to reconcile with a perfectly isotropic and homogeneous universe unless specific symmetry conditions are assumed."
- "Analyses of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) and Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN) have suggested that such scaling behavior [$a^{-6}$] is essentially ruled out by current observations."

## 2. Parent-Inheritance Prior Art
- "Theoretical models, such as the Holographic Rotation-Driven Cyclic Cosmology (HRDCC), suggest that these rotational degrees of freedom are preserved through the transition. This inherited vorticity contributes to the effective cosmological dynamics of the new universe..."

Because torsion is explicitly linked to spin, the 'twist' of spacetime in the parent black hole’s interior is a physical manifestation of the matter's angular momentum, which the new universe inherits as it begins its own inflationary evolution."

## 3. The $\Omega_S$ Erratum Trail
- **Original Publication:** Popławski, N. J., "Cosmology with torsion: an alternative to cosmic inflation", *Physics Letters B*, Volume 694, Issues 4–5, 2010, pages 181–185.
- **Erratum:** An erratum for this article was subsequently published in *Physics Letters B*, Volume 701, Issue 5, 2011, page 672. This erratum addresses corrections to the magnitude and sign of the derived torsion density parameter $\Omega_S$.

## 4. Interior-Vorticity Bounds
- "Models that rely on a torsion-dominated epoch often predict that the energy density of the spin fluid scales as $a^{-6}$ (where $a$ is the scale factor). Analyses of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) and Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN) have suggested that such scaling behavior is essentially ruled out by current observations. While some spin-fluid models might be statistically admissible when looking only at specific datasets like Type Ia supernovae, they struggle to meet the stricter constraints imposed by the CMB and BBN."
