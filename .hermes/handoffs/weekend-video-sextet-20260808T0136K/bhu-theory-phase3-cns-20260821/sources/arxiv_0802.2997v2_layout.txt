                                                    more than 4% different from each other in mass or a massive neutron star with mass M >  ∼ 2M⊙ would
                                                    put in serious doubt or simply falsify the following chain of predictions: (1) nearly vanishing vector
                                                    meson mass at chiral restoration, (2) kaon condensation at a density n ∼ 3n0 , (3) the Brown-Bethe
                                                    maximum neutron star mass Mmax ≈ 1.5M⊙ and (4) Smolin’s ‘Cosmological Natural Selection’
                                                    hypothesis.
