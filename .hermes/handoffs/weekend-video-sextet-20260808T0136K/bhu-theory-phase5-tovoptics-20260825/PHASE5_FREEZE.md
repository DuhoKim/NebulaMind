## Addendum 1 (same session, BEFORE any computation) — K1 was wrong as written

The brief's K1 said: "if τ ≫ 1 across the whole admissible anchor range, the photon channel is
closed, the branch is UNTESTABLE-BY-LIGHT." Working through the physics to design S0, that is
false, and in the interesting direction:

**τ ≫ 1 does not hide the boundary — it creates a photosphere.** An optically thick exterior
means our line of sight terminates on an emitting surface just beyond the shock, at that
matter's brightness temperature. That is not the absence of a signal; it is a strong signal,
and a strong one is easier to exclude, not harder. Opacity makes the model MORE testable.

Corrected S0 disposition, replacing K1:
- τ ≫ 1 → **PHOTOSPHERE branch**: compute the brightness temperature of the emitting surface
  and confront it with the observed sky. Likely the sharpest test this phase can produce.
- τ ≪ 1 → **TRANSPARENT branch**: proceed to S1/S2 as briefed; the perturbation is small and
  the transfer function is the deliverable.
- τ ~ 1 → both effects; carry both.

Neither branch ends the phase at S0. What S0 decides is WHICH physics S1–S3 must compute.
