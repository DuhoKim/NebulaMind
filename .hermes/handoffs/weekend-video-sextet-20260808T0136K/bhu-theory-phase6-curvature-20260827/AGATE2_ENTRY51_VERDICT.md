# Independent gate verdict — entry 51

Status: **LIVE / UNFIRED on the supplied record.** Promote entry 51 to **CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER**, but scope it to the paper's coarse, conditional four-dimensional-ECKS black-hole-mass consequence. It is not a precision forecast, and it is not a direct BHU falsifier.

## Deciding text

The paper first marks the premise as an estimate:

> “We expect that the Cartan density for an electron, ... ~10^51 kg m^-3, approximately gives the order of the maximum density of ordinary matter composed of quarks and leptons.” (pinned text, line 359)

It then states the numerical bound and collider consequence:

> “The mass density of a black hole also cannot exceed rho_Ce, from which its minimum mass in the ECKS theory is ~10^16 kg, corresponding to energy ~10^43 GeV.” (line 368)
>
> “Therefore the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), which can operate at energies up to ~10^4 GeV, cannot produce micro black holes ... if the four-dimensional ECKS theory is a correct theory of gravity.” (line 369)

The abstract gives the same explicit conditional:

> “Thus, if torsion exists and the ECKS theory of gravity is correct, the LHC cannot produce micro black holes.” (line 24)

These sentences supply both parts of the kickoff's tier contract: an author-stated numerical lower bound and a specified observation on the forbidden side of that bound.

## 1. Reachability and the ~10^39 gap

The gap does **not** defeat calibration under the definition in this gate. The proposed test is not “accelerate particles to ~10^43 GeV and scan the boundary.” It is “positively detect a black hole made at an LHC energy of order ~10^4 GeV.” Such an object would lie roughly 39 orders of magnitude below the paper's claimed minimum and would therefore be a direct counterexample. The instrument operates on the forbidden side already; it need not approach the allowed-side boundary.

The independent unit check is consistent with the paper: 10^16 kg is about 5.61×10^42 GeV/c^2, i.e. order 10^43 GeV, and the ratio to 10^4 GeV is about 5.61×10^38 (order 10^39).

This differs from an unbounded sign claim whose magnitude may retreat arbitrarily toward zero. Here the paper fixes an approximate nonzero floor, `M_BH,min ~ 10^16 kg`; a confirmed black hole far below it cannot be rescued by taking the claimed effect smaller while preserving that floor. The large gap strengthens the margin of a positive counterexample rather than emptying it.

The calibration is nevertheless **coarse and model-conditioned**. The source uses “expect,” “approximately,” “on the order,” `~`, and “conjecture” (lines 359–368). It gives no uncertainty interval, production cross-section, likelihood, detector acceptance, or tolerance around 10^16 kg. Therefore this is a number-plus-kill-threshold falsifier under the kickoff's stated taxonomy, not a modern precision-calibrated prediction and not a promise that an LHC black hole should occur.

## 2. What would be falsified

A confirmed LHC micro black hole would directly falsify the paper's composite claim that ordinary fermionic matter in four-dimensional ECKS has the stated maximum density and consequent minimum black-hole mass. It would fire this **ECKS black-hole application/derivation chain**.

It would not, by logic alone, falsify every formulation of ECKS or every torsion theory. The paper itself labels the maximum-density extension as an expectation and the self-gravitating singularity result as a conjecture (lines 359–363), so a counterexample could locate failure in those auxiliary steps rather than in the basic ECKS field equations. Lines 372–375 also say other torsion theories can give different size predictions. The paper's phrase “if the four-dimensional ECKS theory is a correct theory of gravity” supports reporting the observation as a serious refutation of this four-dimensional-ECKS consequence, but not collapsing the conditional chain into a theorem about all torsion gravity.

The paper does not discuss a black-hole-universe model. Entry 51 can constrain a BHU application only downstream, if that application expressly depends on this same four-dimensional-ECKS density/minimum-mass chain. It is not, from this source alone, a direct falsifier of BHU.

## 3. Paper route versus reader-supplied route

The **paper's route is LHC production of a micro black hole**. It appears in both the abstract (lines 22–24) and discussion (lines 368–369).

The complete pinned text contains no occurrence of “primordial,” “PBH,” or “black-hole universe.” A confirmed black hole below ~10^16 kg, whatever its formation channel, is a valid logical counterexample to the stated global minimum; therefore a sub-threshold PBH can be recorded as a **reader-derived discriminator**. It must not be attributed to Popławski as the paper's proposed test, and the additional statement that standard physics permits such a PBH is likewise not established by this paper.

## 4. Direction of firing

Both routes are one-sided and require a **positive, credible detection**:

- paper route: a black hole produced at LHC energy, necessarily far below the claimed minimum;
- reader-derived route: an independently established black hole with mass below the approximate ~10^16 kg floor.

A null LHC search, failure to produce a black hole, or absence of an observed sub-threshold PBH is compatible with the paper and fires nothing. The status is therefore LIVE / UNFIRED on the evidence supplied to this gate.

## 5. Promotion ruling

The prior QUALITATIVE-DIRECTIONAL label is too weak for the entry as written. The paper does more than state a sign or direction: it gives an approximate mass floor and explicitly names a positive observation that would violate it. The promotion is upheld on those merits, independent of the engine that proposed it.

This ruling would reverse only if the canonical tier contract requires a quantified sensitivity/likelihood or a practical ability to scan near the boundary, rather than the kickoff's explicit “number plus threshold, such that a specified observation would refute it.” A separate family-count rule requiring a **direct BHU kill** could also exclude this entry from a BHU live-falsifier tally without changing this entry-level CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER classification.
