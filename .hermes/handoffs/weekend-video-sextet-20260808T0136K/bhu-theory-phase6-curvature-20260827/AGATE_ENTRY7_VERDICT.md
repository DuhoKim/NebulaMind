## Verdict on Entry 7: The Instrument vs. Theory Distinction

**1. On importing a distinction because it is convenient:**
You are not importing the distinction; it is explicitly present in the text and deliberately structured by the authors. The abstract presents a disjunction: "put in serious doubt **or** simply falsify" applied to a four-element chain. 

Crucially, the authors distribute these two outcomes distinctly in the remainder of the paper:
*   **The Instrument (Nuclear Physics):** In the body, they explicitly isolate the strong claim: *"The falsifiable prediction is this: 'Find a neutron star of mass \gtrsim 2M_\odot ... then it FALSIFIES the VM of HLS theory, which in turn FALSIFIES the kaon condensation at \simeq 3n_0.'"* 
*   **The Theory (Astrophysics/Cosmology):** In the closing paragraph, speaking in their own voice about the full scope of their argument, they state: *"A firm observation... would present a SERIOUS OBSTACLE to the BB and CNS scenarios."*

The distinction between the instrument (which is strictly falsified) and the theory (which faces a serious obstacle/doubt) is authored by Brown, Lee, and Rho, not retroactively imported by you.

**2. On resolving the contradiction the other way:**
The sources do not support the strong reading (that Entry 7 falsified CNS outright at >~ 2 Msun). To adopt the strong reading, one must ignore the authors' explicit scoping of "falsifies" in the body and override their final conclusion ("serious obstacle") with an unstated extrapolation. 

Entry 7's text supports that the >~ 2 Msun threshold definitively breaks the HLS/kaon condensation instrument chain that Brown-Bethe relies upon to yield a ~1.5 Msun maximum mass. It does not definitively break CNS, which is why the authors downgraded the impact on CNS to a "serious obstacle" (or "serious doubt" per the abstract's first limb). Therefore, your entry 31 ruling stands, and the coherent record correctly reflects the source.

**3. On the arbitrariness of choosing limb 1:**
It is not an arbitrary pick. The text provides the mapping for the abstract's disjunction. The body explicitly maps limb 2 ("simply falsify") to elements 1 and 2 (HLS and kaon condensation). The closing explicitly maps limb 1 ("serious doubt", phrased as "serious obstacle") to elements 3 and 4 (BB and CNS). Choosing limb 1 for CNS is following the authors' own mapping.

**4. On the body's silence being load-bearing:**
The silence is structurally load-bearing. The sentence begins with, *"The falsifiable prediction is this..."* When authors explicitly define the exact parameters of their falsifiable claim and consciously omit the cosmological element from that definition, that omission bounds the strict falsification. 

**5. On the tier question:**
Your observation is correct. The `CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER` label describes the epistemic *nature* of the claim—it has a threshold that allows it to act as a tripwire. It does not describe its current empirical *status* (whether the tripwire has been crossed). A separate axis (e.g., `FIRED` / `UNFIRED` or `LIVE` / `DEAD`) is necessary to avoid conflating the nature of the claim with its current measurement reality.

**Conclusion:**
The weak reading is the only reading faithful to the entirety of the text. Entry 7 fired the instrument chain; it left CNS facing a "serious obstacle", not falsification. The contradiction with Entry 31 is resolved in favor of your prior ruling.
