I read the pinned paper in full: `../bhu-reading-20260823/sources/0910.1181_clean.txt`.
The decisive source text is at lines 359 and 368-369. The paper estimates a
maximum ordinary-matter density from the electron Cartan density, then states a
minimum black-hole mass and an LHC consequence:

> "mass density of a black hole also cannot exceed rho_Ce"

> "minimum mass in the ECKS theory is ~ 10^16 kg, corresponding to energy ~ 10^43 GeV"

> "the LHC cannot produce micro black holes"

That is number plus threshold plus a specified excluded observation. A positive
observation of a black hole below the stated floor, including an LHC micro black
hole, would contradict the paper's claim.

## Attack 1: reachability

The 10^39 gap does not demote this entry. The paper is not offering a near-term
production target at ~10^43 GeV; it is setting a lower bound and using the LHC
as a sub-threshold falsification channel. If the LHC produced a micro black
hole at ~10^4 GeV scale, that would be 39 orders of magnitude below the paper's
minimum black-hole mass. The threshold is therefore calibrated in a useful
falsifier sense: observations need not approach the allowed side of the
threshold; any robust positive detection far below it fires the test.

This differs from entry 54. Entry 54's magnitude could retreat arbitrarily close
to zero, leaving only a sign. Here the forbidden region is not an adjustable
near-zero sliver. The source gives an order-of-magnitude floor, ~10^16 kg /
~10^43 GeV, and the named collider regime is many orders below it. The
calibration is coarse, but the falsifying side is clear.

The current status is LIVE, not fired. I checked current collider-search context
because "fired" depends on changing experimental results: CMS's latest public
article I found, dated 2025-12-04, says searches still show no evidence for
microscopic black holes and exclude model masses below about 9.0-11.4 TeV; the
2026 CMS arXiv search likewise reports no evidence in 13 TeV data. Sources:
https://cms.cern/news/are-there-quantum-black-holes-evaporate-instantly and
https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10732.

## Attack 2: what it falsifies

The direct target is four-dimensional ECKS gravity with torsion as used in this
paper, not a BHU-specific cosmological construction. The paper's condition is
explicit: "if the four-dimensional ECKS theory is a correct theory of gravity"
(line 369). A collider micro black hole would therefore refute that ECKS
minimum-mass consequence, and by extension would damage BHU applications that
depend on the same four-dimensional ECKS torsion-bounce machinery. It should
not be recorded as a clean, standalone falsification of every possible BHU
model independent of ECKS.

## Attack 3: paper route versus our route

The paper's author-stated route is the LHC route. It also says a discovery of
fermion structure at Cartan-radius scales would be indirect evidence for
torsion (line 336), but that is supportive rather than a black-hole threshold
kill test.

I found no primordial-black-hole route in the paper. The proposed discriminator
"a sub-10^16-kg primordial black hole, which standard physics permits and this
forbids" is a reader inference from the source's minimum black-hole mass. It is
a valid derived implication to discuss separately, but it must not be recorded
as Poplawski's stated test.

## Attack 4: firing direction

The falsifier is one-sided and positive-detection only. Absence of LHC micro
black holes does not confirm ECKS; it merely leaves this consequence alive. A
detected LHC micro black hole, or a securely identified sub-10^16-kg black hole
by another route, would fire it. The kill condition is not non-detection and not
"failure to reach ~10^43 GeV."
