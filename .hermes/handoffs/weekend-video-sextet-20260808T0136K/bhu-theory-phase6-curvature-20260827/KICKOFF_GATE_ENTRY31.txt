## The question

**Is entry 31 still a live CALIBRATED-FALSIFIER, and what is its true current status?**

The relevant facts, all from our record or fetched this session:

1. Smolin's own bar, from §4 of the primary text as recorded in entry 31: a sufficiently heavy
   neutron star refutes S, and "sufficiently high is certainly 2.5 M_sun, although if one is
   completely confident of Bethe and Brown's upper limit of 1.5 solar masses, any value higher
   than this would be troubling."
2. Heaviest well-measured neutron star: **PSR J0952-0607 = 2.35 +- 0.17 M_sun** (Romani et al.
   2022, ApJL 939 L4 / arXiv 2207.05124), tightened to **2.35 +- 0.11** by a later measurement
   (arXiv 2512.05099). That is roughly 1.4 sigma short of 2.5.
3. Entry 31 also records that the INSTRUMENT limb — Brown-Bethe's 1.5 M_sun — was adjudicated
   broken at >= 8 sigma by our own C08/Track C work.
4. Entry 31 records that Smolin's footnote 6 "concedes in print that S could be saved by ad-hoc
   parameter-coupling fixes and disclaims them absent independent support."

## Attack these

1. **Is the falsifier live or effectively reached?** 2.35 +- 0.11 against a 2.5 bar is ~1.4
   sigma. Does "certainly 2.5" mean a hard threshold, or did Smolin intend a softer statement
   that the current measurement already satisfies? Read §4 yourself.

2. **Does a falsifier survive when its instrument is broken?** Smolin's 2.5 is the conservative
   bar; the 1.5 bar is Brown-Bethe's calculation, which our record says is broken at >= 8 sigma.
   If the instrument that would justify 1.5 is dead, is the 2.5 bar still a CALIBRATED
   falsifier, or is it now an unanchored round number?

3. **Does footnote 6 do to entry 31 what section VI did to entry 54?** Entry 54 was demoted
   because the author qualified the magnitude, leaving only a sign. If Smolin concedes in print
   that S can be rescued by parameter-coupling fixes, does that make his falsifier non-binding
   in the same way? This is the question I most want ruled on. Note he "disclaims them absent
   independent support" — that may be a material difference from entry 54.

4. **Is CNS even a black-hole-universe theory** in the sense this bibliography tracks, or is its
   presence in the family a classification artefact? Rule if you have a view; it bears on
   whether the family's falsifier tally means anything.

5. **What is the correct tally statement?** After your ruling, how many LIVE calibrated
   falsifiers does the family have — and is any statement of the form "the family has no live
   calibrated falsifier" defensible?
