Black-hole-universe cosmology is a personal side-interest of the author, not a NebulaMind research programme.

One version of the universe-inside-a-black-hole idea says we were born inside a ROTATING black hole — so galaxies should slightly prefer spinning one way. This video reports what happened when we took that idea's own published numbers to their limit: the universe's baby picture (the CMB) allows almost no global rotation, and even with maximum generosity the predicted lean is about 5 galaxies per 10 million. Counting EVERY galaxy in the observable universe perfectly would still leave that signal below one sigma; reaching confidence would take about 18 observable universes' worth of galaxies. There is no bigger telescope, and there are no more galaxies.

What this does NOT say: not "the black-hole-universe idea is false" — this closes one route for one family member, under the mechanics its sources published. A new amplifying mechanism, or a properly derived amplitude, would reopen the question — and we keep a watchpost for exactly that. Our own galaxy-spin measurement continues on its own merits, independent of this interpretation.
