This packet grades candidate datasets before design as `DOCUMENTED`, `UNDOCUMENTED`, or `NOT-YET-CHECKED`.

The gate checks immutable release identity, field names/types/units/nulls, row selection and duplicate policy, completeness and masks, coordinate frame and epoch, calibration provenance, public-access status, and exact conventions for any observer-motion or kinematic-dipole correction.

An overall candidate passes only when every field and convention needed by the proposed observable is recoverable from primary documentation and public products.

A documented catalogue without a documented selection function, exposure function, directional calibration decomposition, covariance semantics, random catalogue, or correction convention remains `UNDOCUMENTED` for the corresponding study.

This is scope only. No data download, analysis run, scientific result, claim, video, publication, public-surface change, or lane unblock is authorized.
