## Independent diagnosis

The primary 0204 artifact is an eleven-card deck encoded as video. Eight of its eleven states are dominated by a title, a large number, an equation plus paragraph, or prose. Only the funnel and mirrored-spiral states are scientific diagrams, and both are static holds. Eight-second cadence samples repeat the same pixels across the 12-second funnel and mirror holds. Measured mean frame-to-frame luma difference is only 0.0275, with change concentrated at hard cuts.

The visual grammar therefore fails independently of source correctness:

1. The viewer reads documents instead of watching scientific relationships change.
2. The large `667,944` state detaches the count from its population transition.
3. The equation arrives fully assembled, so numerator, denominator, sign, and withheld value are not taught.
4. The mirror scene shows two finished spirals rather than one object undergoing a real horizontal transformation with its label inverting.
5. The controls are a paragraph, not an inspectable control-by-failure-mode design.
6. The open gates are prose, not a review sequence with a visible lock.
7. The presenter still recurs as decoration and reinforces the card template.
8. Long unchanged holds make the absence of audio feel like a broken presentation rather than an intentional silent infographic.
9. Internal paths appear as audience citations in 0204.

The correction from 0648 to 0204 changes provenance, not this diagnosis. The two artifacts share the rejected skeleton.

## Independent visual grammar

Canvas: 1920×1080, 30 fps, dark conference-stage background, no presenter, no music, no atmospheric footage, no full-screen paragraph cards. Audience citations name the survey/release; internal filenames remain in receipts only.

1. **Question aperture** — a sparse title and one-line question over a field of small spiral glyphs; a thin process spine appears along the bottom. No portrait.
2. **Frozen source reservoir** — rows stream into a labeled `Galaxy Zoo 1 data release — Table 2` source node; the `667,944 rows` count remains attached to that node.
3. **Parallel readout funnel** — the source splits into three simultaneous branches, never a false sequential chain. Branch labels and counts reveal while particles continue to flow.
4. **Flag accounting** — the flag branch separates decisive labels from ties with parent-total conservation visible.
5. **Threshold comparison** — the 0.60 and 0.80 branches align on one shared baseline, with counts and the unit `decisively labelled rows` explicit.
6. **Estimator symbols** — two labeled population chips, `N_CW` and `N_ACW`, detach from the active branch and move into a formula workspace.
7. **Numerator construction** — `N_CW − N_ACW` builds term by term and is bracketed as `difference`.
8. **Denominator construction** — `N_CW + N_ACW` builds beneath it and is bracketed as `decisive total`.
9. **Sign rail and lock** — a neutral sign rail explains positive/zero/negative definitions; the formula completes, then a physical lock covers the value field with `VALUE WITHHELD — REVIEW GATES OPEN`.
10. **One-object mirror intervention** — one deterministic spiral crosses a vertical mirror plane and flips continuously; its audience label morphs from `appears clockwise` to `appears anticlockwise`. Persistent label: `CONCEPTUAL — illustration, not data`.
11. **Causal split** — the transformed image feeds two hypotheses: `image-linked → must invert` and `labeling-process → need not invert`. This is a logic diagram, not an outcome.
12. **Bias-control matrix build** — rows reveal one at a time: mirrored image / label inversion; 0.60 and 0.80 thresholds / threshold sensitivity; paired same-object comparison / individual change versus sample composition. Persistent banner: `DESIGN ONLY — OUTCOMES WITHHELD`.
13. **Review-gate timeline** — completed source/method freezes glow cyan; `stored-direction frame`, `independent verdict record`, and `evidence · receipt · referee` remain open amber; a moving pulse stops at a locked `RESULT RENDER` endpoint.
14. **Boundary synthesis** — three vertical regions build from the same timeline: `KNOWN`, `NOT REPORTABLE`, `NEXT GATE`. The process spine persists so the final state is a scientific status map, not a disconnected quote card.
