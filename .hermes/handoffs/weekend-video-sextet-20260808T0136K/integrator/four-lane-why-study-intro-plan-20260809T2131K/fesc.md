## Broad stake to establish first

Reionization is a major transition in cosmic history. Star-forming galaxies can contribute ionizing photons to the intergalactic medium only when some fraction escapes their gas. Escape fraction is therefore a load-bearing term in the symbolic reionization budget and is worth constraining independently of whether this lane later finds any mismatch.

## Result trap

The conditional stake is permitted: if too little ionizing light escaped, galaxies alone could not supply the needed ionization. This remains a question, not this lane's answer.

## Planned opening beats — semantic plan, not narration

1. **Cosmic transition:** visually transform a neutral symbolic intergalactic field into an ionized field without numbers, measured geometry, or a claim about which sources caused it.
2. **Source question:** introduce ordinary star-forming galaxies as a candidate ionizing source and pose the question of whether their photons can reach the intergalactic medium.
3. **Budget structure:** assemble the symbolic factors `UV luminosity density × ionizing-production efficiency × escape fraction`; show no values, relative sizes, or selected factor outcome.
4. **Conditional stake:** show matched, equal-weight possibility cards — sufficient escape would permit a galaxy contribution; insufficient escape would limit it — with `NO OUTCOME SELECTED` and no ordering geometry.
5. **Narrow seam:** reveal that high-redshift escape cannot be measured directly because intervening neutral gas absorbs Lyman-continuum photons; hand off to proxy transport, frozen assumptions, and the existing redshift-sweep method.

The current `galaxy shortfall versus assumption shortfall` framing moves after these beats and remains conditional. It cannot be the first scientific question.
