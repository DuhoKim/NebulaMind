## Current-opening diagnosis

The current opening begins with evolution versus calibration as two explanations for an apparent relation offset. This is the method's disambiguation problem. It does not first give a naive viewer the broader reason galaxy metallicity and the mass–metallicity relation are records of galaxy growth.

## Broad stake to establish first

Metals are produced by stars, moved or expelled by outflows, and diluted by fresh gas. The relation between galaxy mass and gas-phase abundance is therefore an established window onto the baryon cycle: accretion, star formation, feedback, and chemical enrichment. Asking whether that relation remains the same at early times matters even before any evolution or offset is measured.

## Result trap

Never say or imply:

- the mass–metallicity relation evolves;
- high-redshift galaxies are more metal-poor or metal-rich;
- an offset exists or has a sign or magnitude;
- strong-line calibrations fail at high redshift;
- a particular number of direct-Te anchors exists or is insufficient;
- this lane establishes any evolution or null.

The conditional stake is permitted: if the relation changed across cosmic time, that would trace a change in enrichment and gas cycling. Whether local calibrations remain valid at high redshift may be described only as disputed and attributed.

## Planned opening beats — semantic plan, not narration

1. **Metal lifecycle:** show a symbolic cycle linking gas inflow, star formation, metal production, and outflow; no rates, yields, arrows of measured strength, or selected outcome.
2. **Why mass and metallicity belong together:** present stellar mass and gas-phase abundance as two established descriptors connected by a named relation, using non-positional cards rather than a plotted trend.
3. **Conditional history question:** pose whether that relation could differ at early times and why a genuine difference would reveal enrichment history; show `DIRECTION WITHHELD` and no offset geometry.
4. **Ruler problem:** introduce locally calibrated strong-line methods and direct electron-temperature measurements as different rulers whose consistency at high redshift must be tested, not assumed.
5. **Narrow seam:** transition to the need for direct-Te anchors and the existing evolution-versus-calibration method.

The current two-explanation opening moves after the science stake and remains fully conditional.
