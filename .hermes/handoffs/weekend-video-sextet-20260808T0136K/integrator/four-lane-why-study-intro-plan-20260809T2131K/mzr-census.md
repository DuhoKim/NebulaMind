## Current-opening diagnosis

The current opening begins with archive metadata that can look ready in two ways and asks reach versus eligibility. That explains semantic adjudication. It does not first explain why independent reconstruction from public data is itself a scientific-infrastructure question.

## Broad stake to establish first

This is a data-informatics lane, not a metallicity-physics lane. Its stake is whether a published scientific question can be independently reconstructed from findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable records with enough semantic metadata to identify an eligible sample. The public archive is part of the evidence chain, and testing that chain matters even if metadata adjudication were easy.

## Result trap

Never say or imply:

- the archive is inadequate, incomplete, or unusable;
- any table is eligible or ineligible;
- a particular number of records, tables, or candidates exists;
- the census achieved a recall, precision, or adequacy verdict;
- the archive can or cannot answer the underlying science question;
- any mass–metallicity evolution or other physics finding.

The conditional stake is permitted: if the public record could not supply a uniformly eligible sample, an independent reconstruction could not settle the later question from that record as-is. This is a motivation, not this census's finding.

## Planned opening beats — semantic plan, not narration

1. **Evidence chain:** show a paper-level question passing through a public archive and metadata layer to an independent reconstruction; no completion marks or verdict colors.
2. **Why public records matter:** assemble the FAIR properties as equal infrastructure requirements, not a scorecard and not outcomes.
3. **Conditional reproducibility stake:** show matched possibilities — the record could support a uniform reconstruction, or could require additional evidence — with `NO ADEQUACY VERDICT` and no selected branch.
4. **Semantic ambiguity:** demonstrate that the same short symbol can denote different concepts using labelled vocabulary cards, not scientific axes or data points.
5. **Narrow seam:** transition to the existing reach-versus-eligibility semantic adjudication workflow.

Do not introduce metallicity enrichment, offsets, or galaxy-evolution physics as the reason this lane matters.
