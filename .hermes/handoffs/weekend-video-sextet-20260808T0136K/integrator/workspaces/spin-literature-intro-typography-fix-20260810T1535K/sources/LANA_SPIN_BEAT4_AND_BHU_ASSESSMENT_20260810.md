## The three quotable sentences (verbatim — use exactly; do not paraphrase)

1. **Longo (2011) — reported a dipole.** Verbatim abstract:
   > "An unbinned analysis for a dipole component that made no prior assumptions for the dipole axis gives
   > a dipole asymmetry of −0.0408±0.011 with a probability of occurring by chance of 7.9×10⁻⁴."
   Source: M. J. Longo, *Detection of a Dipole in the Handedness of Spiral Galaxies with Redshifts z~0.04*,
   Phys. Lett. B **699**, 224 (2011); arXiv:1104.2815.

2. **Shamir (2012) — reported an asymmetry.** Verbatim abstract:
   > "The results show that the local universe (z<0.3) is not isotropic in terms of galaxy spin, with
   > probability P<5.8×10⁻⁶ of such asymmetry to occur by chance."
   Source: L. Shamir, Phys. Lett. B **715**, 25 (2012); arXiv:1207.5464.
   **Boundary — do NOT include:** Shamir also reports a specific dipole axis (RA/DEC); **omit the axis
   coordinates and any sky-direction graphic.** Reporting that he found an asymmetry is literature;
   putting an axis on screen or implying the universe *has* a preferred direction is the forbidden
   dipole-axis interpretation. Use the asymmetry sentence above; do not quote the paper's title
   ("cosmic parity violation and a dipole axis") on screen.

3. **Land, Slosar, Lintott et al. (2008) — a null after correcting the bias.** Verbatim abstract:
   > "After establishing and correcting for a certain level of bias in our handedness results we find the
   > winding sense of the galaxies to be consistent with statistical isotropy."
   and:
   > "In particular we find no significant dipole signal, and thus no evidence for overall preferred
   > handedness of the Universe."
   Source: K. Land, A. Slosar, C. Lintott, et al., *Galaxy Zoo: the large-scale spin statistics of spiral
   galaxies in the SDSS*, MNRAS **388**, 1686 (2008); arXiv:0803.3247.

# PART 2 — BHU labelled-personal beat: the honesty boundary (ASSESSMENT ONLY — NOT A DRAFT)

**Not authorised to draft.** This is my assessment of *where the line is* if Duho amends his freeze to
allow a labelled-personal mention. Absent that amendment, BHU stays out entirely (forbidden_scope:
"black-hole-universe cosmology support").

## The one distinction that is the whole risk
A sentence is honest only if it gives BHU as **a reason this person got curious**, never as **a reason the
measurement matters**. That is the entire boundary:
- **Personal provenance (stays honest):** why *Duho* became interested. Verbs: "got me interested,"
  "prompted my curiosity," "is a personal interest of mine." First person, explicitly labelled personal,
  past-tense/provenance.
- **Scientific motivation (crosses — forbidden):** any link between BHU and the handedness *result*. Verbs:
  "predicts," "would show," "supports," "is evidence for/against," "tests," "bears on," "connects to,"
  "is a signature of." The moment BHU is tied to what the data could show, it is a scientific driver, and
  the freeze's "no BHU support" is breached.

## Three conditions that must ALL hold for the labelled version to be honest
1. **Explicitly labelled personal and attributed to Duho** — the field's voice must never carry it. Drop
   the personal attribution and it reads as a scientific reason by default.
2. **Explicit dual disclaimer, both clauses present:** (a) *this is not why the field studies galaxy
   handedness*, and (b) *this video tests nothing about it and makes no claim about it*. Omitting either
   lets the mention drift into implied relevance.
3. **Walled off and de-emphasised** — it must not sit among the scientific-motivation beats (isotropy /
   mirror symmetry / angular momentum), and it must not be the climax or the "why it matters" payoff.
   Placement as the emotional peak functionally makes it the driver even if the words are hedged.

## Wording that CROSSES the line (flag any of these on review — reject)
- "Black-hole-universe cosmology predicts / implies a preferred handedness." (predictive linkage)
- "A handedness asymmetry would support / challenge the idea that our universe is a black hole." (evidential
  linkage)
- "This is a test of whether we live inside a black hole." (makes the measurement bear on BHU)
- BHU listed alongside isotropy/parity/angular-momentum as a reason the subject matters. (science-list
  membership)
- "Though not tested here, this connects to black-hole-universe cosmology." ("connects to" re-attaches the
  evidential link the disclaimer just removed — the most likely failure mode)
- Any BHU sentence in the field's voice, or with the personal attribution or either disclaimer dropped.

## Wording that STAYS honest (the safe shape, for reference — not a script)
Pure personal provenance + both disclaimers + walled off, e.g. the *shape*: "This particular investigation
began from a personal interest of mine in black-hole-universe cosmology — that is a personal curiosity, not
a reason the field studies galaxy handedness, and this video tests nothing about it." First person,
labelled, both disclaimers, no linkage verb, not the payoff. (Illustrative shape only; I am not drafting
the copy.)
