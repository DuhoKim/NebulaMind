# Images [\#](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/index.html\#images "Link to this heading")

Images of the sky in the six LSST filters with a variety of calibration levels.

The [schema browser](https://sdm-schemas.lsst.io/) includes tables of image metadata (`ObsCore`).

Image data products are available via the butler, SIA, and TAP services.
See the [Data access](https://dp1.lsst.io/access/index.html) and the [Tutorials](https://dp1.lsst.io/tutorials/index.html) pages to get started with these services.

## Coadd and template images [\#](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/index.html\#coadd-and-template-images "Link to this heading")

Combinations of multiple calibrated images of the same region of the sky to achieve
greater depth (to detect fainter objects),
or for use as templates in difference image analysis.

- [Deep coadd](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/deep_coadd.html)
- [Template coadd](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/template_coadd.html)

## Visit and difference images [\#](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/index.html\#visit-and-difference-images "Link to this heading")

Individual observations are processed and calibrated to produce visit images, and templates are subtracted to produce difference images.

- [Visit image](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/visit_image.html)
- [Difference image](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/difference_image.html)

## Raw exposures [\#](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/index.html\#raw-exposures "Link to this heading")

The unprocessed images received directly from the camera.

- [Raw exposure](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/raw_exposure.html)
