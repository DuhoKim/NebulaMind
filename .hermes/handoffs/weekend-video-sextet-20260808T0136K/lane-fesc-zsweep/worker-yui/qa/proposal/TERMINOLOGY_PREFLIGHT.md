# Paper-naive terminology preflight

This is a conservative worker preflight, not the independent paper-naive verdict.

## Terms already defined adequately

- `Delta`: stated as required minus inferred.
- `closure`: operationally tied to the 16–84% Delta interval spanning zero.
- `z_c=8.045`: tied to the lower interval edge touching zero.
- `z_m=6.328`: explicitly separated as the median crossing.
- 66/83/93%: keyed to z=7/8/9 and Delta greater than zero.
- no-tail 7.615: described as a separate model run with one prior family removed and unpaired draws.
- model versus measurement: persistent chip plus dedicated boundary and closing states.
- escape fraction: S01 defines it as the share of ionizing photons that get out of galaxies.
- finite-Monte-Carlo ranges: S04, S06, and S08 label them as 16–84% resampling bounds rather than observational or total-model uncertainty.

## Terms that may still need a plain-language gloss during integration

1. **Proxy-inferred** — safest gloss: “estimated from other galaxy properties rather than measured directly.” The current S02 explains low-redshift calibration transport, but a very fast viewer may still miss what “proxy” means.
2. **SFRD tail** — narration expands star-formation-rate density; the visual rail still uses the acronym. An integrator can write the term out over two or three lines without changing the science.
3. **Monte Carlo** — S07 says what sits inside and outside the model, but not that Monte Carlo means repeated draws from stated priors. This is optional for a 98-second canary if “40,000 draws per redshift” remains in provenance.

## Integration priority

- The escape-fraction and resampling-label improvements are integrated in v4.
- Proxy/SFRD/Monte Carlo glosses remain minor if narration and visuals remain synchronized.
- None of these terms permits changing the crossing definitions, probability boundary, or exact values.
