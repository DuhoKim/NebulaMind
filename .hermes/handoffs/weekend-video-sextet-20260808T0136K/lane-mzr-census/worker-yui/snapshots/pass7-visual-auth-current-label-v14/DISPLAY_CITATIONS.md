# Display-citation basis

VizieR's foundational service paper is Ochsenbein, Bauer & Marcout (2000), *Astronomy and Astrophysics Supplement Series* 143, 23–32, DOI 10.1051/aas:2000169.[1]

The metadata vocabulary is identified on-screen as IVOA UCD1+ v1.5; the IVOA document describes the controlled terms that build the UCD1+ corpus.[2]
