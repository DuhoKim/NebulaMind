## 2. AM-B — NEYMAN PRIOR ESTIMATOR / SMOOTHING (frozen)

### 2.1 The defect, precisely

The rehearsal-pinned HC-1H harness `handcheck/nm_handcheck.py` **at SHA-256
`cc88fa5ee6e7d7f2ab32ad4b7b0d7d843f9a77ed777c11d259755197eda03bbc`** (the revision every rehearsal
receipt was produced against; recoverable byte-exact as
`git show 199c3168:…/prereg/handcheck/nm_handcheck.py`) computes Neyman weights at **line 3184** as
`N_s · √(a_s(1−a_s))` and **refuses** at **lines 3186–3187**:

```
if total > floor * len(keys) and not any(weights.values()):
    raise HandcheckError("Neyman prior rates yield zero information in every stratum")
```

*(Same guard at line 3205 of the current post-rehearsal working copy, SHA-256
`65c04377734e3cd881bcb4144ae6652c1764429a189f352993033f9639765cd4`. See §3.7 on revision drift.)*

A prior of exactly 0 or exactly 1 makes that stratum's weight vanish; if every prior is degenerate,
the allocator cannot run at all. The rehearsal hit this: at 94.8% recovery, per-stratum empirical
agreement rates saturate at 1.000 in finite cells. It proceeded only by *choosing* Jeffreys
smoothing `(correct + 0.5)/(N_s + 1)` — and the report's own real-run implication (line 103) is
verbatim: *"Freeze the prior estimator/smoothing rule before production rather than selecting it
after seeing synthetic outcomes."*

The frozen text does not specify how the priors are estimated. `LANA_ONE_HUMAN_ATTENUATION_20260814.md`
(SHA-256 `b2590e4213e225f9869fe782cfe0f55d8d8979dcb470752836a5cd31a58453fd`) §1 lines 96–99 says only:
*"Neyman allocation (n_s ∝ N_s·√(a_s(1−a_s))) minimizes σ_a per human label — machines estimate the
allocation from synthetics; a bad allocation costs efficiency, never validity."* The estimator is
a genuine open parameter. It is frozen here.

### 2.2 The three candidates, weighed

**(ii) Fixed uninformative prior `a_s = 0.5` for allocation only.** Every weight becomes
`N_s · 0.5`, so allocation reduces to strictly proportional-to-`N_s`. That discards the entire
purpose of the allocator (LANA §1 role 2: minimize σ_a per human label) and, given the expected
population shape — agree-confident dominating, disagree and low-confidence small — it would send
the overwhelming majority of the 500 real labels into the *easiest* stratum, where `â_s ≈ 1` and
`Var(â_s)` is smallest. It is not merely uninformative; it is anti-informative with respect to the
quantity being minimized. **Rejected.**

**(iii) Allocation ∝ `N_s` alone when priors are degenerate.** Two defects. First, in its branch it
is identical to (ii) and inherits the same anti-informative allocation. Second, and worse, it is a
**branch whose trigger is a realized-data condition** — "did any empirical rate land exactly on 0 or
1?" A rule that changes shape depending on the numbers it sees is exactly the object F-9 exists to
forbid; even though the branch would be pre-declared, a reader cannot verify from the outside that
the branch condition was not steered. **Rejected.**

**(i) Jeffreys smoothing `(c_s + ½)/(m_s + 1)`.** Continuous, total, branch-free, and — this is the
decisive property — **provably never degenerate**: for any integer `0 ≤ c_s ≤ m_s`,

```
1/(2(m_s+1))  ≤  (c_s + ½)/(m_s + 1)  ≤  (2m_s + 1)/(2(m_s + 1))
```

which lies strictly inside (0, 1) for every `m_s ≥ 0`. Hence `a_s(1−a_s) > 0` for every stratum;
combined with `N_s ≥ 30 > 0` guaranteed by AM-C, **every** Neyman weight is strictly positive, and
the zero-information refusal is **unreachable by construction** (independently verified by the
gate, Check 3a). It is not mitigated — it is eliminated. Jeffreys is also the standard
minimal-assumption regularizer (the Beta(½,½) posterior mean), it converges to the empirical rate as
`m_s` grows, and it is the rule the rehearsal exercised end-to-end through the unmodified allocator.
**Adopted.**

### 2.3 AM-B.1 — the frozen rule (binding)

> **The Neyman allocation priors are the Jeffreys-smoothed synthetic sign-recovery rates**
>
> **`a_s^prior = (c_s + ½) / (m_s + 1)`**
>
> **where, for each of the strata surviving AM-C:**
> - **`m_s`** = the number of objects in the **frozen synthetic injection pool** that fall in
>   stratum `s`, binned by the harness's own `_hc1h_strata_from_cutpoints` (pinned revision
>   `cc88fa5e…` lines 686–688) using the frozen committee-state bijection and the frozen |χ|
>   tertile cutpoints computed **once** on the complete accepted real population;

### 2.4 AM-B.2 — the firewall (binding, and the part that matters)

> **The priors used for ALLOCATION are never the values used in the ESTIMATE.**
>
> **`a` is computed per HC-4 (V3 lines 291–303) from human labels alone:
> `a_s = (â_s − ε̂)/(1 − 2·ε̂)` with `â_s` the realized human-vs-instrument agreement, `ε̂` the
> single global synthetic-injection error rate, and `a = Σ w_s·a_s` with POPULATION weights
> `w_s = N_s/ΣN`. `a_s^prior` appears nowhere in `a`, nowhere in `σ_a` (V3 lines 296–297), nowhere
> in `σ_ours`, nowhere in `Â_c`, and nowhere in either F-6 decision region. It appears in exactly
> one place: the integer `n_s`, the number of labels drawn from stratum `s`.**

Why this is not a formality. If the priors leaked into `a`, the machine committee would be inside
the reference — the single failure mode `LANA_ONE_HUMAN_ATTENUATION_20260814.md` §1 (lines 76–89)
identifies as *"quietly poisonous"*: a machine reference inflates `a` **selectively on exactly the
objects where the instrument is wrong**, which switches off HC-5's safety while looking rigorous.
The design's whole defence is that machines get three bounded roles — stratifier, allocator,
diagnostic — **none inside `a`** (LANA lines 90–102). AM-B is an allocator rule and stays there.

**Independently verified in code by the gate (Check 3d):** the flow is
`neyman_prior_rates (JSON) → allocate_neyman → weights → real_allocation {integer n_s} → sealed key
document (records the rates) → labels counted → hc1h_statistics`, and the string `prior` does not
appear in `hc1h_statistics`, `hc1h_verdict`, or anywhere on the estimate path. The priors touch
exactly one thing: the integer `n_s`.

The consequence is that a *wrong* prior is harmless to validity. A stratified estimator with
population weights is unbiased for **any** stratification and **any** allocation, provided sampling
within each stratum is random (LANA lines 91–99). A poor prior therefore costs only efficiency:
`n_s` is misallocated, `Var(â_s)` at the realized counts is larger than it needed to be, and `σ_a`
widens — which makes HC-5's floors *harder* to clear, the safe direction. AM-B cannot bias `a` in
the dangerous direction because it cannot bias `a` at all.

## 3. AM-C — SPARSE-CELL RULE

### 3.1 The defect, precisely

`HC-1H` freezes a floor of **30 real images per stratum** (V3 line 283; LANA line 115). The
allocator enforces it as a hard refusal (pinned revision `cc88fa5e…` lines 3179–3180):

```
if not isinstance(population, int) or population < floor:
    raise HandcheckError(f"stratum {key} cannot satisfy floor={floor}")
```

The frozen text does **not** say what happens when that fires. Today the answer is "the program
crashes," which is not a preregistered outcome.

The rehearsal made the risk concrete (report line 98): *"The first unselected N = 2,000 draw
produced populations 28, 8, and 11 in three cells, so the frozen floor of 30 made full preparation
impossible."* It proceeded only by **engineering** the sample — screening 12,000 deterministic
candidates down to a campaign of exactly 2,000 with ≥ 35 in every cell. Its own real-run
implication is verbatim: *"The real accepted population cannot be engineered this way; if any real
cell has N_s < 30, HC-1H is infeasible and must hold or receive a preregistered sparse-cell rule
before any labels."*

Hwao's projection from the natural draw puts the smallest real cell near **834** — a ~28× margin,
so this likely never binds. But the projection scales committee-state rates measured on
**synthetic** images (rehearsal: `AGREE_CONFIDENT` 87.45%, `DISAGREE` 6.00%, `LOW_CONFIDENCE`
6.55%, report lines 47–49), and the committee has never seen a real image. The margin is softer
than it looks, and in any case the rule cannot be written after the populations are seen.

### 3.2 The three candidates, weighed

**(iii) Proceed with the deficient cell, publishing its per-stratum uncertainty honestly.**
**Rejected**, on three independent grounds. (1) It is not executable: it requires modifying the
allocator's floor guard, which is a gated safety check inside a hash-pinned program, and the
modification would have to be justified by the very condition it is meant to survive. (2) It is
self-defeating: with `N_s < 30` the realized `n_s ≤ N_s`, so `â_s` carries a Wilson half-width of
roughly ±0.18 or worse, and HC-5 floor 2 (`a_s ≥ 0.70`, V3 line 308) is then applied to the
noisiest cell in the design — the most likely outcome is a *spurious* floor trip and
INCONCLUSIVE-BY-POWER anyway, reached by a noisier and less honest route. (3) It collides with
F-10.c's `k ≥ 50` guardrail on ordinary published aggregate cells (V3 lines 178–182) at exactly the
moment P7 publishes that stratum.

**(i) HOLD the hand-check; declare INCONCLUSIVE-BY-POWER.** This is the existing, already-frozen
consequence of every HC-5 failure (V3 line 311: *"Any failure → INCONCLUSIVE-BY-POWER, run does not
start"*), it needs no new code, no new gate, and no new failure surface, and it is what the harness
already does in effect. Its cost is severe and specific: it discards a fully powered measurement
over a **bookkeeping artifact in a stratum carrying negligible population weight**. A cell with 25
members out of ~208,407 contributes `w_s ≈ 0.00012` to `a = Σ w_s a_s`. Voiding the run because
that cell is five members short is a bad trade, and being forced into a bad outcome by an
unconsidered rule is precisely what preregistration exists to prevent.

**(ii) Merge the deficient cell with an adjacent |χ| tertile WITHIN the same committee state.**
Statistically clean: a stratified estimator with population weights is unbiased for **any**
stratification (LANA lines 91–95), so collapsing two cells into one stratum with the combined
population weight leaves `a` unbiased and leaves HC-4 and the σ_a formula untouched — they are
written over "strata," not over "nine." Merging **within** a committee state is the right axis: the
committee state is the informative stratification; the |χ| tertile is the secondary axis, and it is
the one that can be coarsened without destroying the design's purpose. Merging **across** committee
states would destroy it and is forbidden below. **Recommended in principle — and, per Ruling (ii)
and AM-C.3, inoperative until a new gated harness revision exists.**

### 3.3 AM-C.1 — the frozen merge ladder (binding only when AM-C.3's precondition holds)

> **Evaluated exactly once, deterministically, on the complete accepted population, immediately
> after the |χ| tertile cutpoints are fixed and BEFORE any HC-1H preparation call, any blinded
> package, and any human label. No human discretion at any step.**
>
> Let `N(c,t)` be the population of cell (committee state `c`, tertile `t ∈ {0,1,2}`).
> For each committee state `c` in the fixed order `agree-confident`, `disagree`, `low-confidence`:
>
> 1. While some surviving cell of `c` has `N < 30` **and** `c` has ≥ 2 surviving cells:
>    - let `t*` be the deficient surviving cell of smallest population (tie → smallest tertile
>      index);
>    - merge `t*` into the surviving cell of `c` at minimal tertile-index distance (tie → smaller
>      population; tie again → smaller tertile index);
>    - the merged stratum's population is the sum, and its identity is recorded as the ordered set
>      of source tertiles.
> 2. **Merging never crosses a committee state.** No cell of `agree-confident` may ever merge with
>    a cell of `disagree` or `low-confidence`, in any direction, for any reason.
>
> The surviving stratum set `S` has `3 ≤ |S| ≤ 9`. The Neyman total (500) and floor (30) apply
> unchanged over `S`; feasibility is preserved since `30·|S| ≤ 270 < 500`. AM-B's priors are
> computed over `S`. HC-4's `a = Σ w_s·a_s` runs over `S` with population weights, unchanged in
> form. The per-stratum floor `a_s ≥ 0.70` (HC-5.2) is evaluated on `S`.
>
> **AM-C.2 — terminal fallback.** If, after step 1, any committee state has collapsed to a single
> stratum that still has `N < 30`, the run is **HELD** and declared **INCONCLUSIVE-BY-POWER** under
> HC-5 (V3 line 311): no HC-1H package is prepared, no key is sealed, no label is taken, and the
> event is published with the realized populations. At that point an entire committee state is
> essentially empty, which is a statement about the instrument rather than a bookkeeping artifact,
> and the stratifier itself has failed.

**Determinism independently verified by the gate (Check 4a)** by exhaustive simulation rather than
by reading: all 262,144 configurations of {0, 1, 29, 30} across the nine cells, plus 20,000 random
draws. In every case the ladder terminates, conserves population within each state, never strips a
state of its last cell, and leaves all survivors ≥ 30 whenever ≥ 2 cells survive. The tie-breaks are
total; no discretion survives.

### 3.4 What AM-C.1 costs — stated, not minimized

1. **Loss of tertile-resolved diagnostics inside the affected committee state.** P7's per-stratum
   attenuation aggregates and the ε̂_syn,s stratum diagnostics lose resolution there, and HC-5's
   `a_s ≥ 0.70` floor — the design's detector for *difficulty-correlated* human failure (LANA lines
   174–175) — is evaluated on the merged stratum. A failure confined to the deficient tertile is
   therefore diluted rather than tripped. **How much this costs is bounded and small:** the merge
   only ever fires on a cell with < 30 members, so the merged stratum is dominated by its healthy
   partner and the deficient cell was never going to yield a usable `a_s` anyway (`n_s ≤ 29` gives
   a Wilson half-width around ±0.18). The diagnostic being lost is one that could not have been
   measured. It is still a loss, and it is published as one.
2. **The published stratum count changes.** §8 P7 (V3 lines 414–415) reads *"per-stratum attenuation
   aggregates (9 strata, Wilson intervals; never the per-label rows — 850 under HC-1H)."* Under a
   merge, P7 publishes `|S|` strata (3 ≤ |S| ≤ 9) plus the **pre-merge populations and the full merge
   record**. **APPROVED by Ruling (i)** as a shape change, conditional on Ruling (ii) — see §4.
3. **Efficiency:** negligible. Merging moves < 0.02% of population weight.
4. **Implementation: a NEW, separately gated harness revision — not a patch, and not a tuple
