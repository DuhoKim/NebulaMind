# Tori BS-1 licence limb — DESI Legacy DR10.1 South

Recorded: 2026-08-14

## Verdict

**FAIL — validity range not met.**

The validity range is: **“licence permits derived-catalogue publication.”** The public Legacy Surveys page expressly applies CC BY 4.0 to **images**. Its separate scientific-publication section requires an acknowledgement when using **data**, but does not state a licence for the Tractor/sweep catalogues and does not expressly authorize publication of a per-object derived catalogue. That scope gap is material. It is published here as an ambiguity and is not resolved in our favour.

This is a preregistration receipt, not legal advice and not publication authorization. Duho owns acceptance; Kun gates.

## Why CC BY 4.0 does not close the catalogue scope

Primary source: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en [10]

The legal code defines the material in scope verbatim as:

> 06. Licensed Material
>      means the artistic or literary work, database, or other material
>      to which the Licensor applied this Public License.

Its grant then says verbatim:

> 1. **License grant**.
> 1. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Public License,
>        the Licensor hereby grants You a worldwide, royalty-free,
>        non-sublicensable, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to
>        exercise the Licensed Rights in the Licensed Material to:
>       1. reproduce and Share the Licensed Material, in whole or in
>           part; and
> 
>       2. produce, reproduce, and Share Adapted Material.

CC BY 4.0 therefore permits Adapted Material when the underlying material is **Licensed Material**. The Legacy Surveys page applied the licence to “Images”; no primary source located in this audit applies it to the DR10 catalogue. The CC grant cannot itself expand an image-only scope to an unmentioned catalogue.

## Derived-catalogue decision

The proposed artifact would publish measured quantities per object. It is not merely an unchanged Legacy Surveys image and is not merely a scientific paper that cites aggregate findings. Because the official terms found here do not expressly cover the source catalogue or this per-object derived artifact:

- derived-catalogue publication permission: **AMBIGUOUS / NOT ESTABLISHED**;
- BS-1 licence-limb validity range: **FAIL**;
- no derived catalogue may be published under this receipt;
- the repair is an express catalogue/derived-product licence or written confirmation from the relevant rights holder(s), followed by a fresh gate. Acknowledgement alone is not the repair.
