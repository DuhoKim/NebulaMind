## Member A — deterministic symmetrized annular winding tracer

Member A is a training-free geometric tracer, not a neural network. For a finite 128×128 raster it subtracts the median of the outer border, clips negative residual intensity to zero, and uses the fixed geometric centre `(63.5, 63.5)`. It partitions radii 10 through 48 pixels into 12 fixed equal-width annuli. In each annulus it computes the intensity-weighted complex azimuthal mode `C_j = Σ I_p exp(-2 i theta_p)`. Annuli with mode amplitude below `1e-12` are omitted. For each adjacent surviving annulus pair it computes the normalized phase-increment cross product `u_j = Im(C_{j+1} conj(C_j)) / (|C_{j+1}| |C_j|)`, weighted by the geometric mean of the two mode amplitudes. The raw winding score is the negative weighted mean of the `u_j`, or zero when fewer than three annuli survive; the fixed minus sign maps the generator's documented `parity=+1` convention to positive score under the raster's downward-positive row axis. The reported signed score is explicitly antisymmetrized: `chi_A(x) = (q_A(x) - q_A(mirror(x))) / 2`. It accepts a sign only when `|chi_A| > 0.08`; otherwise it abstains. Its accepted sign is `sign(chi_A)`. This construction was chosen because it measures radial winding phase directly, has no learned parameters or label source, differs completely from the CE-ResNet family, and the antisymmetrization wrapper makes mirror sign reversal an algebraic property rather than an empirical hope. There is no random seed because every operation is deterministic; the validation corpus is seeded below.

## Member B — independently trained plain small CNN

Member B is a plain sequential small CNN (a LeNet-style family), deliberately not a residual network and not the CE-ResNet trunk: three convolution blocks with widths 8, 16, and 24, 5×5/3×3/3×3 kernels, stride-2 convolutions, ReLU activations, no skip connections, no batch normalization, adaptive average pooling, and one linear scalar output. Its signed score is `chi_B(x) = (g(x) - g(mirror(x))) / 2`, and it accepts only when `|chi_B| > 0.15`; otherwise it abstains. The accepted sign is `sign(chi_B)`. It is trained from scratch exclusively on frozen-generator synthetic spirals using binary logistic loss on the antisymmetrized score. It receives no primary weights, no real image, no real statistic, no human morphology or chirality label, and no human-derived selection signal. Fresh training seed: integer `20260820`; training corpus: 20,000 balanced synthetics under domain `HC1H-COMMITTEE-B-TRAIN-20260820`, indices 0–19,999; deterministic CPU, one thread, Adam (`lr=0.001`), batch 100, four epochs, epoch permutations from seeds `20260820 + epoch`. This member was chosen because a shallow non-residual convolutional hierarchy can learn pixel morphology independently while remaining architecturally distinct from the primary CE-ResNet; explicit antisymmetrization supplies the required parity identity even though the trunk itself is ordinary.

At the freeze event, the final `state_dict` is serialized once to `member_b_weights_frozen.pt`. The receipt records the file SHA-256 and a canonical SHA-256 over lexicographically ordered parameter names followed by each contiguous little-endian float32 tensor. After serialization and receipt creation, the file is read-only and is never retrained, recalibrated, fine-tuned, pruned, re-exported, or replaced. Any code, architecture, seed, corpus, threshold, or weights change creates a new candidate and requires a new recorded freeze; it must not overwrite this freeze.

## Exact three-state mapping

Each member independently yields `+1`, `-1`, or abstention `0` using the fixed thresholds above. Committee state is exactly:

1. `AGREE_CONFIDENT`: both members are nonzero and have the same sign.
2. `DISAGREE`: both members are nonzero and have opposite signs.
3. `LOW_CONFIDENCE`: at least one member abstains, including one-accept/one-abstain and both-abstain cases.

The mapping is exhaustive and mutually exclusive. No truth label, primary CE-ResNet output, human label, or real-data quantity participates in it.

## Frozen validation plan

Validation uses 10,000 fresh, exactly parity-balanced BS-3 synthetics under domain `HC1H-COMMITTEE-VALIDATE-20260820`, indices 0–9,999, disjoint from training by domain-separated SHA-256 seeds. Generator parameters follow the BS-3 support: parity ±1 alternating by index; pitch uniform 10–40 degrees; inclination uniform 0–60 degrees; peak S/N log-uniform 2–50; arm amplitude uniform 0.5–1.1; phase uniform 0–2π; and the fixed two-arm generator implementation. The training corpus uses the same ranges under its distinct domain. Receipts report, for each member, accepted-sign accuracy, accepted count/coverage, overall correct-sign fraction with abstentions counted as not correct, and failures. The same 10,000 rasters are mirrored by pure index reversal. For each member, the check requires `chi(mirror(x)) == -chi(x)` by numeric value for all 10,000 and an identical mirrored acceptance decision with the opposite accepted sign. Any member with even one antisymmetry or accepted-sign-flip failure is `DISQUALIFIED`; its accuracy is still recorded but the completion marker must be `_HOLD`. The receipt also reports counts and fractions for all three committee states. SymPy is used in a separate receipt to confirm symbolically that `(q(x)-q(Mx))/2` changes sign under involution `M(Mx)=x`.
