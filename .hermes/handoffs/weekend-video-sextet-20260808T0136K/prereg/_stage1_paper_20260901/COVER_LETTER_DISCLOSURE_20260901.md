# COVER-LETTER AI DISCLOSURE — two sentences, per RASTI policy

RASTI requires AI use to be disclosed "both in cover letters to Editors and in
the Methods or Acknowledgements section." The manuscript carries the full
statement (Version A, ratified); this is the cover-letter form, pointing at it.

> **For the cover letter:**
>
> This work was executed by the author directing a set of AI coding agents,
> which drafted text, implemented and verified the pinned software, executed
> the verification and custody operations, and adversarially refereed one
> another; all scientific decisions, the decision to decline a larger
> predecessor design before unblinding, the halt before image analysis, and the
> cryptographic signature freezing the preregistration package were the
> author's. No AI system is an author, and responsibility for the work rests
> with the author alone; a full disclosure of the division of labour appears as
> an unnumbered Methods disclosure subsection in the manuscript.

*(78 words, two sentences.)*

## Placement checklist
