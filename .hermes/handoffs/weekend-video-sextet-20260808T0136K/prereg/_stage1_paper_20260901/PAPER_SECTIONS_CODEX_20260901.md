# A cryptographically frozen, adversarially audited design for galaxy-handedness measurement

## Abstract

Galaxy-handedness studies are unusually vulnerable to choices that can reverse an answer without making a pipeline look broken. We therefore treated handedness as a stringent case for hard preregistration: the population, geometry, power procedure, antisymmetric instrument, absolute-sign convention, gain-gradient counterfactual, and custody rules were fixed before any label was read. The selected geometry had `Var(cos theta) = 0.754664`, compared with the analytic all-sky reference `1/3`; the count-weighted brick-universe value was separately `0.445201` (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:270–278`). In executed pre-image tests, the Stage-P prefix and final re-pass produced `984/1000` and `996/1000` successes respectively (`run/stagep_checkpoints/prefix_05024.json`; `run/stagep_checkpoints/final_repass.json`), against a frozen prose rule of `x >= 962` (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:443–447`). Synthetic machinery produced `1000/1000` antisymmetry identities, `1000/1000` mirror involutions, and maximum absolute residual `0.0`; no real-image path was touched (`run/CODEX_BS6MAP_20260901.md:161`). A fixture-only rehearsal evaluated `5,049 = 99 x 51` cells with `0 flips`; this was not the frozen `invariance_outcome = HELD` and discharged no frozen edge (`gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md:3–15`). No handedness label was read, and this paper reports no handedness measurement. It reports a validated design and instrument, the negative results that stopped weaker designs, and the remaining operational constraint.

## 1. Introduction

The empirical literature makes galaxy handedness a particularly revealing test of research design. Longo’s primary paper reported a dipolar fit from manually classified Sloan images and described randomly mirroring images without a visual cue so that a scanner’s preference would be equally likely to exchange the two labels. The paper’s sign is therefore meaningful only relative to its stated screen and coordinate conventions, not as a free-standing verbal direction ([Longo 2011, primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.2815)).

Land et al. used Galaxy Zoo classifications and reached a different conclusion: after correcting the handedness bias they found winding sense “consistent with statistical isotropy” and no significant dipole. More important for this study is how they identified the bias. Their primary paper compared ordinary and mirrored presentations. If the excess belonged to the images, mirroring should exchange the two class weights; it did not. The authors therefore attributed the excess to a human or interface preference, including possible differences in recognizing the two patterns and button or tutorial effects ([Land et al. 2008, primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3247)). Mirroring was not merely a generic robustness check: it supplied the counterfactual that separated image handedness from the response mechanism. That mechanism is why human accuracy enters the present instrument as calibration rather than being assumed away.

Shamir’s primary analysis changed the annotation route. It transformed galaxy images into radial-intensity plots, identified arm-peak slopes algorithmically, and used an automatically selected catalogue alongside a Galaxy-Zoo-selected catalogue. Thus the positive result it reported was machine-classified at the handedness stage and did not depend on a person pressing a left/right button for each accepted object, although one input route still inherited Galaxy Zoo’s human spiral selection. The paper also tested random mirroring and explicitly discussed selection and survey-pipeline bias as residual possibilities ([Shamir 2012, primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5464)). These are not interchangeable replications: Longo’s guarded manual protocol, Land et al.’s crowd labels and mirrored-bias diagnosis, and Shamir’s radial-intensity algorithm put the consequential uncertainty in different places.

The dispute has persisted for roughly fifteen years because the scarce resource is not simply telescope time. The disagreements concern label behaviour, sign definitions, selection, sky geometry, and the range of analysis choices. A larger catalogue does not settle a result if its footprint has little leverage on the tested axis, and an automated label does not settle it if sign mapping or selection remains adjustable after outcomes are visible.

Ordinary reproducibility practice is insufficient for this parameter. Publishing code, preserving an environment, and obtaining the same output on rerun establish computational repeatability. They do not establish that the output’s positive sign means the same thing in sky coordinates, screen coordinates, mirrored tensors, training labels, calibration labels, and the historical comparator. A single reflection or reversed dictionary can multiply every sign by minus one while row counts, loss curves, permutation distributions, checksums, and reruns remain internally consistent. The error is silent precisely because conventional checks test consistency inside one convention. Detecting it requires an external absolute-sign anchor and an antisymmetry test specified before labels are available. Similarly, rerunning a final selection faithfully does not show that the selection was chosen without seeing the answer.

Our response is to freeze everything decidable in advance and then observe what remains. The package fixes population identity, the tested geometry, per-trial null construction, mirror operator, sign anchor, calibration counterfactual, failure semantics, and custody. Rulings that alter execution order are disclosed with the text they supersede instead of being treated as invisible corrections. Negative gates remain results. This paper is therefore a preregistered design-and-instrument study, not a sky measurement: no handedness label was ever read and no observational handedness statistic is reported.

## 2. Leverage

For a slope-like directional estimator, information depends on the spread of object positions along the tested axis. The relevant design quantity is therefore the selection’s variance in `cos theta`, not its raw object count. The frozen planning selection has `Var(cos theta) = 0.754664`, while the analytic isotropic all-sky reference is `1/3`; the latter follows because `cos theta` is uniform on the unit interval extended symmetrically about zero (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:274–278`; analytic identity recorded in `VERIFIED_NUMBERS_20260901.md`, row 7). The count-weighted full brick universe has the separate value `0.445201`; it describes the available universe and must not be substituted for the adopted selection’s leverage (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:270–273`).

The frozen planning line retained `53,005` objects and reported an equivalent count of `120,002.9` for that geometry (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:274–278`). A later fresh planning execution reports `120,016.65`, calculated as `3 * L_ret`; this is a realized fresh-plan quantity and coexists with, rather than replaces, the frozen-selection value (`run/stagep_plan_20260901.json:52`). After the catalogue-quality cut, the executed mask contained `49,211` objects and its receipt reports `110,982.5`, conventionally rounded to `110,983` (`acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json:9–12`; `PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:487,491`). The three equivalent counts describe successive or distinct artifacts, not competing estimates.

The first negative result came before unblinding. A predecessor offered `208,407 objects` and an image burden of `735.9 GB`, but its `Var(cos theta)` was only `0.0580` and its equivalent count only `36,253` (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:277–278`). Although its population was approximately four times larger than the adopted planning selection, it was rejected before any label was read because the audit found the leverage absent. This is the leverage argument made operational: the larger, more attractive catalogue lost to the smaller footprint because the prespecified geometry, not catalogue scale, governed the decision.

## 3. The preregistration as instrument

The preregistration was constructed as an author–referee exchange rather than a one-pass declaration. Proposed prose and executables were attacked for ambiguous precedence, incomplete schemas, silent convention changes, self-validating fixtures, and failure routes that existed only in prose. A finding had to be repaired, mapped to a surviving limitation, or retained as debt. That process makes the preregistration part of the measurement instrument: it defines which transformations are admissible and what must happen when an assumption fails.

The frozen audit population contains `703 findings` across `84 seat-rounds`. Its disposition split is explicitly heterogeneous: `177` repaired under the strict convention, `192` mapped by citation, and `334` retained as pre-convention audit debt (`gates/KNOWN_DEBT_APPENDIX.md:7–11`). Those categories sum to the audit population, but they do not license the claim that every finding was repaired. The appendix is valuable because it preserves precisely that denominator and its nonuniform treatment.

The known-debt appendix also records limits in the controls used to police its own form. Two FORM-echo limitations were frozen verbatim. The first states: “The changed full-corpus scanner still matches candidates with `[^)]*`, so any backticked `(kind,`-opening candidate containing an interior `)` before its actual closing `)`-backtick is never enumerated; an adjacent nested-parenthesis corruption returned `[]` through shipped `form_check()` for all four forms, contradicting the EVERY-candidate contract while the controls remain green.” The second states: “The changed opening-adjacency predicate compares Python character offsets from the candidate opening to only the kind match's start, not byte-distance to the matched mention span: per-form corrupt candidates opening 896 ASCII bytes after the mention's end evade `form_check()` (while 901-byte UTF-8 gaps can be falsely included), contradicting the declared 900-byte all-candidate boundary.” These quotations are retained as source text, including their embedded figures, rather than asserted as independently sourced study results (`gates/KNOWN_DEBT_APPENDIX.md:636`).

Freezing was cryptographic as well as procedural. The package manifest contains `30 files`; its SHA-256 is `d1be4a3b61975c79f75d6bfafa75e117f69ae86e00dc81ea139a4884f62dc72a` (`P0_PACKAGE_MANIFEST_20260831.txt:1–30`; `P0_FREEZE_SIGNATURE_20260831.md:3`). The detached signature uses `ssh-ed25519` under namespace `nmpr-p0` (`P0_FREEZE_SIGNATURE_20260831.md:5–14`). These mechanisms do not certify scientific truth. They bind later execution and review to an identifiable set of bytes.

Freeze did not mean pretending that later rulings never occurred. A disclosed-supersession rule required an execution-order ruling to name what it replaced and to remain in custody. The signed original was not silently rewritten. This distinction matters when frozen prose and executable order disagree: a reviewer can reconstruct both the rule originally signed and the authority for the subsequent route.

A preregistration that claims completeness invites its unlisted assumptions to masquerade as settled facts. This appendix instead exposes where wording controls were incomplete, where an executable promise was not yet authoritative, and where testimony could not be mechanically replayed. The debt list therefore increases trust in the bounded claims. It changes “everything was checked” into the auditable statement that a defined population was classified under disclosed conventions, with named residue carried forward.

## 4. Methods

### 4.1 Population, release, and sample construction

The population route used Branch B, the pinned DR10.1 branch. The preregistration originally contained a date-gated choice; the principal resolved the branch early by a disclosed ruling, while preserving the record that the original trigger timing had been superseded (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:135–171`; `BS1_EARLY_RESOLUTION_RULING_20260831.md`). This avoided the fiction that an early operational choice had satisfied a later formal date condition.

The executed quality-cut receipt began from the unchanged parent of `65,060` objects and retained `49,211` (`acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json:8–9`). The earlier selection line retained `53,005`; the catalogue-quality cut ran after selection and before sealing, so `53,005 -> 49,211` is attrition between successive populations, not a disagreement between counts (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:274–278,393–403`; `acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json:9–10`).

The same ordering reconciles the brick records only partly, and the remaining failure is retained. The authenticated executed cut mask represents `6,104` unique bricks (`acquire/selected_brickids_cut.txt`; `acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json:15`). A fresh Stage-P planner produced `6,446` candidate bricks, recorded `342 extras`, no missing bricks, `status: FAIL`, and a blocked plan; consequently `6,446` is not interchangeable with the cut mask’s `6,104` (`run/classp_candidates/BS-2s.json:3–6`; `run/stagep_plan_20260901.json:29–50`). The paper reports both because suppressing the failed closure would erase the gate’s operation.

### 4.2 Stage-P exact power

Stage P was specified as an exact per-trial procedure. Every trial was judged against its own `20,000`-permutation null, with no shared reference null in the counting path (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:302–310`). The pass criterion was frozen in prose: across the prescribed battery, `x >= 962`; a receipt may show an outcome but cannot redefine that rule (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:443–447`). Checkpoint success fields were counted directly rather than copied from narrative summaries.

### 4.3 Instrument, sign anchor, and counterfactual

The instrument’s defining property was antisymmetry under the frozen mirror transformation, paired with byte-exact mirror involution. This tests whether the implementation changes sign under the transformation that defines handedness, rather than merely producing plausible aggregate values. An absolute synthetic sign anchor then exercised the named historical convention; its `REPRODUCED-LONGO` token is a convention-path result, never an observation (`run/CODEX_BS6MAP_20260901.md`; `run/classp_candidates/BS-4.json`).

The gain-gradient counterfactual varied the calibration mapping over the ratified gamma grid while holding the appropriate random draw common to its baseline. Mapping and grid were fixed by the ratification record, and the zero-gradient case was the within-draw comparator (`GAMMA_RATIFICATION_20260830.md`; `gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`). The rehearsal used fixtures only and therefore could validate machinery without filling the eventual real-calibration outcome.

### 4.4 Custody

Custody separated the sealed stores, mediator, enumerator, and consumers. Requests were recorded before processing; touches and refusals were committed to an append-only chain; and the enumeration verifier recomputed the relevant event sets rather than accepting a producer’s summary. Verification occurred at five named gates, with predecessor-bound pass records preventing an unreviewed artifact from becoming a downstream premise (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:615–689,1590–1610`; `run/bs2k/mediator/mediator.json`). The mediator carried only the authority and routing material needed for the transition, not a discretionary route around the chain.

## 5. Results

The executed mask retained `49,211` of the `65,060` parent objects (`acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json:8–9`). The frozen selection line’s `53,005` and the mask’s `49,211` are reconciled by the intervening catalogue-quality cut, while the planning/executed brick records remain separately identified as `6,446` and `6,104`, with the fresh-plan closure recorded as failed rather than normalized away (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:274–278,393–403`; `run/stagep_plan_20260901.json:29–50`; `acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json:15`).

The first eligible Stage-P prefix produced `984/1000` successes, and the final re-pass produced `996/1000` (`run/stagep_checkpoints/prefix_05024.json`; `run/stagep_checkpoints/final_repass.json`). These are execution outcomes; the acceptance floor remains the separately sourced frozen prose rule `x >= 962` (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:443–447`). Each trial used its own frozen `20,000`-permutation null, not a shared reference null (`PREREG_SUCCESSOR_DRAFT_V134_20260831.md:302–310`).

On synthetic machinery only, with no real-image path touched, the antisymmetry battery returned `1000/1000` identity passes and `1000/1000` byte-exact mirror involutions, with maximum absolute residual `0.0` (`run/CODEX_BS6MAP_20260901.md:161`). The synthetic absolute-sign anchor returned `REPRODUCED-LONGO` as a convention test, not an observation and not evidence about the sky (`run/classp_candidates/BS-4.json`).

On fixture mask and fixture calibration only, the robustness rehearsal evaluated `5,049 = 99 x 51` cells and found `0 flips`; this machinery result was explicitly not the frozen `invariance_outcome = HELD`, filled no slot, and discharged no BS-6 edge (`gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md:3–15`).

Custody produced a further negative result. Two go-live attempts were voided by post-hoc verification before any downstream consumer used them. One carried a time-unit mismatch; the other exposed disagreement between the escrow-derived public key and the key bound into the mediator and seal (`run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt1/WHY.md`; `run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt2/WHY.md`; `run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`). The important outcome is not that malformed artifacts were emitted, but that emission did not make them authoritative: the consumption barrier caught both. Together with the declined low-leverage predecessor, these halts show the preregistration acting as an instrument rather than a narrative written after success.
