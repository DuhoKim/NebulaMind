# A cryptographically frozen, adversarially audited methodology for galaxy-handedness measurement: a pre-image validation study

## Abstract

Galaxy-handedness measurements are unusually exposed to choices that can reverse or create an apparent signal: a sign convention can invert an answer, while flexible selection and calibration can alter a directional statistic. We therefore treated handedness as a stringent case for hard preregistration. We specified a sampling plan, exact-power procedure, antisymmetric measurement instrument, sign anchor, gain-gradient counterfactual, and cryptographic custody chain; subjected the specification and its executables to adversarial review; froze the resulting package; and executed only the pre-image validation stage. On the authenticated final mask, independently counted checkpoint records gave 984 successes in 1,000 trials for the prefix battery and 996 in 1,000 for the final re-pass, exceeding the frozen floor of 962 successes (`run/stagep_checkpoints/prefix_05024.json`; `run/stagep_checkpoints/final_repass.json`; `run/classp_candidates/BS-5p.json`). Synthetic instrument tests gave 1,000/1,000 bit-exact antisymmetry identities and 1,000/1,000 byte-exact mirror involutions, with maximum residual 0.0 (`run/CODEX_BS6MAP_20260901.md`). A fixture-only robustness rehearsal evaluated 99 draws at 51 gain-gradient values (5,049 evaluations) and found no verdict flip relative to each draw's own zero-gradient baseline (`gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`). The process also exposed a dependency cycle and voided two defective go-live attempts before downstream consumption (`run/BS6_CYCLE_RULING_20260901.md`; `run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt1/WHY.md`; `run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt2/WHY.md`). This is not a handedness detection or a cosmological result: no galaxy image was analysed for science, no handedness statistic χ was measured, and the calibration parameter γ̂ remains unmeasured (`run/STAGE_ONE_TERMINAL_20260901.md`). The image-analysis half was designed but not executed because its real-human calibration requirement could not be met (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/STAGE2_CLOSED_20260901.md`). The contribution is a receipt-backed validation of the pre-image methodology and an empirical account of where a rigid preregistration correctly stops.

## 1. Introduction

This paper presents a preregistered methodology and validation study for a future galaxy-handedness measurement. Its contribution is not an estimated asymmetry, but a tested method for specifying, freezing, auditing, and executing the decisions that precede image analysis. In particular, we validate the sample construction, exact-power machinery, instrument identity, absolute-sign anchor, gain-gradient counterfactual, and custody protocol, and we report the boundary at which the unexecuted image-analysis design requires new resources and a successor preregistration.

Spiral handedness is a natural stress test for preregistration. A convention connecting screen coordinates, celestial orientation, winding direction, and the sign of the reported statistic must remain consistent across acquisition, transformation, inference, calibration, and comparison. An unnoticed reflection or sign change can reverse the conclusion while leaving otherwise plausible outputs. Similarly, freedom to alter catalogue cuts, sky coverage, calibration strata, or robustness ranges after inspecting outcomes can change the effective population or manufacture spatial structure. These risks motivate a protocol in which operational definitions are executable, their identities are pinned, negative outcomes halt rather than invite improvisation, and deviations remain visible.

The study is intentionally bounded. It validates pre-image machinery and records its failures under adversarial use. It does not analyse science images, estimate χ, estimate γ̂, claim parity violation, reproduce a cosmological signal, or complete the end-to-end pipeline (`run/STAGE_ONE_TERMINAL_20260901.md`). Synthetic controls that reproduce a named sign outcome are tests of conventions and code paths, not observations of the sky.

## 3. Methods

### 3.1 Population and release choice

The preregistration offered a date-gated release choice. The principal directed early use of the already available Branch B product, DR10.1, before the frozen fallback date; the disclosed ruling records that this superseded the trigger timing while preserving the branch-invariance requirement (`BS1_EARLY_RESOLUTION_RULING_20260831.md`). The executable itself remained frozen and continued to refuse a formal Branch-B closure stamp before 2026-09-05, so the work proceeded on Branch B without falsifying the receipt date (`run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`). The banked provenance candidate binds the branch name, query and output digests, product `ls_dr10.photo_z`, and join columns (`run/classp_candidates/BS-1b.json`).

### 3.2 Sample construction

The acquisition record begins with 65,060 selected parent rows. The frozen quality predicate required `flux_ivar_r > 8.4000532`, `psfsize_r < 1.5699703`, and `nobs_r ≥ 3`; it retained 49,211 objects in 6,104 bricks, with output SHA-256 `a20682c114508dbdd18ede6a56c61509ea9c16784aaca7eee61f76bf97cdd372` (`acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json`). Query texts, chunk receipts, combined receipt lists, selected catalogues, and selected-brick lists remain under `acquire/` (`acquire/positions_query.adql`; `acquire/quality_query.adql`; `acquire/positions_receipts.json`; `acquire/quality_receipts.json`).

Planning operated on the authenticated release universe rather than recycling the post-cut brick mask. The retained traversal reached its power-planning prefix at prefix 5,024, after which the final quality cut removed bricks emptied by the predicate. The apparent mismatch between a planning set of 6,446 bricks and the final 6,104-brick mask was therefore containment plus quality attrition, not evidence that the final mask had to equal the planning output (`run/stagep_plan_20260901.json`; `run/AGY_CLOSURE_REVIEW_20260901.md`). Traversal, power, and final-selection candidates were separately serialized as `BS-2o`, `BS-5p`, and `BS-2s` (`run/classp_candidates/BS-2o.json`; `run/classp_candidates/BS-5p.json`; `run/classp_candidates/BS-2s.json`).

### 3.3 Stage-P exact power

Stage-P assessed whether the design would meet its prespecified success criterion on the authenticated mask. The promised route judged every trial against its own independently recomputed 20,000-permutation null; a shared reference null was prohibited in the counting path (`run/CODEX_STAGEP_BUILD_20260901.md`). Each checkpoint contains a `results` array rather than a prose total. We counted success fields directly in each file. The full battery contained 1,000 trials, and the frozen acceptance floor was 962 successes (`run/stagep_checkpoints/prefix_05024.json`; `run/stagep_checkpoints/final_repass.json`; `run/classp_candidates/BS-5p.json`).

An early closure check blocked the route because it incorrectly required the planning output to equal the post-quality-cut mask. The later closure analysis established the correct containment relation and authorized the battery (`run/CODEX_STAGEP_BUILD_20260901.md`; `run/AGY_CLOSURE_REVIEW_20260901.md`). We retain both records because the transition from refusal to execution is evidence about the gate, not an inconvenience to erase.

### 3.4 Instrument identity and antisymmetry

The frozen instrument was identified by a weights-file SHA-256 of `83008c1cbdae511af5d30020540e1e281c62c2bd95d3cb05527fc0687bf49e6d` and threshold τ = 4.4006456017494235 (`run/CODEX_BS6MAP_20260901.md`). Correctness was defined by the antisymmetry identity `χ(mirror(x)) = -χ(x)`, coupled to exact mirror involution. This is stronger than comparing approximate aggregate predictions: it checks the sign-changing transformation at the instrument boundary. Tests were synthetic only; the real image path was not touched (`run/CODEX_BS6MAP_20260901.md`).

An absolute-sign anchor complemented the relative identity. A fresh frozen-code fixture run required the negative control to return `INCONCLUSIVE` and the powered positive control to return `REPRODUCED-LONGO`; the candidate stores the exact sign-convention sentence and both fixture outcomes (`run/classp_candidates/BS-4.json`). These labels refer only to synthetic controls. They do not mean that the study reproduced an observational result.

### 3.5 Robustness machinery

The counterfactual machinery perturbed the calibration through a gain-gradient parameter γ under a confirmed Mapping A. The ratified grid ran from −0.25 to +0.25 with spacing 0.01, placing γ = 0 exactly at grid index 25 (`gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`). Common random numbers were used within each draw so that the perturbed outcomes could be compared to that draw's own γ = 0 baseline. The receipt binds the mapping identity and outcome-matrix digest (`gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`).

The broader calibration rehearsal composed seven stages on synthetic data: pinned-root loading, boundary production, stratum production, hand-check allocation, replay, mapping self-test, and terminal ceremony (`gates/CALIBRATION_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`). No rehearsal supplied γ̂, real human accuracy, or a science outcome.

### 3.6 Cryptographic custody

Custody separated provisioning, mediation, event enumeration, verification, and terminal review. The live chain began with an epoch-opening and a Row-A seal signed under the enumerator key. The run applied an enumerate–verify–open discipline through five gates, with independent review before artifacts could be consumed downstream (`run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`). The governing rule was byte derivability: store and event schemas, keys, rosters, digests, and transition evidence had to trace to signed or subsequently receipted bytes.

The custody mechanism was tested in operation, not only by a happy-path rehearsal. Two go-live attempts were preserved as void. In the first, a monotonic reading was recorded in milliseconds where the frozen field required a nanosecond count quantized modulo 1,000,000 ns (`run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt1/WHY.md`). In the second, the signature verified only against the public key derived from escrow shares, while the public key bound into the mediator and seal belonged to a different key generation (`run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt2/WHY.md`). In both cases post-live verification intervened before any downstream verifier consumed the chain (`run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`). The successful opening used epoch 1, predecessor 0, and a 26,000,000 ns quantized reading; its Row-A signature and four-way public-key coherence were independently verified (`run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`).

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Sampling and exact-power validation

The quality predicate retained 49,211 of 65,060 parent rows across 6,104 bricks (`acquire/quality_cut_receipt.json`). Direct counting of the checkpoint arrays gave 984 successful trials out of 1,000 at the planning prefix and 996 out of 1,000 on the final re-pass. Both exceeded the frozen floor of 962 (`run/stagep_checkpoints/prefix_05024.json`; `run/stagep_checkpoints/final_repass.json`; `run/classp_candidates/BS-5p.json`). Because each trial used its own 20,000-permutation null, these counts validate the implemented exact-power route rather than the superseded shared-null shortcut (`run/CODEX_STAGEP_BUILD_20260901.md`).

### 4.2 Instrument and sign validation

The antisymmetry battery passed 1,000/1,000 bit-exact identity tests and 1,000/1,000 byte-exact mirror involutions, with maximum absolute residual 0.0 and overall verdict `PASS` (`run/CODEX_BS6MAP_20260901.md`). The fresh synthetic sign battery returned `BATTERY-SIGN: PASS`; its powered positive control returned `REPRODUCED-LONGO` (`run/classp_candidates/BS-4.json`). These are synthetic correctness results only.

### 4.3 Robustness and composition validation

The robustness rehearsal comprised 99 draws across 51 γ values, or 5,049 evaluated cells. It found zero verdict flips against each draw's own γ = 0 baseline (`gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`). Its scope caveat is load-bearing and is quoted here exactly: “HELD means only that no flip was found ON THE EVALUATED GRID; it is a machinery statement about fixture data, is NOT invariance_outcome = HELD, fills no slot, and discharges no BS-6 edge.” (`gates/CALIBRATION_ROBUSTNESS_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`). The separate seven-stage synthetic chain passed end to end (`gates/CALIBRATION_REHEARSAL_RECEIPT_20260831.md`).

### 4.4 Custody and adversarial audit

The custody epoch opened only after two defective go-live attempts were voided and preserved. The defects were a time-unit mismatch and divergence between escrow-derived and bound public keys; neither chain was consumed (`run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt1/WHY.md`; `run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt2/WHY.md`; `run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`). The accepted Row-A seal was independently verified (`run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`).

Across the signed text-referee record, 84 seat-rounds represented 703 findings. Of these, 177 have strict per-finding repaired dispositions, 192 are mapped by citation, and 334 remain explicit pre-convention audit debt (`gates/KNOWN_DEBT_APPENDIX.md`). This audit found consequential defects rather than stylistic disagreements: an executable/text power-test conflict, a dependency cycle linking robustness to image access, checks that passed after catching unrelated exceptions, fixture controls that tested their own construction rather than the production behavior, incomplete or drifting schemas, paraphrases presented as quotations, and missing failure routes (`gates/KNOWN_DEBT_APPENDIX.md`; `gates/AGY_HARNESS_VERIFY_20260831.md`; `run/BS6_CYCLE_RULING_20260901.md`). The correct result is therefore not “all defects repaired,” but a frozen method whose repaired, mapped, and unresolved parts are distinguishable.

## 5. The pre-image boundary

The stage-one stop is a substantive methodological result. The frozen science estimator requires a human-calibration term, `Â_L = β̂/(2â−1)`. Here â must be estimated from a human handedness reference on real accepted objects, corrected using that same human rule's known-answer synthetic error. Machine output or known-answer synthetics alone cannot replace the real-object agreement term (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/STAGE2_CLOSED_20260901.md`; `../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/CODEX_LOW_HUMAN_OPTIONS_20260901.md`).

The allocation requires three calibration bins crossed with nine inherited strata. With a floor of 10 labels per live cell and 30 per live stratum, the all-live minimum is `9 × max(30, 3 × 10) = 270` real labels (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/CODEX_LOW_HUMAN_OPTIONS_20260901.md`). That arithmetic is a coverage constraint, not merely a precision preference: lowering the budget below the floor removes strata or bins and changes the population supported by the result.

Every identified route was costed. A single independent volume checker would have to complete the inherited stream of 850 presentations—500 real objects, 200 blind synthetics, and 150 mirrored repeats—while the available principal could perform only a small protocol audit, not population calibration (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/CODEX_LOW_HUMAN_OPTIONS_20260901.md`). No such checker was available (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/STAGE2_CLOSED_20260901.md`). A three-person-majority panel requires, at the 270-real-object floor, 1,860 decisions and at least 38 people if each is capped at 50 decisions; the inherited real-object budget requires 2,550 decisions and 51 people under the same cap (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/CODEX_PANEL_DESIGN_20260901.md`). These are optimistic lower bounds before replacements or dropout.

Existing external labels did not close the gap. The external-label review found that modern Galaxy Zoo releases report winding tightness rather than direction. The one release containing chirality votes lacked defensible coverage of the accepted DR10.1-south population, known-answer controls for the required human-error correction, and an independently anchorable mapping from its screen-relative sign to the study's East-of-North convention. A large DESI catalogue contained model predictions rather than the human reference required inside â (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/CODEX_EXTERNAL_LABELS_20260901.md`).

Finally, loosening the label floors did not yield the same study at lower precision. Budgets below 120 decisions supported only interface checks or restricted-stratum pilots; the 120-decision case could at most support a prospectively redefined two-stratum upper-limit or calibration-feasibility study, not the original nine-stratum claim (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/CODEX_LOOSENING_COST_20260901.md`). The image half therefore closed without science-image analysis. No χ was measured and γ̂ remains unmeasured (`../_stage2_image_analysis_20260901/STAGE2_CLOSED_20260901.md`).

## 6. Discussion

The validation succeeded in the sense appropriate to a hard preregistration: it produced green tests where the machinery was defined and an enforced stop where it was not. The sample and exact-power route passed their prespecified receipts; the antisymmetric instrument and synthetic sign anchor behaved exactly; the fixture robustness and composition chains ran; and custody defects were intercepted before consumption. None of these statements implies a handedness detection.

Several failures illustrate what ordinary, softer practice might miss. First, the frozen text contained a circular dependency: a real robustness receipt was required before image access, but the receipt needed calibration that could only be measured after image access and hand checking. The run did not infer a placeholder γ̂. It disclosed the cycle and relocated the real sweep by ruling while preserving the rule that a failed sweep halts (`run/BS6_CYCLE_RULING_20260901.md`). Second, the custody ladder caught both a plausible-looking unit error and a subtler key-generation divergence after emission but before use (`run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt1/WHY.md`; `run/bs2k/chain/ABORTED_golive_attempt2/WHY.md`). Third, adversarial review showed that some fixtures passed for the wrong reason: broad exception handling converted unrelated crashes into apparent successes, and a purported identity fixture tested ordinary dictionary reference behavior rather than the harness binding (`gates/AGY_HARNESS_VERIFY_20260831.md`). Fourth, appendix review repeatedly found excerpt-versus-full-quotation and punctuation-fidelity failures before the debt population was accurately surfaced (`gates/V117_APPENDIX_REVIEW_CODEX.md`; `gates/V117_APPENDIX_REVIEW_GPT56.md`; `gates/V118_APPENDIX_REVIEW_CODEX.md`; `gates/V118_APPENDIX_REVIEW_GPT56.md`).

The costs are also real. The adversarial loop was long, generated machinery whose own audit surface expanded, and did not converge every historical finding to the same disposition standard (`gates/KNOWN_DEBT_APPENDIX.md`; `gates/KGATE_PREREG_NECESSITY_20260830.md`). Freeze protected against opportunism but also made a contradiction between an executable and a disclosed ruling impossible to repair locally; the formal Branch-B receipt retained its date gate even after the scientific branch choice had been made (`BS1_EARLY_RESOLUTION_RULING_20260831.md`; `run/RUN_STATE_20260831.md`). A future implementation should freeze fewer duplicated normative surfaces, require production-path mutation tests rather than self-referential fixtures, separate scientific invariants from custody implementation details, and settle human-calibration recruitment before freezing the image phase.

The broader lesson is not that cryptographic custody guarantees scientific truth. It guarantees a more limited and useful property: changes, missing derivations, and failed transitions become harder to conceal. Scientific validity still depends on population coverage, calibration transport, sign conventions, and appropriate uncertainty. Here those requirements led to an honest non-result at the image boundary. That refusal is part of the validated methodology.
