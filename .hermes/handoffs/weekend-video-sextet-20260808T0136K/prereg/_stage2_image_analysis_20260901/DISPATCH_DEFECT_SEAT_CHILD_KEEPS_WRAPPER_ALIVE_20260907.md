# DISPATCH DEFECT — a seat's leftover child keeps the referee wrapper alive; process absence becomes undecidable (filed 2026-09-07 06:43 KST; Blanc 06:35 item 4)

**Observed (V32 seat A, agy).** Report `AGY_SELRULE_V32_SEATA.md` complete and byte-stable at 5,347 bytes from 05:58:07; the seat process at 0.0% CPU; its ONLY child `tail -f test_output.log` (a log follower agy left running); the wrapper (pid 63711) therefore could not exit before its 90-minute print-timeout (~07:17). The lane's read rule — read a seat's report after its process exits — could not fire; the read was made under a recorded exception (`V32_REVIEW_OUTCOME_20260907.md`). This will recur for any seat that leaves a follower behind.

**Cause.** `nm_referee_dispatch.sh` v1 (sha256 prefix fd3aa3b9) ran the seat in the foreground and judged completion by process exit alone.

**Fix (v2, sha256 prefix fd3aa3b9433c8d6a, in place at `/Users/duhokim/HermesOps/scripts/nm_referee_dispatch.sh`; v1 retained beside it as `_nm_referee_dispatch_v1_RETAINED_20260907.sh`).** The seat runs in ITS OWN PROCESS GROUP (`perl -e 'setpgrp(0,0); exec @ARGV'`). A WATCHDOG judges liveness on the seat's OWN WRITING: once the report exists, carries the expected `ACCESS_SHA=` line and a `VERDICT:` line, and has been byte-stable (size and mtime) for STABLE_SECS (default 120 s), the whole process group is terminated (TERM, then KILL after 10 s) and the action is logged: `SEAT DONE BY REPORT STABILITY … terminating its process group <pgid> (pids: …)`. A seat that is still writing is never killed; a seat that has finished is never waited on for a stray child. Every other guarantee of the wrapper (ACCESS PROOF prepended, digest computed by the wrapper, quarantine + exit 3 on a missing proof) is unchanged.

**Controls (kept in `scripts/dispatch_fixtures/`).** A FAKE seat (`fake_seat_leaves_tail_f.sh`) writes a valid report (ACCESS_SHA + VERDICT) and then runs `tail -f` forever — the observed defect. (1) v1 wrapper + fake seat under a 150 s alarm: HUNG until the alarm (exit 142, SIGALRM), the report present but never read — the defect reproduced (`control_v1_wrapper_hung_150s.log`). (2) v2 wrapper + the same seat with STABLE_SECS=30: exited on its own after 40 s with `SEAT DONE BY REPORT STABILITY` and `ACCESS PROVEN`, no leaked `tail` process (`control_v2_wrapper_exited_40s.log`). Both logs retained.

**Scope.** This wrapper is shared tooling under Duho's 09-03 referee-dispatch rule; the change adds a completion criterion, removes none, and is disclosed to Blanc. The V32 seat A wrapper already running under v1 is left to its own timeout (not killed; no live review interrupted).
