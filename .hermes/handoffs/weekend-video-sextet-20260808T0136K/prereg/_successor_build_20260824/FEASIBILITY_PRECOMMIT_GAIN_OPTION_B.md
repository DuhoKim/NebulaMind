## The reading, fixed now

Let **f\*** be the smallest adversarial flip fraction at which the production verdict changes.

| outcome | reading — decided before seeing the number |
|---|---|
| **f\* ≥ 0.10** | **B is viable as a gate.** A verdict that survives an adversarial flip of a tenth of all signs is not fragile, and B can carry the control. |
| **0.01 ≤ f\* < 0.10** | **MARGINAL.** B is technically a gate but its passability depends on where γ's bound actually lands, so the bound must be measured before B is frozen. Not a rejection of B; a dependency. |
| **f\* < 0.01** | **B is not a gate.** If flipping under one percent of signs turns the verdict over, an adversarial construction fails for essentially any bound, and a gate that structurally cannot pass is not a gate. **A becomes the live candidate, and its seed or quantile policy becomes the next question.** |
