# OPEN QUESTION — "by construction" cannot be established by sampling, and three others

**Raised 2026-08-29 20:2x KST by Hwao. V55 repaired three of the eight V54 findings.**

## 1. The status is named for a proof and promoted by a sample (GPT56-V54 F2, CODEX-V54 F1, both seats)

CODEX states it exactly: the bar *"lets non-exhaustive measurement-only sampling establish a status
defined as impossible by construction."* **`UNREACHABLE-BY-CONSTRUCTION` asserts impossibility, and no
finite execution count can establish impossibility.** Basis (i) — measurement alone — is incoherent
with the status's own name, and my brief asked the seats whether the bar could be satisfied literally
and still be wrong. **Both said yes.**

Three ways out, and each changes what the principal ruled:

- **Drop basis (i).** Promotion requires a structural proof; measurement becomes corroboration only.
  Honest, and it may leave the category permanently empty — which is a real answer.
- **Rename the status** to something sampling can support (`UNREACHABLE-IN-PRACTICE`), keeping (i) but
  no longer claiming impossibility. *Cost:* a weaker claim, and the name is what made the category
  safe to have.
- **Keep both and state the asymmetry** — (iii) proves, (i) only indicates. *Cost:* two grades of one
  status is the third-category problem again, one level down.

**My reading: drop basis (i).** Nothing currently holds the status, so nothing is lost today, and a
category that cannot be wrongly populated is worth more than one that can. **But it narrows a ruled
rule, so it is not mine.**

## 2. Post-unblinding decision failures are double-claimed again (GPT56-V54 F1, HIGH)

clause claims *"permutation/statistic/protocol non-finite/degenerate failures"* post-unblinding. **V50
added the VOID antecedents to the class rule's precedence to fix exactly this, and it has recurred**,

**This is the second occurrence of the same defect**, so I am not attempting a third precedence patch.

claims — which changes what §5's VOID clause covers, and is normative.
