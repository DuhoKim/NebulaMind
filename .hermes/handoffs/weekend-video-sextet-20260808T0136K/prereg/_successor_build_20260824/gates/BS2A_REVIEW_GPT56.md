# BS-2a acceptance-design referee — GPT56

## Verdict

**NOT CLEAR.** The draft correctly refuses `abs(chi_net)` as confidence, separates the pre-image design slot from the realized partition, gives the 65,060-parent partition a genuine set-equality contract, and puts the operative threshold in one proposed home. But the central outcome-blindness defect is not closed. The proposed replacement quality channel need only be mirror-even, which still permits it to equal or proxy handedness amplitude; the draft itself admits that a pixel model can reconstruct that amplitude. The first three predicates likewise consume runner/verifier-authored per-object assertions and χ-bearing cutout digests rather than independently proving the asserted facts. Retry eligibility can be created selectively after inference by withholding a completed receipt. Finally, the strongest blindness fixtures hold the acceptance receipt fixed while mutating the sealed result, so they cannot detect an outcome-bearing producer. These are mechanism failures, not missing labels. BS-2a must remain REFUSED / UNFILLED and BS-6 blocked.

## Numbered findings

### F1 — BLOCKER — R5's proposed quality prerequisite does not make confidence incapable of carrying the outcome

**Clause / field at issue.** R5, lines 152–178; R6, lines 184–207; fixtures `BS2A-MIRROR-EVEN`, `BS2A-SIGN-SWAP-INVARIANT`, and `BS2A-AMPlITUDE-NOT-QUALITY`, lines 640–650; residual risks 2–3, lines 808–817. Fields: `quality_score`, `quality_receipt_digest`, `quality_design_digest`, and `QUALITY_MIN`. Producer: proposed `quality_score_v1()`.

**Why it fails as a promise.** Bit-exact mirror invariance excludes a signed scalar, but it does not exclude handedness amplitude. The draft's own current counterexample is decisive: `abs(chi_net(x))` is mirror-even whenever `chi_net(mirror(x)) = -chi_net(x)`. A new pixel model can also reconstruct a monotone proxy for `abs(chi_net)` without calling a symbol named `chi_net` or receiving a stored χ value. The source/call-graph prohibition therefore does not establish the required information-flow property. The draft concedes exactly this at lines 814–817, leaving the target and training-data constitution for a future design. Calling that a residual risk is too weak: it defeats the brief's most important requirement, that the confidence quantity be incapable of carrying or correlating with handedness as an instrument outcome.

The proposed fixture also attacks only sign symmetry. It does not require invariance while handedness amplitude varies, and finite synthetic examples cannot prove that an unrestricted pixel-consuming model lacks an amplitude proxy. Thus option (1), as presently specified, can reproduce the original `abs(chi_net)` defect one level down while satisfying every named field, range, mirror, digest, and threshold rule.

**Smallest sufficient repair.** REFUSE a pixel-derived or post-inference confidence channel under this contract unless its admissible inputs are narrowed to a frozen pre-inference, non-image quality witness whose producer cannot access pixels, χ-bearing digests, instrument outputs, geometry, or outcome-trained parameters. Pin the exact input schema, producer, calibration population, target, training constitution (if any), numeric threshold, and end-to-end invariance tests before the first image byte. If scientifically useful confidence requires pixels or instrument features from which amplitude can be reconstructed, remove `CONFIDENCE_BELOW_THRESHOLD` and redo calibration/power for all finite outputs; mirror-evenness alone is not a sufficient repair.

### F2 — BLOCKER — the cutout predicates expose χ-bearing verification oracles and lack an independent expected-byte truth

**Clause / field at issue.** R2, lines 60–74; replacement §2.7(2)–(3), lines 234–253; `AcceptanceEvidenceV1.expected_cutout_sha256` and `.actual_cutout_sha256`, lines 341 and 347; nullability rule, lines 370–374; residual risk 4, lines 819–823. Producers: the BS-9 canonical cutout producer and `verify_cutout_bytes_v1()`.

**Why it fails as a promise.** Both checksums are digests of the image bytes. The immediately preceding §6 referee finding established that such digests are χ-bearing verification oracles; moving them into a narrow dataclass does not make them non-outcome fields. The acceptance worker is expressly allowed to receive and compare both values. A known-image or candidate-image lookup can recover outcome information from a digest even if the worker never opens the cutout. The design therefore violates its own R6 claim that the worker's inputs are incapable of carrying handedness information.

The expected digest is also not shown to be independent. It is declared by the same canonical cutout producer whose output is later verified. No ordering or immutable external witness requires the expected digest to precede the actual bytes. A producer can emit selected bytes and the matching “expected” digest, making `actual == expected` a self-consistency check rather than byte-integrity evidence. The draft admits that a universal independent expected-byte witness is unavailable, but still treats the first reason family as repaired.

**Smallest sufficient repair.** Keep image digests inside a pinned, mediated byte store. Define an independently witnessed expected-byte source or, if none exists, stop claiming expected/actual equality proves integrity and specify the narrower transport/completeness property that can actually be proved. Pin declaration order so the expected witness is immutable before the corresponding actual-byte verification. Export to the acceptance worker only the exact enumerated predicate result plus an authenticated proof/receipt bound to the parent ID and source object; do not export either χ-bearing digest. The verifier and its proof checker must be named, digest-pinned, hermetic, and required to refuse absent, late, self-issued, mismatched, duplicate, or extra-parent evidence.

### F3 — BLOCKER — R4 recomputes decisions from assertions, but does not independently recompute the truth of several assertions

**Clause / field at issue.** R2 lines 63–74; R4 lines 103–135; schema lines 341–365; predicate implementation lines 470–510. Fields: `actual_tensor_shape`, `actual_dtype`, `instrument_attempt_count`, `selected_attempt_index`, `execution_state`, `output_present`, `output_finite`, and the three quality fields. Producers: cutout verifier, BS-9 instrument runner, attempt-manifest producer, and future quality producer. Verifier: `validate_acceptance_partition_v1()`.

**Why it fails as a promise.** The shown validator recomputes `P_SHAPE`, `P_EXEC`, and `P_QUAL` by reading typed row fields. It does not recompute shape/dtype from the actual cutout; it does not recompute completion/presence/finiteness from an independently retained raw execution event; and it does not recompute quality from the quality producer's permitted inputs. `require_attempt_joins()` and receipt-digest matching can establish that an assertion appears in a manifest, not that the assertion is true. A runner that sees the outcome can still write `NO_COMPLETED_RECEIPT`, `output_present=False`, `output_finite=False`, or a selected attempt state for an unwanted object and make the manifest agree. Canonical serialization and digest closure faithfully preserve the false assertion.

This repeats the original hole with lower-level labels: terminal `EXCLUDED / low confidence` has been replaced by runner-authored booleans and receipt states that deterministically produce the same exclusion. The draft constrains the acceptance worker more strongly than the producers that author its decisive evidence.

**Smallest sufficient repair.** For every predicate, name one immutable source event and one independent deterministic verifier that derives the exported predicate from that source. The cutout verifier must derive shape/dtype from mediated bytes; a supervisor must derive attempt/completion state from an append-only launch/exit/output log; a pinned in-store output verifier must derive presence/finiteness from the committed instrument output before any operator can inspect or suppress it; and the quality proof checker must rerun or verify the frozen quality computation from its permitted inputs. `validate_acceptance_partition_v1()` must reject any evidence row lacking those externally pinned proofs and must not accept runner-authored truth bits merely because their digests and joins are self-consistent.

### F4 — BLOCKER — the retry contract permits outcome-selective suppression of a completed attempt

**Clause / field at issue.** Replacement §2.7(5), lines 266–272; retry fixtures, lines 607–616; choices 9–10, lines 770–777. Fields: `execution_state`, `instrument_attempt_count`, `selected_attempt_index`, `execution_receipt_digest`, and the attempt manifest. Producer: BS-9 instrument runner / attempt-manifest producer.

**Why it fails as a promise.** “Retry only for process-level absence of a completed execution receipt” is outcome-blind only if absence cannot be chosen after the process has computed or exposed the outcome. The draft does not establish that. A runner, operator, crash handler, or writable wrapper can observe an undesirable χ and fail to publish the completed receipt; attempt 1 then becomes lawful, and “first successful attempt by index” selects it. Enumerating all attempts and refusing two published successes does not reveal the suppressed success. The same problem affects acquisition if a process can inspect image bytes before deciding whether an attempt counts as failed.

The fixtures exercise manifest shapes after the fact. They do not demonstrate that a computed result cannot be converted into `NO_COMPLETED_RECEIPT`, which is the exact post-inference freedom the retry rule must close.

**Smallest sufficient repair.** Put retries under a pinned external supervisor that registers each attempt before launch, mediates all process output, atomically commits launch/exit/output-presence state, and withholds outcome access from operators. Define the exact retry-eligible terminal states from supervisor evidence, including crash and timeout boundaries. Once any output bytes exist or inference reaches the committed-output transition, suppression or crash must refuse the run rather than authorize a retry. Every launch, exit, timeout, output commitment, and retry authorization must form one authenticated append-only chain; fixtures must inject crashes before and after output creation and prove that post-output failures cannot become retry eligibility.

### F5 — BLOCKER — the blindness fixtures and call-graph probe exercise invariance after freezing the possible covert channel

**Clause / field at issue.** R6 barrier 4, lines 197–200; fixture battery lines 632–650; custody fixture lines 654–659. Verifiers: source/call-graph inspection and `run_bs2a_fixtures_v1()`.

**Why it fails as a promise.** `BS2A-SIGN-SWAP-INVARIANT` changes sealed χ while holding the acceptance receipt unchanged. `BS2A-AXIS-PERMUTATION-INVARIANT` changes geometry while holding permitted evidence fixed. `BS2A-AMPlITUDE-NOT-QUALITY` changes `abs(chi_net)` while holding the quality receipt fixed. All three are true by construction even if the receipt producer originally encoded sign, amplitude, or axis position into `quality_score`, `execution_state`, an attempt choice, or a digest. They test the consumer after the suspected channel has been frozen, not the producer that could create the channel.

A reachable-globals/callables inspection is not a capability boundary in Python. Dynamic imports, filesystem/network access, subprocesses, native extensions, environment state, object-ID lookup tables, and opaque digest lookups need not expose a forbidden symbol in the inspected call graph. Likewise, calling a dataclass constructor “private” does not prevent minting or deserialization across a process boundary without an external authenticator. The fixtures can all pass while the mechanism remains outcome-bearing.

**Smallest sufficient repair.** Make the fixture battery end-to-end: rerun every evidence producer from transformed admissible inputs, then byte-compare the complete evidence bundle, predicates, ledger, and partition. Include handedness reversal, amplitude-changing pairs that preserve allowed pre-inference quality evidence, attempted digest/attempt-state encoding, late expected-digest issuance, selective runner suppression, dynamic import/file/network escape, and forged validated-partition objects. Run producers in a hermetic allowlist sandbox with no ambient filesystem, network, subprocess, environment-secret, dynamic-code, or sealed-store capability; verify the sandbox profile and runtime provenance externally. Authenticate the validated partition with an externally pinned verifier/signature rather than relying on Python constructor privacy.

## Checks that held / failed attacks

1. **Partition closure held at the design-contract level.** The draft requires exact set equality against an independently pinned 65,060-ID parent witness for both evidence and ledger, strict uniqueness/order, and exactly one terminal row. Count equality alone is explicitly rejected. If the named validator is implemented against an external parent witness, this is a real partition rather than an asserted total.
2. **Caller-label replay held at the terminal-row level.** Status, reason, predicate bits, evidence digest, and row digest are all recomputed and compared; a caller cannot merely flip `ACCEPTED` to `EXCLUDED` without refusal. F3 concerns truth of the lower-level evidence authored upstream, not terminal-ledger replay.
3. **Threshold-home attack held in the proposed revision.** The draft puts operative `QUALITY_MIN`, its comparison rule, quality-producer digest, and calibration receipt only in BS-2a and requires old `tau` to become historical/non-operative or the design to refuse. No second operative threshold is authorized by this draft.
4. **Boundary behavior held.** Exclusion is strict `< QUALITY_MIN`; equality is accepted; binary64/`nextafter` fixtures are named. No tolerance or execution-time boundary choice remains.
5. **Reason-vocabulary attack held syntactically.** The four terminal reasons and fixed precedence are closed, with no `other`, manual-review, morphology, scientific-interest, sign-balance, or free-text path. Findings F1–F4 concern whether the evidence that activates those reasons is outcome-blind and true.
6. **The draft does not falsely claim BS-2a is filled.** It consistently says the quality producer, target/calibration constitution, numeric threshold, code, schemas, isolated worker, canonical bytes, fixtures, and conforming edits must all be gated before BS-6 and before the first image byte. There is no authorized list of decisions that may be made after images exist; the defect is that the presently selected quality mechanism would still be insufficient even if those placeholders were filled as currently specified.
