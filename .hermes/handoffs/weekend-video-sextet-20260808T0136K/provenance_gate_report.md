# Provenance-gate documentation: GRB angular clustering and parity-odd galaxy 4PCF

**Scope.** Primary official documentation and primary analysis papers only [unverified]. `UNDOCUMENTED` means “not specified in the checked primary sources,” not proof that no unpublished/internal convention exists [unverified]. A split grade is used when the survey release is documented but the exact analysis input is not [unverified].

## A. GRB angular clustering

### Fermi GBM

| Gate item | Primary evidence (exact URL and verbatim short quote) | Grade | Missing load-bearing item(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed release identity | Fourth catalog paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11460 — “covering the 10 year time period from trigger enabling on 2008 July 12 to 2018 July 11.” The HEASARC table is explicitly live: https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/w3browse/fermi/fermigbrst.html — “The information in this table is updated automatically.” [1][2] | **DOCUMENTED** for the fixed fourth/10-year catalog; **UNDOCUMENTED** for a frozen extract of the live HEASARC table | If the live table is used: exact query, retrieval timestamp, returned-row manifest, and file checksum(s). |
| Coordinate frame | https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/w3browse/fermi/fermigbrst.html — “The Right Ascension of the burst in decimal degrees. The equinox for this coordinate is selected by the Equinox parameter. Equinox 2000.0” [1] | **DOCUMENTED** | None if the query is explicitly pinned to Equinox 2000.0. |
| Localization uncertainty | HEASARC: “The one-sigma statistical error radius of the location of the burst, in degrees.” Fourth-catalog paper: “There is additionally a systematic error that we have characterized for HitL localizations as a core-plus-tail model” and “Probability maps reflecting the total uncertainty … are the convolution of the statistical uncertainty with the best current model for the systematic errors.” https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/w3browse/fermi/fermigbrst.html ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11460 [1][2] | **DOCUMENTED**, with an important distinction between the catalog radius and total localization PDF | Per-burst choice of localization source; exact HEALPix probability-map filename/version/checksum; rule for bursts without a total-error map. The scalar `Error_Radius` alone is not total uncertainty. |
| Exposure / angular selection function | The fourth-catalog paper documents time-dependent operations, e.g. “During weekend times trigger algorithms 22–26 were disabled” and says non-nominal trigger intervals are listed online, but neither it nor the checked HEASARC table provides a catalog-matched sky-exposure map. https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11460 [2] | **UNDOCUMENTED** | Machine-readable exposure versus sky position and time for the exact catalog interval, including Earth occultation, SAA/instrument-off intervals, detector/trigger enable states, and their uncertainty. |
| Trigger efficiency / completeness | The live table exposes trigger algorithm, timescale and energy range, but the checked primary catalog sources do not publish a calibrated detection-efficiency function or a catalog-wide completeness model. https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/w3browse/fermi/fermigbrst.html ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11460 [1][2] | **UNDOCUMENTED** | Detection probability as a joint function of peak flux/fluence, duration, spectrum, sky position, background, detector geometry, and time-dependent trigger configuration; a documented complete subsample rule. |

**Fermi gate summary:** release/coordinates/localization are documented only after fixing the 10-year catalog and the per-burst total localization products [1][2]. The exposure and catalog-matched trigger/completeness functions remain load-bearing undocumented inputs [1][2].

### BATSE 4Br

| Gate item | Primary evidence (exact URL and verbatim short quote) | Grade | Missing load-bearing item(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Release identity | https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_basic.html — “specifies the locations and times for 1637 triggered gamma-ray bursts observed from 19 April, 1991 until 29 August, 1996.” It also states: “The current version (4Br) has been revised from the version first circulated on CD-ROM in September 1997 (4B).” [3] | **DOCUMENTED** | A modern cryptographic checksum is not stated on the documentation page; pin the downloadable table bytes if byte-level identity is required. |
| Coordinate frame | Same official page: “right ascension (J2000) in decimal degrees” and “declination (J2000) in decimal degrees.” https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_basic.html [3] | **DOCUMENTED** | None. |
| Localization uncertainty | Primary 4B paper: “The tabulated error radius represents the radius of a circle that has the same area as the 68% statistical confidence region.” It then gives the core/tail systematic model: “78% of the time the systematic error belongs to the core with a value of 1.85°, while the remainder … [has] 5.1°.” https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9903205 [7] | **DOCUMENTED** | Exact implementation choice among the simple core/tail model and the data-type-dependent model; whether to represent the non-circular statistical contour or only the equal-area circle. |
| Sky exposure | https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_exposure.html — “specifies the fraction of the time that a burst at a particular point on the sky is detectable by BATSE, and accounts for earth blockage, SAA passages, times when the burst trigger is disabled, and times when a burst readout is in progress.” It also states: “The uncertainty in the exposure fractions are roughly estimated at about 4%.” [4] | **DOCUMENTED** | None for the published declination-dependent bright-burst exposure, provided its stated ≈4% uncertainty is propagated. |
| Nominal trigger efficiency and trigger-history corrections | https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_efficiency.html — “The following table gives the efficiency for generating a burst trigger as a function of peak flux on each of the three trigger time scales.” The configuration history is public at https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_trigger_criteria.html — “contains a history of the GRB trigger criteria.” [5][6] | **DOCUMENTED**, but conditional | The nominal efficiency assumes a spectral index and nominal settings. The page states: “The effects of atmospheric scattering are not included in this table. As a result, the efficiencies at low fluxes are underestimated.” |
| Exact catalog-wide completeness | The exposure page requires a “separate efficiency correction”; the efficiency applies only to nominal thresholds/energy range, while the criteria changed over time. https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_exposure.html ; https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_efficiency.html ; https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_trigger_criteria.html [4][5][6] | **UNDOCUMENTED** as one exact joint selection function | A combined time × declination × flux × spectrum × duration completeness function that incorporates every non-nominal trigger interval, atmospheric scattering, and telemetry gaps. |

**BATSE gate summary:** fixed release, J2000 coordinates, localization model, bright-burst exposure, nominal trigger efficiency, and trigger history are public [3][4][5]. The missing load-bearing object is a single exact joint completeness function across the heterogeneous release [4][5][6].

## C. Exact kinematic-dipole subtraction convention

| Probe | Finding | Grade | Missing load-bearing item(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fermi GBM angular catalog | The checked Fermi HEASARC documentation and fourth-catalog paper specify J2000 positions, localization errors/maps, trigger settings and operations, but do **not** specify a kinematic number-count dipole or aberration subtraction. [1][2] | **UNDOCUMENTED**; not a documented catalog preprocessing step | If such a subtraction is required by the estimand: observer velocity vector, rest frame, sign convention, spectral/count-slope factor, aberration treatment, and whether it is applied to events, exposure, or model. |
| BATSE 4Br angular catalog | BATSE documents **exposure** dipole/quadrupole moments: “The values of <sin(dec)> and <sin²(dec)> − 1/3 are the dipole and quadrupole moments in the equatorial coordinate system.” This is an exposure characterization, not a kinematic-dipole subtraction. No kinematic subtraction convention appears in the checked 4Br pages or primary paper. https://gammaray.msfc.nasa.gov/batse/grb/catalog/4b/4br_exposure.html ; https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9903205 [4][7] | **UNDOCUMENTED**; no exact kinematic subtraction is part of the documented 4Br corrections | Same observer-velocity/rest-frame/count-slope/aberration convention if an additional kinematic correction is intended. |
| BOSS DR12 parity-odd 4PCF | The checked primary 4PCF method documents converting sky coordinates to Cartesian coordinates with a fiducial cosmology, random-catalog edge correction, survey weights, mocks and covariance; searches for `kinematic`, `CMB frame`, and `heliocentric` found no subtraction convention in the primary parity paper. https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.03625 [12] | **UNDOCUMENTED**; no named kinematic-dipole subtraction is a documented step | If imposed as an extra systematic treatment: precise redshift rest frame, observer velocity/vector, aberration/Doppler remapping, and whether mocks/randoms receive the same operation. |
| DESI DR1 parity-odd 4PCF | The same targeted terms (`kinematic`, `CMB frame`, `heliocentric`) do not appear as a subtraction convention in the checked DESI parity paper; the documented corrections are catalog completeness/fiber treatment, random edge correction and covariance calibration. https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20132 [20] | **UNDOCUMENTED**; no named kinematic-dipole subtraction is a documented step | Same rest-frame/vector/remapping convention, and exact propagation to data, randoms and mocks, if required externally. |

**Interpretation:** none of the four checked primary method chains documents an exact kinematic-dipole subtraction [2][7][20]. BATSE’s published dipole moments refer to the instrument exposure [4]. For the galaxy 4PCFs, the documented coordinate operation is fiducial-cosmology conversion, not observer-motion subtraction [12][20]. No claim is made here that an extra kinematic convention is scientifically required; only that no such convention is part of the public provenance chain checked [12][20].

## Bottom-line documentation grades

- **Fermi GBM:** release/coordinates/localizations **DOCUMENTED** after a fixed 10-year release and map manifest are pinned; exposure and trigger/completeness **UNDOCUMENTED** [1][2].
- **BATSE 4Br:** release/coordinates/localizations/exposure/nominal efficiency/history **DOCUMENTED**; exact joint catalog-wide completeness **UNDOCUMENTED** [3][4][5].
- **BOSS DR12:** release/masks/randoms/weights/completeness/fiber corrections/coordinates/mocks/covariance construction **DOCUMENTED**; frozen final 4PCF covariance artifact **UNDOCUMENTED** [8][11][12].
- **DESI DR1:** public DR1 products and method components **DOCUMENTED**; exact parity-paper LSScats version/flavor **UNDOCUMENTED**; public altMTL history **UNDOCUMENTED/unavailable**; frozen final 4PCF covariance artifact/schema **UNDOCUMENTED** [13][14][20].
- **Kinematic-dipole subtraction:** **UNDOCUMENTED** in all checked primary chains; BATSE’s exposure-dipole moments are not such a subtraction [2][7][12].
