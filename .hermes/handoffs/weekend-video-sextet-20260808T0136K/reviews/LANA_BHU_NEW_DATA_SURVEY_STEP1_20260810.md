## Step 1 — BHU's content, sorted, from the sources

### (a) Predictions that follow from the idea
- **A closed, nonsingular universe from a torsion bounce.** Primary, verbatim — Popławski, *Universe in a
  black hole with spin and torsion*, arXiv:1410.3881: *"This scenario can thus explain inflation without a
  scalar field and reheating."* The interior universe is nonsingular and closed (no initial singularity; a
  bounce). **Assessment: genuine, but non-distinguishing.** A closed/nearly-flat universe is consistent with
  current data and is predicted by many models; a bounce is not observable in a BHU-unique way. Nothing new
  data could measure would single out BHU here.
- **A preferred axis inherited from the parent black hole's rotation.** Reported (SECONDARY — press/summary,
  **not** found in the primary abstracts I fetched; must be primary-verified before use): that "the universe
  born in a rotating black hole inherits the axis of rotation of the black hole as its preferred axis."
  **Assessment: a genuine *qualitative* prediction (a global anisotropy / preferred axis), but with no
  predicted amplitude — so it is not sharply falsifiable — and its signatures are degenerate (see c).**
  ⚠️ Do not put this quote on any surface without confirming it verbatim from Popławski's own paper; I have
  it only from secondary reporting, and the GZ1 line died on exactly this kind of unverified directional
  claim.

### (b) Observations people have merely ASSOCIATED with it (not BHU predictions)
- **Galaxy spin-direction asymmetry.** Primary, verbatim — Shamir, *Galaxy spin direction asymmetry in JWST
  deep fields*, arXiv:2403.17271: *"among 34 galaxies for which their rotation of direction can be
  determined by the shapes of the arms, 24 rotate clockwise, and just 10 rotate counterclockwise"*; *"The
  one-tailed binomial distribution probability to have asymmetry equal or stronger than the observed
  asymmetry by chance is ~0.012."* **Note the tell: the abstract itself makes no reference to BHU, isotropy,
  a preferred axis, or a rotating universe** — the BHU connection is added by press and interpretation, not
  by the empirical paper. The claim is also small-sample and **contested**, and the contest is precisely the
  classifier-bias problem that killed GZ1.
- **CMB hemispherical asymmetry / "axis of evil"; the radio-galaxy cosmic dipole.** Associated with a
  preferred axis; not BHU predictions.

### (c) Consistent with BHU but equally with much else — where the subject dissolves
A galaxy-spin dipole or a preferred cosmic axis is **also** expected or accommodated by: **primordial parity
violation from inflation**; a generic **rotating (Gödel-type) or anisotropic (Bianchi) cosmology**; and
**residual classification systematics**. Therefore even a robust detection would be *consistent with* BHU
but would **not uniquely confirm it**, and a null would **not kill it** (its amplitude is unpredicted). This
is the category-(c) dissolution the order warned about, and it is where BHU's one axis-related prediction
lands.
