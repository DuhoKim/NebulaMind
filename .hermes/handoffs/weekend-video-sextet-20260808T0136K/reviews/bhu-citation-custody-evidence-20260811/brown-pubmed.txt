TI  - Kaon condensation, black holes, and cosmological natural selection.
PG  - 091101
AB  - It is argued that a well-measured double neutron-star binary in which the two
neutron stars are more than 4% different from each other in mass or a massive
neutron star with mass M > or approximately 2M(middle dot in circle) would put in
serious doubt or simply falsify the following chain of predictions: (1) a nearly
vanishing vector meson mass at chiral restoration, (2) kaon condensation at a
density n-3n0, (3) the Brown-Bethe maximum neutron-star mass Mmax approximately
1.5M(middle dot in circle), and (4) Smolin's "cosmological natural selection"
hypothesis.
