## The finding that decided it (assessed against the paper's own source, not from memory)
`paper-backups/quartet-rewrites-20260723T141609Z/p1_z9-10-metallicity-deficit/after.tex`

- **No original measurement exists in the paper.** Every high-z abundance and stellar mass is
  adopted from published work (Nakajima+2023 direct-Te subset; Pollock+2026 unlensed field;
  Isobe+2026 stacked-Te). Both local anchors are published parametrizations (Curti+2020;
  Andrews & Martini 2013). The central operation is a subtraction:
  Δ = (12+log O/H)_obs − MZR(log M★). No spectrum was reduced, no line flux measured, no
  abundance derived. Sample: N=5–6 galaxies.
- **The conclusion was already published, by a larger sample the paper itself cites.**
  Isobe+2026 report the same normalization deficit from ~1500 stacked JADES spectra. A five-
  galaxy re-analysis agreeing with a 1500-galaxy published result is a consistency check.
- **What was genuinely ours is second-order but real**: the single-Te-scale restriction, the
  unlensed-only cut (after Deep Research caught that the "clean" subset was lens-contaminated),
  the local-anchor swap quantifying that systematic at 0.042 dex, leave-one-out, the error
  budget, and the Te-scale Monte Carlo that deflated a formal ~22σ to ~4.5σ. The deflation is
  the most valuable act in the paper — the crew refusing a number its systematics cannot carry.
- **Framing risk**: the title's "Independent" means independent *of lensed anchors*; a reader
  hears "independent measurement". The conclusion does state plainly "not a validated
  measurement and not a formal statistical detection", and the review loop records that no
  human ever reviewed it and the referee was a local 70b model (astrosage-70b).

## Standard this sets
A flagship study must contain something that exists nowhere else — a measurement, a census, or
a negative result established under a pre-registered contract. Assembly of published values plus
a systematics commentary does not qualify, however careful the commentary. This is the same bar
that rejected the nine autopilot papers; it now applies to hand-guided lanes too.
